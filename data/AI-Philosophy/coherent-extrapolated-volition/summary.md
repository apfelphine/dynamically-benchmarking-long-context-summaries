Coherent extrapolated volition (CEV) is a theoretical framework in the field of AI alignment proposed by Eliezer Yudkowsky in 2004 as part of his work on friendly AI. It describes an approach by which an artificial superintelligence (ASI) would act not according to humanity's current individual or collective preferences, but instead based on what humans would want—if they were more knowledgeable, more rational, had more time to think, and had matured together as a society.
