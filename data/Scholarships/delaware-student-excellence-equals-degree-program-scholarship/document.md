# Delaware Student Excellence Equals Degree Program Scholarship

## Criteria
The criteria to be awarded are:

1. Must be a resident of Delaware and planning/are to be a full time student.[1]
2. Must not have been convicted of any felony.[1]
3. Have already applied for other forms of financial aid.[1]
4. Must have graduated from a Delaware High School and have obtained a 2.5 GPA in high school (on a 4.0 scale).[1]
  - The student must maintain at least a 2.5 GPA throughout college as well.[1]
5. Gain admission to Delaware Tech or the University of Delaware and satisfy their respective admissions standards.[1]
  - A Delaware Tech student is not eligible for the scholarship if they've been admitted "by special admission standards."[1]
6. Must attend college classes no later than the fall semester after high school.[1]
7. Must be eligible for the scholarship under their admitted colleges implemented criteria.[1]


If the student lived in foster care while 16 - 18 years old, points 4 and 6 do not apply. In this case the student must have started their Associates degree before the age of 25 and take no more than 5 years to obtain it.

In order to maintain eligibility and funding for the scholarship, a student must:

1. Earn at least 24 credits as a full time student per year.[1]
  - This requirement has been waived due to the COVID-19 pandemic.[4]
  - If the student has lived in foster care while 16 - 18 years old, this requirement does not apply.[1]
2. Maintain continuous enrollment of at least 2 semesters per year.[1]
  - The college may grant an exception and waive this requirement.[1]
3. Maintain a college GPA of at least 2.5.[1]
  - The college may grant an exception and waive this requirement.[1]
4. The student’s family must certify that the student has not been convicted of a felony.[1]
