# Khalifa University

## Campuses
Khalifa University has two campuses in Abu Dhabi – the Main Campus and the Sas Al Nakhl Campus.

The KU Main Campus is located on Abu Dhabi Island. At Main Campus, the central administration for the University is located and all general education courses are taught. It has 135,000 square meters and houses 105 laboratories, including aerospace and underwater robotics labs, 72 classrooms, sports center, retail and food outlets, and multi-story parking.

A campus extension was completed in 2016 with a new building that won the award for best Cultural and Institutional Project at the 2015 Middle East and North Africa (MENA) Interior Design and Architecture Awards. It also has one library, a clinic, a student hub with a dining room, and numerous cafes and restaurants. 

In July 2024, the Abu Dhabi Investment Office (ADIO) and Khalifa University awarded a project through its public-private partnership program to develop world-class student accommodation and facilities in the UAE capital. Awarded to a consortium named KUnnected Living, made up of Plenary Group, Besix and Mazrui International. It includes a 23-year concession to design, build, finance and maintain 3,260 student rooms and communal facilities located across Khalifa University's Main Campus and its Sas Al Nakhl (SAN) Campus.

The Sas Al Nakhl Campus hosts the ADNOC Research and Innovation Center (ADRIC), a research facility comprising 39 labs covering reservoir rock characterization and reservoir fluid characterization. It has student residences, exercise rooms, sports courts and fields, dining facility, cafeterias, two libraries, and an Independent Learning Center (ILC). The campus also has First Aid Clinics.

### International & Civil Security
Distinct in the Gulf, Khalifa University's Institute for International and Civil Security (IICS) seeks to "produce research which will strengthen the security of the UAE in a way that is responsive to the needs of national and global security decision-makers ... enabling the programme to make major contributions that enhance regional, international and human security." ## Collaborations
In 2019 Khalifa University launched Emirates Nuclear Technology Centre in collaboration with Emirates Nuclear Energy Corporation and the Federal Authority for Nuclear Regulation.

In February 2024, the ELT Group and Khalifa University signed an MoU (Memorandum of Understanding) to expand the knowledge of the management of the Electromagnetic Spectrum in the UAE.

In 2023 within the framework of the memorandum of understanding (MoU) signed earlier to establish academic cooperation in developing aeronautical education and related research in the UAE, Khalifa University of Science and Technology and Dassault Aviation of France announced they are collaborating on a research project that focuses on developing two new material technologies for aerospace applications with potential for patenting and commercialization.

Naval Group and Khalifa University signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) to collaborate in innovative research fields for naval and maritime applications in the United Arab Emirates.


## History
Khalifa University of Science and Technology was established in February 2017, following a decree issued by the UAE President Sheikh Khalifa Bin Zayed Al Nahyan, on the merger of three universities – Khalifa University of Science, Technology and Research, The Petroleum Institute and Masdar Institute of Science and Technology.

Originally established in 1989 as the Etisalat College of Engineering (ECE), the school's main campus was built in Sharjah, and it aimed to supply the Emirates Telecommunications Corporation (Etisalat – now e&) with qualified, trained and fully-equipped human capital.

In 2007, the legacy institution Khalifa University of Science, Technology and Research (KUSTAR), had been established by a decree issued by the UAE President Sheikh Khalifa Bin Zayed Al Nahyan, in an effort to support a knowledge-based economy.


## Academic partnership
In July 2024, Khalifa University launched a dual PhD program in the field of Biomedical Sciences and Engineering, in partnership with KU Leuven of Belgium, providing students from both institutions the opportunity to gain international research experience.

Earlier, Khalifa University established a number of international academic and research partnerships. They include associations with the Georgia Institute of Technology, Texas A&M University Nuclear Security Science & Policy Institute, and KAIST. In April 2011, Khalifa University signed a memorandum of understanding with the University of Bristol to establish and create the Visual Signal Analysis and Processing (VSAP) research centre at the university's Abu Dhabi campus.

On December 8, 2019 Khalifa University, the University of Milan and the Scuola Superiore Sant’Anna of Pisa signed an agreement to set up a joint lab for embodied artificial intelligence ( AI) in Abu Dhabi.

In 2022, Khalifa and Manchester Universities launched a partnership in the field of graphene innovation.
