**University Profiles: Global Comparison**

This summary compares leading global universities, detailing their unique features, advancements, and associated institutional matters.

**University of New South Wales (UNSW):** UNSW is a technology-focused institution that broadened into a generalist university in 1958, operating across multiple campuses (including Randwick, Manly Vale Northern Beaches, and UNSW Canberra, which has a defense focus). It is structured around six faculties (Arts, Design & Architecture, Business, Engineering, Law and Justice, Medicine & Health, and Science), ranked #4 nationally in AFR 2024, and boasts high graduate employment and research strength (e.g., Lowy Cancer Research Centre). Undergraduate entry requires an ATAR, and support includes scholarships, a Co-op program, and overseas exchanges with 250+ partners. A subsidiary, UNSW College, provides education for students from years 3 through 12 internationally and has notable political and business alumni. However, UNSW has faced several controversies, including legal disputes over copyright, staff misconduct, privacy breaches, and investigations into research misconduct and underpayment of casual staff.

**Other University Highlights:**

*   **National Autonomous University of Mexico (UNAM):** Mexico's top secular national university (est. 1910) serving over 324,000 students, noted for its leadership in AI and robotics and its commitment to inclusivity and distance learning.
*   **University of Hong Kong (HKU):** A highly ranked international university (#11 in QS 2026) emphasizing internationalism through English-taught programs while maintaining a Chinese cultural focus.
*   **University of Melbourne:** An Australian research university (#19 in 2026 QS) recognized for its "Melbourne Model" of generalized three-year degrees, supported by a substantial endowment.
*   **National University of Singapore (NUS):** Established in 1980 (#8 in QS 2026), NUS blends British and American educational styles, specializing heavily in Engineering and Computer Science across 21 research institutes.
*   **University of British Columbia (UBC):** Manages a large budget and prioritizes sustainability and Indigenous support alongside robust research.
*   **Cornell University:** A large, non-profit R1 university with a \$10.7 billion endowment, currently managing discussions regarding antisemitism and DEI.
*   **University of Toronto (U of T):** Since 1827, this decentralized university excels in global affairs, public health, and economic theory.
*   **Federal University of Rio de Janeiro (UFRJ):** A major public institution (est. 1920) integrating academics with Rio's health sector and employing ENEM/affirmative action policies.
*   **University of Oxford:** Dating back to the 12th century, Oxford operates as a non-centralized "city university" reliant on college affiliation, holding an £8.708 billion endowment and using the UCAS tutorial system.
