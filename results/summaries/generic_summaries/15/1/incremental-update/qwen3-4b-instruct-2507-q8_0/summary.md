It maintains a main campus in Ithaca with diverse architectural styles and historic landmarks, and operates in New York City via Weill Cornell Medicine and Cornell Tech (on Roosevelt Island), as well as international partnerships in Asia and Qatar. The University of Hong Kong (HKU) has a main campus in Pok Fu Lam featuring historic British colonial architecture, including the Main Building (a declared monument), Swire Building, Hung Hing Ying Building, and Tang Chi Ngong Building. The **National University of Singapore (NUS)** was founded in 1905 as a medical school and evolved through mergers: with Raffles College (1949), Nanyang University (1955), and the University of Singapore (1962), culminating in the formation of NUS in 1980. NUS operates across three campuses and includes a music conservatory, with 17 faculties and schools.
