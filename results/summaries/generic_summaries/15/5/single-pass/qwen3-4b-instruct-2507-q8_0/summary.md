### **Cornell University**
Founded in 1865 by Ezra Cornell and Andrew Dickson White, Cornell is a land-, sea-, and space-grant university with a strong emphasis on research and innovation. It began as New York’s first land-grant institution and evolved into a leader in academic excellence, with top rankings in research, engineering, and the humanities. Key developments include becoming one of the first electrified universities in 1883, establishing the Atkinson Center for Sustainability, and opening Weill Cornell Medical College in Qatar. The Ithaca campus features a mix of architectural styles, with notable buildings listed on the National Register of Historic Places. Cornell’s decentralized structure includes 16 colleges, with strong philanthropic support (endowment of $10.7 billion as of 2024). It is known for its commitment to sustainability, international programs, and student life, including a diverse student body and vibrant student organizations.

### **National University of Singapore (NUS)**
Established in 1905, NUS began as a medical school and evolved into a leading research university through mergers with Raffles College and Nanyang University in 1962. It is known for its high international profile, diverse faculty (65% foreign), and strong research output. NUS is ranked among the world’s top universities in QS and THE rankings, with excellence in engineering, computer science, and medicine. The university operates across three campuses in Singapore, including the Centennial Campus, and maintains a strong focus on entrepreneurship through the NUS Entrepreneurship Centre. NUS has a diverse student body (36% foreign nationals) and is recognized globally for its academic reputation, research output, and international collaborations.

### **Federal University of Rio de Janeiro (UFRJ)**
Founded in 1920, UFRJ is a public university with a long history of academic and scientific excellence. It is known for its interdisciplinary structure, with six major academic centers and a strong emphasis on research. The university has a large student population and a strong tradition of innovation, including pioneering work in biotechnology and computing. UFRJ is home to the prestigious Institute of Physics and the Centre for Biomedical Sciences. The university has a strong commitment to student welfare, with a history of student activism and a notable legacy in science and engineering. It is also known for its extensive research infrastructure and its role in advancing medical and technological innovation in Brazil.

### **University of Toronto**
Founded in 1827 as King's College, the University of Toronto is one of Canada’s most prestigious institutions, with a long history of academic and social innovation. It is known for its strong research output, with a focus on medicine, engineering, and the social sciences. The university has a diverse student body and is known for its strong student life, with a vibrant student union and a wide range of student organizations. The university is home to the prestigious Rotman School of Management and the Dalla Lana School of Public Health. It is also known for its contributions to research in artificial intelligence, stem cell biology, and public health. The university has a strong commitment to sustainability and is recognized for its leadership in climate change research and education.

### **University of New South Wales (UNSW)**
Founded in 1949 as the New South Wales University of Technology, UNSW has evolved into a leading research university with a strong focus on engineering, business, and health sciences. It is known for its strong research output, with a focus on innovation and applied research. UNSW is known for its strong international partnerships, with over 250 overseas exchange programs. The university has a diverse student body and is recognized for its strong focus on student engagement and student life. It is also known for its strong research output in areas such as cancer research and renewable energy. However, it has faced criticism for its handling of student mental health and administrative practices.

### **University of Oxford**
One of the oldest universities in the world, Oxford was founded in the 12th century and is renowned for its academic excellence, research output, and global influence. The university is structured around a collegiate system with 39 colleges, each with its own unique identity and traditions. It is known for its rigorous academic standards, with a strong focus on tutorial teaching and research. Oxford is a leading institution in the fields of law, humanities, and science, and is recognized globally for its contributions to knowledge and innovation. The university has a strong tradition of academic leadership and is known for its rich history and cultural heritage. It is also recognized for its contributions to public policy, science, and the arts.

### **University of British Columbia (UBC)**
Founded in 1908, UBC is a leading research university in Canada with a strong focus on sustainability, innovation, and public service. The university is known for its strong research output, with a focus on environmental science, health, and engineering. UBC has a diverse student body and is known for its strong student life, with a vibrant student union and a wide range of student organizations.
