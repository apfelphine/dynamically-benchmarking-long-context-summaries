## **Cornell University**

### **History**
Cornell University was founded in **1865** by Ezra Cornell and Andrew Dickson White, initially as a land-grant institution in Ithaca, New York. It became one of the first electrified universities in **1883**, emphasizing research and outreach. The university expanded its academic and research capabilities, including the establishment of the **Weill Cornell Medical College in Qatar** in **2004**. Cornell has faced challenges, such as the **1967 dormitory fire** and student activism during the **1960s**, but has consistently ranked among the top universities globally.

### **Campuses**
- **Ithaca Campus**: The main campus, located on East Hill, features historic buildings, modern facilities, and sustainability initiatives. It includes the **Cornell Botanic Gardens** and is recognized for its architectural diversity.
- **New York City Campuses**:
  - **Weill Cornell Medical College**: Located in Manhattan, it is affiliated with NewYork-Presbyterian Hospital.
  - **Cornell Tech**: A graduate campus in Roosevelt Island, focusing on technology and entrepreneurship.
- **Other Facilities**: Includes research labs, study abroad programs, and the **Cornell University Press**, one of the oldest university presses in the U.S.

### **Organization and Administration**
Cornell operates under a **decentralized structure** with **16 colleges**, including **12 privately endowed** and **4 publicly supported statutory colleges**. The university is governed by a **64-member board of trustees**, with the president as the chief executive. Notable governance changes include the transition of **Michael Kotlikoff** to interim president in **2024**.

### **Academics**
Cornell is a **research-intensive university**, offering a wide range of undergraduate and graduate programs. It is known for its **highly competitive admissions** and strong rankings in various fields, including **engineering, medicine, and business**. The university has a **need-blind admission policy** and provides extensive financial aid, including scholarships and grants.

### **Research**
Cornell is classified as an **"R1: Doctoral University"** with significant research funding. Key research areas include **nuclear physics, space exploration, automotive safety, and computing technology**. The university has a strong patenting record and has contributed to advancements in **medicine, engineering, and environmental science**.

### **Student Life**
Cornell offers over **1,000 student organizations**, including **club sports, performing arts, political groups, and media**. The university has a **large fraternity and sorority system**, with notable traditions such as **Slope Day** and **Dragon Day**. Student media includes **The Cornell Daily Sun** and **The Cornell Review**. The university is known for its **diverse student body** and commitment to sustainability.

### **Notable People**
Cornell has produced **over 250,000 alumni**, including **34 Marshall Scholars and 31 Rhodes Scholars**. Notable figures include **Willis Carrier** (inventor of air conditioning), **Christopher Reeve** (actor), and **Bill Nye** (science educator).

## **National University of Singapore (NUS)**

### **History**
NUS was established in **1962** through the merger of the **University of Singapore** and **Nanyang University**. The university adopted English as its primary language of instruction and has since grown into a **global research institution**. NUS celebrated its **120th anniversary in 2025**, marking significant milestones in its academic and research achievements.

### **Campuses**
- **Kent Ridge Campus**: The main campus, located in Singapore, includes **17 faculties and schools**, such as the **NUS Business School** and **Yong Loo Lin School of Medicine**. It is known for its modern architecture and extensive green spaces.
- **Bukit Timah and Outram Campuses**: Additional campuses for specialized programs, including the **Duke-NUS Medical School**.
- **Other Facilities**: Includes the **NUS Libraries**, **Digital Gems Digital Newspaper Archive**, and **University Town**.

### **Organization and Administration**
NUS is governed by a **vice-chancellor** and a **board of trustees**. The university operates under a **semester-based modular system**, with **17 faculties and schools** across three campuses. Notable figures include **Tan Eng Chye**, the current vice-chancellor.

### **Academics**
NUS offers a wide range of **undergraduate and graduate programs**, with a focus on **internationalization and interdisciplinary research**. The university is known for its **strong rankings in business, engineering, and medicine**. NUS is committed to fostering a **diverse and inclusive academic environment**.

### **Research**
NUS is recognized for its **cutting-edge research** in areas such as **biomedical science, physical science, engineering, and humanities**. The university has established several **research institutes and centers**, including the **Cancer Science Institute of Singapore** and the **Centre for Quantum Technologies**.

### **Student Life**
NUS has a **diverse student population**, with **36% of students being international**. The university offers numerous **student organizations**, including **sports clubs, cultural groups, and academic societies**. Notable traditions include **University Town events** and **student-led exchange programs**.

### **Notable People**
NUS has produced many **distinguished alumni**, including **Lee Kuan Yew** (former Prime Minister of Singapore), **Margaret Chan** (former Director-General of the World Health Organization), and **Kishore Mahbubani** (former President of the United Nations Security Council).
