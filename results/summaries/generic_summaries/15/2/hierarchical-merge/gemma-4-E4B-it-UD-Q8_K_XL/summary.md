## Global University Profiles

### Notable Institutions by Region

**North America & Latin America**
Cornell University is a highly selective, nonprofit, research-intensive institution with a large endowment, noted for its STEM excellence and sustainability focus. In Latin America, the National Autonomous University of Mexico (UNAM) is a massive, highly ranked institution recognized for its research and historical student activism, and it notably supports minority advocacy, including initiatives to promote women in technology.

**United Kingdom & Oceania**
The University of Oxford, a historic "city university," operates through a unique college and tutorial system and possesses vast resources like the Bodleian Libraries. In Australia, the University of Melbourne is a high-ranking, historic institution facing recent operational hurdles, while the University of New South Wales (UNSW) has achieved high graduate employer satisfaction, though it has contended with controversies regarding staff pay and research misconduct.

**Asia & Canada**
The National University of Singapore (NUS) is a highly internationalized institution, ranking globally and featuring strong research in biomedical science and engineering, while promoting entrepreneurship. In Canada, the University of Toronto (U of T) is a vast, decentralized leader with Canada's largest research budget, and UBC is recognized for its commitment to sustainability and Indigenous relations.

**Other Major Universities**
In Europe, the University of Oxford exemplifies historical depth, while in South America, the Federal University of Rio de Janeiro (UFRJ) is a large public autarchy focused on research and public health. Globally, the University of Hong Kong (HKU) is a highly international research institution, recognized for its diverse student body, despite facing political scrutiny over academic freedom.
