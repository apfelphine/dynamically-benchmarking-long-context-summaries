## Summary

### National Autonomous University of Mexico (UNAM)
Established in 1910, UNAM is recognized for its secular, autonomous status, academic freedom, Nobel laureates, leadership in AI and renewable energy, and its UNESCO-listed Ciudad Universitaria campus.

### University of Melbourne
Founded in 1853, the University of Melbourne is distinguished by its “Melbourne Model,” broad undergraduate and specialized graduate study, 11 faculties, strong graduate employability, staff reductions, and privacy concerns.

### University of New South Wales (UNSW)
Established in 1949, UNSW excels in engineering, health, and environmental sciences, innovative teaching, vibrant student organizations, distinguished alumni, and strong research ethics.

### University of British Columbia (UBC)
Founded in 1915, UBC leads in sustainability, marine and quantum research, Indigenous partnerships, global collaboration, and features active student life and notable alumni.

### University of Hong Kong (HKU)
Founded in 1911, HKU champions diversity, has transitioned from British to Chinese governance, receives strong funding, provides English-language programs, and counts Sun Yat-sen among its alumni.

### University of Oxford
Dating to the 12th century, Oxford is renowned for its academic breadth, merit-based admissions, collegiate system, extensive libraries, financial stability, sustainability initiatives, scholarships, and globally influential alumni.

### National University of Singapore (NUS)
Founded in 1905 and restructured in 1980, NUS excels in science, engineering, law, business, and biomedicine, with strong graduate employability, an innovative campus, entrepreneurial focus, partnerships, and notable graduates.

### University of Toronto
Established in 1827, U of T is Canada’s largest university, with three campuses, strong academics, leading research output, vast libraries, advanced medicine and entrepreneurship, diverse student activities, and prominent alumni including Prime Ministers, Nobel laureates, and business founders.

### Cornell University
Founded in 1865 as New York’s land-grant institution, Cornell is highly selective, need-blind, and acclaimed for academic excellence, substantial research investment, campus traditions, quality housing and dining, robust governance, the leading university press, and arXiv. Health services and campus police support well-being and safety. By 2024, Cornell boasts over 250,000 alumni, including 62 Nobel laureates and global leaders.

### Federal University of Rio de Janeiro (UFRJ)
UFRJ, Brazil’s oldest higher education institution, has shaped the national university system and is noted for autonomy, public service, and academic excellence. Governed by an elected rector and administrative council, it serves over 58,000 students and offers hundreds of undergraduate and postgraduate programs across multiple campuses. Admissions use the national ENEM exam and SiSU system, with affirmative action. UFRJ is distinguished by a robust medical network (nine hospitals and institutes), renowned cultural venues (including the National Museum and House of Science), extensive libraries, outreach programs, and famous alumni such as Artur Ávila, Oscar Niemeyer, Clarice Lispector, and Vital Brazil.

The university has a long history of student activism, notably through the DCE student union and resistance during Brazil’s military regime, such as the 1968 "Massacre da Praia Vermelha." Academic centers also represent students by course.

UFRJ’s Technological Park on the College City campus is a major research hub in energy, oil, and gas, partnering with Petrobras and hosting globally relevant research centers (including CENPES, CEPEL, CETEM, COPPE), and facilities for international corporations, driving innovation and attracting hundreds of companies.

Key projects include a campus newspaper, maritime research (UFRJ Sea), extensive high-school outreach, renewable energy initiatives (Plant Waves), the eco-friendly MagLev Cobra train, and LabOceano, a world-class marine simulation tank. Notable university institutions include the Law School, National Museum, House of Science, and Laboratory School.

### General Themes
The universities profiled share a commitment to academic excellence, impactful research, innovation, student engagement, leadership in sustainability, active outreach, notable alumni, and global influence.
