# Global Education System Overviews and Challenges

## Comparative National Systems
Various nations present distinct models. India is transitioning its education structure under NEP 2020, though it suffers from low quality, resource shortages, and intense competition, while China maintains state control and faces pressure from high-stakes examinations. Greece offers diverse educational pathways while tackling issues like corruption and bullying.

In contrast, the German system is highly decentralized among its 16 federal states. It features distinct secondary tracks, including academic and vocational routes such as the *Duale Ausbildung*, with compulsory education being tuition-free until age 18.

## Overarching Challenges of Equity and Access
A significant challenge across many systems is the influence of socioeconomic background on academic outcomes. In Germany, tracking into different secondary schools is debated as a factor potentially favoring wealthier parents, leading to achievement gaps among students from lower socioeconomic backgrounds.

Other noted global concerns include disparities between urban and rural areas (India), unequal access to digital technology, and integrating large populations of international students. Furthermore, issues like rote learning and student mental health stress are prevalent in specific national contexts.
