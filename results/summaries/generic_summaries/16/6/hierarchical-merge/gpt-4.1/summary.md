## Lund University
Founded in 1666, Lund University in Sweden is acclaimed for its research in medical technology and sustainability, and it ranks 75th in the QS World University Rankings 2025. The university fosters international collaboration, offers diverse English-language programs, and maintains a vibrant student life alongside historic organizations and an esteemed alumni network with Nobel laureates and influential leaders.

## University of Jordan
The University of Jordan, established in 1962, is the nation’s premier institution with extensive accredited faculties and innovative research centers. It is renowned for top medical facilities, active student unions, global partnerships, and an alumni base of over 159,000 members who have significantly impacted various sectors regionally.

## Imperial College London
Imperial College London, founded in 1907, is a top-ranked global university excelling in science, engineering, medicine, and business. It is noted for substantial research funding, strong industry links, distinguished alumni including Nobel laureates, and a dynamic campus life with a wide array of student organizations and traditions.

## Boston College
Established in 1863, Boston College is a Jesuit institution known for academic excellence and inclusivity. The campus supports a vibrant student life with numerous organizations, athletic programs, and traditions. Prominent alumni contribute across public service and entertainment, utilizing advanced facilities and robust alumni networks.

## Western University (University of Western Ontario)
Western University, founded in 1878 in Ontario, excels in research, sustainability, and graduate employability. It boasts leadership programs, international exchanges, and strengths in neuroscience and HIV/AIDS research, alongside a vibrant campus community and distinguished alumni, including Nobel laureates and public figures.

## Harvard University
Founded in 1636, Harvard University is a prominent global research institution with the largest academic library and influential alumni, including U.S. presidents and Nobel laureates. Notable for academic breadth, affiliations with MIT, and strong student governance, Harvard also faces recent scrutiny over labor and leadership controversies.

## State University of Campinas (Unicamp)
Unicamp, established in the 1960s, drives Brazil’s scientific innovation with tuition-free, competitive programs and an interdisciplinary research focus. Despite funding challenges, it is recognized for its community-based governance and reputation for ground-breaking research and inclusivity within Latin America.

## Peking University
As China’s leading academic institution, Peking University boasts Asia’s largest university library, exceptional research, global partnerships, and impactful alumni. Its Global Excellence Strategy enhances international influence, and its legacy includes contributions to intellectual and social movements in China.

## University of Manchester
The University of Manchester, formed in 2004, is renowned for excellence in sciences, engineering, and humanities. It features elite research centers, striking architecture, and a diverse international community, with twenty-six Nobel laureates highlighting its leadership in research and innovation.

## RMIT University
RMIT University, headquartered in Melbourne and established in 1887, operates campuses in Australia, Vietnam, and Europe. As a dual-sector institution, it specializes in vocational and higher education, offering applied learning, strong industry collaboration, and dynamic student life. Globally ranked among the top 200–400 universities, RMIT is distinguished by successful graduate employment, multiple organizations, extensive research centers, and an active arts culture.

## University of Auckland
The University of Auckland, ranked #65 in QS 2026, is New Zealand’s largest and highest-ranked university with notable achievements in research and campus innovation. Despite controversies around staff layoffs and campus changes, it boasts a diverse student body of 47,443, vibrant student representation, and celebrated alumni.

## São Paulo State University (Unesp)
Unesp is a leading Brazilian public university, spanning 23 campuses and 33 academic institutes. Renowned for research in bioenergy and nanotechnology, it offers broad academic programs, maintains over 140 international partnerships, and boasts a strong reputation reinforced by successful graduates.

## University of Sydney
Established in 1850, the University of Sydney is Australia’s oldest university, known for iconic architecture, academic innovation, and commitment to merit and inclusivity. With strong faculties in arts, business, medicine, and health, the university features a vibrant campus life and notable alumni, but has faced reputational challenges related to staff and student concerns.

## University of the Andes (Uniandes, Colombia)
Founded in 1948, Uniandes is Colombia’s first nonsectarian, politically neutral university, emphasizing pluralist values and ethical leadership. Its interdisciplinary programs, international exchanges, and support services foster distinguished alumni in politics, science, and the arts, symbolized by a spirited campus mascot.
