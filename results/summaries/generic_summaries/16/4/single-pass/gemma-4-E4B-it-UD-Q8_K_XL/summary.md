## University of Western Ontario
The University of Western Ontario (UWO), founded in 1878 as The Western University of London, Ontario, began with Arts, Divinity, Law, and Medicine faculties, and became non-denominational in 1908. It purchased its current site in 1916 and renamed itself in 1923. Post-WWII, it significantly expanded, adding faculties like Graduate Studies (1947), Engineering (1957), and Law (1959). In 2012, it rebranded to "Western University," though its legal name remains UWO.

The campus is in London, Ontario, and features Collegiate Gothic and later modernist architecture. Western Libraries manages six libraries, while the university hosts the Artlab Gallery and the McIntosh Gallery. Housing options range from traditional to suite-styled residences. The university manages off-campus research parks, including Discovery Park and Advanced Manufacturing Park, and prioritizes sustainability through the President's Advisory Committee on Environment & Sustainability.

## Imperial College London
Imperial College London, originating from institutions like the Royal College of Chemistry (1845) and the Royal School of Mines, was established with a Royal Charter in 1907 to unify scientific education. It gained full degree-awarding powers in 2003 and became fully independent of the University of London in 2007.

The South Kensington campus is the main site, surrounded by major cultural attractions. Imperial operates in three main locations: South Kensington, the innovation-focused White City, and the ecological research site at Silwood Park. The college is structured into four faculties: Engineering, Medicine, Natural Sciences, and Business. It maintains a strong reputation, with its research profile assessed as 66% world-class in the 2021 REF.

## State University of Campinas (Unicamp)
Founded in 1962, Unicamp was established to promote development in São Paulo state. Despite rapid early expansion, the university experienced a major crisis in the late 1970s due to conflicts between the academic community and the conservative military regime. After restructuring in the 1980s, it consolidated its position.

The flagship campus occupies 350 hectares in Barão Geraldo, featuring a unique radial design centered around a circular plaza. It houses the Hospital das Clinicas, a major tertiary care center. Unicamp offers 70 undergraduate programs and 153 postgraduate programs. The university is heavily reliant on state government funding, which poses ongoing financial challenges.

## Lund University
Lund University traces its history to a Franciscan studium generale in 1425, becoming a fully recognized university in 1666. It was founded partly to integrate the Scanian region with Sweden.

The main campus is in Lund, and it operates satellite campuses in Malmö and Helsingborg. Student life is structured around student nations, the Academic Society (AF), and various student unions. Lund is renowned for its research, with numerous alumni contributing to major global innovations, including diagnostic ultrasound and Bluetooth technology. The university ranks highly globally in sustainability metrics.

## University of Manchester
The University of Manchester originated from the Mechanics' Institution in 1824, with its early heritage linked to the industrial city of Manchester. It became a constituent college of Victoria University of Manchester in 1880, eventually merging with the University of Manchester Institute of Science and Technology (UMIST) in 2003.

The main site is integrated into the city's fabric, comprising North and South campuses. Key institutions include the Jodrell Bank Observatory and the John Rylands Research Institute. The university is highly recognized globally, known for its strong performance in science and engineering, and has a vast research income stream.

## University of Jordan
Founded in 1962, the University of Jordan was established to meet the growing national need for skilled professionals. The main campus is in Jubaiha, Amman, and a southern branch is located in Aqaba (established 2009).

The university is structured into Scientific, Humanitarian, and Medical faculties, encompassing 78 undergraduate and 143 postgraduate programs. The University Jordan Library (UJL) is a major repository of historical and scientific materials. The institution is noted for its high ranking within the Arab world in global university rankings.

## University of Auckland
The University of Auckland began as a constituent college of the University of New Zealand in 1883. It gained its Māori name, Waipapa Taumata Rau, in 2021.

The university has multiple campuses across Auckland, including the City, Grafton, and Newmarket campuses. Strategic initiatives focus heavily on innovation and entrepreneurship through entities like the Centre for Innovation and Entrepreneurship (CIE). The institution maintains a strong commitment to international collaboration and is recognized for its high student satisfaction and research commercialization.

## Boston College
Boston College was founded in 1849 by the first Jesuit community, driven by the need for an educational institution for the Irish Catholic immigrant population. It moved to its current campus in Chestnut Hill in 1907, seeking to establish a beacon of Jesuit scholarship.

The university is organized into nine schools and colleges, with its core mission rooted in its Catholic and Jesuit tradition. It has significantly grown its endowment, and its commitment to reform is highlighted by its "Church in the 21st Century" program. Boston College has a strong presence in academic and professional fields.

## University of Sydney
Established by the University of Sydney Act in 1850, it was Australia's first secular and non-denominational university. It was one of the first in the world to admit women on equal terms in 1881.

The main campus is in Camperdown and Darlington, noted for its Neo-Gothic architecture. The university has multiple satellite campuses, including in the CBD. The university is renowned for its high academic reputation, consistently ranking among the top universities in Australia and globally. It has also navigated controversies regarding staff underpayment and student satisfaction.

## Harvard University
Harvard was founded in 1636 by the Massachusetts Bay Colony. Its original purpose was to advance learning for the ministry, reflecting its Puritan roots, though it gradually secularized its curriculum.

The university maintains the largest endowment globally, valued at approximately $50.7 billion. It operates on a multi-campus system: the main campus in Cambridge, the business/athletics hub in Allston, and the medical school in Longwood. Harvard remains a preeminent global research institution with exceptional alumni achievements across politics, science, and arts.

## University of the Andes (Colombia)
Founded in 1949, the University of the Andes was established as the first nonsectarian and non-politically affiliated university in Colombia, modeled after American institutions. Its mission is to foster pluralism and critical, ethical formation to address Colombian social problems.

The main campus is located in the historical center of Bogotá. Academically, it offers a highly interdisciplinary structure, allowing students to design their own curricula across 10 faculties. The university maintains strong international ties, evidenced by its extensive exchange programs with over 119 universities worldwide.

## Peking University
Peking University was established in 1898, initially as the Imperial University of Peking, aiming to modernize China's education system following the Sino-Japanese War. It later transformed into the National University of Peking.

The main campus retains traditional Chinese landscaping. The university is globally recognized for its high academic standing, consistently ranking in the top tier of Asian and global universities. It is a major center for applied research, notably recognized for Nobel laureate Tu Youyou’s work on artemisinin. The university actively pursues global excellence through numerous international partnerships.

## São Paulo State University (Unesp)
Founded in 1976, Unesp was created to coordinate and expand higher education across the state of São Paulo, adopting a multi-campus model inspired by UC Irvine.

The university features 23 campuses across the state. It is known for its focus on continuous growth, supported by a vast network of specialized institutes. While it has seen success in research output, it currently faces a funding crisis due to high payroll costs relative to state economic conditions.

## Royal Melbourne Institute of Technology (RMIT)
RMIT originated from the Working Men's College of Melbourne in 1887, founded by Francis Ormond to provide technical education to working men. It became a public university in 1992.

RMIT operates across multiple sites in Melbourne, including the historic City campus and the sprawling Bundoora campus. Its focus is heavily on applied research, design, and technology, with a strong industry partnership model. The university has successfully expanded its presence internationally through campuses in Vietnam and Spain.
