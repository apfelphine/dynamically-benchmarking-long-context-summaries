### **Royal Melbourne Institute of Technology (RMIT): Structure, Reputation, and Global Impact**

RMIT, a **public university founded in 1887** as a working-class institution, operates across **seven campuses** (Australia: City, Bundoora, Brunswick; Asia: Vietnam/Hanoi; Europe: Barcelona) and is governed by a **34-member Council/Board** with elected staff and student representation. Its **diverse academic structure** includes **four colleges**:
- **Business and Law** (finance, management, law),
- **Design and Social Context** (architecture, art, education),
- **STEM** (computing, engineering, health sciences),
- **Vocational Education** (trades, nursing, media).

The university’s **employer satisfaction stands at 84.7%**, with **71.8% of undergraduates and 98% of postgraduates employed within six months**, earning **A$66,800–A$98,000** post-graduation. Globally ranked **#199–#300 (2024–2026)** and **#10 nationally**, RMIT’s **applied research** focuses on sustainability, globalisation, and industry collaboration, supported by **50+ research centres** and **libraries/galleries** (e.g., RMIT Gallery, Informit database).

#### **Student Experience & Global Reach**
RMIT’s **student life** is vibrant, with **RUSU (Student Union)** offering **arts, sports, cultural events, and media outlets** (*The Swanston Gazette*, *Catalyst*, RMITV, SYN). Accommodation includes **RMIT-operated housing** (e.g., RMIT Village, Cambridge Court) and **college-affiliated residences** (e.g., International House, Newman College). The university’s **global expansion** blends vocational roots with international prestige, with **demographics reflecting gender diversity** (54% male, 46% female in higher education; 53% male, 47% female in vocational studies).

#### **Spiritual & Cultural Traditions**
RMIT’s **historic chapel (1860)** on the City campus provides multi-faith services, while the university hosts a **town-and-gown graduation parade** from City to Federation Square, featuring academic regalia and military escort.

#### **Notable Achievements**
RMIT’s **employability rankings** (51st globally in 2011) and **280,000+ alumni** across 130 countries highlight its impact. Notable alumni include athletes (e.g., Lydia Lassila), actors (e.g., Travis Fimmel), directors (e.g., James Wan), and artists (e.g., Charles Billich). Its **global campus network** and **state-funded, applied-research model** position RMIT as a leader in **industry-aligned education and sustainability innovation**.
