## **Universities of São Paulo State (Unesp) and University of São Paulo (USP)**

### **History**
Universities in Brazil are relatively recent, with USP founded in 1933. Before the 20th century, higher education was either public or church-run. Unesp was established in 1976, inspired by the University of California’s multi-campus model, and initially included campuses in Araçatuba, Araraquara, Botucatu, and others. The university’s governance and administration were structured to balance academic autonomy with state oversight. USP, the oldest public university in Brazil, has a long history of scientific and cultural contributions, with notable figures like Nobel laureates and influential scholars.

### **Facilities**
Unesp boasts extensive facilities, including 1,900 laboratories, 30 libraries with over 900,000 books, and 62.8 million square meters of developed area, including museums, gardens, and five experimental farms. It also operates major hospitals like the Hospital de Clínicas de Botucatu and manages veterinary and dental clinics. USP’s facilities include libraries, research centers, and affiliated hospitals, contributing to Brazil’s healthcare and scientific research.

### **Campuses**
Unesp has 23 campuses across the state, including specialized schools like the School of Medicine in Botucatu and the School of Engineering in Bauru. USP’s main campus in São Paulo is complemented by satellite campuses and research centers, including the School of Dentistry and the School of Medicine in Ribeirão Preto.

### **Organization and Administration**
Unesp is governed by a rector appointed by the state governor, with a rectorate overseeing academic and administrative affairs. USP operates under a similar structure, with a chancellor and a senate managing its governance. Both universities emphasize research and innovation, with strong ties to industry and government.

### **Academics**
Unesp offers 171 undergraduate programs and 118 postgraduate programs, with over 45,000 students. USP provides similar academic offerings, with a focus on research and interdisciplinary studies. Admissions are competitive, with standardized exams for undergraduate programs. Both universities are known for their rigorous academic standards and contributions to scientific research.

### **Admissions**
Admissions to both universities are highly competitive, with Unesp’s vestibular exam and USP’s national exam determining eligibility. USP is particularly renowned for its medical, engineering, and law programs. Both universities prioritize merit-based selection, though Unesp includes a social inclusion program to promote diversity.

### **Research**
Unesp and USP are major centers for scientific research, with Unesp ranked among the top universities in Brazil for research output. USP’s research spans natural sciences, medicine, engineering, and social sciences, with significant contributions to global scientific discourse.

### **Notable People**
Unesp and USP have produced numerous notable alumni, including scientists, politicians, and cultural figures. Notable figures include Nobel laureates, scholars, and leaders in various fields, reflecting the universities’ impact on Brazilian society.

## **Royal Melbourne Institute of Technology (RMIT), Australia**

### **History**
RMIT traces its origins to the Working Men’s College of Melbourne, founded in 1887. It evolved into the Royal Melbourne Technical College in 1954 and became RMIT University in 1992. The university has expanded globally, with campuses in Vietnam, Spain, and partnerships with institutions worldwide. RMIT is known for its strong focus on vocational education and applied research.

### **Campuses**
RMIT operates three main campuses in Australia: the historic City campus in Melbourne, the Bundoora campus in the northern suburbs, and the Brunswick campus for vocational design schools. RMIT also has international campuses in Ho Chi Minh City and Hanoi, Vietnam, and a center in Barcelona, Spain.

### **Organization and Administration**
RMIT is governed by a council, with a vice-chancellor overseeing academic and administrative affairs. The university is divided into two divisions: Higher Education and Vocational Education and Training (VET), each offering distinct academic programs.

### **Academics**
RMIT offers a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs, with a strong focus on vocational education and applied research. The university is known for its innovative teaching methods and strong industry connections. Admissions are competitive, with a focus on academic merit and practical skills.
