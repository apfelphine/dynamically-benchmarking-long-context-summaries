# Universities Overview

## Boston College (USA)  
Boston College (BC) is a mid-19th-century Jesuit institution ranked 39th nationally, known for its Carroll School of Management (3rd best undergraduate business school by Bloomberg 2016). It enrolls a diverse student body from across the U.S. and 75 countries with a selective admission rate (~16%). BC emphasizes a strong research culture in law, philosophy, theology, and economics, supporting vibrant student life featuring media outlets, NCAA Division I athletics, and a tradition of activism on social issues. Its alumni include prominent figures such as John F. Kerry and Amy Poehler. The university upholds Jesuit values, operates multiple campuses, and pursues an ambitious $3 billion fundraising campaign.

## Imperial College London (UK)  
Founded in 1907, Imperial College London specializes in science, engineering, medicine, and business, ranking 2nd globally in QS 2026. It comprises four faculties—Engineering, Medicine, Natural Sciences, and Business—and enrolled about 22,420 students in 2023/24, with a competitive 30% undergraduate admission rate. Renowned for high-impact research, including COVID-19 modeling, Imperial hosts over 300 student clubs and signature events like Commemoration Day. With a £1.309 billion income and £235.2 million endowment, it boasts distinguished alumni such as Nobel laureates and Fields Medalists.

## University of Jordan  
The University of Jordan, established in 1962 in Amman, is Jordan’s oldest and largest university with a branch in Aqaba. It offers accredited programs in Engineering (ABET), Business (AACSB), and hosts the country’s first Faculty of Medicine, supported by institutions like Jordan University Hospital. Ranked 11th in the Arab world, it emphasizes research through centers like the Institute of Archaeology and maintains vibrant international exchanges and student governance. Notable alumni include Jordanian prime ministers, members of the royal family, diplomats, and leaders in science and the arts.

## Lund University (Sweden)  
Founded in 1666, Lund University is one of Scandinavia’s largest historic research universities with nine faculties covering humanities, engineering, medicine, law, and economics. It offers about 275 programs, many in English, and ranks 75th globally (QS 2025) and 1st worldwide in sustainability (QS 2026). Known for innovations like diagnostic ultrasound and Bluetooth, Lund partners closely with Skåne University Hospital and global institutions such as the University of California and University of Tokyo. Its notable alumni include Nobel laureates, Swedish prime ministers, and top business leaders like founders linked to Tetra Pak, H&M, and IKEA.
