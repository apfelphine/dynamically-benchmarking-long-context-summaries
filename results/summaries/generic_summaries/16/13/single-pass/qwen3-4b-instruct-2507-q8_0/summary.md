### University of the Andes (Colombia)
Founded in 1949 by Mario Laserna Pinzón, the University of the Andes is Colombia's first nonsectarian, non-political university. It emerged from political tensions following the 1948 assassination of Jorge Eliécer Gaitán, aiming to train professionals for national development and international engagement. Modeled after American universities, it offers interdisciplinary curricula and includes a strong emphasis on liberal education. The university has a diverse student body (55% male, 45% female), with 95% of undergraduates being top 1% high school students. It maintains a mission of pluralism, academic excellence, and civic responsibility. It has 14 academic units and over 20 administrative units, with assets valued at $426.1 million (2020). Notable alumni include César Gaviria, María Ángela Holguín, and Fernando Botero.

### University of Sydney
Established in 1850 as Australia’s first public, secular university, the University of Sydney has grown into a leading research institution. It expanded through the 19th and 20th centuries, admitting women equally with men in 1881 and developing strong faculties in arts, sciences, and medicine. It has faced controversies including sexual harassment, staff underpayment, and political controversies. The university has a large campus in Sydney’s inner-city, with additional facilities in Camden, the CBD, and overseas. It maintains strong international ties, with over 140 exchange agreements. Notable alumni include eight Australian prime ministers and Nobel laureates. It is ranked among the top universities globally, with high research output and strong student outcomes.

### São Paulo State University (Unesp)
Founded in 1976, Unesp is a public university in São Paulo, Brazil, inspired by the University of California’s multi-campus model. It operates 23 campuses across the state, including in Araçatuba, Bauru, and Botucatu. Unesp focuses on applied research in bioenergy, biotechnology, and public policy. It has a large student body (over 45,000) and a strong international presence. The university emphasizes vocational training and has a robust research program. Notable alumni include Néle Azevedo, Ana Maria Braga, and Rafael Coutinho. It is ranked among Brazil’s top public universities.

### University of Auckland
Founded in 1883 as Auckland University College, the University of Auckland is New Zealand’s largest university. It has grown into a leading institution with a strong focus on innovation and entrepreneurship, home to the Unleash Space and the Newmarket Innovation Precinct. The university has a rich history of academic freedom and research, with notable achievements in engineering and medicine. It has faced controversies, including student protests over rent increases and the handling of the pandemic. It has a diverse student body and is known for its high graduate employability. Notable alumni include Australian skier Lydia Lassila and composer Peter Maxwell Davies.

### Royal Melbourne Institute of Technology (RMIT)
Founded in 1887 as the Working Men's College of Melbourne, RMIT evolved into a public university in 1992. It is known for its vocational and applied education, with a strong focus on design, technology, and health. The university operates multiple campuses in Melbourne and has international branches in Ho Chi Minh City and Hanoi. RMIT is renowned for its innovation, with over 400 startup ventures. It has faced controversies over student housing and rent increases. Notable alumni include Australian film director James Wan and musician Andrew Stockdale.

### University of Manchester
Established in 1824 as the Mechanics' Institution, the University of Manchester became a major research university in the 20th century. It merged in 2004 with the University of Manchester Institute of Science and Technology (UMIST) to form the current institution. The university is a leader in science and engineering, with over 25 Nobel laureates among its alumni and faculty. It has a strong focus on research, with a large endowment and significant research funding. The university has faced controversies, including student protests over rent and housing. It is ranked among the top universities in the UK and globally.

### Peking University
Founded in 1898 as the Imperial University of Peking, Peking University is China’s most prestigious university. It has a long history of academic excellence and has produced numerous Nobel laureates, including Tu Youyou. The university has expanded significantly, with new campuses in Shenzhen and a strong international presence. It is known for its research in science, medicine, and engineering. Peking University is a global leader in education and research, with a strong emphasis on innovation and interdisciplinary studies.

### State University of Campinas (Unicamp)
Founded in 1962, Unicamp is a major public university in São Paulo, Brazil, with a large campus in Campinas and a presence in Limeira and Piracicaba. It is known for its strong research in engineering, life sciences, and technology. The university has a unique campus design and is a leader in applied research. It has faced internal conflicts and financial challenges due to high staff salaries. Unicamp is known for its strong academic programs and research output, with over 100 research centers and 70 undergraduate programs.

### Harvard University
Founded in 1636, Harvard is one of the world’s oldest and most prestigious universities. It has a strong emphasis on liberal arts, research, and leadership. The university has a large endowment and is a global leader in research, with over 160 Nobel laureates among its alumni and faculty. It has faced controversies, including student protests and government scrutiny over antisemitism. Harvard is known for its rigorous academic standards and high graduate employability. It has a strong global presence and is a leading institution in business, medicine, and the humanities.

### University of Western Ontario
Founded in 1878 as the Western University of London, Ontario, the University of Western Ontario is a public university with a strong focus on research and education. It has a large campus in London, Ontario, with additional facilities in Newton and Brighton. The university is known for its strong programs in medicine, engineering, and business. It has faced controversies over student housing and sexual violence. The university is known for its strong research output and international student body.

### Boston College
Founded in 1849 as the Jesuit college in Boston, Boston College is a Catholic university with a strong emphasis on liberal arts and professional education. It has a large campus in Chestnut Hill, Massachusetts, and a growing international presence. The university is known for its strong programs in business, law, and medicine. It has faced controversies, including student protests over racism and housing. Boston College is known for its strong alumni network and high graduate employability.

### Imperial College London
Established in 1907 as a merger of several scientific institutions, Imperial College London is a leading research university in the UK. It is known for its strong programs in engineering, medicine, and business. The university has a large endowment and is a leader in research and innovation. It has faced controversies over student housing and research ethics. Imperial is known for its strong international collaborations and high graduate employability.
