# University of Jordan – Concise Summary

## History and Development

Founded in 1962 in Amman, the University of Jordan is the country's oldest and largest higher education institution. Initially established to meet national demand for local university education, it began with the Faculty of Arts, expanding rapidly to include sciences, business, and professional fields. Political and economic changes influenced its development, leading to adjustments in autonomy, governance, and academic structure. The university pioneered admissions systems and educational models in the region, and established key research centers and its teaching hospital. Post-2000, it expanded further, opening the Aqaba campus, developing blended learning, and intensifying research and internationalization efforts, including patent registrations and significant scholarly output.

## Campuses and Facilities

The main campus in Amman is centrally located and well-integrated into the city’s student life, featuring extensive academic infrastructure, recreational areas, and multiple entrance gates. The Aqaba campus, established in 2009, represents national efforts to enhance higher education in southern Jordan, with modern facilities, ongoing expansion (such as a new women's dormitory), and a focus on regional development.

## Academic Structure

The University of Jordan offers 78 undergraduate and 143 postgraduate programs, organized into scientific, humanitarian, and medical faculties. Notable schools include the School of Medicine (nation’s first), School of Engineering (all departments ABET accredited), and King Abdullah II School of Information Technology. The university is a leader in business and Sharia education and provides extensive options in languages, arts, international studies, and rehabilitation sciences. Graduate offerings have widened to cover over a hundred master’s and doctoral programs.

## Research, Library, and Centers

UJL, the university library, is among the region’s most prominent, holding over a million items and offering specialized collections, digital services, and historical archives. Numerous research and cultural centers support advanced studies in areas such as photonics, water/environment, Arabic language, Islamic studies, and strategic policy. The Jordan University Hospital is a central teaching and research hospital, holding international accreditations and serving as a hub for clinical training and health advancements.

## Rankings and International Affiliations

The University of Jordan is consistently ranked as the top university in Jordan and among the leading institutions in the Arab world, according to QS, THE, and ARWU rankings. Its admission scores are the highest nationally for many programs. It maintains hundreds of global partnerships for student and staff exchanges and is affiliated with major international academic networks.

## Student Life and Governance

Student life is vibrant, with strong traditions of activism and representation through the Student Union. The university has historically influenced national political movements. It has adapted its governance regulations several times, most recently strengthening student involvement in decision-making.

## Alumni and Faculty

Alumni include prime ministers, ministers, diplomats, scientists, authors, and business leaders across Jordan and the region. The faculty comprises influential scholars in various fields, with recognized contributions to science, humanities, law, and public service. The university has produced leading figures in academia, government, literature, and international organizations.

## Additional Features

The university owns significant museum and scientific collections, including rare manuscripts and important fossil remains. It publishes numerous peer-reviewed journals promoting research in medical, social, and applied sciences, and fosters innovation through dedicated research offices.
