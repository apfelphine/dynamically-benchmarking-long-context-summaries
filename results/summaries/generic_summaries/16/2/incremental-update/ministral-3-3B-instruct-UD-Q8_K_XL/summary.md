### **Global Academic Presence & Rankings**
RMIT University, with campuses in **Melbourne (four locations)** and global hubs in **Hong Kong, Shanghai, Singapore, and Malaysia**, ranks **#199–300 globally (QS 2024–2026)** and **14th nationally**, excelling in **applied research in design, technology, and sustainability**. Harvard University, founded in **1636**, dominates globally with **#1 (Academic Ranking, THE-QS since 2003)**, **#3 (QS 2025)**, and **#5 (THE 2025)**, boasting a **$50.7B endowment** and a legacy of **100+ Nobel laureates** and **14 U.S. Presidents**. Peking University ranks **#14 (QS 2025) and #13 (THE 2025)**, leading in **STEM, humanities, and social sciences**, while Harvard’s **Harvard Business School** and Peking’s **Mathematics/Physics programs** are globally preeminent.

### **Campus & Structural Evolution**
**RMIT** operates across **Melbourne (City, Bundoora, Brunswick, Williams)** and global locations like **Barcelona**, governed by the **RMIT Council** and spanning **four Colleges**. **Boston College** features a **175-acre Gothic-style Chestnut Hill campus** with expansions to **Newton (Law), Brighton (Theology), and Brookline (Messina College, 2024)**, funded by a **$1B budget** and aiming for a **$3B fundraising goal**. **Peking University** combines historic imperial gardens (e.g., **Weiming Lake**) with modern facilities, including branches in **Beijing, Hanzhong, Shenzhen**, and the **Shenzhen Graduate School (founded 2001)**. **Harvard’s** sprawling **18,000-acre Harvard Yard** and **150+ buildings**—including **Harvard Library (20.4M+ volumes)**—contrast with its **Gothic Revival architecture**, with modern expansions in **Allston (HBS), Longwood (medical schools), and global hubs (D.C., Italy, China)**.

**RMIT** achieves **71.8% undergrad employment** within six months, with alumni like **Lydia Lassila (Olympic skier)** and **Mai Phương Thúy (Miss Vietnam)**. **Boston College** admits **15.9%** of applicants (Class of 2028) with a **diverse student body (50 states + 75 countries)**, producing **3 Rhodes and 22 Truman scholars**. **Peking University** ranks **#1 in Asia**, **top 15 globally**, and hosts **~7,000 international students** with dual-degree programs (e.g., **Waseda, Cornell, Yale, Stanford**). **Harvard** maintains a **6% admit rate**, **40% international students**, and **100+ Nobel laureates**, including **14 U.S. Presidents** and **199 Nobel Prize winners** (most of any institution).
