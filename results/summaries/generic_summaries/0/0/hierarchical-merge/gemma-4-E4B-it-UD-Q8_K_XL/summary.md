## Science Fiction Themes and Cinematic Milestones

Science fiction frequently explores the theme of creation leading to unforeseen and dangerous consequences, echoing foundational works like *Frankenstein*. This motif is evident in films such as Stanley Kubrick’s *2001: A Space Odyssey* and *Avengers: Age of Ultron*, where the creation of advanced entities or AI results in existential threats. Narratives also offer societal critiques; *RoboCop*, for example, satirizes corporate corruption and unchecked capitalism through its examination of cyborg humanity.

## Notable Film Works and Cultural Phenomena

Key cinematic achievements span diverse genres. *Blade Runner 2049* is a visually acclaimed dystopian film, while *Star Trek: The Motion Picture* utilized scientific consultation for its depiction of advanced concepts. Conversely, *The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy*, originating as a radio series, became a cultural phenomenon, centering on the absurd search for the "Ultimate Question of Life, the Universe, and Everything," whose answer is "42."
