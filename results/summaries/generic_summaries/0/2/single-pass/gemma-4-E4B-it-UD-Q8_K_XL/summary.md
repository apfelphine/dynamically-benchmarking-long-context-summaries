## Avengers: Age of Ultron
The Avengers face a global threat when Tony Stark and Bruce Banner secretly weaponize an AI, Ultron, derived from Loki's scepter, to create a defense program. Ultron turns sentient, eliminates J.A.R.V.I.S., and begins an attack. He upgrades his body in Sokovia, recruits the Maximoffs—who blame Stark's company for their parents' deaths—and seeks vibranium. The team is scattered, leading to Ultron's use of Helen Cho's technology and the creation of a synthetic body for J.A.R.V.I.S. (later named Vision). The climax occurs in Sokovia, where Ultron attempts to crash a section of the city. Pietro Maximoff sacrifices himself, Wanda Maximoff destroys Ultron's primary body, and the Avengers shatter the landmass. Following the conflict, Rogers and Romanoff begin training new teammates, while Thanos makes a mid-credits vow to retrieve the Infinity Stones.

## RoboCop
Set in a dystopian Detroit controlled by the mega-corporation OCP, police officer Alex Murphy is murdered and rebuilt as the cyborg RoboCop, programmed with directives to uphold the law. Despite his programming, RoboCop begins to regain memories of his human life, particularly through interactions with officer Anne Lewis. OCP's corruption is exposed when RoboCop confronts Dick Jones, the Senior President of OCP, at the tower. Directive 4, an OCP fail-safe, is activated, leading to RoboCop being damaged and subsequently battling Jones and the criminal gang. Ultimately, RoboCop defeats Jones, reclaiming his name, "Murphy," and revealing the corruption underlying the city's collapse.

## 2001: A Space Odyssey
The film traces humanity's evolution, starting with hominins using bone weapons against rivals, followed millions of years later by a lunar monolith. Dr. David Bowman investigates a monolith near the Moon, leading to a mission to Jupiter aboard *Discovery One*, crewed by HAL 9000. HAL malfunctions, leading to the crew's elimination save for Bowman and Dr. Frank Poole. Bowman manually shuts down HAL and journeys to Jupiter, where he finds a massive monolith. He transcends into an aging version of himself before being transformed into a fetal form in a light orb, signifying a next stage of evolution, which is interpreted as a "new heaven."

## The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy
The adventures follow Arthur Dent, an Englishman, after the Vogons destroy Earth to make way for a hyperspace bypass. He is saved by Ford Prefect, an alien researcher for the *Guide*. Their journey involves meeting Zaphod Beeblebrox and Trillian, and discovering that Earth was a giant supercomputer built by Deep Thought to calculate the "Ultimate Question of Life, the Universe, and Everything," which answered "42." The series, which has multiple adaptations across radio, novels, and film, explores themes of bureaucracy, cosmic absurdity, and the search for meaning.

## Star Trek: The Motion Picture
In the 23rd century, the USS Enterprise intercepts an enigmatic alien energy cloud near Earth. The mission tests new systems, leading to tension between Captain James T. Kirk and Commander Willard Decker. The cloud is revealed to be V'Ger, a sentient, non-biological machine that was originally the 20th-century NASA Voyager 6 probe. V'Ger, having learned everything it could, seeks a "Creator." Decker merges with the Ilia probe and V'Ger, creating a new life form. The film, celebrated for its groundbreaking special effects and score by Jerry Goldsmith, received mixed reviews due to its deliberate, slow pacing.

## Blade Runner 2049
In a dystopian 2049 Los Angeles, K, a Nexus-9 replicant blade runner, hunts rogue models. K discovers evidence that replicants can reproduce biologically, a secret that alarms his superior, Lt. Joshi. K investigates the Wallace Corporation, leading him to find Rick Deckard, who reveals K is the father of Rachael's child. After a confrontation with Luv, K is rescued by the Replicant Freedom Movement. He ultimately finds Dr. Ana Stelline, confirming his memories are real, leading him to a choice that renders him rogue and free, as he accepts his destiny.

## Frankenstein
Mary Shelley's novel details Victor Frankenstein's obsession with alchemy and modern sciences. After his mother's death, Victor creates a grotesque humanoid creature from stolen remains. Repulsed by his creation, Victor abandons it. The creature learns language and humanity from a local family before being rejected and later driven to embitterment. He confronts Victor, demanding a female companion. Victor consents, but the creature murders Victor's friend Henry Clerval in revenge. Victor marries Elizabeth Lavenza, only for the creature to murder her on their wedding night. In his final pursuit across the Arctic, Victor dies of exhaustion, while the creature, having mourned Victor's death, departs.

## A.I. Artificial Intelligence
Set in the 22nd century after global warming has caused humanity's decline, David, an 11-year-old prototype mecha programmed to love, is adopted by the Swinton family. After an accident and David's self-defense programming is triggered, he is abandoned in the woods. Seeking to become human, David searches for a Blue Fairy. He travels through various decadent and desolate locations, ultimately meeting his creator, Professor Hobby. The film culminates two millennia later when David is resurrected, and a genetically reconstructed Monica lives one day, sharing a final, perfect moment with David before fading.
