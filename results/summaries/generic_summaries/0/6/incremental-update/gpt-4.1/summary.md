## Summary

*A.I. Artificial Intelligence* is set in a climate-ravaged future, following David, a robot child grappling with identity and humanity. The film blended Spielberg and Kubrick's styles, featured memorable visual effects, John Williams’s score, and a standout performance by Haley Joel Osment. Premiering at Venice, it grossed $235 million worldwide and sparked philosophical debate.

Mary Shelley's *Frankenstein* (1818) established lasting themes of creation, ethics, and responsibility, inspiring extensive adaptations and scholarly analysis.

*Blade Runner 2049* explores replicant K’s quest for truth in a dystopian Los Angeles, connecting Anna Stelline and Deckard. Noted for its stunning visuals and performances by Ryan Gosling and Harrison Ford, it remains influential in science fiction.

*Star Trek: The Motion Picture* examines A.I., consciousness, and the Enterprise’s encounter with V’Ger. Despite mixed reviews, it influenced sequels, showcased innovative effects, and is remembered for Goldsmith’s score.

*The Hitchhiker’s Guide to the Galaxy*, originating as a 1978 radio series, expanded into various media. Its comedic sci-fi focuses on Arthur Dent and the “Ultimate Question” (“42”), leaving a lasting cultural imprint.

*2001: A Space Odyssey*, by Kubrick and Clarke, revolutionized science fiction with its visionary artistry, focusing on the monolith and HAL’s AI crisis. Its classical music, symbolic narrative, and explorations of human error and evolution are highly influential.

*RoboCop* presents dystopian Detroit and officer Murphy’s transformation into RoboCop, delving into crime, identity, and corporate satire. Renowned for effects, its cyberpunk style, and Poledouris’s score, it won awards, grossed $53.4 million, and inspired a franchise.

### Avengers: Age of Ultron and Sequels

**Avengers: Age of Ultron** centers on the Avengers battling Ultron, an AI bent on human extinction, while exploring team dynamics and introducing a new hero lineup. Directed by Joss Whedon, the film balances spectacle with complex character interactions, expands the MCU with global filming, advanced digital effects, and Tyler/Elfman’s score. It was a marketing success, grossing $1.403 billion, earning generally positive reviews for its cast, action, and scope, though criticism targeted the villain and translation issues in China. The film received several nominations for visual effects and animation, with few major award wins, and was released digitally and on Blu-ray/DVD as part of MCU Phase Two.

*Avengers: Infinity War* (2018) and *Avengers: Endgame* (2019), directed by Anthony and Joe Russo and written by Markus and McFeely, continue the story from Age of Ultron. These sequels feature most of the original cast alongside new characters from across the Marvel Cinematic Universe, further expanding the MCU’s reach and narrative complexity.
