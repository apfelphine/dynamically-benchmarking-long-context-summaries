# Science Fiction Media Analysis

## Themes and Key Works
Science fiction media frequently explores deep themes concerning humanity, artificial intelligence, and the perils of scientific advancement. Key works demonstrate this range, from philosophical explorations of evolution in Stanley Kubrick's *2001: A Space Odyssey* to the satirical absurdity in Douglas Adams' *The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy*.

The nature of identity is often examined through artificial beings; foundational texts like Mary Shelley's *Frankenstein* and modern examples such as *A.I.* and *Blade Runner 2049* dissect selfhood through sentient androids. This genre also encompasses diverse subgenres, including dystopian narratives like the *RoboCop* franchise, which critiques corporate overreach through its cyborg protagonist.

## Franchise Focuses
### RoboCop Franchise
The *RoboCop* franchise, originating in 1987, functions as a dark social satire criticizing unchecked corporate power, specifically the privatization of Detroit by OCP. The narrative centers on the cyborg officer RoboCop battling deep corruption and is notable for its sharp commentary and innovative design, continuing across various media.

### Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU)
In the MCU, *Avengers: Age of Ultron* introduced the AI Ultron, created by Tony Stark and Banner using stolen vibranium. This film significantly expanded the universe by introducing Vision and characters like Scarlet Witch, while simultaneously setting up global conflict. This installment led into major commercial successes, *Avengers: Infinity War* and *Avengers: Endgame*.
