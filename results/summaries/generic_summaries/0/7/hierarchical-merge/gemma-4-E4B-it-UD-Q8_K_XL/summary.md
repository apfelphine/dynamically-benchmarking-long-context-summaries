# Master Summary

## Literary Themes and Genre Analysis
Literature covers diverse themes, ranging from the Romantic and Gothic exploration of scientific overreach in Mary Shelley's *Frankenstein*—which mirrors the Adam myth—to the cosmic absurdity of Douglas Adams' *The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy*. The latter series, which features Arthur Dent surviving Earth's destruction, reveals Earth was a supercomputer seeking the ultimate answer, "42," and possesses a massive cultural footprint across media.

## Science Fiction Cinema: Dystopia, Evolution, and Action
Cinematic explorations span profound philosophical inquiries to high-stakes action. Influential sci-fi films include Stanley Kubrick's *2001: A Space Odyssey*, noted for its cosmic evolution narrative and practical effects, and *Blade Runner 2049*, which depicts a dystopian future examining identity through its aesthetic. Culturally significant works like *RoboCop* satirize corporate control in a futuristic setting, while the MCU saga, exemplified by *Avengers: Age of Ultron*, chronicles global conflicts against AI threats.
