### **UNCF: Scholarships and Legacy**
The **United Negro College Fund (UNCF)** was founded in 1944 to address educational inequities for African Americans, though its scholarships are open to all ethnicities. It supports students at its member institutions and beyond, with notable alumni including civil rights leaders like **Dr. Martin Luther King Jr.** and **Spike Lee**. The UNCF has expanded its impact through major donations, such as **MacKenzie Scott’s $70 million gift in 2025**, aimed at sustaining historically Black colleges and universities (HBCUs).

#### **Key Institutions**
UNCF operates through **19 historically Black colleges and universities (HBCUs)** across the U.S., including:
- **Alabama:** Miles College, Oakwood University, Talladega College, Tuskegee University
- **Florida:** Bethune-Cookman University, Edward Waters College
- **Georgia:** Clark Atlanta University, Morehouse College, Spelman College
- **Louisiana:** Dillard University, Xavier University of Louisiana
- **Tennessee:** Fisk University, LeMoyne-Owen College

#### **Motto & Scholarship Philosophy**
The UNCF’s motto, *"A mind is a terrible thing to waste,"* reflects its mission to empower students through education. Scholarships are awarded based on merit, financial need, and academic achievement, with a focus on fostering leadership and innovation among HBCU graduates.

### **Bolashak Scholarship**
Instituted by **Kazakhstan’s President Nursultan Nazarbayev in 1993**, the **Bolashak Scholarship** ("Future") supports Kazakh students studying abroad. The program emphasizes Western democratic values but often lacks authority to implement reforms. By 2018, over **12,000 scholarships** had been awarded, with a focus on students from Kazakh, Russian, and Korean ethnicities due to language requirements.

#### **Key Features**
- **Eligibility:** Kazakh citizens with strong academic records.
- **Recipients:** Primarily students from Kazakhstan, though some international applicants are considered.
- **Focus:** Encourages exposure to Western education and leadership development.

### **Thurgood Marshall College Fund (TMCF)**
Founded in **1987**, the **TMCF** supports public HBCUs, distinguishing itself from the UNCF by focusing on **scholarships, capacity-building, and advocacy**. It operates through **55 member schools**, including:
- **Howard University** (highest endowed, $712.4 million)
- **North Carolina A&T State University** (highest enrollment, 13,322 students)
- **Prairie View A&M University** (highest endowment, $84.3 million)

#### **Notable Achievements**
- **$50 million+ in funding** from major donors like **Apple and the Charles Koch Foundation**.
- **Leadership Institute** for HBCU students.
- **Acquired Opportunity Funding Corporation (OFC)**, merging to enhance entrepreneurial support.

### **National Merit Scholarship Program**
The **National Merit Scholarship Program** selects **15,000 finalists** from over **1.5 million applicants** based on the **Preliminary SAT/NMSQT** scores. Recipients receive **$2,500 scholarships** and are eligible for additional awards from corporate sponsors. The program has faced criticism for its reliance on standardized testing and lack of transparency in selection criteria.

#### **Selection Process**
1. **Commended Recognition:** Top 50,000 scorers.
2. **Semifinalists:** ~16,000 selected by state.
3. **Finalists:** ~15,000 chosen for scholarships, with **8,000 awarded** annually.

#### **Notable Alumni**
- **Bill Gates** (1973)
- **Mae Jemison** (1973, first Black woman in space)
- **Oprah Winfrey** (1984)

### **Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes**
The **Studienstiftung** is Germany’s premier merit scholarship program, supporting **undergraduate, graduate, and doctoral students** with financial and academic support. Founded in **1925**, it operates on **BAföG criteria** and accepts students nominated by schools, professors, or through assessment tests.

#### **Key Features**
- **Eligibility:** Exceptional academic performance, leadership, and social commitment.
- **Scholarship:** €300/month + need-based support.
- **Notable Alumni:** Nobel Prize winners like **Manfred Eigen** and **Peter H. Seitz**.

---

### **Bright Futures Scholarship Program**
The **Florida Bright Futures Scholarship** provides merit-based funding for students, with four levels:
- **Florida Academic Scholar (FAS):** Covers 100% tuition (SAT/ACT ≥ 1330/29).
- **Florida Medallion Scholar (FMS):** Covers 75% tuition (SAT/ACT ≥ 1190/24).
- **Gold Seal Vocational Scholars (GSV):** For career-focused programs.
- **Gold Seal CAPE Scholars (GSC):** For vocational training.

#### **Controversies**
- **2011 Reforms:** Increased minimum SAT scores, reducing eligibility for minority students.
- **2021 Changes:** Added the **Classic Learning Test (CLT)** as an alternative.

### **Shuping Scholarship**
Founded by **Koo Soh Bing** in **1939**, the **Shuping Scholarship** supports students from poor families in **Shanghai, China**. The program includes:
- **Full tuition coverage** for scholars.
- **Vocational training** (typing, nursing, martial arts).
- **Healthcare and education** for scholars and their families.

#### **Legacy**
- **6,000+ scholars** supported since its revival in **1986**.
- **Philanthropic impact:** Funded schools and hospitals in Huzhou, China.

---

### **Kennedy Scholarship**
Created in **1966** as a memorial to **John F. Kennedy**, the **Kennedy Scholarship** enables British students to study at **Harvard or MIT**. The program emphasizes **leadership, public service, and intellectual excellence**.

#### **Selection Criteria**
- **Literary and scholastic attainments.**
- **Potential for public service.**

#### **Notable Alumni**
- **Ed Balls** (Shadow Chancellor)
- **Sonia Sotomayor** (U.S. Supreme Court Justice)

---

### **Harkness Fellowship**
The **Harkness Fellowship** supports **healthcare policy and practice** through research in the U.S. Founded in **1925**, it funds **12-month fellowships** for scholars from **Australia, Canada, France, Germany, Netherlands, New Zealand, Norway, Switzerland, and the UK**.

#### **Key Features**
- **Focus:** Health policy, public health, and medical innovation.
- **Notable Alumni:** **Dr. Anthony Fauci** (former NIH director).

---

### **Norway Scholarship**
The **Norway Scholarship** enables students from **Royal Frederik University (now University of Oslo)** to study at **Wadham College, Oxford**. Founded in **1919**, it supports research and academic excellence.

---

### **Margaret Busby New Daughters of Africa Award**
The **New Daughters of Africa** anthology, edited by **Margaret Busby**, celebrates the work of **200+ women writers of African descent**. Published in **2019**, it includes contributions from **Chimamanda Ngozi Adichie, Toni Morrison, and Zadie Smith**.

#### **Legacy**
- **Inspired global literary movements** in African diaspora writing.
- **New Daughters of Africa Award:** Supports African women students.

### **King’s Scholar**
The **King’s Scholar** program at **King’s College, Cambridge**, and **Eton College** selects students for academic excellence. At **Eton**, scholars live in **College** and wear black gowns, while at **Cambridge**, they receive a **£350 prize** for first-class degrees.

### **Rhodes Scholarship**
Founded in **1902** by **Cecil Rhodes**, the **Rhodes Scholarship** supports postgraduate study at **Oxford University**. The program emphasizes **leadership, public service, and intellectual rigor**.

#### **Selection Criteria**
- **Literary and scholastic attainments.**
- **Energy and commitment to public service.**

#### **Controversies**
- **Exclusion of women:** Admitted in **1977** after the **Sex Discrimination Act**.
- **Criticism of Rhodes’ colonial legacy.**

---

### **Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program**
The **Muskie Internship Program** provides **summer internships** for students from **Eurasia** in the U.S. Department of State. Named after **Senator Edmund Muskie**, it supports **career development and professional exchange**.

### **British Council**
The **British Council** promotes **UK culture, education, and diplomacy** globally. Founded in **1934**, it operates through **100+ offices** and offers:
- **English language programs.**
- **Examinations (IELTS).**
- **Cultural exchange initiatives.**

#### **Controversies**
- **Russian sanctions:** Suspended activities in **2025** due to "undesirable organization" status.
- **Criticism of human rights policies.**

### **Marshall Scholarship**
The **Marshall Scholarship** supports **American students** to study in the UK. Founded in **1953**, it emphasizes **leadership and academic excellence**.

#### **Selection Criteria**
- **High academic achievement.**
- **Potential for public service.**

#### **Notable Alumni**
- **Stephen Breyer** (U.S. Supreme Court Justice)
- **Madeleine Albright** (Former U.S. Secretary of State)

### **Fulbright Program**
The **Fulbright Program** is the world’s largest **international educational exchange program**, supporting **students, scholars, and professionals** in the U.S. and abroad. Founded in **1946**, it includes:
- **Fulbright U.S. Student Program:** Supports U.S. students abroad.
- **Fulbright Foreign Student Program:** Supports international students in the U.S.

#### **Notable Alumni**
- **Nobel Prize winners** (e.g., **Kofi Annan**).
- **U.S. Presidents** (e.g., **Jimmy Carter**).

### **Miss America**
The **Miss America Pageant** began in **1921** as a beauty contest but evolved into a platform for **feminist and social activism**. Key milestones include:
- **First Black contestant (1970):** Cheryl Browne.
- **First Asian-American winner (2014):** Nina Davuluri.
- **Controversies:** Racism, body-shaming, and scholarship transparency.
