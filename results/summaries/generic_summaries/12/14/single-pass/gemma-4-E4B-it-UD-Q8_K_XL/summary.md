## Educational Scholarship Programs Overview

### Bright Futures Scholarship Program (Florida)
This program, modeled after Georgia's HOPE Scholarship, initially provided merit-based funding for Florida high school graduates without considering financial need. Funding cuts enacted in 2011 increased SAT score requirements and reduced award percentages, disproportionately affecting Black and Latino students. In 2018, the program was overhauled with new tiers (FAS, FMS, Gold Seal Vocational, Gold Seal CAPE) and a new Academic Top Scholar (ATS) award. Requirements for all levels include GPA and community service hours, and scholarships are renewable based on sustained academic performance.

### Kennedy Scholarship (UK)
Established in 1964 following John F. Kennedy's assassination, the program features two parts: a permanent memorial in Runnymede, England, and a scholarship to attend Harvard or MIT. Selection considers intellectual attainment, communication readiness, and the suitability of the proposed course of study, with standards comparable to the Rhodes and Marshall Scholarships. The current trustees include figures from various academic and professional fields.

### National Merit Scholarship Program
Students qualify by taking the Preliminary SAT/National Merit Scholarship Qualifying Test (PSAT/NMSQT) in their junior year. The competition progresses through "Commended," "Semifinalist," and "Finalist" rounds. About 8,000 Finalists receive Merit Scholarship awards, which are proportional to their state's representation in the nation's graduating seniors. The program has faced criticism from NACAC regarding its reliance solely on PSAT scores for initial screening.

### National Achievement Scholarship Program
Established in 1964 to encourage Black American youth in education, this program previously allowed Black students to compete in both the National Merit and National Achievement Programs. As of 2015, this program transitioned its funds to the United Negro College Fund (UNCF) to support high-achieving, underrepresented college graduates.

### Norway Scholarship
Conceived in 1919 to foster close association between Norwegian and British youth, this scholarship facilitates one year of study at Wadham College in Oxford. Initial funding proved inadequate, but subsequent fundraising efforts increased the support.

## International and Comparative Scholarships

### Marshall Scholarship
Announced in 1953, this scholarship aims to enable intellectually distinguished young Americans to study in the UK, fostering mutual understanding between the US and Britain. Selection requires high academic ability and character, with preference given to candidates who can actively engage with the UK university life. The program is highly selective and is known for producing scholars who often pursue careers in academia and research.

### Fulbright Program
Established in 1945, this program's mission is to promote peace and friendship through educational exchange between the U.S. and other nations. It offers various grants, including the U.S. Student Program for U.S. citizens studying abroad, and the Fulbright Foreign Student Program for international scholars studying in the U.S. The program operates via bilateral commissions with numerous countries.

### Rhodes Scholarship
Founded in 1902 based on Cecil John Rhodes's will, the scholarship promotes unity within the British Empire and strengthens UK-US diplomatic ties. It is highly selective, with a global acceptance rate of 0.7%. Criteria emphasize scholastic attainment, character, courage, and a commitment to leadership, though it faces criticism regarding Rhodes's colonialist views.

### Harkness Fellowship
Established in 1925 by Anna Harkness's son, the Commonwealth Fund, the Harkness Fellowships were intended to reciprocate the Rhodes Scholarships by allowing British graduates to study in the U.S. Since 1997, the focus has shifted primarily to health care policy and practice, with Fellows conducting research at major American institutions.

### Studienstiftung
Founded in Dresden in 1925, this German scholarship supports highly talented students and doctoral candidates studying in Germany, the EU, or Switzerland. Eligibility is based on principles of "excellence, initiative and responsibility," and applicants are admitted through various channels, including school nominations and assessment tests.

## Specialized and Regional Programs

### Bolashak (Kazakhstan)
Instituted in 1993 by Nursultan Nazarbayev, this scholarship aims to instill Western, democratic values in Kazakhstan's youth. It has expanded significantly since its inception, offering study opportunities in over 32 countries.

### Thurgood Marshall College Fund (TMCF)
This fund supports public Historically Black Colleges and Universities (HBCUs). TMCF focuses on providing programmatic support, capacity building, and scholarships, differentiating itself from the UNCF by supporting a more limited number of schools.

### UNCF
Founded in 1944 to fund Historically Black Colleges and Universities, UNCF provides financial assistance to HBCUs. It has received significant donations, notably from MacKenzie Scott in 2025.

### Shuping Scholarship (Shanghai)
Founded in 1939 by Koo, this scholarship initially supported poor, academically strong secondary students in Shanghai. Its founding principle, "Return to the Society what you have gained from it," mandates service to the community. The program was revived in 1986 and continues to emphasize moral and physical education alongside academic support.

## Other Mentions

*   **Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program:** A professional exchange program supported by the U.S. Department of State, providing financial support and training to Eurasian scholars studying in the U.S.
*   **Margaret Busby New Daughters of Africa Award:** This award benefits an African woman student and is associated with the anthology *New Daughters of Africa*, which collects the work of women of African descent globally.
*   **Miss America Pageant:** The pageant has evolved significantly since its 1920s origins, facing controversies over race and sexism, and recently underwent format changes to emphasize "competition" over "beauty."
