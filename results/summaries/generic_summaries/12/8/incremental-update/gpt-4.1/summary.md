## Global Scholarship Programs

### Shuping Scholarship
Founded in 1939 in Shanghai, the Shuping Scholarship offers financial, healthcare, and enrichment support to talented but disadvantaged students, serving approximately 700 annually via its Hong Kong branch.

### Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes
Established in 1925, this scholarship is Germany’s leading academic funding body, fostering excellence and civic engagement among thousands of students, with prominent alumni across diverse fields.

### Rhodes Scholarship
Since 1902, the Rhodes Scholarship has brought exceptional postgraduates from around the world to Oxford, expanding access to women and Black Africans and engaging with its complex colonial legacy.

### Bolashak Scholarship
Kazakhstan’s Bolashak program, initiated in 1993, finances international studies for future leaders and advocates of Western values, boasting over 12,800 distinguished alumni.

### Harkness Fellowship
Founded in 1925, this fellowship supports international health policy research in the US and has built a substantial cross-national expert network.

### TMCF & UNCF Initiatives
The Thurgood Marshall College Fund and United Negro College Fund offer scholarships and leadership programs for African-American and HBCU students, raising hundreds of millions and helping to develop prominent graduates.

### Daughters of Africa Initiatives
Rooted in Margaret Busby’s anthologies, initiatives including SOAS scholarships and major fundraising drives advance educational opportunities for African women.

### British Council
Since 1934, the British Council fosters UK education and culture through language teaching, exchanges, and scholarships, while facing criticism over elitism and outsourcing practices.

### National Merit & Achievement Scholarship Programs
The National Merit Program celebrates the top 1% of US high school students, with criticism for test bias. Now merged, UNCF supports Black students’ graduate studies.

### Fulbright Program
Launched in 1946, Fulbright enables academic exchanges in 160+ countries and counts over 325,000 alumni, including Nobel and Pulitzer winners. Geopolitical disputes, like the 2024 Russia suspension, have affected its operations.

### Bright Futures Program
Florida’s Bright Futures funds college tuition, but tougher eligibility standards have restricted access for minorities and low-income students.

### Miss America
Transformed from a beauty pageant, Miss America now functions chiefly as a scholarship program with a focus on diversity, inclusion, and continual modernization.

### Kennedy Scholarship
Founded in 1963, it supports British scholars at Harvard and MIT, prioritizing intellectual excellence and public service, with nearly 600 distinguished alumni.

### Marshall Scholarship
Initiated in 1953, the Marshall Scholarship enables American graduates to study in the UK, producing alumni who excel in public service, government, literature, and science.

### Norway Scholarship
Begun in 1919, this scholarship enables Oslo University students to study at Oxford, enhancing Norway-Britain academic ties.

### King's and Queen's Scholars
King's and Queen's Scholars are prestigious foundation scholars at historic British schools, colleges, and cathedrals, selected for academic or musical merit and recognized by unique traditions. At Westminster School, the scholar's title changes with the monarch and involves special privileges and ceremonies. At King's College, Cambridge, undergraduates with first-class degrees are honored at special ceremonies. King's Scholarships at Henry VIII’s re-founded schools are awarded for academic or musical achievement, often with financial benefits. Scholars enjoy traditions such as cathedral admission ceremonies, special attire, lifelong foundation membership, and ceremonial rights—including marriage or burial in cathedral grounds—with traditions adapted for co-education and gender inclusion over time.

#### King's Scholar at Eton and Cambridge
At Eton College, the King's Scholar tradition began in 1440, selecting Collegers through competitive exams for means-tested scholarships. Many have become influential in British culture and government, with Oppidan Scholars recognized separately. At Cambridge, King's Scholars are distinguished undergraduates honored for academic excellence.

## Edmund S. Muskie: Influence, Legacy, and Public Service

Edmund S. Muskie, son of Polish immigrants, was a transformative Maine politician and public servant, key to major US environmental and civil rights laws, budget reforms, and foreign policy during critical moments as Secretary of State. His legacy includes shaping the Democratic Party in Maine, service on the Tower Commission, multiple honors, and institution-building such as the Muskie School of Public Service.

### Muskie Internship Program
Established by Congress in 1992, this program provides internships, career training, and financial support to Eurasian scholars and graduate students in the US, honoring Muskie’s dedication to global leadership and public service. Relaunched in 2015, it continues to extend his educational impact.
