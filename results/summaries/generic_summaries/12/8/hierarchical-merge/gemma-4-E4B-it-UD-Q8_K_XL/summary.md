# Academic and Scholarship Programs

Various domestic and international scholarship programs provide substantial educational support. Merit-based options include the Studienstiftung, the Rhodes Scholarship, and the Kennedy Scholarship (which funds studies at Harvard or MIT). Specific support is offered to minority students through the Thurgood Marshall College Fund (TMCF) and the UNCF, which aids African-American students at Historically Black Colleges and Universities (HBCUs).

International opportunities are facilitated by programs like the Fulbright Program and the Marshall Scholarship, which supports American citizens for postgraduate study at UK universities. Other specialized grants exist, such as the Norway Scholarship allowing University of Oslo students to study at Oxford. The title "King's Scholar" has varied definitions across institutions, ranging from ancient traditions at Cambridge to merit-based awards at Eton College.

# Political Legacy and Recognition

Edmund S. Muskie was a significant Maine politician who served as a Congressman, Governor, U.S. Senator (1958–1980), and U.S. Secretary of State. Renowned for his environmental advocacy as "Mr. Clean," Muskie championed major legislation including the Clean Air Act of 1963 and the Clean Water Act of 1972, in addition to supporting the 1964 Civil Rights Act.

Muskie's enduring influence is maintained through the Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program, which assists Eurasian scholars, and the observance of "Edmund S. Muskie Day" in Maine. Other forms of public recognition include social initiatives like the Miss America Pageant and national memorials honoring figures such as JFK.
