## Education and Scholarship Programs

### Bolashak Scholarship (Kazakhstan)
Instituted in 1993 by Nursultan Nazarbayev, "Bolashak" translates to "Future" and aims to instill Western democratic values in Kazakhstan's youth. Historically, scholarships have been granted to thousands of students, many of whom now hold prominent state and private positions. The program has seen fluctuating applicant numbers and rigorous selection processes involving language and psychological testing.

### National Merit Scholarship Program (USA)
This program uses the Preliminary SAT/National Merit Scholarship Qualifying Test (PSAT/NMSQT) as an initial screening tool for over 1.5 million entrants. Candidates advance through stages: Commended recognition (top 50,000 scorers), Semifinalist status (around 16,000 recognized), and Finalist standing (around 15,000). Ultimately, about 8,000 Finalists receive Merit Scholarship awards, with corporate sponsors providing additional aid.

### National Achievement Scholarship Program
Established in 1964 to encourage Black American youth to continue their education, the program supported over four million African-Americans. While Black students could previously compete in both the National Achievement and National Merit programs, the National Achievement Scholarship transitioned to the United Negro College Fund (UNCF) in 2015, and Black American high school students no longer compete for Achievement Scholarship awards.

### UNCF (United Negro College Fund)
Founded in 1944 by educational activists, UNCF supports historically Black colleges and universities (HBCUs). Despite its focus on African American students, its scholarships are open to all ethnicities. The UNCF has received significant donations, including \$70 million from MacKenzie Scott in 2025, and utilizes the motto, "A mind is a terrible thing to waste."

### Thurgood Marshall College Fund (TMCF)
TMCF supports public HBCUs, distinguishing its mission from the UNCF. Since its acquisition of the Opportunity Funding Corporation in 2013, it has aimed to enhance entrepreneurship curricula within HBCUs. TMCF has received substantial funding from sources like Apple (\$50 million in 2015).

### Kennedy Scholarship (UK)
Created after John F. Kennedy's assassination, this British memorial provides a scholarship for students to attend either Harvard or MIT. Selection is a national competition where trustees consider intellectual attainment, readiness to express themselves, and the suitability of the proposed course of study, mirroring selection criteria of the Rhodes and Marshall Scholarships.

### Marshall Scholarship (UK-US)
Established in 1953, the Marshall Scholarship aims to promote US-UK understanding by enabling intellectually distinguished young Americans to study in the UK. Selection prioritizes intellect, character, and potential to contribute to their society, with a basic tenure of two years. The program is comparable in selectivity to the Rhodes and Mitchell Scholarships.

### Rhodes Scholarship
Founded in 1902 by Cecil Rhodes, the scholarship aims to foster unity within the British Empire and strengthen US-British diplomatic ties. While originally restricted to single men under 25, the scholarship was opened to women in 1977. Modern scholarship highlights ongoing critiques regarding Rhodes's colonialist views and the career paths of alumni, who often pursue careers in business over public service.

## International Exchange & Global Programs

### Fulbright Program
The Fulbright Program, initiated after WWII, promotes peace and mutual understanding through educational exchange. It operates on a bi-national basis, offering programs like the U.S. Student Program for Americans abroad and the Foreign Student Program for international scholars in the U.S. The program's administration involves the Bureau of Educational and Cultural Affairs, and its prestige is reflected in the notable alumni who have held high office.

### British Council
This royal charter charity promotes UK culture and the English language globally in over 100 countries. Its activities include running the IELTS exam and various cultural exchange programs like Erasmus+. The Council has faced controversies related to its operational closures in politically volatile regions (e.g., Russia, Syria) and issues concerning outsourcing and cultural diplomacy.

### Harkness Fellowship
The Commonwealth Fund initiated these fellowships in 1925 to reciprocate the Rhodes Scholarships, initially for British graduates to study in the US. Since 1997, the focus has narrowed significantly to healthcare policy and practice, with fellows conducting research at major American institutions.

### Studienstiftung (Germany)
This German foundation provides financial and academic support for exceptionally talented students pursuing bachelor's or master's degrees, with studies required in Germany or an EU country. Admission is highly selective and can be gained through various channels, including nominations from schools or universities, or via an assessment test.

## Domestic US Financial Aid and Merit Programs

### Bright Futures Scholarship Program (Florida)
Originally emulating Georgia's HOPE Scholarship, this merit-based program rewarded academic performance in Florida. After funding cuts in 2011, the program was overhauled in 2018, introducing multiple levels (FAS, FMS, GSV, GSC) with specific requirements related to GPA, test scores (ACT/SAT/CLT), and community service hours.

## Miscellaneous Scholarship & Literary Anthologies

### Shuping Scholarship (Shanghai)
Founded in 1939 by Koo to support academically excellent students from poor families, the scholarship operates under the motto, "Return to the Society what you have gained from it." The program emphasizes not just financial aid but also moral and physical education, including voluntary charity work.

### Margaret Busby New Daughters of Africa Award
This award is connected to the anthology *Daughters of Africa*, which is a landmark compilation of literature by women of African descent. The award, partnered with SOAS, aims to benefit an African woman student, recognizing the vast and varied contributions of these global writers.

### King's Scholars (UK)
This historic program, dating back to 1317, grants scholarships at Eton College and other UK institutions, often based on academic or musical attainment. At Eton, King's Scholars reside in the central College and are distinguished by wearing a black academic gown, though modern scholarships are increasingly means-tested.

## Political/Professional Biography Summary

### Edmund S. Muskie
A highly influential American politician, Muskie served as a Maine State Representative, Governor of Maine (1955–1959), and later as a U.S. Senator (1959–1980). He was a principal driver of the environmental movement, co-writing the Clean Air and Clean Water Acts, leading to his nickname "Mr. Clean." As Secretary of State (1980–1981), he engaged with the Soviet Union during the Cold War and managed the Iran hostage crisis. He is widely regarded as the "father of the federal budget process."

### Comparative Summary of International Exchange Programs
Numerous organizations facilitate international understanding. The Fulbright Program promotes peace through educational exchange. The Marshall Scholarship focuses on cultivating future leaders' understanding of Britain. The Rhodes Scholarship emphasizes character and global leadership. The Kennedy Scholarship targets high-achieving students for study at Harvard or MIT. The British Council promotes cultural understanding of the UK and the English language.
