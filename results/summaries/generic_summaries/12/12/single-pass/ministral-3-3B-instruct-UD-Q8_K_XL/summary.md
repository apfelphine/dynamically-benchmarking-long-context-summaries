### **Bolashak Scholarship**
**Overview**
Instituted by Kazakhstan’s first President, Nursultan Nazarbayev, in 1993, the **Bolashak** Scholarship aims to cultivate Western democratic values among Kazakhstan’s youth. Initially limited to 785 students (1994–2004), it expanded significantly, with **12,831 scholarships awarded by 2018**. The program emphasizes academic excellence and leadership, with many graduates holding key positions in state and private organizations.

**Ethnic Distribution**
The scholarship prioritizes Kazakh students (93.6% of candidates), followed by Russian (3%), Korean (0.9%), and Tatar (0.5%) applicants. This reflects the program’s emphasis on Kazakh language proficiency. However, the national ethnic composition has shifted, with Kazakhs now constituting 71% of the population, while Russian speakers make up 14.9%.

**Universities**
Bolashak scholars can study at leading universities worldwide, including institutions in Australia, the UK, Germany, Canada, and the US. Notable universities include:
- **Australia:** Australian National University, Curtin University
- **UK:** University of Cambridge, University of Oxford
- **Germany:** Heidelberg University, Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München
- **Canada:** University of Toronto, McGill University
- **US:** Harvard University, Stanford University

### **National Merit Scholarship Program**
**Overview**
The **National Merit Scholarship Program** (NMSP) is a U.S. initiative offering merit-based scholarships to high-achieving high school students. Administered by the National Merit Scholarship Corporation (NMSC), it began in 1955 and has awarded over **340,000 scholarships** since its inception.

**Eligibility & Selection Process**
To qualify, students must:
1. Take the **Preliminary SAT/NMSQT** in their junior year.
2. Achieve a **Selection Index** ranking in the top 50,000 nationally.
3. Meet additional criteria, including academic performance, leadership, and community service.

**Awards & Recognition**
- **Commended Students:** ~34,000 students receive letters of commendation.
- **National Merit Semifinalists:** ~16,000 students advance to the next round.
- **National Merit Finalists:** ~15,000 students compete for scholarships, including **$2,500 National Merit Scholarships** and **college-sponsored awards**.
- **Honorary Merit Scholars:** Recognized for outstanding achievement without monetary awards.

**Criticism**
Critics argue that the program’s reliance on the **PSAT/NMSQT**—a standardized test—overlooks other forms of merit, such as creativity or community impact. The **National Association for College Admission Counseling (NACAC)** has called for a broader evaluation of applicants, while **FairTest** has raised concerns about transparency in the selection process.

### **National Achievement Scholarship Program**
**Overview**
Established in 1964, the **National Achievement Scholarship Program** was created to encourage African-American youth to pursue higher education. It has awarded over **4 million scholarships** to Black students, with **228,000 recipients** receiving program recognition and **34,000** receiving Achievement Scholarship awards.

**Transition & Legacy**
The program transitioned to the **United Negro College Fund (UNCF)** in 2015, redirecting funds to support historically Black colleges and universities (HBCUs). While Black students can no longer compete for Achievement Scholarship awards, they remain eligible for the **National Merit Scholarship Program**.

### **Bright Futures Scholarship Program**
**Overview**
The **Bright Futures Scholarship Program** (Florida) provides merit-based scholarships to high school graduates, covering tuition and fees at Florida’s public universities. Originally established in 1997 to emulate Georgia’s HOPE Scholarship, it has faced criticism for **racial disparities** and **budget cuts**.

**Scholarship Levels**
- **Florida Academic Scholar (FAS):** Covers **100% of tuition** (minimum SAT/ACT scores: 1330/29).
- **Florida Medallion Scholar (FMS):** Covers **75% of tuition** (minimum SAT/ACT scores: 1190/24).
- **Florida Gold Seal Vocational Scholars (GSV):** For vocational/technical programs.
- **Florida Gold Seal CAPE Scholars (GSC):** For career and professional education.

**Controversies**
- **Racial Bias Allegations:** The U.S. Department of Education investigated claims of **disparate impacts** on Black and Latino students.
- **Budget Cuts:** In 2011, the program’s funding was reduced, leading to stricter eligibility requirements.
- **Criticism of Merit-Based System:** Some argue the program **subsidizes wealthier families** using lottery proceeds.
