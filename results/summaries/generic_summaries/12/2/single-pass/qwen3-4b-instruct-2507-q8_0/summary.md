### Miss America
The Miss America pageant originated in 1919 as a beauty pageant in New York City, distinct from the later Atlantic City-based competition. The modern pageant began in 1921 with a contest in Atlantic City, initially focused on promoting tourism. Over time, it evolved into a major national event, featuring a talent competition and scholarships. The pageant faced criticism for sexism and racism, leading to protests in the 1960s. It became racially segregated under Rule Seven, which was abolished in 1950. Notable milestones include the first African-American contestant, Cheryl Browne, in 1971, and the first African-American winner, Vanessa Williams, in 1984. In 2018, the pageant underwent a reform known as "Miss America 2.0," removing physical appearance judging and introducing a focus on personal narratives. In 2023, some of these changes were reversed. The pageant has faced controversy over scholarship claims and has been criticized for its handling of diversity and inclusivity.

### Fulbright Program
Established in 1946, the Fulbright Program promotes international educational exchange to foster mutual understanding. It operates through bilateral agreements with countries, offering scholarships for students and scholars in various disciplines. The program has faced criticism for political interference, particularly in Russia, where it was declared "undesirable" in 2025. The U.S. government has also faced scrutiny over its scholarship claims and financial transparency. Despite controversies, the program remains a significant vehicle for cultural and academic exchange.

### Marshall Scholarship
Founded in 1953, the Marshall Scholarship provides opportunities for American students to study in the UK. It emphasizes academic excellence, leadership, and a commitment to public service. The program has been criticized for its historical exclusivity to men and for its perceived elitism. Notable alumni include prominent figures in politics, science, and public service. The scholarship has evolved over time, with recent expansions to include more diverse applicants and a broader range of fields.

### British Council
The British Council promotes cultural and educational exchange between the UK and other countries. It offers language teaching, cultural programs, and educational initiatives. The organization has faced criticism for its outsourcing practices and political bias, particularly in relation to its operations in India and Russia. Despite these challenges, the British Council remains a significant player in international cultural diplomacy.

### King's Scholar
The King's Scholar designation is awarded at several institutions, including Eton College and King's College Cambridge. At Eton, it is a prestigious award given to students who demonstrate academic excellence and leadership. At King's College, it is awarded to undergraduates who achieve first-class degrees. The designation reflects a long-standing tradition of academic and personal excellence.

### Shuping Scholarship

### National Merit Scholarship Program
The National Merit Scholarship Program is a U.S.-based competition that awards scholarships to high school students based on academic achievement. The program has faced criticism for its reliance on standardized tests and for its lack of transparency in the selection process. Despite these challenges, it remains a significant source of financial support for college-bound students.

### Thurgood Marshall College Fund
The Thurgood Marshall College Fund supports public historically black colleges and universities (HBCUs). It provides scholarships, programmatic support, and capacity-building initiatives. The organization has grown significantly, with over $500 million in funding raised to date. It has been instrumental in promoting access to higher education for students from underrepresented communities.

### UNCF
The United Negro College Fund (UNCF) provides scholarships and support to students at historically Black colleges and universities (HBCUs). It has a long history of promoting educational equity and has received significant donations from prominent figures. The organization continues to support students in various fields, including business, politics, and the arts.
