# Global Exchange and Scholarship Programs

Numerous international and national programs facilitate global understanding and academic pursuit. International initiatives include the Fulbright Program and the Marshall Scholarship, which support U.S. educators and leaders in the UK, alongside organizations like the British Council. Specialized awards such as the Rhodes, Kennedy, and Harkness Fellowships, and the Shuping Scholarship, provide targeted support.

Various national scholarships offer focused aid; for example, Florida's Bright Futures Scholarship relies on GPA, while the National Merit Scholarship Program is based on PSAT/NMSQT scores. Support is also provided to African American students through the UNCF and the Thurgood Marshall College Fund (TMCF), and the Kazakhstani Bolashak Scholarship promotes democratic values among Kazakh youth.

# Institutional Support and Notable Figures

Data concerning Historically Black Colleges and Universities (HBCUs) is maintained through UNCF affiliates, which track metrics like endowment size and enrollment for member institutions such as Spelman College and Clark Atlanta University. Archival resources are available, including a 2015 collection detailing materials related to institutions like Oxford and LSE.

Edmund S. Muskie was a significant U.S. political figure, noted for his advocacy in civil rights and environmental protection, notably advancing the Clean Air and Clean Water Acts. His career included serving as Governor of Maine and as U.S. Secretary of State under Jimmy Carter, where he managed crises such as the Iran hostage crisis.
