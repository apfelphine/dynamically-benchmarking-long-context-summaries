**Support for African American Education and HBCUs**
The United Negro College Fund (UNCF), founded in 1944 and now inclusive of all ethnicities, received a \$70 million donation from MacKenzie Scott in 2025 to bolster HBCUs, including Clark Atlanta University, Spelman College, and Tuskegee University. Additionally, the Thurgood Marshall College Fund (TMCF), established in 1987, supports 55 public HBCUs through scholarships and capacity building. TMCF raised over \$500 million and acquired the Opportunity Funding Corporation (OFC) in 2013 to promote HBCU entrepreneurship, tracking members such as Howard University and North Carolina A&T State University.
