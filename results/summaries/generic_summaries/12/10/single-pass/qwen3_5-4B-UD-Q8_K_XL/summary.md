# Scholarships and Educational Exchanges

The provided text details the histories, missions, and structures of numerous international and domestic scholarship programs designed to support higher education, foster cultural exchange, and promote public service. These initiatives range from US-based programs focusing on historical Black colleges to global exchanges between the United States and other nations.

Key domestic initiatives include the **Thurgood Marshall College Fund**, established in 1987 to support public Historically Black Colleges and Universities (HBCUs), and the **United Negro College Fund (UNCF)**, founded in 1944 to address educational inequities for African Americans through scholarships and grants. Both organizations have grown significantly, with major donors like Apple, Boeing, and Netflix contributing substantial funding. Another US-specific program is the **Bright Futures Scholarship**, a state-level initiative in Florida that provides merit-based aid to public university students, though it has faced criticism regarding its impact on minority populations and recent legislative changes. Additionally, the **National Merit Scholarship Program** identifies high-achieving students based on standardized test scores, offering various tiers of awards including college-sponsored scholarships.

Internationally, the **Rhodes Scholarship** remains one of the world's most prestigious postgraduate awards, originally established in 1902 to promote unity within the British Empire. While initially restricted to male students, it opened to women in 1977 and expanded globally in 2018. The **Marshall Scholarship**, created in 1953, serves as a reciprocal program for American students to study in the UK, emphasizing academic excellence and the potential for public service. Other notable exchanges include the **Fulbright Program**, which facilitates educational exchange between the US and over 160 countries, and the **Harkness Fellowship**, which supports international researchers in health care policy. The **Kennedy Scholarship** and **Norway Scholarship** also facilitate study at top US and British institutions, respectively, often mirroring the selection processes of the Rhodes Scholarship.
