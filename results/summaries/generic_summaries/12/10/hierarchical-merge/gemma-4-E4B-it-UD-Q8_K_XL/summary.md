## Global Scholarship Programs
Numerous highly competitive global scholarships support exceptional talent across various fields. Prominent international opportunities include the Rhodes Scholarship (focusing on leadership) and the Fulbright Program (for international exchange). Specialized awards target specific demographics, such as the Thurgood Marshall College Fund (TMCF) and UNCF for African American students, while other niche programs support low-income, gifted scholars.

Beyond international options, other notable academic awards exist, including the Kennedy Scholarship, which supports merit-based candidates for Harvard or MIT, and the Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes for advanced European studies. Additionally, the Marshall Scholarship promotes U.S.-U.K. understanding by funding postgraduate study in the UK.

## Political Figures
Edmund S. Muskie was a significant American politician who served as Governor of Maine and in the U.S. Senate. He was instrumental in passing landmark legislation, including the Clean Air Act of 1963, the Clean Water Act of 1965, and the Civil Rights Act of 1964. Muskie later served as U.S. Secretary of State under Jimmy Carter and is recognized for his role in shaping the federal budget process.

## Miss America Pageant
The Miss America Pageant, originating around 1920, has evolved from a business promotion to an award of college scholarships. The pageant has navigated issues of sexism and racism, marked by milestones such as the first Black contestant in 1971 and the first Black winner in 1984. Recent transformations include the 2018 removal of swimsuit judging and the 2022 crowning of the first transgender titleholder. The organization has also faced recent structural instability, filing for Chapter 11 bankruptcy in late 2024.
