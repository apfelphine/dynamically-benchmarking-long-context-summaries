# International Scholarships and Fellowship Programs

## Norway Scholarship
Established in 1919, the Norway Scholarship enables students from the University of Oslo to study for a year at Wadham College, Oxford, aiming to foster closer cultural and educational ties between Norway and Britain. Funding challenges led to periods of suspension and revitalization through fundraising and engagement of alumni. The program, marked by annual events and participation from notable Norwegians including King Harald V, has supported scholars in diverse fields since 1920.

## King's Scholar Programs
King's Scholars originated in the 14th century at Cambridge and expanded to Eton and Westminster Schools, recognizing students through competitive exams and awarding privileges and traditions. At Eton, King's Scholars are distinguished by academic gowns and unique customs, while financial support for scholarships is increasingly means-tested. Similar programs exist at King's College Cambridge and several King's Schools, blending academic excellence with enduring ceremonial rights and privileges.

## Kennedy Scholarship
Created in 1964 as a memorial to President John F. Kennedy, this scholarship funds British students for graduate study at Harvard or MIT. Selection mirrors the rigor of programs like Rhodes and Marshall Scholarships, focusing on intellect, leadership, and public service commitment. The Kennedy Memorial is both a scholarship and a commemorative site at Runnymede, with ongoing involvement from the Kennedy family and notable British figures.

## Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program
Named after US Secretary of State and Maine Governor Edmund Muskie, this program supports Eurasian scholars and graduate students in the US through summer internships and career training. Muskie’s legacy includes landmark environmental legislation (Clean Air and Water Acts), civil rights advocacy, and public service innovation, with the program reflecting his commitment to education and public service.

## Bright Futures Scholarship Program
A Florida state program established in the late 1990s, Bright Futures provides academic merit-based scholarships for post-secondary education at several levels, covering tuition and fees for eligible Florida high school graduates. Requirements include minimum GPA, standardized test scores, and service hours. The program has faced multiple reforms over time, including increased score requirements, changes in award amounts, and consideration for vocational pathways.

## Marshall Scholarship
Founded in 1953, the Marshall Scholarship sends talented American graduates to study at UK universities, promoting transatlantic understanding. Selection is highly competitive, emphasizing academic excellence, leadership, and engagement in British society. Borrowing inspiration from the Rhodes model, the program has evolved to expand eligibility, host annual forums, and maintain a notable alumni network.

## National Merit Scholarship Program
This US program identifies high-achieving high school students via the PSAT, awarding scholarships based on academic excellence and additional criteria. Finalists may receive awards funded by NMSC, corporations, or participating colleges. The program is prestigious but has faced criticism over reliance on standardized testing and transparency concerns.

## Bolashak Scholarship
Launched by Kazakhstan in 1993, Bolashak funds students for overseas study in leading global universities, aiming to modernize the country through exposure to international education and values. The majority of recipients are ethnic Kazakh, reflecting language eligibility. Thousands of graduates now serve in major roles within Kazakhstan’s public and private sectors.

## Fulbright Program
Established in 1946, Fulbright is a globally renowned US government-funded educational exchange, promoting peace through international academic collaboration. It offers grants for students, scholars, researchers, and professionals and operates through bilateral agreements and commissions in over 160 countries. Fulbright alumni include Nobel laureates, heads of state, and leading academics. Recent challenges include political interference and program suspension in Russia.

## UNCF (United Negro College Fund)
Founded in 1944, UNCF provides scholarships and institutional support mainly to students at its member historically Black colleges and universities (HBCUs) but also to others. Funded through charitable donations, high-profile campaigns, and major corporate gifts, UNCF’s motto “A mind is a terrible thing to waste” is iconic. The Fund’s impact includes notable alumni and strengthened HBCU capacity.

## Thurgood Marshall College Fund
Founded in 1987, TMCF represents and supports public HBCUs with scholarship, leadership development, and capacity-building programs. It operates separately from UNCF and receives substantial corporate philanthropy. TMCF also promotes entrepreneurship and talent identification in the HBCU community.

## British Council
Established in the 1930s, the British Council promotes UK culture, language, and education abroad through English teaching, cultural exchange, and support for international collaboration. It operates globally, provides resources like IELTS exams, and manages research and artistic programs, though political, financial, and operational controversies have impacted aspects of its work.

## Rhodes Scholarship
Founded in 1902, the Rhodes Scholarship is a prestigious international award for postgraduate study at Oxford, emphasizing academic excellence, leadership, and service. Originally limited to males, it is now open globally and has produced influential leaders, academics, and innovators. The program has faced criticism regarding its colonial legacy and evolving recipient career paths.

## Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes
Germany’s premier scholarship foundation, Studienstiftung offers financial and academic support to exceptionally talented students and doctoral candidates via rigorous nomination and selection processes. The program includes general and specialized scholarships, internal and open programs, and a strong alumni network across scientific, business, and cultural fields.

## Margaret Busby New Daughters of Africa Award
Inspired by Busby’s landmark anthology, this scholarship supports African women’s education in the UK in partnership with SOAS and International Students House. The award, funded by the editor, publisher, and contributing authors, highlights the literary and cultural achievements of women of African descent and extends Busby’s legacy of representation and advocacy.

## Shuping Scholarship
Founded in Shanghai in 1939 by Koo, the Shuping Scholarship supports financially disadvantaged but promising students through secondary and tertiary education. With a strong emphasis on service, community, and self-governance, the program continues through alumni associations and a foundation, positively impacting education and society in China.

## Harkness Fellowship
Established in 1925 as a reciprocal to the Rhodes Scholarship, the Harkness Fellowships support study and research in the US, now focusing on health policy for professionals from select countries. Administered by the Commonwealth Fund, fellows undertake research and participate in policy seminars, joining an influential international alumni community.

# Cultural and Academic Programs

## Miss America
Originating in 1920, Miss America is a national competition blending beauty, talent, and scholarship. Over time, it has evolved in response to social change, controversies, and calls for greater inclusivity, shifting focus towards talent and achievement. The program has provided scholarships to contestants and engaged with broader cultural debates, leadership changes, and modernized competition formats.

## Daughters of Africa Anthologies and Legacy
Margaret Busby's anthologies 'Daughters of Africa' and 'New Daughters of Africa' compile writing from over 400 women of African descent, celebrating literary and cultural achievements across centuries. Critically acclaimed, these works offer an invaluable reference and inspiration, fostering global understanding, representation, and educational opportunity through associated awards and outreach initiatives.
