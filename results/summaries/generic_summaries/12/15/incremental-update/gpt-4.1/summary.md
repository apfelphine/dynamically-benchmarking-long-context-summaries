## Prestigious International Scholarships

Internationally recognized scholarships promote academic excellence, leadership, and diversity across many fields.

### Rhodes Scholarship

Founded in 1902, the Rhodes Scholarship selects outstanding students worldwide based on academic achievement, leadership, service, and commitment to justice and diversity. Notable for historic strides in inclusion, its alumni include leaders in government, science, law, medicine, literature, and culture, inspiring similar programs globally.

### Kennedy, Marshall, Harkness, Knight-Hennessy, Schwarzman, Gates Cambridge

- **Kennedy Scholarship (est. 1964):** Supports British students at Harvard and MIT, fostering transatlantic leadership.
- **Marshall Scholarship (since 1953):** Enables top Americans to study in the UK, strengthening UK–US ties and producing prominent alumni.
- **Harkness Fellowship (est. 1925):** Evolved from general study to focus on health policy research for international fellows at leading US institutions.
- **Knight-Hennessy, Schwarzman, Gates Cambridge:** Prestigious programs modeled on earlier scholarships, offering global study and emphasizing leadership.

### Additional Key Scholarships

- **Norway Scholarship:** Fosters UK-Norwegian academic exchanges at Oxford.
- **Bright Futures (Florida):** State merit-based scholarships with equity debates.
- **King’s Scholars:** Historic elite programs at Eton, Westminster, and King’s College Cambridge.
- **National Merit:** US scholarships via standardized tests.
- **Bolashak (Kazakhstan):** Develops national leaders abroad.
- **Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes:** Germany’s largest scholarship for gifted EU students.
- **Shuping Scholarship:** Expands opportunities for disadvantaged youth in China.
- **UNCF and TMCF:** Major US organizations supporting African American education at HBCUs and PBIs.
- **Miss America:** A leading women’s scholarship competition.

### British Council

Advances UK culture and education globally through programs like Education UK and Erasmus+, with ongoing debates around inclusivity.

### Fulbright Program

Established in 1946, the Fulbright facilitates international academic exchange, producing leading global alumni and promoting cross-cultural understanding.

## Daughters of Africa and Literary Influence

Edited by Margaret Busby, **Daughters of Africa** (1992) and **New Daughters of Africa** (2019) are landmark anthologies of writings by women of African descent, highlighted for their historical scope, diversity, and thematic richness. The anthologies, acclaimed globally, emphasize generational links and explore themes of race, feminism, family, and identity. The 2019 volume features over 200 contributors and received a 2020 NAACP Image Award nomination. Its launch spurred educational initiatives, including donations to UK schools and African libraries, and inspired the Margaret Busby New Daughters of Africa Award at SOAS, providing scholarships for African women, supported by contributors and publishers.

---

These scholarship programs and cultural initiatives have shaped generations of leaders and creators across disciplines, sparking ongoing conversations about access, inclusion, equity, and the amplification of marginalized voices in both academia and the literary world.
