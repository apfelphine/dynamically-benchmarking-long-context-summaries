### Scholarship and Award Programs
A wide range of academic support is available, including global opportunities such as the **Kennedy Scholarship**, **King's College Cambridge Scholars**, **King's Schools/Westminster School Scholars**, **Norway Scholarship** (Oxford's Wadham College), **Rhodes Scholarship** (which has increased inclusion for women and transgender/non-binary scholars since 1977 and 2018, respectively), **Fulbright Program**, and **Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes**. Specialized grants include the **Harkness Fellowship** (focusing on health care policy since 1997). Domestic aid encompasses the **National Merit Scholarship Program**, Florida's **Bright Futures Scholarship Program**, **Bolashak**, the **Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program** for Eurasian scholars, and support from the **UNCF** and **TMCF** for African American students at HBCUs, in addition to the **Shuping Scholarship** for community service.

### Edmund S. Muskie's Career and Legacy
Edmund S. Muskie (b. 1914) was a significant American politician who served in the Maine House of Representatives and as Governor of Maine (1954-1959). As a U.S. Senator (1958–1980), he advocated for landmark legislation, including the **Clean Air Act of 1963**, the **Civil Rights Act of 1964**, and the **Clean Water Act** (1972, 1977). He finished his career as U.S. Secretary of State under Jimmy Carter (1980–1981), managing foreign policy during the Iranian hostage crisis.

### Miss America Pageant History and Milestones
Since its start in 1921, the "Miss America Pageant" has faced ongoing criticism regarding sexism and racism, despite improvements in Black representation since the 1970s. Structural changes have occurred, including "Miss America 2.0" (2017–2022), and the 2023 iteration featured fitness modeling and a "HER Story" category. Nonetheless, the organization remains under scrutiny and filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy in 2024.

### International Cultural Promotion and Challenges
The **British Council**, a global UK charity founded in 1934, promotes British and English culture through education and IELTS administration. Its operations face risks such as potential geopolitical expulsion from Russia in 2025 and regional violence. Other ongoing challenges include concerns over historical data security, political bias from outsourcing, elitism critiques, the Israel-Palestine conflict, issues involving Chinese writers, internal staff disputes, IELTS corruption in China, and visa scams in Pakistan.

### Literature of African Descent
Margaret Busby's anthologies are pivotal in documenting the literature of African descent. ***Daughters of Africa*** **(1992)** was a key work featuring women of African descent. This legacy continues with ***New Daughters of Africa*** **(2019)**, an international anthology featuring over 200 contributors from the 19th century onward, organized by birth decade to chart the black feminist literary canon across Africa and the Diaspora. This acclaimed work has been published across the UK, US, South Africa, and Germany, and has been nominated for a 2020 NAACP Image Award. To further support this field, the **Margaret Busby New Daughters of Africa Award** was established, with initial recipients announced in 2020 and 2023.
