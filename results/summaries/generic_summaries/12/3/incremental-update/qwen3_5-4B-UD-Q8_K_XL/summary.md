# Comprehensive Summary of Scholarship Programs

## **US Educational Scholarship Landscape**
The United States offers diverse scholarship pathways, primarily distinguished between advocacy and merit-based funding. The **National Urban League (NUL)**, spanning 13 states with member institutions ranging from under $1 million to over $700 million in endowments, serves as a pivotal network for Black Americans, operating distinctively from the scholarship-focused **United Negro College Fund (UNCF)**. UNCF manages significant resources through campaigns like "A mind is a terrible thing to waste," while the **National Merit Scholarship Program** functions as a competitive annual contest requiring PSAT/NMSQT participation. This program offers three selection rounds (Commended, Semifinalist, Finalist), with approximately 8,000 Finalists receiving scholarships totaling $2,500 from the National Merit Scholarship Corporation (NMSC), alongside corporate and college-specific awards contingent on full-time enrollment in accredited U.S. colleges. The 2018-19 report identified the University of Florida as the top recipient with 270 total scholars (231 college-sponsored). However, the program faces criticism from organizations like NACAC and FairTest regarding its reliance on the PSAT/NMSQT as the sole pre-screening tool, its exclusion of 99% of test-takers, and a lack of transparency in state-level winner data. Additionally, the **National Achievement Scholarship Program**, established in 1964 for African American students, transitioned to the UNCF in 2016, shifting its focus from high schoolers to high-achieving Black college graduates.

## **International and Ethnic Distribution Initiatives**
The **Bolashak Program**, instituted in 1993 by Kazakhstan's first president, Nursultan Nazarbayev, aims to send Kazakhstani youth abroad to study Western democratic values and technologies. Highly selective and predominantly benefiting candidates of Kazakh ethnicity due to language requirements, the program granted over 12,800 scholarships by 2018. While statistics from 2008 revealed that 93.6% of candidates were ethnic Kazakhs—contrasting sharply with the country's more diverse 2021 census data—critics note that graduates often lack the authority to implement changes in their home country.

## **Eligible Institutions for the Bolashak Program**
For the 2023 academic year, the Bolashak Program lists numerous eligible universities across global regions. These institutions span major educational hubs including:
*   **United States:** Harvard, Stanford
*   **United Kingdom:** Oxford, Cambridge
*   **Australia:** UNSW, Melbourne
*   **China:** Peking, Tsinghua
*   **Europe:** Germany, France, Netherlands
*   **Asia:** Japan, Korea, Singapore
*   **Other Regions:** Canada, Russia, and South Africa

The program offers opportunities for academic study in all specialties across these diverse international partners.
