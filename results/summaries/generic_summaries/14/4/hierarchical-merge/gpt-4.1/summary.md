## Stanford University

Founded in 1885 in Silicon Valley, Stanford University stands as a leading research institution that drives global innovation and entrepreneurship. Home to seven academic schools and major research centers such as SLAC and the Hoover Institution, Stanford boasts a $36.5 billion endowment and a distinguished community of Nobel and Turing laureates. The university maintains a vibrant student life, prioritizes inclusion and campus safety, and remains at the forefront of technological and social advancements.

## Tsinghua University

Established in 1911 in Beijing, Tsinghua University is widely recognized as China’s premier research university, excelling in engineering, sciences, technology, business, and economics. Known for selective admissions and a robust research infrastructure, Tsinghua influences national education and policy and nurtures alumni who are prominent leaders in science, industry, and politics. The campus is defined by its rich heritage and dynamic student activities, with deep engagement in government and academia.

## University of California, Berkeley

UC Berkeley is a globally prominent university renowned for groundbreaking innovations such as Unix, CRISPR, and radiocarbon dating. The campus offers over 350 degree programs, features iconic architectural landmarks, and supports an active student community. Berkeley’s alumni and faculty include Nobel, Pulitzer, and Academy Award winners, and the university remains committed to both tradition and activism, while facing ongoing financial and social challenges.

## Zhejiang University (ZJU)

Founded in 1897, Zhejiang University is a top comprehensive university in China specializing in STEM and medical education, operating seven hospitals and supporting a vast student population. ZJU’s reputation is anchored by its formidable research infrastructure and acclaimed faculty, including Nobel laureates, though it continues to navigate governance and campus-related challenges.

## California Institute of Technology (Caltech)

Located in Pasadena, Caltech is internationally recognized for excellence in science and engineering, managing NASA’s Jet Propulsion Laboratory and making pioneering contributions to gravitational wave research. With a highly selective admissions process and 48 Nobel laureates among its affiliates, Caltech fosters a collaborative academic culture and underscores sustainability and entrepreneurial accomplishments.

## University of Chicago

The University of Chicago is celebrated for its rigorous academics, transformative research in economics, science, and the humanities—including the Chicago school of economics and the first nuclear chain reaction—and a vibrant student community. Its vast resources, including extensive libraries and the nation’s largest university press, support ongoing innovation and academic leadership. Despite financial challenges, the university maintains a selective admissions process, a diverse population, and a legacy of Nobel laureates under President Paul Alivisatos.

## Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT)

MIT in Cambridge, Massachusetts is a world leader in scientific and technological innovation, known for rigorous academics, technical excellence, and gender equity. Through programs such as UROP, MIT facilitates hands-on research, contributing to breakthroughs in digital technology, radar, AI, and cryptography. Faculty and alumni—including numerous Nobel laureates—drive the founding of influential companies like Intel and Bose. MIT’s global impact is amplified by vibrant campus life, over 500 student organizations, and leadership in academia, industry, and government.

## King’s College London

King’s College London is a prestigious public research university and founding member of the University of London, renowned for its strengths in arts, sciences, law, business, and health. With five central London campuses and affiliations with leading hospitals, King's is noted for major scientific contributions, including the discovery of DNA’s structure. The university features a diverse and selective student body, significant research income, and a celebrated network of Nobel laureates, supporting an active academic community enriched by strong student engagement and exceptional campus resources.
