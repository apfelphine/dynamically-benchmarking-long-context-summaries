# Global University Profiles and Strengths

**Elite STEM and Research Powerhouses (US & International)**
Leading US institutions include Stanford University, famous for its innovation culture and 4% undergraduate acceptance rate, and UC Berkeley, a major public research center for scientific breakthroughs like CRISPR. Caltech is recognized for its intense focus and excellence in STEM fields, often supported by agencies like NASA and NSF, while MIT is globally renowned for its polytechnic culture, engineering, and pioneering work in AI and computing.

**Leading Global Institutions**
Internationally, Tsinghua University is ranked No. 1 in China, excelling in fields such as Engineering and Computer Science. King's College London (KCL) is a prominent UK university noted for its strong graduate employability, involvement in healthcare education, and historical scientific contributions. Other notable global universities include the University of Chicago, known for its liberal arts core, and Zhejiang University (ZJU).
