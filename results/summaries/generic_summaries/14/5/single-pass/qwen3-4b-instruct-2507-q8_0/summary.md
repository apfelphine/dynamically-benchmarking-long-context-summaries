### **Tsinghua University**
- **History**: Founded in 1911 as Tsinghua College, initially a preparatory school for U.S. study. Merged with Peking and Nankai Universities during WWII to form the National Southwestern Associated University. After the Communist Revolution (1949), Tsinghua's leadership fled to Taiwan, establishing a separate institution. In 1952, reorganized into a polytechnic focusing on engineering and natural sciences. Key developments include establishing political counselor programs, contributions to transistor technology during the Cultural Revolution, and the creation of the Schwarzman Scholars program in 2016.
- **Academics**: Offers 51 bachelor’s, 139 master’s, and 107 PhD programs across 20 schools and 58 departments. Strong in engineering, science, and social sciences. Notable research includes breakthroughs in computer science and materials science. The university is a member of C9 League, APU, and other global academic networks.
- **Rankings & Reputation**: Ranked #1 in China and the Asia-Pacific by THE, #1 in Asia in QS Graduate Employability, and #1 in the ARWU for Asia-Pacific. Recognized for high research output and global impact.
- **Notable Alumni**: Includes Xi Jinping, Hu Jintao, and numerous Nobel laureates in physics and chemistry. Known for producing top political and scientific leaders.
- **Campus & Culture**: Located in Beijing, recognized as one of the world’s most beautiful campuses. Features a strong tradition of student life, with notable student organizations and academic traditions.

### **Zhejiang University**
- **History**: Founded in 1897 as Qiushi Academy, evolved into a modern university after WWII relocations. Key developments include the establishment of the "New Plan" curriculum under Robert Maynard Hutchins, the creation of the undergraduate college, and a major expansion in the 1990s. The university merged with four other institutions in 1998 to form the modern Zhejiang University.
- **Academics**: Offers a broad range of programs across 15 colleges and schools, with strong emphasis on engineering, medicine, and the sciences. The university has 11 State Key Laboratories and is known for its research in life sciences and environmental studies.
- **Rankings & Reputation**: Ranked #24 globally in THE, #44 in QS, and #33 in Times Higher Education World Reputation Rankings. Consistently ranks among the top universities in Asia.
- **Notable Alumni**: Includes Nobel laureates, scientists, and prominent leaders such as Chen Duxiu, Li Qiang, and Xu Guangxian. Known for its contributions to science and public service.
- **Campus & Culture**: Operates across seven campuses, including the main Zijingang campus and the Haining International Campus. Known for its strong traditions, student life, and cultural diversity.

### **Stanford University**
- **History**: Founded in 1885 by Leland and Jane Stanford as a tribute to their son. Known for its progressive values and early commitment to inclusivity. Key developments include the establishment of the Stanford Industrial Park, the founding of the Stanford Research Institute, and the development of Silicon Valley through the work of faculty and alumni.
- **Academics**: Offers a wide range of programs, with strong emphasis on engineering, business, and computer science. The university is home to the Stanford Graduate School of Business, which is consistently ranked among the top business schools globally.
- **Rankings & Reputation**: Ranked #2 globally in the ARWU, #3 in the U.S. by THE, and consistently ranked among the top universities in the world. Known for its innovation and research output.
- **Notable Alumni**: Includes Herbert Hoover, Bill Gates, and numerous tech industry leaders. Known for producing influential leaders in technology and business.
- **Campus & Culture**: Located in Palo Alto, California, with a focus on innovation and entrepreneurship. Features a vibrant student life, with a strong tradition of student organizations and academic excellence.

- **Notable Alumni**: Includes Nobel laureates, economists, and prominent political figures such as Barack Obama and Milton Friedman. Known for its influence in economics and public policy.
- **Campus & Culture**: Located in Hyde Park, Chicago, with a focus on academic excellence and research. Features a strong tradition of student life and academic excellence.
