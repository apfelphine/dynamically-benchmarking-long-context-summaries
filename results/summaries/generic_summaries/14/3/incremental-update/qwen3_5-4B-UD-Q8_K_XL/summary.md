### **University of Chicago: Prestige, Alumni, and Campus Culture**

Ranked consistently among the top 10 globally, the University of Chicago upholds its elite status through exceptional academic output, hosting 101 Nobel laureates and notable alumni such as Barack Obama and T.S. Eliot. Its governance is overseen by a board of trustees led by David Rubenstein, managing a $5.2 billion budget and a $10.1 billion endowment, though the institution recently navigated financial challenges requiring budget cuts. Academically, the university's scope extends beyond undergraduate and graduate research to include K-12 schools, specialized treatment programs, the University of Chicago Press, and a library holding 11 million volumes.

Operationally, UChicago runs on a quarter system with a highly selective admissions process that eliminated standardized test requirements in 2018, enrolling nearly 19,000 students in fall 2024. This academic rigor is complemented by a vibrant campus culture featuring over 400 recognized student organizations, a diverse student body, and a distinctive house system for undergraduate housing. Iconic traditions include the annual Scavenger Hunt, the Kuviasungnerk winter festival, and the Summer Breeze carnival. Furthermore, the university maintains a rich presence in pop culture, serving as a setting for characters in *When Harry Met Sally* and *The Martian*, as well as a filming location for major movies, balancing its rich heritage with modern operational demands.
