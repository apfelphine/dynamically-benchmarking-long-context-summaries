# Comprehensive Summary: Zhejiang University (ZJU)

## Historical Evolution and Modern Development
Zhejiang University (ZJU) traces its origins to the 1897 Qiushi Academy, evolving through mergers to become the National Chekiang University in 1928. Its history is deeply marked by political shifts, including wartime relocations to Guizhou during the 1937 Sino-Japanese War, a return to Hangzhou in 1945, and restructuring into an engineering-focused institution following the 1949 Communist takeover. Operations were severely disrupted during the Cultural Revolution (1966–1976) before recovering in the 1980s. Modern milestones include its designation as a "211 Project" university in 1995, the 1998 merger of four universities to form the current institution, and the subsequent establishment of the Ocean College and international joint institutes.

## Campus Infrastructure and Administration
Today, ZJU comprises seven main campuses spanning 6.22 square kilometers, including the main Zijingang campus in Hangzhou and locations in Ningbo, Zhoushan, Haining, and others. Recent infrastructure events include the 2013 sale of the Huajiachi campus due to local opposition and proposals in 2016 to restore the Meitan Campus in Zunyi, though the university clarified in 2018 it had no substantial restoration plans. The Hubin Campus, formerly Zhejiang Medical University, was largely demolished in 2007 for the Kerry Center commercial complex, with only the former High Court site preserved as an exhibition hall.

Governed as a national university jointly funded by central and provincial governments, ZJU is operated by the Communist Party Committee, with the President and Party Secretary appointed by the CPC Organization Department. The institution is organized into 17 administrative departments managing strategy, research, finance, and operations. Academically, ZJU is divided into seven faculties covering eleven branches of learning, with notable strengths in the Faculty of Information (top-ranked in AI and Computer Science) and the Faculty of Agriculture, Life and Environment Science (holding the most A+/A/A- rated subjects). The university oversees the Faculty of Medicine, which manages seven affiliated teaching hospitals, including the First, Second, and Sir Run Run Shaw Hospitals. ZJU holds the **second-largest budget in China** (26.1 billion CNY in 2022) and is a top recipient of alumni donations.

## Global Standing, Research, and Rankings
ZJU is a top-tier academic institution, consistently ranking in the top 50 globally and top 3 in China. In 2024, it placed **#33 worldwide** in aggregate performance (THE, QS, ARWU) and **#3 in scientific research output** by the Nature Index. Its global recognition is further evidenced by a **2025 Academic Ranking of World Universities (ARWU) of #24** and a **2024 QS World University Rankings of #44**. It is ranked **4th globally** in CWTS Leiden Research Performance and **5th** by SCImago in 2024.

The university leads in several disciplines, including Computer Science, Chemical Engineering, and Physics, with **15 of its 22 disciplines** in the top 1% of the Essential Science Indicator (ESI). It conducts research across 12 disciplines, boasts over 1,800 professors, and maintains **11 State Key Laboratories**. While the institution continues to face ongoing discussions regarding academic leadership and the development of affiliated holding companies, it remains a leading research institution recognized for its significant academic output, research capabilities, and financial strength.

## Identity, Culture, and Student Life
The university's identity is anchored by its motto, "Seeking Truth" (*Qiúshì*), established in 1938 from Yangmingism, which was expanded in 1988 to include "Pursuing Innovation" to reflect the era of reforms. Its anthem was composed in 1941 by Professor Ying Shangneng after earlier lyrics were deemed too obscure. Official symbols include the **Qiushi eagle**, which has appeared in seals and logos since the 1920s, and the current logo featuring calligraphy from Mao Zedong, adopted in 1991. Campus culture is influenced by former President Chu Kochen's vision to educate leaders rather than just experts, with his guiding questions prominently displayed for freshmen. This vision is realized through the **Chu Kochen Honors College (CKHC)**, founded in 2000, which offers highly selective, creative entrance exams and specialized classes in Engineering, Medicine, and Humanities. Additionally, the **Qiushi College**, established in 2008, focuses on liberal arts education and student support.

Regarding student life, ZJU enrolled approximately **65,821 full-time students** (undergraduates, masters, and doctoral candidates) as of June 2023, including over 5,000 international students. A student-run forum, CC98, has served as a discussion platform since 2002.

## Notable Alumni and Faculty
The university boasts a prestigious list of alumni, including Nobel laureate Tsung-Dao Lee, Premier Li Qiang, and entrepreneur Wang Jianzhou. The faculty includes distinguished scholars such as Fields Medalist Shing-Tung Yau and Wolf Prize winners. The university library system holds over **7.9 million volumes**, one of China's largest academic collections, consisting of six campus libraries, three college-specific libraries, and a network supporting inter-library borrowing. ZJU also offers international dual-degree programs with partners such as the University of Edinburgh.
