## **History and Origins**
*   **Caltech** evolved from Throop College of Technology, founded in 1891 by Amos G. Throop. It became a premier scientific research institute through the leadership of George Ellery Hale and Arthur Amos Noyes, establishing national prominence in the 1920s.
*   **UC Berkeley** was founded in 1868 as a land-grant university, inheriting the College of California and federal funding eligibility. It relocated to Berkeley in 1873 and gained global fame for its political activism and scientific breakthroughs, such as the discovery of 16 chemical elements at Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory.
*   **MIT** was chartered in 1861 to promote practical education for the industrial classes. It transitioned from a vocational school to a research powerhouse through the "Technology Plan" and the establishment of the Radiation Laboratory during World War II.
*   **Stanford University** was founded in 1885 by Leland and Jane Stanford. It grew into a financial powerhouse through the development of Silicon Valley and the establishment of the Stanford Research Park under Frederick Terman.
*   **King's College London** was founded in 1829 as a rival to London University, emphasizing Anglican tradition before merging with other institutions to become a major UK research center.
*   **Tsinghua University** was established in 1911 using funds from the US Boxer indemnity. It merged with Peking University and Nankai University during the Second Sino-Japanese War and later became a multidisciplinary powerhouse in China.
*   **University of Chicago** was reincorporated in 1890 as the new University of Chicago. It became famous under presidents like Robert Maynard Hutchins for its Great Books curriculum and research, including the Manhattan Project.
*   **Zhejiang University (ZJU)** traces its roots to Qiushi Academy in 1897. It underwent significant reorganization in the 1998 merger, becoming one of the largest higher education institutions in Asia.
