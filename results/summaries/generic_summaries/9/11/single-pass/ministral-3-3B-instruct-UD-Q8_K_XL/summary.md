## **Education in Poland**

### **Compulsory Education**
Poland’s compulsory education spans **12 years**, beginning at age **6** (with kindergarten attendance required before starting primary school). The system has undergone significant reforms, notably the **2017 restructuring**, which extended primary education from **6 to 8 years** and merged junior high school (gimnazjum) into the primary school curriculum.

- **Primary School (Szkoła Podstawowa, 1–8 grades)**: Focuses on foundational subjects like Polish, math, science, and foreign languages (English/German). Prior to 2017, primary education lasted **6 years**, followed by a 3-year gimnazjum (junior high school).
- **Junior High School (gimnazjum)**: Previously a separate 3-year stage, it was integrated into primary school, now lasting **4–5 years** (grades 7–12). Students take standardized exams in humanities, science, and foreign languages.

### **Upper Secondary Education**
After compulsory education, students pursue **upper secondary education**, which can be **general, vocational, or specialized**. The system includes:
- **General Secondary Schools (liceum)**: Offer the "Matura" exam, granting access to higher education.
- **Vocational Schools (technikum/zasadnicza szkoła zawodowa)**: Provide technical and trade training, with diplomas allowing entry into the workforce or further education.
- **Special Schools (szkoła specjalna)**: For students with disabilities, preparing them for the Matura.

### **Tertiary Education**
Poland follows the **Bologna Process**, offering **3-year bachelor’s degrees** followed by **2-year master’s degrees**. Doctoral programs take **3 years**. Teacher training requires **3 years of study**. Universities use a **numerical grading system (2–5)**, with additional increments (e.g., 2.5 not officially recognized).

### **Foreign Languages**
English is the most widely taught foreign language (67.9% of students), followed by German (33.3%), French (13.3%), and Spanish (10.2%). The **2016 reform** mandated learning **two foreign languages** in junior high school, with standardized exams contributing to university admissions.

### **History & Reforms**
Poland’s education system has roots in **12th-century monastic schools** and evolved through reforms like the **1999 and 2017 overhauls**. The **2017 reform** emphasized **national pride**, removing references to communism and aligning history education with a **Polish-centric curriculum**. The **"zero tolerance" policy** (2006) aimed to curb school violence, though challenges persist.

## **Education in Pakistan**

### **Stages of Formal Education**
Pakistan’s education system is **non-uniform**, with **primary education** being the most accessible but **high dropout rates** (67.5% completion). The system is **English-inspired**, with **pre-school (3–5 years)** leading to **junior school (1–5)**, **middle school (6–8)**, and **secondary education (9–12)**.

- **Primary Education**: Focuses on **Arts, Computer Studies, Modern Languages (Urdu/English), Math, Religious Education, Social Studies, and Pakistan Studies**. Literacy rates are low (60.65% in 2023), with **gender disparities** (42% female literacy vs. 67% male).
- **Secondary Education**: Begins at **Grade 9**, with **SSC (Secondary School Certificate)** and **HSSC (Higher Secondary School Certificate)** exams. Students choose between **pre-medical, pre-engineering, humanities, computer science, or commerce** streams.

### **Tertiary Education**
Pakistan’s tertiary education is **fragmented**, with **6% university graduates** (9% male, 3.5% female). Universities offer **bachelor’s (2–4 years), master’s (2 years), and PhD (3 years)** degrees. **Vocational education** is provided by **post-secondary schools (szkoła policealna)**, with programs lasting **2.5 years**.

### **Gender Disparity & Challenges**
- **Gender inequality** remains severe, with **18% of women receiving 10+ years of schooling**. **Low female enrollment** persists, particularly in rural areas, due to **cultural norms, safety concerns, and economic barriers**.
- **Literacy rates** are **lower than neighboring South Asian countries** (60.65% in 2023), with **rural areas lagging significantly**. **Technical and vocational education (TVET)** is underfunded and lacks **equity and relevance**.

### **Alternative & Informal Education**
- **Madrassas** (Islamic seminaries) are popular among the poor, offering **free food and shelter** alongside religious education. **Non-formal education** is provided by NGOs, often targeting **dropouts and low-skilled individuals**.
- **Distance learning** has grown due to COVID-19, with initiatives like **Teleschool** (TV-based education) and **Allama Iqbal Open University (AIOU)**.

### **International Education & Achievements**
Pakistan is a **top student-sending country** to the U.S., with **100,000+ students** studying abroad. Notable alumni include:
- **Abdus Salam** (Nobel Prize in Physics)
- **Atta-ur-Rahman** (Natural Product Chemist)
- **Arfa Abdul Karim Randhawa** (Youngest Microsoft Certified Professional)

## **Education in the Czech Republic**

### **Elementary and Secondary Education**
The Czech Republic’s education system is **9-year compulsory**, divided into **elementary (grades 1–9)** and **secondary (grades 10–12)** education.

- **Elementary School (Základní škola)**: Covers **9 grades**, with **two stages** (grades 1–5 and 6–9). Students learn **Czech, math, science, foreign languages (English), history, geography, and arts**.
- **Secondary School**: Offers **general education (liceum)** or **vocational training (technikum)**. Students take the **maturita exam** (equivalent to a high school diploma), which is required for university admission.

### **Tertiary Education**
The Czech Republic follows the **Bologna Process**, with **3-year bachelor’s degrees**, **2-year master’s degrees**, and **PhD programs**. Universities are **free for first-time students under 26**, with tuition fees for international students ranging from **€2,000–€3,000/year**.

### **Vocational and Special Education**
- **Vocational Schools**: Offer **2–3 year certificates** in trades like **technology, agriculture, and business**.
- **Special Schools**: Provide education for **students with disabilities**, with **integrated education** becoming more common.

### **History & Reforms**
The Czech Republic’s education system traces back to the **14th-century Charles University (Prague)**, founded in 1348. Modern reforms (2016) introduced **standardized entrance exams** for university admission. The system is **highly regulated**, with **Inspectie van het Onderwijs (Education Inspection)** overseeing quality.

## **Education in Russia**

### **Levels of Education**
Russia’s education system is **divided into general and professional education**, with **compulsory education lasting 11 years** (grades 1–11).

- **Preschool (0–6 years)**: Kindergartens are **regulated by regional authorities**, with **free access** for protected groups (refugees, Chernobyl veterans).
- **Primary (1–4 years)**: Focuses on **basic skills** in math, language, and science.
- **Middle (5–9 years)**: Introduced in **2007**, now compulsory.
- **Secondary (10–11 years)**: Prepares students for **university entrance exams (USE)**.

### **Vocational Training & Higher Education**
- **Vocational Schools**: Offer **3-year diplomas**, with **1.41 million students** enrolled.
- **Higher Education**: Follows the **Bologna Process**, with **4-year bachelor’s**, **2-year master’s**, and **PhD programs**. **Postgraduate studies** require **2–4 years**, with **Candidat Nauk (PhD equivalent)** and **Doktor Nauk (Doctorate)** degrees.

### **Special Education & Challenges**
- **Students with disabilities** receive **specialized education**, with **segregated or integrated schools**.
- **Criticisms**: **Overcrowded classrooms, outdated curricula, and teacher shortages** persist. **Distance learning** has grown due to **COVID-19**, with initiatives like **Teleschool**.

## **Education in Canada**

### **Compulsory Education**
Canada’s education system is **provincial**, with **compulsory education** varying by province. **Kindergarten (age 4–5)** is optional, while **secondary education** lasts **9–12 years** (Grade 12).

- **Primary and Secondary Education**: Follows a **K–12 model**, with **co-educational schools** being the norm. **English and French** are taught in **English and French schools**, respectively.
- **Vocational and Technical Education**: Offered through **colleges and technical schools**, preparing students for **trades and technical careers**.

### **Tertiary Education**
Canada’s universities are **prestigious**, with **top-ranked institutions** like the **University of Toronto, McGill, and UBC**. **Tuition fees** are high, but **scholarships and government funding** are available.

### **International Education & Challenges**
- **International Schools**: Over **400** in Canada, offering **English-medium curricula**.
- **Criticisms**: **Grade inflation, social promotion policies, and lack of critical thinking** have been noted. **Gender disparities** and **racialized data collection** in schools are also concerns.

## **Education in South Korea**

### **School Grades & Structure**
South Korea’s education system is **highly competitive**, with **98% of students completing secondary education**.

- **Elementary School (1–6 grades)**: Focuses on **Korean, math, science, and English**.
- **Middle School (7–9 grades)**: Prepares students for **university entrance exams**.
- **High School (10–12 grades)**: Offers **specialty tracks** (science, foreign language, art) or **general education**.

### **Vocational Education & Challenges**
- **Meister Schools**: Revamped **vocational education** to prepare students for **high-skilled trades**. **95% employment rate** after graduation.
- **Criticisms**: **Extreme pressure** from **entrance exams**, **overcrowded classrooms**, and **high dropout rates** among gifted students.

### **Tertiary Education**
South Korea’s universities are **prestigious**, with **top-ranked institutions** like **Seoul National University, Korea University, and Yonsei University**. **Admission is highly competitive**, based on the **College Scholastic Ability Test (CSAT)**.

## **Education in Bangladesh**

### **Stages of Education**
Bangladesh’s education system is **divided into primary (3–8), secondary (9–12), and tertiary levels**.

- **Primary Education**: **Compulsory for 12 years**, with **English or Bengali** as the medium. **Literacy rates** are **74.66%** (2022).
- **Secondary Education**: **SSC (10th grade) and HSC (12th grade)** exams determine university admissions.

### **Tertiary Education**
Bangladesh has **44 government, 101 private, and 3 international universities**. **Public universities** are **heavily subsidized**, while **private universities** rely on tuition fees.

### **Gender Disparity & Challenges**
- **Gender parity** has improved, but **female enrollment remains low** in rural areas.
- **Literacy rates** are **lower than neighboring countries** (51.77% in 2011, 74.66% in 2022).
- **Criticisms**: **Poor infrastructure, lack of critical thinking, and rote-learning** dominate the system.

## **Education in the Netherlands**

### **Compulsory Education**
The Netherlands has **compulsory education** from **age 5–18**, with **two stages**:
- **Primary School (1–8 grades)**: Focuses on **reading, writing, math, and foreign languages**.
- **Secondary School**: Offers **VMBO (vocational), HAVO (general), and VWO (academic)** tracks.

### **Tertiary Education**
The Netherlands follows the **Bologna Process**, with **3-year bachelor’s**, **2-year master’s**, and **PhD programs**. **Higher education** is divided into **WO (universities) and HBO (universities of applied sciences)**.

### **Vocational Education**
- **MBO (Middle-Level Applied Education)**: Prepares students for **vocational careers** in **1–4 years**.
- **HBO (Higher Professional Education)**: Offers **4-year bachelor’s degrees** in **business, engineering, and healthcare**.

## **Education in Saudi Arabia**

### **Compulsory Education**
Saudi Arabia’s education system is **free for all students**, with **compulsory education** from **age 6–18**.

- **Primary School (6–12 years)**: Focuses on **Arabic, Islamic studies, math, science, and English**.
- **Intermediate and Secondary Education**: **3-year programs**, with **specialized tracks** in **science, commerce, and agriculture**.

### **Tertiary Education**
Saudi Arabia has **24 government universities**, with **60% of students studying in humanities and social sciences**. **Women’s enrollment** has increased significantly, with **60% of university students being female**.

### **Gifted Education & Special Education**
- **Gifted Education**: Focuses on **STEM subjects** and **enrichment programs**.
- **Special Education**: Provides **vocational training** for students with disabilities.

## **Education in New Zealand**

### **Compulsory Education**
New Zealand’s education system is **free for all citizens**, with **compulsory education** from **age 5–16**.

- **Primary School (1–8 grades)**: Focuses on **English, math, science, and social studies**.
- **Secondary School (9–13 grades)**: Offers **NCEA (National Certificate of Educational Achievement)** exams.

### **Tertiary Education**
New Zealand’s universities are **highly ranked**, with **top-ranked institutions** like **University of Auckland and University of Otago**. **Tertiary education** is **regulated by the New Zealand Qualifications Framework**.

### **Gender Disparity & Challenges**
- **Gender parity** has improved, but **Māori and Pacific Island students** face **achievement gaps**.
- **Criticisms**: **Poor infrastructure in rural areas, lack of critical thinking, and high dropout rates**.
