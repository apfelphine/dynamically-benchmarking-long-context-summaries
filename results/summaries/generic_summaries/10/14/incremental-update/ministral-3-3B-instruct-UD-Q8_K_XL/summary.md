### **Education Systems in Armenia, Norway, Indonesia, Sweden, Egypt, Hong Kong, Sri Lanka, Ghana, Israel, England, Ireland, France, Nigeria, Turkey, Taiwan, and Iran – Recent Trends & Innovations**

#### **Education Systems Overview**
The education systems of **Armenia, Norway, Indonesia, Sweden, Egypt, Hong Kong, Sri Lanka, Ghana, Israel, England, Ireland, France, Nigeria, Turkey, Taiwan, and Iran** reflect **historical legacies, colonial influences, and adaptive reforms**, addressing **access, equity, and performance challenges**. Recent reforms emphasize **modernizing curricula, digital integration, vocational pathways**, and **global alignment**, with systems varying significantly—from **Turkey’s vocational-religious blend** to **Taiwan’s strict discipline and mandatory exams**, **Iran’s Islamized curricula**, **France’s secularism**, and **Nigeria’s mixed government/private institutions**.

---

#### **Key Highlights by Country: Iran’s System**
Iran’s education system integrates **ancient Persian and Islamic traditions** with **modern reforms**, balancing **historical rigor and contemporary demands**. Key features include:

- **Structure & Funding**:
  - **20% of government spending (5% of GDP)** allocated, with **50% for secondary and 21% for tertiary education**, per the **Fourth Five-Year Plan (2005–2010)**.
  - **Mandatory Islamic practices**: Daily **half-hour prayer in schools** and study of *General Soleimani’s will* in colleges, alongside **gender-segregated curricula**.

- **Admissions & Grading**:
  - **Nationwide *Konkour* exam** determines university access; **Islamic Azad University** offers alternative programs.
  - **Grading**: Numerical scale (10–20), with **pass/fail thresholds (GPA ≥ 10)**.

- **Higher Education**:
  - **Top institutions**: University of Tehran, Tarbiat Modares, Sharif, Amirkabir, and IUST lead in **engineering/science**, with **~1M global medical students**.
  - **Vocational & STEM focus**: **335,000 STEM graduates annually (2016)**, with **70% female engineers**—Iran ranks **first globally in primary female-to-male enrollment (1.22:1)**.
  - **Entrepreneurship**: Supported by the **KARAD Plan**, with courses at Tehran and Sharif universities.

- **Digital & Distance Learning**:
  - **Payame Noor University** dominates distance education (512 branches), offering **state-run online courses**.
  - **Hybrid models**: Post-pandemic, **Shad software and LMS** enabled remote learning, aligning with **global knowledge and Islamic values**.

- **Specialized Pathways**:
  - **Gifted education**: Supported by **NODET schools** (competitive, government-funded) and **Nemoone Dolati** selective schools.
  - **International programs**: **IB schools** (e.g., Mehr-e-Taban, Tehran International) and **STEM-focused curricula**.

- **Challenges & Controversies**:
  - **Teacher shortages**: ~60,000 missing teachers; **half of schools lack counselors**, particularly in **rural regions**.
  - **Privatization pressures**: Rising costs—**public schools cost ~14M toman/year**, while **private schools charge ~50M toman/year**.
  - **Political restrictions**: **2024 Law** restricts protests against universities without **public approval**; **"Starred students"** (politically/religiously charged) face exclusion.

#### **Recent Trends Across Systems**
- **Gender Equity & STEM Leadership**:
  - Iran’s **70% female students** and **70% female engineers** highlight **post-revolution reforms**, contrasting with **Taiwan’s gender-segregated vocational schools** and **France’s secularism**.
  - **Global female enrollment dominance**: Iran ranks **first in primary gender parity (1.22:1)**, driven by **STEM-focused education**.

- **Vocational & Digital Reforms**:
  - **Iran’s vocational programs** and **Taiwan’s 12-year system** prioritize **skills-based education**, with **apprenticeships and digital literacy initiatives** (e.g., **95% targets by 2030**).
  - **France and Hong Kong** lead in **AI-driven innovations**, while **Nigeria and Turkey** focus on **rural digital integration**.

- **Curriculum & Assessment Innovations**:
  - **Iran’s 2005–2010 reforms** and **Taiwan’s 2014 12-year system** adapt to **societal shifts**, emphasizing **research-based learning** and **vocational alignment**.
  - **Hong Kong’s tech-driven reforms** and **Sweden’s STEM focus** reflect **global curriculum flexibility**, while **France’s secularism** and **Iran’s mandatory prayer** reflect **historical/political priorities**.

- **International Collaboration**:
  - **Erasmus+, Türkiye Scholarships, IB programs**, and **Taiwan’s school assignment policies** enhance **global mobility, diversity, and exchange opportunities**, with **equity and inclusion** as core principles.

#### **Digital Integration & Future Directions**
The future of education in these regions hinges on **digital transformation, vocational alignment, and equity-focused reforms**. Iran’s system exemplifies this with:
- **Hybrid education models**: Scaling **digital access** for **rural populations** via **Payame Noor University** and **Shad software**.
- **Vocational pathways**: Expanding **apprenticeships, NABTEB certifications, and mobile training** to **boost economic participation**.
- **Gender and literacy equity**: Leveraging **UNESCO partnerships, NODET schools**, and **digital literacy initiatives** to **reduce disparities**.
- **Curriculum innovation**: Adapting to **global challenges** (e.g., **AI, digital skills**) while maintaining **secular (France) or Islamized (Iran)** frameworks.
- **Global mobility**: **Iran’s English/French programs** and **Taiwan’s Mandarin initiatives** position these systems as **international hubs**, fostering **cultural exchange**.

#### **Emerging Themes**
- **Mandatory Education vs. Flexibility**:
  - **Iran’s compulsory K-12 system** and **Taiwan’s 12-year mandatory education** ensure **universal access**, while **France’s secularism** and **Nigeria’s tiered system** balance **equity with flexibility**.

- **Digital Divide Mitigation**:
  - **Iran’s distance education** and **Taiwan’s hybrid models** prioritize **rural inclusion**, with **France/Hong Kong** leading in **AI integration**.

- **Religious vs. Secular Balances**:
  - **Turkey’s Islam-secular blend**, **Iran’s Islamized reforms**, and **France’s *laïcité*** reflect **cultural tensions**, while **Taiwan’s secularism** and **Nigeria’s mixed models** offer **compromises**.

- **STEM Leadership & Gender Inclusion**:
  - **Iran’s record in female STEM graduates** and **Taiwan’s vocational STEM focus** set benchmarks for **global equity and innovation**.
