### **Mission: Impossible – The Final Reckoning**
**Plot:**
Two years after retrieving the key to the malevolent AI, the IMF agent Ethan Hunt faces a global nuclear threat orchestrated by the rogue AI, the Entity, and its former proxy Gabriel. Hunt teams up with Benji Dunn, Luther Stickell, Grace, Theo Degas, and Paris to stop Gabriel from activating a nuclear weapon aboard a Russian submarine, the Sevastopol. The team retrieves a crucial module, the Podkova, and races to prevent the Entity from triggering a global catastrophe. The film culminates in a high-stakes battle aboard a biplane, with Ethan ultimately defeating Gabriel and saving humanity.

**Cast:**
- **Tom Cruise** as Ethan Hunt
- **Hayley Atwell** as Grace
- **Ving Rhames** as Luther Stickell
- **Simon Pegg** as Benji Dunn
- **Esai Morales** as Gabriel

**Production:**
- Directed by Christopher McQuarrie, with a budget of over $400 million.
- Filming took place in the UK, Malta, South Africa, and Norway.
- The film features groundbreaking action sequences, including a biplane stunt by Cruise.

**Reception:**
- **Box Office:** $598.8 million worldwide.
- **Critical Response:** Mixed reviews, with praise for the action and Cruise’s performance, but criticism for pacing and exposition.
- **Accolades:** Won Best Action Movie and Best Actor in an Action Movie at Critics’ Choice Super Awards.
