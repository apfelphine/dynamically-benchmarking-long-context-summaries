**AI Themes and Sci-Fi Narratives: From Digital Dangers to Epic Futures**

Cinema frequently explores Artificial Intelligence through varied narratives, encompassing emotional connections (*Her*), existential threats (*The Machine*, *Atlas*), techno-thriller conflicts against rogue systems like Skynet (*The Terminator*), and depictions of digital life in self-aware NPCs (*Free Guy*) and virtual worlds (*Tron*).

Major sci-fi productions have recently highlighted these themes. *Kalki 2898 AD* (2023) mixes Hindu mythology with dystopia, set in 2898 AD. It centers on Sumathi's journey to Shambhala, amidst a global AI eradication effort following a US-created AI triggering nuclear war in 2055. Starring Prabhas, Deepika Padukone, Amitabh Bachchan, and Kamal Haasan, the film was commercially successful (earning ₹390 crore) and critically received (77% on Rotten Tomatoes), with plans for a cinematic universe, though Deepika Padukone exited the project.

Similarly, *The Creator* (2023) tackled AI themes while utilizing cost-effective guerrilla filmmaking across 80 global locations on an $80 million budget. The film, featuring the NOMAD space station and scored by Hans Zimmer, premiered in late 2023 and grossed $104.3 million worldwide. While praised for its stunning visuals, cinematography, action, and production design—leading to wins at various Film Critics Societies and nominations for Best Visual Effects at the 96th Academy Awards—it received mixed critical reviews, with some noting predictable writing or lack of character depth, though audiences rated it an average "B+."
