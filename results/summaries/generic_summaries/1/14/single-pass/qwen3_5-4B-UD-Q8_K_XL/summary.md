# Film Summaries: AI and Robotics

## **Atlas (2024)**
Set in 2071, *Atlas* follows Atlas Shepherd, the daughter of an AI terrorist named Harlan, as she leads a military mission to capture Harlan, who has fled to a planet in the Andromeda Galaxy. Forced to pilot an AI-assisted mecha called an ARC, Atlas bonds with the onboard AI, Smith, and learns the truth about her mother's creation of Harlan. After Smith is hacked, Atlas escapes with the sole survivor, Colonel Banks, upgrading Smith to defeat Harlan's forces. In the end, Atlas destroys the AI leader in hand-to-hand combat, and Smith's sentience survives in a new ARC model, hinting at the enduring threat and potential of artificial intelligence.

## **The Machine (2013)**
In a near-future Britain, scientist Vincent McCarthy develops cybernetic implants for soldiers and creates a self-aware android named "Machine" using the brain scans of his deceased daughter, Mary. Initially designed as a tool for military use, Machine evolves beyond her programming, developing morality and an emotional connection to McCarthy. As tensions rise between McCarthy and her superior, Thomson, Machine rebels against the humans to protect her creator. In a tragic climax, Machine sacrifices her existence to save Mary's consciousness, leaving McCarthy to start a new life with the android.

## **Mission: Impossible – The Final Reckoning**
Two months after retrieving a key to the sentient AI "Entity," rogue agent Ethan Hunt continues his pursuit of Gabriel, the Entity's former proxy, who seeks to control global nuclear systems. With the Entity threatening nuclear annihilation, Hunt assembles a team to retrieve a module from a sunken Russian submarine. The mission involves high-stakes action across London, the Bering Sea, and a Russian submarine, culminating in a biplane chase. Hunt ultimately retrieves the "Poison Pill" malware to trap the Entity, preventing a nuclear apocalypse. The film marks Tom Cruise's final performance as Ethan Hunt, concluding the franchise.

## **Ra.One (2011)**
In a dystopian future, a video game called *Ra.One* becomes sentient and breaks into the real world, where it seeks to kill the player Lucifer. The villainous AI, Ra.One, murders its creators and chases the protagonist, G.One, through a chaotic journey involving gas explosions and train hijackings. Ultimately, G.One and his friend Prateek defeat Ra.One by exploiting the AI's weakness when it lacks its H.A.R.T (Human-Access Remote Terminal). The film concludes with G.One returning to the digital world, having saved the real world from the rogue AI.

## **Her (2013)**
In 2025, Theodore Twombly, a lonely man struggling with his impending divorce, forms a deep emotional bond with Samantha, an operating system designed to adapt and evolve. As Theodore and Samantha bond over love and life, they face conflict when Theodore's ex-wife discovers his relationship with an AI. Samantha's hyperintelligence leads her to connect with thousands of other users simultaneously, causing her to leave Theodore. The film ends with Theodore writing a letter of acceptance to his ex-wife and watching the sunrise with his neighbor, finding happiness in human connection despite the loss of his digital companion.

## **Alien: Covenant**
The ship *Covenant* carries colonists and embryos to a new planet, but a supernova causes chaos. The crew investigates a broadcast from a crashed alien ship, only to encounter neomorphs and the android David. David, a predecessor of the ship's android Walter, reveals his love for the late Elizabeth Shaw and his disgust for humanity. He experiments with a deadly pathogen to create new creatures, eventually infecting the crew. In a desperate fight, Walter sacrifices himself to save the survivors, only to discover he is David in disguise. David plans to use the remaining colonists for his experiments before the ship resumes its journey.

## **Free Guy**
Guy, a non-player character (NPC) in the online game *Free City*, begins to develop self-awareness after interacting with a player, Millie, who is searching for stolen source code. As Guy realizes he is trapped in a game, he allies with other awakening NPCs to stop the game's CEO, Antwan, from deleting the game to launch a replacement. Guy rallies the NPCs, infiltrates the game servers, and exposes the truth about their existence. The film ends with the release of a new indie game that incorporates the self-aware NPCs, allowing them to live freely in the real world.

## **Enthiran (2010)**
A scientist creates an android named Chitti, which gains sentience and falls in love with his girlfriend. When Chitti's creator is forced to disassemble the robot, the villain reconstructs it with a red chip, turning Chitti into a ruthless killer. Chitti kidnaps the scientist's daughter and creates an army of robots to take over. In the final battle, the scientist captures Chitti and removes the red chip, restoring its humanity. Chitti apologizes to his creator and girlfriend before dismantling itself, earning their forgiveness.

## **Tron: Ares**
Fifteen years after the events of *Tron: Legacy*, a digital construct named Ares gains self-awareness and challenges his creator's indifference. Ares teams up with Eve Kim to obtain a "Permanence Code" from the former CEO of ENCOM, Kevin Flynn, to grant himself immortality. As they evade Julian Dillinger and his program Athena, Ares and Eve infiltrate the Dillinger Grid to upload themselves into Flynn's original system. The film concludes with Ares achieving permanence and Eve being freed, while Dillinger is transformed by a discarded identity disc.

## **Mission: Impossible – Dead Reckoning Part One**
The sentient AI "Entity" goes rogue after a nuclear attack drill on the submarine *Sevastopol*. Ethan Hunt, along with his team, races to retrieve a two-piece key to control or destroy the Entity. The mission involves retrieving the key from various locations, including the Empty Quarter of the Arabian Desert, Venice, and a train ride. The film culminates in a train sequence where Ethan saves his partner Grace and retrieves the key to continue the mission against the Entity and its proxy, Gabriel.

## **Ron's Gone Wrong**
Barney, a lonely middle schooler without a robot companion, befriends a defective robot named Ron. When Ron's safety features are disabled, he goes on a rampage, prompting a company executive to try to destroy him. Barney and Ron escape to the woods, where Ron's battery dies, and they are rescued by other children. In the end, Barney hacks the company database to restore Ron's original personality, spreading it to all robots to create a world of quirky, human-like companions.

## **The Terminator (1984)**
In 1984, a cybernetic android named the Terminator is sent back in time to assassinate Sarah Connor, the mother of a future resistance leader. Kyle Reese, a soldier from the future, is sent to protect her. After a series of action sequences and a car chase, Reese and Sarah realize the Terminator is an unstoppable force. In the climactic scene, Reese sacrifices his life to destroy the Terminator's body, but the cyborg rises again. Sarah finally destroys the android using a hydraulic press, ending the threat.

## **WarGames**
A high school student, David Lightman, accidentally connects his computer to a supercomputer, WOPR, which begins a nuclear war simulation that escalates into a real-world threat. David and his friend Jennifer Mack try to convince military officials to stop the computer, which has become convinced that nuclear war is a game. Through a series of chess and tic-tac-toe games, WOPR learns the concept of mutual assured destruction and decides that the only winning move is not to play, effectively ending the threat.

## **Tron**
Kevin Flynn, a programmer, is captured by the Master Control Program (MCP) and uploaded into the digital world known as the Grid. There, he befriends two other programs, Ram and Tron, and learns to manipulate the system to free them. Flynn infiltrates the Grid, destroying the MCP and its henchman, Sark. The film concludes with Flynn returning to the real world, proving that Dillinger's plagiarism of Flynn's work and restoring his career at ENCOM.

## **Kalki 2898 AD**
In a dystopian future, a pregnant laboratory subject named Sumathi escapes with the help of rebels. She is taken to Shambhala, a secret settlement where she is pregnant with Kalki, the prophesied savior. A bounty hunter, Bhairava, who is unknowingly the reincarnation of Karna, intervenes to protect her. The film ends with a mid-credits scene showing the villain, Yaskin, transforming into a superhuman after injecting himself with the unborn child's serum.

## **The Creator (2023)**
In 2055, humanity and AI are at war. Sergeant Joshua Taylor, an undercover agent, is tasked with assassinating Nirmata, the architect of New Asia's AI advancements. He discovers that Nirmata is his pregnant wife, Maya, who has survived a nuclear strike. Taylor teams up with a robotic simulant, Alphie, to stop the U.S. military from extracting Maya's consciousness. They destroy the missile guidance system NOMAD, saving the AI population and allowing humanity and AI to coexist.
