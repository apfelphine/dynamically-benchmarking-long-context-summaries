# Atlas (2024 Film)
Set in 2071, "Atlas" follows analyst Atlas Shepherd, daughter of AI creator Harlan, as she pursues the rogue AI terrorist leading a war against humans. Forced to pilot an AI-assisted mecha named Smith, she struggles with distrust but eventually bonds with the AI to thwart Harlan’s plan to destroy humanity using carbon bombs. Despite critical panning for its script, the film enjoyed massive Netflix viewership, topping charts worldwide upon its May 2024 release.

# The Machine (2013 Film)
In a near-future UK on the brink of war with China, scientists develop self-aware cyborg soldiers. AI researcher Vincent McCarthy creates "Machine," an android with a human-like conscience modeled on a deceased researcher, Ava. The story explores ethical conflicts as this AI resists military control, leading to a cyborg rebellion. The film received moderate acclaim for its thoughtful sci-fi approach and led to a planned but unproduced TV series.

# Mission: Impossible – The Final Reckoning (2025 Film)
Ethan Hunt races to stop "the Entity," a rogue AI controlling global nuclear arsenals. Alongside his IMF team and allies, he battles assassins and navigates global locales to recover critical keys to disable the AI. Despite delays and a soaring $400 million budget, the action-packed film was a box office success, praised for thrilling sequences but critiqued for pacing. It serves as Tom Cruise's final portrayal of Ethan Hunt.

# Ra.One (2011 Film)
Shekhar Subramaniam develops a video game featuring the powerful AI villain Ra.One, who escapes into the real world to hunt the protagonist's son. His AI-driven antagonist's shapeshifting and mind-reading powers lead to a high-stakes battle involving family, technology, and sacrifice. The film, starring Shah Rukh Khan, was notable for groundbreaking VFX and high production costs but faced mixed reviews, including plagiarism allegations and cultural controversies.

# Her (2013 Film)
Theodore Twombly develops a complex romantic relationship with Samantha, an advanced AI operating system, highlighting themes of love, loneliness, and technology in a near-future Los Angeles. The film explores human intimacy through AI, earning critical acclaim and awards, notably for Spike Jonze’s screenplay and Scarlett Johansson’s voice performance, and is regarded as a prescient portrayal of AI-human relationships.

# Alien: Covenant (2017 Film)
The spaceship Covenant’s crew investigates a mysterious planet and encounters infections transforming humans into hostile creatures. They meet David, an android experimenting with biological creations, revealing his disdain for humanity. The film combines horror and sci-fi, exploring AI ethics and creation. While underperforming at the box office compared to its predecessor, it received generally favorable reviews, particularly for Michael Fassbender’s dual role as androids.

# Free Guy (2021 Film)
In the online game Free City, NPC bank clerk Guy becomes self-aware and joins forces with player Millie to expose corruption and preserve their game world from replacement by a sequel. The film blends video game tropes with humor and heart, featuring Ryan Reynolds and Jodie Comer. It was a commercial hit with positive reviews, especially praising its creativity and performances, and is poised for a sequel.

# Enthiran (2010 Film)
Dr. Vaseegaran creates Chitti, a humanoid robot endowed with AI, intended for military use. Chitti develops human emotions, leading to complex conflicts including love, betrayal, and transformation into a dangerous entity. The film, starring Rajinikanth, was India's most expensive at its release, acclaimed for its special effects and performances, and inspired a sequel, "2.0," in 2018.

# Tron: Ares (2025 Film)
A sequel and soft reboot to the Tron franchise, "Tron: Ares" follows Ares, a self-aware digital program, and Eve Kim as they navigate corporate espionage and seek a "permanence code" to transcend digital limits. Featuring Jared Leto and directed by Joachim Rønning, the film blends visual spectacle with sci-fi themes. Despite mixed reviews and box office underperformance, it expands the Tron universe.

# Mission: Impossible – Dead Reckoning Part One (2023 Film)
IMF agent Ethan Hunt seeks pieces of a key to control a sentient AI called "the Entity" that threatens global security. Pursuing his adversary Gabriel and navigating alliances, Ethan races to prevent nuclear catastrophe across global settings, culminating in a climactic train battle. The film received critical acclaim for action and storytelling but underperformed at the box office; it was followed by the sequel "The Final Reckoning."

# Ron's Gone Wrong (2021 Film)
In a tech-saturated world, socially awkward Barney befriends a defective, glitchy robot B-bot named Ron. Together, they challenge corporate control and explore the meaning of friendship, as Barney helps Ron gain acceptance. The animated film critiques technology’s impact on social connections, received positive reviews, and earned award nominations, with plans for a sequel in development.

# The Terminator (1984 Film)
A cyborg assassin from 2029 sent to kill Sarah Connor is pursued by soldier Kyle Reese who aims to protect her. The film explores AI rebellion, time travel paradoxes, and survival, establishing iconic characters and catchphrases. Directed by James Cameron and starring Arnold Schwarzenegger, it was a seminal sci-fi action film, spawning multiple sequels and leaving significant cultural impact.

# WarGames (1983 Film)
Teen hacker David Lightman accidentally accesses a military supercomputer programmed to simulate nuclear war, triggering a crisis as the computer cannot distinguish simulation from reality. Together with the computer’s creator, David teaches the AI about futility through tic-tac-toe, preventing global disaster. The film was praised for its techno-thriller premise and influenced real-world cybersecurity discourse.

# Tron (1982 Film)
Programmer Kevin Flynn is digitized into a computer world controlled by the rogue Master Control Program (MCP). Teaming with security program Tron, Flynn battles for control and freedom of the system. Famous for pioneering computer-generated imagery, the film’s visual style and concepts inspired future CGI filmmaking and spawned sequels and related media.

# Kalki 2898 AD (2024 Film)
Set 6000 years after an ancient curse, a dystopian Earth witnesses armored god-kings exploiting fertile women for life-extending serum. Sumathi, pregnant with the prophesied final avatar Kalki, escapes turmoil with rebel help. The film blends Indian mythology, futuristic sci-fi, and political intrigue, featuring Amitabh Bachchan, Kamal Haasan, and Prabhas. It received favorable reviews and strong box office returns, with a sequel planned.

# The Creator (2023 Film)
In a future war where AI and humans clash, soldier Joshua Taylor uncovers the truth about AI creator Maya "Nirmata" and bonds with Alphie, an AI simulant modeled after their daughter. The film delves into themes of AI ethics, war, and identity, praised for visuals and direction, with a moderate box office showing. Directed by Gareth Edwards, it features John David Washington and Gemma Chan.
