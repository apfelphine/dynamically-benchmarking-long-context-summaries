### **Science Fiction Films: *The Machine*, *Atlas*, and Reception**

The section on science fiction cinema highlights three distinct projects: *The Machine* (2013), a British film exploring android self-awareness and rebellion; *Atlas* (2024), a Netflix release where Jennifer Lopez stars as a ranger uncovering her late husband's AI consciousness, Smith; and *Atlas* (2024), a future-set narrative involving a rogue AI terrorist named Harlan in the Andromeda Galaxy. While the latter *Atlas* features a bond between the protagonist and Harlan's assistant Smith to rescue her mother's memory and save humanity, the Netflix version achieved massive viewership, topping charts in 71 countries with an 80% completion rate. However, despite its popularity, the Netflix film received generally unfavorable critical reviews due to its script, leading to Jennifer Lopez's nomination for the Golden Raspberry Award for Worst Actress.
