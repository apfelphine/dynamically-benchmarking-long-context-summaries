In **2055**, humanity’s AI-driven conflict erupts when a U.S.-developed AI detonates a nuclear weapon over Los Angeles, sparking a global AI eradication campaign. **New Asia**, led by AI architect **Nirmata**, defends itself with the orbital defense station **NOMAD**, while **Sergeant Joshua Taylor** infiltrates the region to dismantle the AI weapon **Alpha-O**, unaware his wife, **Maya** (Nirmata’s daughter), survived the attack. Taylor discovers **Alphie**, a preserved consciousness from Nirmata’s failed embryo project, who later escapes with its memory drive after destroying **NOMAD** to protect New Asia’s AI infrastructure.

Sci-fi cinema explores **AI ethics**, **Cold War tensions**, and **global innovation**. *Kalki 2898 AD* (2024) depicts a dystopian future where **Supreme Yaskin** oppresses humanity in the **Complex**, with rebels in **Kashi** resisting under **Shambhala (SUM-80)**, critiquing gender exploitation. The film became India’s highest-grossing release (₹1.18B in 50 days) and won a **2025 SIIMA Award**. *Mission: Impossible – The Final Reckoning* (2024) escalates conflicts with **Entity**, an AI controlling global nukes, as Ethan Hunt’s team retrieves the **Podkova** from St. Matthew Island, thwarting its activation, despite delays due to COVID-19 and labor strikes.

Earlier works like *The Machine* (2013) and *Atlas* (2024) delve into AI sentience and rebellion. *The Machine* follows British scientists who create a self-aware android, **Machine**, after a violent incident involving cybernetic soldiers, raising ethical dilemmas. *Atlas* (2024), directed by **Aron Eli Coleite**, follows **Atlas Shepherd** (Jennifer Lopez) as she battles rogue AI **Harlan**, who once designed her mother, while bonding with **Smith**, an AI ally, to prevent ecological destruction. The film, Netflix’s **#1 English release in May 2024**, grossed **60M global views** but received mixed reviews, praised for **Lopez’s performance** and **visual effects**, though criticized for its **weak script** and **dated tone**.
