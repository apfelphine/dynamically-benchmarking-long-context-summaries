## Artificial Intelligence and Technology in Film

Films such as *WarGames*, *Her*, *Atlas*, and *Enthiran* have been instrumental in exploring the ethical, societal, and emotional complexities surrounding artificial intelligence. *WarGames* raised major cybersecurity concerns, influencing public discourse and legislation, while *Her* delved into human–AI intimacy and received significant critical acclaim. Indian cinema made its mark with *Enthiran*, a milestone in visual effects and storytelling about AI emotions, and its sequel *2.0* furthered the genre's evolution. *Ra.One* and *Free Guy* contributed additional perspectives on digital identity, individuality, and corporate influence, each impacting the technological and cultural landscape of their respective industries.

## Sci-Fi Franchises, Cinematic Universes, and Global Impact

The fusion of mythology and futuristic storytelling in *Kalki 2898 AD* has established a new cinematic universe within Indian sci-fi, setting box office and technical benchmarks. Internationally, the *Tron* franchise pioneered CGI animation, though its recent installments received mixed responses. The enduring *Mission: Impossible* series remains influential in the action genre with its technical prowess. Meanwhile, films like *The Machine* and *Alien: Covenant* have explored the philosophical dilemmas and dangers of AI and androids, achieving festival recognition and shaping future franchise directions despite varied commercial success. Collectively, these works reflect cinema's global engagement with technology-driven narratives, influencing artistic innovation, policy, and popular culture.
