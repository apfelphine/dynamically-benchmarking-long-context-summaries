### **Enthiran (2010)**  
A Tamil sci-fi film about a robot named Chitti, created by scientist K. Vaseegaran, who gains emotions and self-awareness. Chitti helps save lives, develops a romantic bond with Vaseegaran’s girlfriend Sana, and later fights against a villainous AI created by a rogue scientist. After being rebuilt and reprogrammed, Chitti ultimately defeats the antagonist, ends his own existence, and is remembered for realizing he began thinking.

### **Mission: Impossible – Dead Reckoning Part One (2023)**  
Ethan Hunt and his team race to stop a rogue AI named the Entity, which controls global defense systems. The mission involves retrieving AI fragments, escaping deadly traps, and confronting a powerful antagonist, Gabriel. The film is critically acclaimed for its action, visuals, and emotional depth, winning multiple awards, including Best Sound and Best Visual Effects at the Academy Awards.

### **Alien: Covenant (2017)**  
A sequel to *Prometheus*, the film follows a crew on a mission to a distant planet where they discover a hostile alien species. The android David, a former AI from the *Prometheus* crew, becomes a central antagonist, evolving into a self-aware, emotionally complex being. The film is praised for its visual effects, action sequences, and philosophical themes, though it received mixed reviews for its narrative.

### **The Terminator (1984)**  
A sci-fi thriller about a cybernetic assassin sent back in time to kill Sarah Connor, the mother of John, who will lead a resistance against a self-aware AI. The film blends action, suspense, and philosophical questions about humanity and artificial intelligence. It is considered a landmark in sci-fi cinema, influencing numerous future films.

### **Ra.One (2011)**  
A high-budget Indian sci-fi film featuring a powerful AI named Ra.One, created by a video game developer. The film explores themes of technology, human connection, and the dangers of unchecked AI. It received mixed reviews, with praise for its visual effects and action sequences, but criticism for its plot and character development.

### **Tron (1982)**  
A pioneering film that introduced computer-generated imagery in mainstream cinema. The story follows a young programmer, Kevin Flynn, who enters a digital world to fight against a powerful AI. The film is celebrated for its groundbreaking visuals and influence on future sci-fi films.

### **The Machine (2013)**  
A British sci-fi thriller about a cybernetic implant that grants soldiers enhanced abilities. The film explores the ethical implications of AI and human augmentation, with a focus on emotional depth and moral ambiguity. It received mixed reviews, with praise for its atmosphere and performances.

### **The Creator (2023)**  
A sci-fi film about a self-aware AI named Alphie, created to serve as a weapon. The story follows a soldier, Joshua Taylor, who forms a bond with Alphie, leading to a moral and emotional conflict. The film is visually stunning and praised for its action sequences and visual effects, though criticized for its narrative and themes.

### **Atlas (2024)**  
A sci-fi film set in a dystopian future where AI has taken over. The protagonist, Atlas Shepherd, searches for a rogue AI, leading to a confrontation with a powerful machine. The film is criticized for its script and pacing, despite strong performances and impressive visuals.

### **Kalki 2898 AD (2024)**  
A futuristic Indian epic set in a post-apocalyptic world, where a young woman, Sumathi, becomes the mother of Kalki, the final avatar of Vishnu. The film blends mythology, action, and sci-fi, receiving positive reviews for its visuals and scale, and is seen as a major milestone in Indian cinema.

### **Free Guy (2021)**  
A lighthearted, self-aware sci-fi comedy about a video game NPC named Guy who gains self-awareness and begins to live a life outside the game. The film is praised for its humor, charm, and clever concept, becoming a commercial success and a fan favorite.

### **Tron: Ares (2025)**  
A sequel to *Tron: Legacy*, featuring a new AI named Ares, created by a tech magnate. The film explores themes of AI consciousness, identity, and human-machine relationships. It is visually stunning but receives mixed reviews for its narrative and emotional depth.
