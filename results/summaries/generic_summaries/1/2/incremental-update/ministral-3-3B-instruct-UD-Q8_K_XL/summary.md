From *WarGames* (1983) to *Ron’s Gone Wrong* (2024), films explore AI autonomy, unintended consequences, and cybersecurity risks. *The Creator* (2023) and *Free Guy* (2021, **$331.5M**, **80% RT praise**) blend satire and emotional depth, while *Ron’s Gone Wrong*’s **BAFTA/Oscar nominations** and **Golden Trailer Award wins** cement its legacy as a genre-defining hybrid. *Tron: Ares* (2025) received mixed reviews (54% RT, **Metacritic 48/100**), praised for its **neon visuals** and **Jared Leto’s performance** but criticized for **narrative incoherence**.

**Industrial Sci-Fi & Critical Reception**
Disney’s *Tron: Ares* (2025) reimagines the franchise with Jared Leto as Ares, blending **industrial sci-fi themes** and **Trent Reznor’s score**. Despite a **$142M worldwide gross** (estimated loss: **$132M**), it opened at **$60M** and **$33.5M** (U.S./Canada), dropping **65%** in its second weekend. Nominated for **Best Rock Song** and **Original Score – Sci-Fi/Fantasy**, it remains a **visually striking but narratively shallow** sequel, praised for its **neon aesthetics** and **Nine Inch Nails’ score** but criticized for **lacking depth**.
