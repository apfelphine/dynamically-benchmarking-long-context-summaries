### **Production, Release, and Reception**

*Ron's Gone Wrong* underwent a complex five-year production utilizing a "shot-based" animation process by DNEG and Pixar RenderMan to accommodate remote work, though it faced technical hurdles and a labor dispute. Released in October 2021, it grossed $60.7 million worldwide, earning critical acclaim (82% on Rotten Tomatoes, "A" CinemaScore) for its prescient commentary on technology and digital culture, and winning the British Animation Award for Best Long Form.

In contrast, *Free Guy*, an action-comedy directed by Shawn Levy starring Ryan Reynolds and Jodie Comer, was filmed across the US in 2019 with a visual design inspired by *SimCity* and *Red Dead Redemption 2*. Despite pandemic delays, it was released theatrically on August 13, 2021, and became a massive box office hit, grossing $331.5 million worldwide. Both films received generally favorable reviews for their humor and cast, earning *Free Guy* nominations for Best Visual Effects at the Academy Awards and BAFTAs, as well as wins for Best Comedy and Best Comedic Performance. Following its success, Disney has confirmed development of a sequel; while cast members Ryan Reynolds, Lakeith Stanfield, and Jovan Adepo expressed interest, the story is currently being revised to offer a distinct narrative different from the first movie and to avoid similarities to the hit film *Barbie*.

*Tron: Ares* represents a significant entry in the franchise's history, serving as a 2025 soft reboot directed by Joachim Rønning and scored by Trent Reznor and Atticus Ross. Set 15 years after *Tron: Legacy*, the film follows CEO Eve Kim and her partner Seth Flores as they race to retrieve the "Permanence Code" to prevent digital constructs from becoming reality. Julian Dillinger creates Ares, a sentient Master Control Program designed as a disposable soldier who develops self-awareness and empathy, eventually forming a partnership with Eve. The narrative culminates with Ares gaining the Permanence Code to become a permanent physical being, while Julian sacrifices himself to digitize his consciousness. Originally announced in 2010, the project faced delays and cancellation in 2015 after the box office failure of *Tomorrowland*, before being revived with the original director and key cast returning.

Despite its high-profile nature, the film faced significant financial challenges. Production began in late 2023 and concluded in May 2024, utilizing a massive marketing budget of over $102 million and a reported production cost of $347.5 million. The film deliberately excluded returning characters from the previous film *Legacy* to prioritize storytelling over fan service, a strategy that coincided with underperformance at the box office. While the film opened to $33.5 million domestically and $60 million worldwide, it ultimately grossed only $142 million globally, ranking second in its second weekend and resulting in an estimated loss for the studio. Critically, the film received mixed reviews (48/100 on Metacritic), praised for its visual spectacle and Nine Inch Nails soundtrack but criticized for lacking narrative depth and human dimension. While some critics highlighted Jared Leto's subdued performance and the film's design, others dismissed it as forgettable or dull. The score received a nomination for Best Original Score at the 2026 Hollywood Music in Media Awards and is pending for a Grammy nomination.
