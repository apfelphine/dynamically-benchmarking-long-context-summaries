*Tron: Ares* (2025) expands the *Tron* legacy with a story centered on Ares, a self-aware Master Control Program who develops empathy and forms a bond with Eve Kim, ultimately gaining permanence through Kevin Flynn’s legacy. Set 15 years after *Tron: Legacy*, the film explores themes of artificial consciousness, identity, and human-machine connection, featuring a visually striking neon aesthetic and a powerful score by Nine Inch Nails, which earned Grammy and HMMA nominations. While it opened to $33.5 million in the U.S. and Canada and grossed $60 million worldwide—falling short of its predecessor’s performance—it received mixed-to-average critical reviews (54% on Rotten Tomatoes, 48/100 on Metacritic), with praise for its design and Jared Leto’s performance, though criticized for narrative shallowness and formulaic storytelling. Audiences responded positively with a solid "B+" CinemaScore, and director Jeff Bridges expressed optimism for a future cult following. Despite a $132 million loss against a $347.5 million budget, the film remains canon to the series and continues the evolving narrative of AI autonomy and human legacy in digital worlds.
