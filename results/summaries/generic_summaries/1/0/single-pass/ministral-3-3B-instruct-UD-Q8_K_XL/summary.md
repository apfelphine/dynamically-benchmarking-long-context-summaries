### **Ra.One (2011)**
**Plot:**
In London, tech entrepreneur Jenny Nair introduces a wireless technology allowing digital objects to enter the real world. Shekhar Subramaniam, a video game designer, is tasked with creating a game with a villain more powerful than the protagonist. The game, *Ra.One*, features a faceless antagonist, Ra.One, with advanced AI and powers, and a protagonist, G.One, who is Shekhar’s own likeness. The game’s success leads to Ra.One escaping into reality, leading to a high-stakes chase involving Shekhar, his son Prateek, and his wife Sonia. The film explores themes of artificial intelligence, identity, and the blurred line between human and machine.

**Key Characters:**
- **Shekhar Subramaniam** (Shah Rukh Khan): A video game designer.
- **Prateek Subramaniam** (Armaan Verma): Shekhar’s son.
- **Ra.One** (Arjun Rampal): The villainous AI antagonist.
- **Jenny Nair** (Shahana Goswami): Jenny’s role in enabling the technology.
- **Sonia Subramaniam** (Kareena Kapoor): Shekhar’s wife.

**Production & Reception:**
- Directed by Anubhav Sinha, Ra.One was a box-office success, earning ₹1.14 billion in India and worldwide. It received mixed reviews but was praised for its visual effects and action sequences.
- The film faced plagiarism allegations but was ultimately cleared.
- It won several awards, including the National Film Award for Best Special Effects.
