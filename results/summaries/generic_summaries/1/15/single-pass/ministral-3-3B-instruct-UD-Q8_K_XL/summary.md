### **The Machine (2013)**
**Plot:** In a near-future United Kingdom, a cybernetic implant allows brain-damaged soldiers to regain lost functions, but the AI researcher Vincent McCarthy develops a sentient android, "Machine," which exhibits human-like emotions and defies orders. The film explores themes of artificial intelligence, ethics, and human-machine relationships as Machine rebels against its creators, leading to a high-stakes confrontation with military forces.

**Key Themes:** AI sentience, human-machine conflict, ethical dilemmas in technology.
