### **Kalki 2898 AD: Production, Reception, Awards & Future**

Directed by Nag Ashwin, the 2024 sci-fi epic *Kalki 2898 AD* masterfully blends Hindu mythology with futuristic aesthetics, starring Prabhas, Deepika Padukone, Amitabh Bachchan, and Kamal Haasan. Produced on a record ₹600 crore budget using advanced technology and practical effects by Mahindra & Mahindra, the film achieved massive commercial success, grossing an estimated ₹1,180 crore against its cost. While critics praised its visual spectacle and technical brilliance—winning four major SIIMA Awards including Best Film, Best Supporting Actor (Male) for Bachchan, Best Supporting Actor (Female) for Anna Ben, and Best Negative Role for Kamal Haasan—they noted flaws in writing and pacing. The film's legacy extends beyond its 2024 success, as a sequel (*Part 2*) is confirmed for the *Kalki Cinematic Universe*, with approximately 60% of filming already underway and production scheduled to begin before 2026, though producers have since parted ways with Deepika Padukone for the upcoming installment.
