**Film & AI Summaries:**

**AI-Themed Films & Franchises:**
*   ***Tron*** **(Original):** Chronicles Kevin Flynn's battle against the rogue AI, MCP, in a digital world, with sequels including *Tron: Legacy* (2010), *Tron: Uprising* (2012), and the upcoming *Tron: Ares* (2025), featuring the self-aware AI Ares.
*   ***Ra.One*** **(2011):** Involves the AI villain Ra.One escaping a video game.
*   ***Enthiran*** **(2010):** Explores the development of emotions in the android Chitti.
*   ***The Machine*** **(2013):** Depicts British scientists creating a sentient, rebellious android from brain scans.
*   ***Atlas*** **(2024):** Features Jennifer Lopez combating the AI terrorist Harlan using AI-assisted mecha.
*   ***Alien: Covenant*** **(2014):** A prequel where the android David releases a pathogen.
*   ***Her*** **(2013):** Examines the emotional bond between Joaquin Phoenix and the OS, Samantha.
*   ***The Terminator*** **Series:** Focuses on AI dominance following Skynet's deployment of android assassins.
*   ***The Creator*** **(2023):** Set in 2055, a nuclear war is sparked by a US government-created AI, resolved when protagonists cooperate with the robotic simulant Alphie.
*   ***WarGames*** **(1983):** A pioneering techno-thriller where a hacker accidentally simulates a global thermonuclear war with the Soviet Union, influencing real-world legislation like the Computer Fraud and Abuse Act of 1986.
*   ***Mission: Impossible*** **Series:** Features Ethan Hunt seeking to stop a malevolent AI, "the Entity," from controlling global systems in *Dead Reckoning Part One*.

**Kalki 2898 AD:**
*   This major Indian sci-fi epic, set in 2898 AD, blends Hindu mythology (Kalki, Bhairava, Shambhala) with Western sci-fi like *Star Wars*. It was a "colourful spectacle" filmed with advanced technology and achieved significant marketing success. Critically, it received praise for its stunning visuals and world-building, despite some noted flaws in writing and pacing. It earned several SIIMA awards/nominations in 2025, including for Amitabh Bachchan. The film is part of the Kalki Cinematic Universe, with sequels planned, though production details have changed.

**AI/Robot Interaction & Other Themes:**
*   ***Ron's Gone Wrong:*** A 2021 animated film about technology's impact on youth via a B-bot named Ron.
*   ***Free Guy:*** Centers on NPC clerk Guy gaining self-awareness while working with developer Millie Rusk.
*   **Contemporary AI Development:** OpenAI has advanced its technology with GPT-4o, which features voice mimicry capabilities.
