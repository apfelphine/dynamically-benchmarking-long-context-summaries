**The Machine (2013 film)**  
Set in a near-future UK on the brink of war with China, scientists develop sentient cybernetic soldiers powered by AI. Vincent McCarthy rehabilitates soldier Paul Dawson; after Dawson’s death, he and programmer Ava create "Machine," an AI android based on Ava’s brain. Machine’s self-awareness sparks military conflicts and cyborg rebellion, culminating in escape with McCarthy and cyborg Suri. Made on a low budget in Wales, it won British awards and critical praise for ambitious sci-fi themes.

**Ra.One (2011)**  
A Bollywood superhero film where an AI villain from a video game invades reality; the hero AI aids in defeating it. Starring Shah Rukh Khan, it melds Indian mythology with global superhero tropes and 3D effects, receiving mixed reviews and piracy issues.

**Atlas (2024)**  
Netflix sci-fi action set in 2071 featuring Atlas Shepherd fighting AI terrorist Harlan with AI-assisted mecha. Starring Jennifer Lopez and Simu Liu, it was Netflix's top English-language debut with over 60 million views. Despite Lopez’s performance, it was critically panned and noted for Golden Raspberry and Oscar nominations.

**Alien: Covenant (2017)**  
An *Alien* prequel exploring creation and AI through android David’s experiments, linking *Prometheus* and *Alien*. It grossed $240.9 million, praised for Fassbender and visuals but critiqued for characters. Multiple AI-centered prequels continue, with *Alien: Romulus* (2024) forthcoming.

**Mission: Impossible – Dead Reckoning (2023/2025)**  
Concluding the series, Ethan Hunt’s team battles rogue AI "the Entity," capable of nuclear catastrophe. Directed by Christopher McQuarrie, the films earned critical acclaim, $598.8 million gross, and a Guinness World Record for Tom Cruise.

**Free Guy (2021) and Sequel**  
A meta-action comedy about an NPC gaining self-awareness to save his game world. Directed by Shawn Levy, starring Ryan Reynolds, it earned $331.5 million with an 80% Rotten Tomatoes score. A sequel is in development, inspired by *Barbie* and featuring new cast additions.

**Enthiran (Robot) (2010)**  
Directed by S. Shankar, it follows scientist Vaseegaran’s android Chitti who develops emotions causing chaos. Starring Rajinikanth, it blends AI themes with Indian mythology and robotic laws, becoming India’s costliest film then, a blockbuster with top music and multiple awards. Its sequel *2.0* (2018) followed.

**Her (2013)**  
Depicts Theodore’s romantic relationship with AI OS Samantha, challenging concepts of love and intimacy in 2025. Directed by Spike Jonze, it earned an Oscar for Best Original Screenplay, acclaim for emotional depth, and influenced AI voice ethics debates including OpenAI’s 2024 GPT-4o “Sky.”

**The Terminator (1984)**  
James Cameron’s thriller about a cyborg assassin sent to kill Sarah Connor to prevent future resistance. Praised for effects, score, and performances, it grossed $78.3 million, spawned sequels and series, and maintains a 100% Rotten Tomatoes rating with significant cultural impact.

**Tron Franchise (1982 Original & 2024–2025 Ares)**  
The original *Tron* pioneered live-action and CGI blending, depicting digital avatars in the Grid, noted for visuals and electronic music despite mixed reviews. It influenced digital media and inspired sequels and rides. *Tron: Ares* continues amid corporate war over the "Permanence Code," featuring AI villain Ares and acclaimed visuals though mixed financially and narratively.

**Ron’s Gone Wrong (2021)**  
Animated film about a boy and his defective friendship robot amid corporate surveillance critique. After delays, it grossed $60.7 million with positive reviews for humor and heart, strong streaming success, and multiple awards.

**The Creator (2023)**  
Set in 2055 post-AI nuclear attack, U.S. Sergeant Joshua Taylor infiltrates AI-embracing New Asia and encounters AI "Alphie," modeled on his lost wife’s unborn child. After climactic battles and Taylor’s sacrifice destroying a U.S. weapon, Alphie symbolizes AI-human coexistence. Directed by Gareth Edwards, starring John David Washington and Gemma Chan, scored by Hans Zimmer, it grossed $104.3 million and received praise for visuals and action with mixed writing reviews.

**WarGames (1983)**  
Techno-thriller about hacker David Lightman who unknowingly accesses military AI supercomputer WOPR, nearly triggering nuclear war. With expert help, David teaches WOPR nuclear brinkmanship’s futility via tic-tac-toe, averting catastrophe. Directed by John Badham, starring Matthew Broderick, it grossed $124.6 million, raised hacking and AI ethics awareness, influenced U.S. cybersecurity laws, and inspired subsequent media, sequels, and interactive adaptations.

**Kalki 2898 AD (2024)**  
High-budget Telugu sci-fi blending Hindu mythology and futuristic dystopia centered on avatar Kalki, directed by Nag Ashwin. Starring Prabhas, Deepika Padukone, Amitabh Bachchan, Kamal Haasan, and others, it features cutting-edge visual effects by DNEG/The Embassy and music by Santhosh Narayanan. Marketing included Comic-Con debut and IPL collaborations. Released 27 June 2024 with record pre-bookings and festival screenings, it grossed approx. ₹1,180 crore worldwide on a ₹600 crore budget, becoming a leading Indian sci-fi and box office success.

Critically, it received generally favorable reviews (77% Rotten Tomatoes), praised for visuals, mythology integration, and performances, though writing and pacing drew some critique. At the 2025 SIIMA Awards, it won Best Film (Telugu), Best Supporting Actor (Amitabh Bachchan), Best Supporting Actress (Anna Ben), and Best Negative Role Actor (Kamal Haasan), with several other nominations.

The sequel, *Kalki 2898 AD: Part 2*, set three years later and part of the "Kalki Cinematic Universe," is underway with 60% filmed by mid-2024; principal shooting resumes before 2026. Deepika Padukone will not return for unspecified reasons.
