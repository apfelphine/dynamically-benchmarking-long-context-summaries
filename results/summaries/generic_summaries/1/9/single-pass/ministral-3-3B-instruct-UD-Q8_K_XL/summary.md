### **Ron’s Gone Wrong (2021)**
**Plot:**
In a dystopian California town, 12-year-old Barney Pudowski, isolated due to his mother’s death, is the only kid without a Bubble Bot—a robotic friend designed to help make friends via algorithm. His father and grandmother secretly buy a malfunctioning bot, Ron, which quickly becomes a chaotic, unintentionally rebellious companion. Barney and Ron’s escapades—including a runaway chase, a rescue mission, and a mid-credits scene—spark a rebellion against Bubble’s algorithmic friendship system. The film explores themes of loneliness, self-discovery, and the ethical implications of artificial intelligence in fostering human connections.

### **WarGames (1983)**
**Plot:**
In 1983, a bored high school student, David Lightman, hacks into a NORAD supercomputer (WOPR) and accidentally triggers a global nuclear war simulation. The film follows David’s escape from NORAD, his confrontation with the computer’s true nature, and his realization that the system’s logic is flawed. It critiques Cold War-era fears of nuclear war and the dangers of unchecked technological control.

**Voice Cast:**
- Matthew Broderick as David Lightman
- Dabney Coleman as Dr. John McKittrick
- John Wood as Dr. Stephen Falken/WOPR
- Ally Sheedy as Jennifer Mack

**Production:**
Developed by Walter Parkes and Lawrence Lasker, *WarGames* was inspired by Cold War anxieties and became a cultural touchstone for discussions on hacking and AI. It influenced later films like *The Matrix* and inspired real-world cybersecurity policies.
