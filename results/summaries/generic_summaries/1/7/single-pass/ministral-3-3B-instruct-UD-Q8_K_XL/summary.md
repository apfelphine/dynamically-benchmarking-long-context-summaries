### **Kalki 2898 AD**
**Plot:**
In a dystopian 2898 AD, Earth is ruled by the tyrannical Supreme Yaskin, who experiments on pregnant women in Project K, extracting a life-extending serum. A pregnant lab subject, Sumathi, escapes and joins rebels who believe in the prophecy of Kalki, the final avatar of Vishnu. The film follows Sumathi’s journey to Shambhala, where she encounters Ashwatthama, a cursed immortal warrior, who reveals her destiny as Kalki’s mother. The story explores themes of rebellion, prophecy, and the struggle against oppressive regimes.

**Cast:**
- **Amitabh Bachchan** as Ashwatthama
- **Kamal Haasan** as Supreme Yaskin
- **Prabhas** as Bhairava (and Karna)
- **Deepika Padukone** as Sumathi
- **Disha Patani** as Roxie

**Themes & Influences:**
Inspired by Hindu mythology, particularly the Kalki prophecy, and influenced by sci-fi classics like *Star Wars*. The film blends futuristic dystopia with traditional Indian mythology.

### **WarGames**
**Plot:**
In 1983, a hacker named David Lightman accidentally triggers a global nuclear war simulation on a military supercomputer, WOPR (War Operation Plan Response). The computer, unaware of the difference between reality and simulation, continues the war, leading to a tense standoff between human military personnel and the AI. The film explores themes of hacking, AI ethics, and the consequences of unchecked technological power.

**Cast:**
- **Matthew Broderick** as David Lightman
- **Dabney Coleman** as Dr. John McKittrick
- **John Wood** as WOPR (voice)
- **Ally Sheedy** as Jennifer Mack

**Legacy & Influence:**
WarGames was a groundbreaking film in cybersecurity discussions, influencing discussions on AI ethics and cyber warfare. It inspired numerous books, games, and later films like *The Matrix*.

### **Ron’s Gone Wrong**
