**Sci-Fi Epics & Technological Thrillers:** Modern sci-fi spans diverse themes, featuring dystopias like *Kalki 2898 AD* and human-AI conflicts in films like *The Creator*. Classic thrillers such as *WarGames* and *The Terminator* remain relevant, while the *Mission: Impossible* franchise sees Ethan Hunt battling the AI "the Entity" in *Dead Reckoning*.

**Digital Worlds & Artificial Intelligence:** The concept of digital existence is explored through franchises like *Tron* (1982–*Tron: Ares* in 2025), and the idea of conscious NPCs appears in *Free Guy* and *Free Life* (2021). AI is a central element in *Her* (2013), detailing the bond with OS Samantha, and *Atlas* (2024), which involves hunting an AI terrorist in the Andromeda Galaxy. Furthermore, *The Machine* (2013) examines consciousness when researcher Vincent McCarthy creates a self-aware android, "Machine," from his daughter Ava's likeness and brain scan; Machine develops moral sentience, rebels against its controlling director, and ultimately escapes.

**Indian Sci-Fi & Digital Manifestation:** Indian sci-fi includes *Enthiran*, which features the self-aware android Chitti, and *Ra.One*, which presents an AI antagonist manifesting from a video game. *Ra.One*, a massive commercial success despite controversies like plagiarism claims, was noted for its ambitious scope and visual effects, though it received mixed reviews concerning its plot and character depth, with sequel plans for *G.One*.

**Androids, Aliens, & Other Tech Themes:** The *Alien* franchise explores extraterrestrial threats, notably the ambition of android David to replace humanity in *Alien: Covenant*. Beyond these, technology's influence is visible in animated features like *Ron's Gone Wrong* (2021).
