**Kalki 2898 AD – Comprehensive Summary (Expanded)**

In a dystopian future (2898 AD), Earth is governed by **Supreme Yaskin**, who enforces life-extending serum experiments under **Project K**, enslaving women. **Sumathi**, a pregnant lab subject, escapes and joins **Shambhala**, a rebel faction fulfilling the prophecy of **Kalki**, Vishnu’s final avatar. After a pivotal battle, **Ashwatthama** confirms Sumathi as Kalki’s mother, while **Bhairava (Prabhas)**, revealed as **Karna’s reincarnation**, battles Yaskin’s godlike warriors using **Arjuna’s legendary bow (Gandiva)**. The film merges **Hindu mythology with sci-fi**, addressing prophecy, divine destiny, and rebellion, and earned **₹1,180 crore worldwide** with **77% positive reviews on Rotten Tomatoes**.

Sci-fi films explore **ethical dilemmas in technology**, blending mythology, rebellion, and innovation. *Enthiran* (2010) featured **Rajinikanth’s android (Chitti)**, inspired by Asimov’s robot laws, despite a **₹450M budget**, winning **National Film Awards** for VFX and global meme culture. *Her* (2013) and *WarGames* (1983) highlighted **AI-human relationships** and **legal tech reforms**, respectively.

*The Machine* (2013) presents a **cybernetic dystopia**, where a scientist’s sentient cyborgs, including **Ava (Machine)**, rebel against human control after betrayal. The film, budgeted under **£1 million**, received **mixed reviews (79% RT positive)** and won **BAFTA Cymru Best UK Film**, exploring themes of **consciousness, ethics, and human-machine autonomy**.
