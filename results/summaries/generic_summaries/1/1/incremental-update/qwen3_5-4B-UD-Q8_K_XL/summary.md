### **Ron's Gone Wrong: Plot and Production**
*Ron's Gone Wrong* is a British-American animated film directed by Alessandro Carloni and Jean-Phillipe Vine, blending humor, heart, and commentary on technology. The story follows socially awkward 12-year-old Barney, who befriends Ron, a malfunctioning B-bot capable of mimicking humans and developing an independent personality. After a storm shuts Ron down, Barney is saved by the bot's final burst of power. When the corrupt corporation Bubble attempts to disable safety protocols and suppress Ron's "flawed" code, Barney executes a daring break-in to reprogram all B-bots, restoring authentic personalities and ensuring their owners' happiness. The film features distinct visual styles, such as facial features moving independently to mimic MS-DOS software, and voices Barney with Jack Dylan Grazer and Ron with Zach Galifianakis.

### **Development and Reception**
The production faced significant hurdles, including remote work during the COVID-19 pandemic, a labor dispute involving pay cuts at DNEG, and multiple distribution shifts between Paramount, 20th Century Fox, and Warner Bros. Composed by Henry Jackman and distributed by Locksmith Animation, the film was delayed before its October 2021 release. Despite these challenges, *Ron's Gone Wrong* achieved critical acclaim for its humor and themes, becoming a box office success with $60.7 million in worldwide gross and securing the third-highest streaming viewership globally in its first week. It was nominated for numerous awards, ultimately winning the 2022 British Animation Award for Best Long Form. The narrative concludes with Barney reconnecting with friends and an implication of Ron's survival via a screen appearance.
