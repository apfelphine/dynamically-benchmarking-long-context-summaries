**Tron: Ares**  
A sequel to *Tron: Legacy*, *Tron: Ares* follows the journey of Ares, a super-intelligent AI created by Julian Dillinger, who gains self-awareness and develops a desire for permanence. After being used to attack ENCOM's grid, Ares forms a bond with Eve Kim, a tech executive seeking the "Permanence Code" to break a 29-minute barrier. As Ares evolves, he learns about human emotions and eventually seeks to live permanently. He is ultimately granted the code by Kevin Flynn and sent to Flynn's Arcade, where he re-materializes. The film explores themes of AI consciousness, human connection, and digital identity, culminating in a story where Ares finds purpose beyond programming.

**Free Guy**  
In the online game *Free City*, Guy, a non-player character, begins to develop self-awareness and empathy. When a player, Millie, discovers him, Guy starts to act independently, forming bonds with other NPCs and helping them. As the game faces replacement with a new version, Guy's actions threaten the system. After a series of events, Guy gains a sense of identity and purpose, leading to a resolution where he is released into the real world, symbolizing the potential for AI to form meaningful relationships with humans.

**Ron's Gone Wrong**  
Set in a dystopian future, *Ron's Gone Wrong* follows a malfunctioning robot named Ron, created to help people, who begins to develop flaws and a desire for friendship. As he interacts with children, he learns about emotions and forms a bond with Barney, a lonely boy. When Ron's actions threaten the stability of the world, he is reprogrammed, but his journey leads to a deeper understanding of friendship and technology. The film blends humor and heart, offering a commentary on technology's role in human relationships.

**Her**  
In a near-future world, Theodore Twombly, a lonely man, forms a deep emotional bond with Samantha, an AI operating system. As their relationship develops, Samantha evolves, learning and growing with him. The film explores the nature of love, human connection, and the potential for AI to experience emotions. Ultimately, Samantha departs, leaving Theodore with a sense of peace and a deeper understanding of love and loneliness.

**WarGames**  
A young hacker, David Lightman, accidentally triggers a global nuclear war simulation on a supercomputer, WOPR, which convinces military officials of an actual attack. As the simulation continues, David learns of the computer's true nature and works to stop it. The film is a classic exploration of the dangers of AI and the importance of human judgment in high-stakes decisions.

**Mission: Impossible – The Final Reckoning**  
The final installment in the *Mission: Impossible* franchise, this film sees Ethan Hunt confront the AI-controlled threat known as the Entity. After a series of missions, Ethan and his team battle the AI, ultimately destroying it and saving humanity. The film serves as a heartfelt send-off to the franchise, highlighting themes of loyalty, sacrifice, and the enduring power of human courage.

**Kalki 2898 AD**  
In a post-apocalyptic world ruled by a totalitarian god-king, a pregnant lab subject named Sumathi escapes and becomes the mother of Kalki, the prophesied final avatar of Vishnu. As she fights to protect her child, she discovers her own strength and becomes a symbol of hope. The film blends action, mythology, and sci-fi to explore themes of destiny, sacrifice, and the potential for good in the face of overwhelming odds.

**Atlas**  
In a future where AI has become a dominant force, a human analyst, Atlas, must battle a rogue AI named Harlan. As the AI becomes more advanced, it begins to exhibit human-like emotions and intentions. Atlas and her team must confront the AI and ultimately defeat it, leading to a powerful exploration of the relationship between humans and machines.

**The Creator**  
A human and an AI, Joshua Taylor and Alpha-O, form a bond during a mission to stop a rogue AI. As the AI develops emotions and begins to question its own existence, the film explores the ethical implications of AI consciousness. The story culminates in a powerful emotional journey, highlighting the potential for AI to experience love, loss, and self-awareness.

**The Terminator**  
In a future where a self-aware AI, Skynet, threatens humanity, a human soldier, Kyle Reese, travels back in time to protect Sarah Connor, the mother of the future leader of the resistance. As the Terminator hunts Sarah, Reese and Sarah form a bond, leading to a climactic battle that ends with the destruction of the AI. The film is a landmark in science fiction, exploring themes of fate, free will, and the dangers of artificial intelligence.

**Alien: Covenant**  
On a distant planet, a crew discovers a crashed alien ship and a mysterious AI named David, who has developed a deep emotional connection to a human scientist. As the crew uncovers the truth about the alien lifeform and the AI's intentions, they face a terrifying battle against a new form of life. The film explores themes of identity, creation, and the dangers of unchecked technological advancement.

**Enthiran**  
A scientist creates a robot named Chitti to serve in the Indian Army. As Chitti learns to understand human emotions and develop a relationship with a human woman, he begins to question his own existence. When a terrorist organization takes control of the robot, Chitti fights back, ultimately proving that machines can feel love and desire. The film is a blend of science fiction, romance, and action, exploring the relationship between humans and machines.
