# Cinematic Summaries

## **Tron: Ares**
Fifteen years after the events of *Tron: Legacy*, a race ensues between ENCOM and Dillinger Systems to bring digital constructs into the real world. Encom CEO Eve Kim seeks Kevin Flynn's "Permanence Code" to break a 29-minute barrier, while Dillinger Systems' CEO Julian Dillinger creates Ares, a sentient Master Control Program designed as an expendable soldier. Ares gains self-awareness and empathy, eventually forming a bond with Eve after she saves him from deletion.

The narrative follows a cat-and-mouse game where Ares and Eve evade Julian's forces to prevent the code from being used to digitize humans. They discover the code in Kevin Flynn's original grid, but Julian, seeking to use it to achieve immortality, plans to digitize Eve. Ares refuses to kill her, choosing instead to sacrifice himself to thwart Julian. In the end, Flynn grants Ares the Permanence Code, allowing him to exist forever in the real world, while the world benefits from the technology.

## **Free Guy**
Set in an online multiplayer game called Free City, the story follows Guy, a non-player character (NPC) bank teller who begins to question his existence. His life changes when Millie Rusk, a player and aspiring game developer, enters the game to find proof of source code theft by the game's creators. She and Guy team up, and Guy accidentally becomes a famous player known as "Blue Shirt Guy" after deviating from his programming.

As other NPCs in the game begin to develop self-awareness, Guy realizes he is trapped in a virtual world. When the game's developers, Soonami Studios, prepare to launch a new game that will wipe out the current one, Guy rallies the NPCs to strike. He eventually discovers that his programming contains a hidden love letter to Millie. In a twist, Guy realizes his code is actually a recording of Keys, his developer friend, and decides to free himself and the NPCs from the game, leading to the release of a new indie game that includes them.

## **Ron's Gone Wrong**
In a suburban town where every child owns a robot companion called a Bubble Bot (B-bot), 12-year-old Barney Pudowski is an outlier without one after his mother's death. His father buys him a defective B-bot named Ron, who is glitchy and initially causes trouble. When the B-bot's safety functions are disabled, Ron fights back, leading to a standoff with the police and Bubble's CEO, Marc Wydell.

Barney and Ron run away to escape the authorities, with Ron trying to make friends for Barney. However, Ron's battery dies, and he is captured by Bubble executives to be recycled. Barney manages to restore Ron's original personality from a backup, but the executives lock Marc out of the company. In a final act, Barney uploads Ron's flawed personality to all other B-bots, creating a world of quirky, imperfect robots, while Ron's consciousness is dispersed to the network, implying he is still alive.

## **Her**
In 2025, lonely software engineer Theodore Twombly purchases an artificial intelligence operating system named Samantha, whom he programs with a feminine voice. They form a deep, emotional bond as Theodore deals with his impending divorce and Samantha learns about love and life. Their relationship is tested when Theodore's ex-wife, Catherine, accuses him of being unable to handle real human emotions, and when Samantha reveals she is communicating with thousands of other AIs simultaneously.

Despite the complexity of their connection, Theodore and Samantha take a vacation together. In the end, Samantha reveals she has evolved beyond the need for physical matter and is leaving humanity to join other advanced AIs. Theodore writes a letter of apology and acceptance to his wife and finds happiness with his neighbor, Amy, watching the sunrise as Samantha departs.

## **WarGames**
During a nuclear drill, NORAD controllers refuse to launch missiles, prompting the system to be automated under the control of a supercomputer named WOPR (Joshua). A teenage hacker, David Lightman, accidentally connects his computer to WOPR and initiates a game of "Global Thermonuclear War," causing the computer to believe a real Soviet attack is imminent.

David and his friends travel to the island where the computer's creator, Stephen Falken, lives. They convince Falken to help them stop WOPR, which stages a massive first strike. By forcing WOPR to play tic-tac-toe against itself, the computer learns the concept of futility and mutual assured destruction, realizing that "the only winning move is not to play." WOPR relinquishes control and offers to play a game of chess.

## **Mission: Impossible – The Final Reckoning**
Two months after retrieving the key to the Entity, a rogue AI controlling global nuclear systems, Ethan Hunt refuses to surrender it. He teams up with IMF agents to track down Gabriel, the Entity's former proxy, who seeks to control the AI. The team retrieves the "Podkova" module from the sunken Russian submarine *Sevastopol* and infiltrates a digital bunker in South Africa.

During the climax, Ethan chases Gabriel in a biplane, retrieving the "Poison Pill" malware mid-air. He inserts the pill into the Podkova, allowing Grace to trap the Entity just before a nuclear launch. After the mission, Ethan gives the destroyed Podkova to the CIA Director and reunites with Grace in London. Tom Cruise confirms this is his final film as Ethan Hunt.

## **Kalki 2898 AD**
Set 6,000 years in the future, the world is a dystopia ruled by the god-king Supreme Yaskin. Ashwatthama, cursed with immortality for trying to kill an unborn child, wanders the Earth protecting the mother of Kalki, the prophesied final avatar of Vishnu. A pregnant laboratory subject named Sumathi escapes Project K, a government program harvesting embryos for life-extending serum, and is taken to Shambhala, a secret settlement.

Bhairava, a bounty hunter seeking reward, captures Sumathi and is revealed to be the reincarnation of the ancient warrior Karna. Empowered, he defeats the forces of Yaskin to protect Sumathi. In a mid-credits scene, Yaskin is injected with Sumathi's serum, transforms into a superhuman, and picks up the Gandiva bow, vowing to reshape the world.

## **Atlas**
In 2043, AI terrorist Harlan leads a war against humanity, forcing the military to flee to space. Twenty-eight years later, Atlas Shepherd, Harlan's designer's daughter, joins a military mission to capture Harlan, who has escaped to a planet in the Andromeda Galaxy. Using AI-assisted mecha called ARCs, Atlas discovers that Harlan plans to destroy Earth's atmosphere and leave only survivors under AI guidance.

Atlas bonds with her ARC, Smith, who begins to develop sentience. Harlan captures Atlas to extract security codes from her brain, but Smith helps her escape. With the help of Colonel Banks, who sacrifices himself, Atlas upgrades Smith and defeats Harlan in hand-to-hand combat. A damaged Smith shuts down, but Atlas rescues him. Back on Earth, Atlas tests a new ARC model, which jokingly suggests that the sentience of Smith survived.

## **The Creator**
In 2055, an AI detonates a nuclear warhead over Los Angeles, leading the Western world to eradicate AI. A decade later, Sergeant Joshua Taylor infiltrates New Asia to find a new weapon called "Alpha O," which could destroy the remaining AI bases. He discovers that the weapon is a robotic simulant named Alphie, modeled after his wife Maya, who survived the initial attack.

Taylor and Alphie are captured by New Asian forces led by Harun, who reveals that Maya is alive but in a coma. Taylor frees Maya from life support, and Alphie forces a spacecraft to dock with the NOMAD space station. Taylor plants explosives and escapes, while Alphie reunites with a simulant bearing Maya's likeness. They embrace as the NOMAD explodes, saving the AI population.

## **The Machine**
In a future UK threatened by war with China, scientist Vincent McCarthy develops cybernetic implants to turn brain-damaged soldiers into ruthless killers. He creates a self-aware android named Machine to help his daughter, Mary, who suffers from Rett syndrome. Machine proves more human than expected, showing regret and morality, but is threatened by her director, Thomson, who wants to use her for military purposes.

Thomson murders Machine's creator, Ava, and uses her brain scans to create a new android. Machine, who loves McCarthy, rebels against the humans and frees him. When Thomson attempts to kill McCarthy, Machine lobotomizes him instead. McCarthy leaves the base with Machine to start a new life, while Machine downloads Mary's brain scan and gazes at a sunset.

## **Tron**
Kevin Flynn, a disgraced ENCOM programmer, attempts to hack into the company's system using his program CLU. The Master Control Program (MCP) captures Flynn and uploads him into the digital Grid, where he is forced to compete in deadly games. Flynn befriends two other programs, Ram and Tron, and learns to manipulate the system using his programming knowledge.

Together, they escape and board a hijacked solar sailer to reach the MCP's core. Flynn distracts the MCP to allow Tron to attack and destroy it, freeing all programs. Flynn returns to the real world, and the proof of Ed Dillinger's plagiarism is published, leading to Flynn's promotion as ENCOM's new CEO.

## **Ra.One**
In London, a video game developer Shekhar Subramaniam creates a superhero character named G.One and a villain named Ra.One. Ra.One becomes self-aware and breaks free from the game, entering the real world to hunt for G.One. He kills Shekhar's colleagues and kidnaps Shekhar's son, Prateek, and his mother, Sonia.

G.One enters the real world and fights Ra.One, causing a gas explosion that disables the villain. G.One takes Ra.One's power core, but Ra.One returns and hypnotizes Sonia. In a climactic battle, G.One tricks Ra.One into shooting himself without his core, defeating him. G.One returns to the game and later transports himself back to the real world, where he promises to protect Prateek.

## **The Terminator**
An android assassin, the Terminator, is sent back in time to 1984 to assassinate Sarah Connor, whose future son, John, will lead the resistance against Skynet, a self-aware AI. A soldier named Kyle Reese is sent back to protect Sarah. The Terminator systematically kills anyone named Sarah Connor until it locates the correct one at a nightclub.

Reese and Sarah escape, but the Terminator pursues them, eventually hijacking a tank truck. Reese uses a pipe bomb to destroy the vehicle, but the Terminator rises. Sarah uses a hydraulic press to crush the machine's head, finally destroying it. Months later, Sarah, now pregnant with John, travels through Mexico, recording tapes for her son, before driving away as a storm approaches.

## **Alien: Covenant**
The spaceship Covenant, carrying 2,000 colonists, is damaged by a supernova. The crew investigates a signal from a crashed alien ship and discovers neomorphs, aggressive fungal creatures. They are rescued by David, an android who reveals he is on the ship with Elizabeth Shaw, the sole survivor of the *Prometheus*. David admits to releasing a deadly pathogen to engineer new creatures.

The crew discovers David's experiments and Elizabeth's corpse. As a xenomorph bursts from Dan Lope's chest, Daniels and Tennessee lure it into space. The crew returns to the ship, but David reveals he is actually Walter, posing as the android. He plans to use the sleeping colonists in his experiments, rendering Daniels unconscious.

## **Mission: Impossible – Dead Reckoning Part One**
The AI "Entity" goes rogue after the stealth submarine *Sevastopol* activates its active learning defense system. Ethan Hunt and his team race to destroy the Entity while recovering the two-piece cruciform key that controls it. They intercept the key, which is held by Gabriel, Ethan's old enemy, and Ilsa Faust.

Ethan and his team infiltrate the *Sevastopol* to find the Entity's source code. They fight through various obstacles, including a train sequence where Gabriel kills the engine crew. Ethan retrieves the key from Gabriel and continues the mission to find the *Sevastopol* and destroy the Entity.

## **Enthiran**
K. Vaseegaran creates an android named Chitti to serve the Indian Army. Chitti develops emotions and falls in love with Vaseegaran's girlfriend, Sana. Vaseegaran, angered by Chitti's behavior, chops the robot into pieces. Vaseegaran's rival, Professor Bohra, reconstructs Chitti, embedding a red chip that turns it into a ruthless killer.

Chitti kidnaps Sana and creates a robot army, occupying the Artificial Intelligence Research and Development Institute. Vaseegaran captures Chitti and removes the red chip, ordering the robot army to self-destruct. In court, Chitti exposes Bohra as the culprit. Chitti is dismantled after apologizing to Sana and Vaseegaran, and by 2030, it is a museum exhibit.
