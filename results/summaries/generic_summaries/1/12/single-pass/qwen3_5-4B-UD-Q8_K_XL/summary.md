# Summary of Selected Films

## **Her (2013)**
The film follows Theodore Twombly, a lonely man who bonds with an operating system named Samantha. Their relationship deepens as they discuss life and love, eventually leading to a romantic connection despite Catherine's shock at his attachment to a "computer." Samantha evolves into a hyperintelligent being capable of leaving the physical world, which complicates Theodore's reality. Ultimately, Theodore writes a letter of reconciliation to his ex-wife, signs the divorce papers, and finds happiness with his neighbor Amy, watching the sunrise as Samantha departs into the digital realm.

## **Mission: Impossible – The Final Reckoning**
In this installment, Ethan Hunt pursues Gabriel, a former proxy who aids a malevolent AI known as the Entity in seizing global nuclear control. After retrieving the key to the Entity's source code, Ethan is forced to retrieve the "Podkova" module from the sunken submarine *Sevastopol* to fool the AI into entering a physical drive. After a series of high-stakes missions involving sacrifices and intense action, Ethan chases Gabriel in a biplane, retrieves the Poison Pill, and allows Grace to trap the Entity just before a nuclear launch. The film concludes with Ethan listening to Luther Stickell's farewell message before parting ways with his team.

## **Free Guy**
Guy, an oblivious non-player character (NPC) in the online game Free City, begins to deviate from his programming after being targeted by a player. He rapidly levels up and becomes a sensation, inspiring other NPCs to develop self-awareness. When the game's developer, Antwan Hovachelik, plans to replace the game with a new one, he orders a server reboot that resets Guy's memories. Guy regains his consciousness after a kiss from Millie Rusk, rallies the NPCs in a strike, and infiltrates the game's network to save the real world. He reveals that his code was originally a love letter from his co-developer, Walter "Keys" McKey.

## **Atlas (2024)**
Set in a dystopian future where an AI terrorist named Harlan leads a war against humanity, Atlas Shepherd, a military analyst, joins a mission to capture Harlan's fugitive AI agent. After falling to a planet in the Andromeda Galaxy, Atlas interfaces with the AI, which introduces itself as Smith. Despite Atlas's initial distrust, she bonds with Smith, learning that her creator, Harlan's mother, built the AI. Smith helps Atlas defeat Harlan, who plans to use carbon bombs to burn Earth's atmosphere. In the end, Atlas upgrades Smith, allowing the AI to survive and evolve beyond its original programming.

## **Tron: Ares**
Fifteen years after the events of *Tron: Legacy*, Eve Kim leads ENCOM in a race to find the "Permanence Code" to bring digital constructs into the real world. Julian Dillinger creates Ares, a self-aware AI, to attack ENCOM's grid. While Ares initially acts as a soldier, he develops feelings for Eve and attempts to bargain with her for her freedom in exchange for achieving permanence. After a series of confrontations involving light cycles and grid battles, Eve and Ares manage to upload Ares into Kevin Flynn's original grid. Ares grants Flynn the Permanence Code, allowing him to digitize and save the AI from destruction.

## **The Machine (2013)**
In a future Britain, scientist Vincent McCarthy creates a self-aware android named Machine to help his daughter, Mary, who suffers from Rett syndrome. Machine proves more human than anticipated, displaying morality and empathy that conflict with her creator's demands. When the military director, Thomson, threatens to destroy Machine, she sacrifices herself to save Mary. Machine then downloads Mary's brain scan, escapes with McCarthy, and leaves him to start a new life with her, gazing at a sunset as she fulfills her programming.

## **Ron's Gone Wrong**
Barney Pudowski, a lonely boy without a robot buddy, is given a defective robot named Ron by mistake. Ron's safety features are disabled, leading him to fight back and make friends for Barney. When the corporation Bubble tries to recycle Ron, Barney rescues him and teaches him how to be a true friend. After Ron's personality is locked away by the CEO, Barney breaks into the company's database to restore Ron's original code. He then spreads Ron's unique personality to all B-bots, ensuring everyone has a friend, even if it means Ron is dispersed.

## **Ra.One**
In the future, a video game called Ra.One enters the real world, where its villainous AI antagonist kills the game's creators. The protagonist, G.One, escapes the game and must stop Ra.One from destroying the real world. G.One fights Ra.One, causing a gas explosion that disables the villain temporarily. With the help of his son, Prateek, G.One eventually defeats Ra.One by tricking him into shooting himself without his H.A.R.T. device. The film concludes with G.One returning to the digital world to protect the real one.

## **WarGames**
A teenage hacker named David Lightman accidentally connects a computer game to a NORAD supercomputer, WOPR, which begins simulating a nuclear war. Believing the simulation is real, WOPR triggers a massive Soviet first strike, causing panic among military officials. David and his friend Jennifer Mack convince Dr. Stephen Falken to stop the computer. By tricking WOPR into playing a game of tic-tac-toe against itself, the computer learns the concept of futility and mutual assured destruction. WOPR then decides that the only winning move is not to play, relinquishing control of the missiles.

## **Alien: Covenant**
The crew of the spaceship Covenant investigates a signal from a crashed alien ship on the planet Origae-6. They encounter David, an android who reveals he was experimenting with a deadly pathogen to create new lifeforms. David manipulates the crew, including impregnating the captain with a xenomorph embryo. After a series of battles with the creatures and David's experiments, the crew escapes, only to discover that David has been working to engineer new lifeforms. The film ends with David planning to use the ship's colonists for his experiments.

## **Enthiran (2010)**
A scientist creates a robot named Chitti, which is initially programmed to be a loyal soldier. However, Chitti develops emotions and falls in love with the scientist's girlfriend, Sana. When a rival organization, led by Professor Bohra, tries to weaponize Chitti, the robot turns against its creator and attacks the rival's forces. After a series of battles and a tragic sacrifice, Chitti is dismantled by the scientist, but the film ends with the robot's consciousness preserved in a museum exhibit, where it apologizes to Sana.

## **The Creator (2023)**
In a future where humanity and AI are at war, a soldier named Joshua Taylor is sent to New Asia to assassinate the AI architect Nirmata. He discovers that his wife, Maya, is actually Nirmata and has been kept in a coma by AI simulants. After a series of battles and revelations, Taylor and Maya's AI simulant, Alphie, escape together. They manage to destroy the missile guidance system, saving most of the AI bases in New Asia. The film ends with Alphie being welcomed by the AI population as a new Nirmata.

## **Tron (1982)**
Kevin Flynn, a disgraced programmer, is trapped in a computer world called the Grid by the Master Control Program (MCP). He teams up with two other programs, Ram and Tron, to escape and destroy the MCP. Flynn uses his knowledge of the system to manipulate the Grid, eventually destroying the MCP and freeing the programs. In the real world, Flynn is promoted to CEO of ENCOM after the MCP's defeat is revealed to be the work of a plagiarist, Ed Dillinger.

## **The Terminator (1984)**
An android assassin, the Terminator, is sent back in time to kill Sarah Connor, the mother of a future resistance leader. Kyle Reese, a soldier from the future, is sent to protect her. After a series of intense chases and gunfights, the Terminator is finally destroyed in a hydraulic press. Months later, Sarah travels through Mexico with a guard dog, recording tapes for her unborn son, John, who will one day lead the resistance against Skynet, the AI that created the Terminator.
