### Her (2013)  
Spike Jonze’s *Her* delves into Theodore Twombly’s relationship with Samantha, an AI operating system, examining identity and human intimacy. It won an Oscar and shaped discourse on human-AI connections.

---

### Mission: Impossible – Dead Reckoning Parts One (2023/2025) and The Final Reckoning  
Ethan Hunt confronts a sentient AI Entity threatening global security. Despite production challenges and strong critical praise, *Dead Reckoning Part One* underperformed financially but won multiple technical awards. The sequel, *The Final Reckoning* (2025), delayed by a strike, marks Tom Cruise’s final Ethan Hunt portrayal, with plans to continue the franchise.

---

### Free Guy (2021)  
Focusing on an NPC’s self-awareness in a video game, this film explores AI consciousness and digital rights. Starred Ryan Reynolds, grossing $331.5 million with positive reviews.

---

### Atlas (2024)  
Set in 2071, pilot Atlas Shepherd combats a rogue AI terrorist with an AI mech. Despite criticism, Jennifer Lopez’s performance and Netflix viewership highlighted AI conflict and redemption themes.

---

### Tron Franchise and Origins  
The *Tron* series centers on AI programs navigating virtual worlds under authoritarian control. The 1982 original, directed by Steven Lisberger, broke new ground in CGI and synthetic sound design amid production challenges. Though initially a financial disappointment, *Tron* became a cult classic influencing studios like Pixar, music artists, and visual effects history. Its legacy continues with *Tron: Legacy* (2010), the animated *Tron: Uprising* (2012), and *Tron: Ares* (2025), supported by extensive home media and Disney park attractions.

---

### The Machine (2013)  
British sci-fi exploring AI consciousness and ethics through military-created sentient cyborgs, noted for atmospheric treatment.

---

### Ron's Gone Wrong (2021)  
Animated film inspired by *Her*, examining friendship and technology via a malfunctioning robot; won the 2022 British Animation Award.

---

### Ra.One (2011)  
Indian sci-fi superhero blending AI with mythology, starring Shah Rukh Khan. Despite mixed reception and controversy, it set box office records and earned technical awards.

---

### WarGames (1983)  
Cold War thriller where a teen hacker nearly causes nuclear war via military AI, highlighting AI’s ethical dilemmas. It remains culturally influential, spawning adaptations.

---

### Alien: Covenant (2017) and Franchise Developments  
Blends alien horror with AI android David’s experiments; continues with *Alien: Romulus* (2024).

---

### Kalki 2898 AD (2024)  
High-budget Indian sci-fi epic merging Hindu mythology, AI, and genetic engineering, commercially successful with sequels planned.

---

### Enthiran (2010) and Sequel (2.0)  
Directed by S. Shankar, starring Rajinikanth, portrays AI evolving from military tool to sentient being, blending sci-fi with Indian mythology. It was record-breaking in India with a global sequel *2.0* (2018).

---

### The Creator (2023)  
Set post-nuclear strike amid AI conflict, it follows a man uncovering AI secrets. Directed by Gareth Edwards with ILM effects and Hans Zimmer’s score, it received positive reviews despite modest box office.

---

### The Terminator (1984)  
James Cameron’s foundational sci-fi thriller features a cyborg assassin sent from 2029 to kill Sarah Connor, linking human survival and AI rebellion. Notable for groundbreaking practical effects, a distinctive synthesizer score, and a compelling narrative, it launched careers of Cameron and Schwarzenegger. Despite initial mixed reviews, it became seminal in sci-fi and action genres, praised for pacing, effects, and tension.

Post-release, the film faced plagiarism claims by Harlan Ellison’s estate, resolved with screen credit. Thematically, it explores masculinity as fusion of man and machine, AI rebellion, and genre blending across sci-fi, action, and slasher elements. It is often credited as pioneering the “tech-noir” genre. The film’s home media presence spans VHS (1985) to a 4K restoration Blu-ray (2013) and Ultra HD release (2024). It achieved universal critical acclaim, winning Saturn Awards and securing top AFI rankings, with recognition by the Library of Congress. Franchise expansions include five sequels (1991–2019) and a TV series continuing canon post-*Terminator 2*, with Schwarzenegger reprising his role frequently and Cameron returning to helm key sequels.
