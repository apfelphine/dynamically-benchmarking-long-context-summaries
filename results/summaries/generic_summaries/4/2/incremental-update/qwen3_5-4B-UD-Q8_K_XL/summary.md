### Noam Chomsky: Linguistics, Activism, Evolution, and Legacy

Noam Chomsky, born in 1928 into a politically active Jewish family, is a transformative figure who revolutionized linguistics through **biolinguistics** and **generative grammar**. His theories on the "Language Acquisition Device" and universal grammar sparked the "cognitive revolution," influencing fields from computer science to psychology while challenging behaviorist theories.

**Political Stance and Activism**
Politically, Chomsky identifies as a **libertarian socialist**, opposing "elite corporatocracy" and advocating for direct worker control. His activism covers:
*   **U.S. Foreign Policy:** Criticizing American imperialism, including U.S. support for Ukraine, while condemning the Russian invasion.
*   **Economics:** Rejecting binary ownership models in favor of a spectrum between autocratic and democratic control.
*   **Israeli–Palestinian Conflict:** Transitioning from labor Zionism to anti-Zionism, criticizing settlements, the Gaza blockade, and U.S. support for Israeli actions.
*   **Media and Education:** Arguing in *Manufacturing Consent* that media reinforces state power and advocating for educational reform.

**Controversies and Challenges**
Despite global acclaim and numerous awards, Chomsky faces significant criticism, including accusations of antisemitism, Holocaust denial, and hostility toward the U.S. He has endured government surveillance by the CIA and personal threats. Notable controversies include backlash over defending Robert Faurisson's free speech, perceived cherry-picking of facts, and scrutiny regarding his past associations with Jeffrey Epstein. Despite these challenges, he remains committed to civil disobedience and intellectual integrity, supporting figures globally from East Timorese independence to the Occupy movement.

**Legacy and Recognition**
Chomsky's influence extends to the natural world and popular culture, with eponyms awarded to a bee species, a frog species, an asteroid, and a building complex at Jamia Millia Islamia university. Additionally, actors and musicians like Viggo Mortensen and Buckethead dedicated their 2003 album *Pandemonium from America* to him. Following a 2023 stroke and his retirement from MIT in 2002, Chomsky moved full-time to Brazil, continuing his lifelong dedication to social justice while maintaining a private, anti-fame lifestyle.
