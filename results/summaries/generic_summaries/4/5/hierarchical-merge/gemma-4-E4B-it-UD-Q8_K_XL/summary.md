# Expert Intellectuals and Polymaths

A range of influential thinkers shaped modern disciplines. John von Neumann was a multifaceted genius contributing to mathematics (Set Theory), economics (game theory), and computer science hardware development, while also playing a role in the Manhattan Project. Other foundational figures include Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz, who advanced calculus and proposed metaphysical concepts, and Bertrand Russell, a philosopher who championed humanitarianism and opposed imperialism.

Leading intellectuals like Noam Chomsky founded modern linguistics with generative grammar, while also serving as a political dissident criticizing U.S. foreign policy. Charles Sanders Peirce, an American polymath, developed Pragmatism—an epistemology based on practical effects—and formulated a comprehensive system including semiotics and modes of inference.

# Specialized Figures

Garry Kasparov was a highly successful and dynamic chess master, achieving World No. 1 status from 1984 until his retirement in 2005. Beyond his professional chess career, Kasparov became a prominent global political activist, known for criticizing authoritarian regimes, such as Vladimir Putin's administration.
