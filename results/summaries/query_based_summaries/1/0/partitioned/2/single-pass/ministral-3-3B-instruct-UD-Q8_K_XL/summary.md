**Ra.One** is a 2011 Indian superhero film directed by Anubhav Sinha and produced by Shah Rukh Khan under Red Chillies Entertainment. The film follows G.One, a video game character designed by Shekhar Subramaniam (played by Shah Rukh Khan), who gains sentience and enters the real world, facing off against Ra.One, a faceless, AI-driven supervillain with enhanced abilities. The story explores themes of technology, identity, and the blurred line between reality and virtual worlds.

The film's marketing was extensive, with a significant budget allocated for internet promotions, teaser trailers, and a multi-city tour by the cast. It was released in over 4,000 theaters worldwide, breaking box office records for the widest Hindi film release at the time. The visual effects were widely praised, contributing to the film’s technological and action-packed appeal.

**Reception and Legacy:**
Upon release, *Ra.One* received mixed reviews, with critics praising its visual effects and action sequences but criticizing its plot and character development. Despite this, it became a box office success, grossing over ₹2 billion worldwide. The film’s unique concept of a superhero emerging from a video game set it apart in Indian cinema and marked a significant step in Bollywood’s embrace of modern superhero narratives. Shah Rukh Khan’s performance as both G.One and Shekhar Subramaniam was particularly noted, and the film’s influence extended into discussions about technology and AI in cinema. The film also included a cameo appearance by Rajinikanth as Chitti from *Enthiran*, adding a touch of inter-cinematic crossover appeal.
