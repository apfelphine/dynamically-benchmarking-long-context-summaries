# Ra.One Summary

**Marketing and Franchise Development:**
The marketing for *Ra.One* involved a significant budget, with ₹100 million spent on promotion, including ₹150 million dedicated solely to internet promotions. Promotional activities included releasing posters in December 2010 and teaser trailers during the 2011 ICC Cricket World Cup. The film aimed to facilitate a franchise by utilizing merchandise and games; Red Chillies Entertainment partnered with Sony Computer Entertainment Europe to release "Ra.One – The Game" for PlayStation 2 and PlayStation 3 on October 5, 2011.

**Release and Reception:**
The Hindi version of *Ra.One* achieved a massive theatrical release in India across over 4,000 screens, breaking the record for the widest Hindi film release previously held by *Bodyguard* (2011). Overseas, the film was released in 904 prints across various territories. The film faced censorship objections regarding its action scenes but was ultimately cleared with a 'U' certificate, provided disclaimers were shown. Critically, *Ra.One* received mixed reviews, with Rotten Tomatoes showing a 61% approval rating. The film also generated controversy regarding plagiarism allegations and suffered cybertheft, including the leaking of a song before its official release.

**Production and Content Notes:**
The film's plot revolves around a video game where the protagonist, G.One (played by Shah Rukh Khan), battles the antagonist, Ra.One (played by Arjun Rampal), who is imbued with a self-learning AI. The film was notable for its extensive use of digital prints, reportedly making up 50–60% of the total release to lower costs. Rajinikanth made a cameo appearance in the film.
