## Ra.One Summary

Ra.One is a 2011 Indian superhero film prominently marketed with a ₹520 million budget, including ₹150 million spent on internet promotions, breaking records for extensive pre-release buzz and distribution. The film's innovative marketing featured merchandise, gaming tournaments, and a dedicated video game ("Ra.One – The Game") developed in collaboration with Sony and UTV Indiagames. Released simultaneously in Hindi, Tamil, and Telugu across thousands of screens worldwide, Ra.One set records for the widest release of a Hindi film, extensive use of digital prints, and a large-scale 3D rollout. Despite these efforts, pirated versions appeared online shortly after release.

Production of Ra.One involved Shah Rukh Khan as lead, with Kareena Kapoor as the female lead and Arjun Rampal cast as the antagonist. The film employed advanced visual effects with over 1,200 artists working for two and a half years, and utilized 3D conversion during post-production. Khan’s superhero suit and Rampal’s costume were specially designed and costly, presenting challenges during filming. The soundtrack was composed by Vishal–Shekhar with notable international collaborations. Ra.One received mixed critical reviews: while praised for its technological ambition, visual effects, and action sequences, critics found the plot inconsistent and lacking originality. Financially, Ra.One broke several box office records on opening but faced sharp declines thereafter. Plans for a sequel, tentatively titled "G.One," were discussed but met with mixed industry opinions and remained uncertain.
