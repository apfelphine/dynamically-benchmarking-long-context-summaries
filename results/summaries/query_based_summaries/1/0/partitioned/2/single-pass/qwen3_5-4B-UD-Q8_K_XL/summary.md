# Ra.One

**Ra.One** is a 2011 Hindi science fiction action film produced by Shah Rukh Khan under his banner, Red Chillies Entertainment, and directed by Anubhav Sinha. The narrative centers on a video game programmer, Shekhar Subramaniam, who creates a video game featuring a protagonist, G.One, and a powerful, faceless antagonist named Ra.One, which possesses self-learning AI and shapeshifting abilities. Upon the game's launch, Ra.One gains sentience, breaches the digital barrier into the real world, and pursues the player characters. The film follows the journey of Shekhar, his son Prateek, and his wife Sonia as they attempt to restore G.One to life to defeat Ra.One, ultimately leading to a climactic battle where G.One absorbs the villain's power and defeats him. The story is notable for its extensive use of visual effects, heavy reliance on digital prints for its theatrical release, and its marketing strategy which included merchandise, video games, and a significant online presence.

Despite its ambitious production, the film faced significant controversies, including plagiarism allegations regarding its similarities to various superhero movies and hacking incidents targeting its official website. Censorship issues also arose, with the Central Board of Film Certification imposing conditions regarding the film's train-based stunts due to fears of imitation by children. While the film achieved record-breaking pre-release buzz and set several box office records for a Hindi film upon its release, it received mixed critical reviews domestically and was later panned for its "Bollywoodization" of the superhero genre. Despite these challenges, Ra.One remains a landmark project in Indian cinema, highlighting the industry's rapid integration of advanced technology and its attempt to create a high-concept franchise.
