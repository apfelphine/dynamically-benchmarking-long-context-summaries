## Ra.One: Key Information

**Production and Concept**  
Ra.One is a Hindi-language science fiction film centered on a video game designer who creates a digital villain, Ra.One, imbued with advanced artificial intelligence and greater powers than the game's hero, G.One (also played by Shah Rukh Khan). The film’s concept grew from director Anubhav Sinha’s fascination with remote-controlled humans; it evolved into a superhero story with a mythological undertone, reflecting aspects of the Ramayana. Ra.One explores themes of technology intersecting with real life and the relationship between good and evil, with Khan aiming to create an iconic Indian superhero.

**Cast, Filming, and Technical Groundbreaking**  
Shah Rukh Khan headlines as both Shekhar/G.One, while Arjun Rampal portrays Ra.One, alongside Kareena Kapoor and other supporting actors. Filming occurred in India and London, involving an international crew. Ra.One employed extensive visual effects—over 1,200 artists worked on them, making the film notable for pioneering CGI and 3D in Indian cinema. The superhero suits were custom-designed, and the post-production phase faced delays due to the complexity of effects and conversion to 3D.

---

**Release, Marketing, and Reception**  
Ra.One was promoted with an unprecedented budget and strategy, including internet campaigns, merchandise, and even a PlayStation game. It had a record-breaking wide release in multiple languages and formats, including 3D. The film earned over ₹2 billion ($45 million) worldwide and set new benchmarks for pre-release business and marketing.

Critical responses were mixed in India and generally positive overseas. Ra.One was celebrated for its ambitious visual effects and technical innovation but received criticism for its screenplay and character development. It won major technical awards (including National and Filmfare Awards for Best Special Effects) and generated sequel speculation. However, plans for a direct sequel remain uncertain, with Khan stating it would require significant pre-production and technological investment.

**Controversies and Legacy**  
Ra.One faced several controversies, including allegations of plagiarism regarding its concept and criticism over cultural representation. Technical challenges such as hacking and the piracy of its soundtrack preceded release. Despite mixed opinions, Ra.One is recognized for elevating visual standards in Bollywood and contributing to discussions about Indian superhero cinema.
