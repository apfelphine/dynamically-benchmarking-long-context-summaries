**Ra.One** is a 2011 Indian science fiction action film directed by Anubhav Sinha and starring Shah Rukh Khan in a dual role as the protagonist G.One and the antagonist Ra.One. The film, produced by Red Chillies Entertainment, is notable for its ambitious use of visual effects, 3D technology, and a unique gaming-inspired narrative where digital characters enter the real world. The story follows a video game developer who creates a game featuring a powerful villain, Ra.One, which gains self-awareness and escapes into reality, leading to a series of high-stakes action sequences involving stunts, chases, and battles between the protagonist and antagonist. The film's production involved extensive use of CGI, special effects, and a large-scale marketing campaign, including a significant budget and a wide release across India and internationally. Despite receiving mixed critical reviews in India, the film was generally well-received overseas and achieved commercial success, becoming one of the highest-grossing Hindi films of its time. It also sparked discussions about the potential for a sequel, though no official sequel has been released to date. The film is recognized for its technological achievements and has been cited as a milestone in Indian cinema's use of digital effects.
