**Ra.One** is a 2011 Bollywood action film directed by Anubhav Sinha and produced by Shah Rukh Khan under Red Chillies Entertainment. The film follows the story of **G.One**, a superhero created from a video game, who battles the faceless villain **Ra.One**, a more powerful antagonist with advanced AI capabilities. The plot revolves around a family in India whose son, Prateek, accidentally unleashes Ra.One from the digital world into reality, forcing G.One to protect him and his mother from the villain's relentless pursuit.

The film was a technological and commercial success, blending Bollywood action with a futuristic gaming theme. Shah Rukh Khan played dual roles as the protagonist, G.One, and the game's creator, Shekhar Subramaniam. Arjun Rampal portrayed Ra.One, while Kareena Kapoor and Armaan Verma played key supporting roles. The film featured groundbreaking visual effects, including the first-ever 3D superhero film in Bollywood, and was widely praised for its action sequences and Khan’s performance.

**Ra.One** was a box-office hit, earning over ₹2 billion worldwide and breaking multiple records for the widest release of a Hindi film. It received mixed reviews from critics but was celebrated for its innovative approach to superhero storytelling in Indian cinema. The film also sparked discussions about potential sequels, though Khan has expressed cautious optimism about its future.
