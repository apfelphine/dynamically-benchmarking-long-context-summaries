# Ra.One

**Ra.One** is a 2011 Indian superhero film that garnered significant attention for being titled after the antagonist rather than the protagonist, highlighting the growing importance of villains in Bollywood. Directed by Anubhav Sinha and produced under Shah Rukh Khan's Red Chillies Entertainment, the film revolves around a video game where the villain Ra.One possesses greater powers and self-learning AI. Shah Rukh Khan plays a dual role as Shekhar Subramaniam, the game programmer, and the superhero G.One, who comes to life to protect his family from Ra.One's threats. The character Ra.One is faceless and endowed with immense shapeshifting and mind-reading capabilities, posing a formidable challenge to G.One.

The film's production involved extensive marketing and innovative promotions, including internet campaigns, merchandise, and gaming tie-ins such as "Ra.One – The Game" for PlayStation consoles and a social game with an independent plot. Ra.One released on over 4,000 screens worldwide, setting records for the widest release of a Hindi film and employing a significant portion of digital prints. Despite mixed critical reviews—praising its ambitious technology and visual effects while critiquing the story and direction—Ra.One achieved notable box office success, breaking Diwali opening records and earning substantial revenues domestically and overseas. The film received several awards, notably for its technical excellence in special effects, and generated discussions about a potential sequel, though its development status remains uncertain.
