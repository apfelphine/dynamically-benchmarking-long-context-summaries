## Ra.One

**Ra.One** is a 2011 Bollywood superhero film centered on a technologically advanced video game brought to life. The plot follows Shekhar, a game designer at Barron Industries, who creates a game where the antagonist, Ra.One, possesses superior powers and self-learning AI. During gameplay, Ra.One escapes the digital world into reality, threatening Shekhar's family. Shekhar's son Prateek and the protagonist program G.One engage in a climactic battle with the villain. Notably, the film features Shah Rukh Khan in a dual role as Shekhar and G.One, with Arjun Rampal portraying Ra.One.

The production of Ra.One involved cutting-edge technology, blending Indian and international crews, and a significant budget, making it one of the most expensive Bollywood films to date. The film's title highlighted the importance of the villain, a departure from traditional norms. Extensive marketing efforts, including merchandise, gaming tie-ins, and social media engagement, preempted its release. Ra.One broke box office records during its Diwali opening but experienced steep declines afterward. Critics had mixed reactions, praising the visual effects and action while critiquing the story and character development. The film won several technical awards, including the National Film Award for Best Special Effects. Discussions of a sequel titled *G.One* surfaced, with plans to expand the universe, though the sequel's production status remains uncertain.
