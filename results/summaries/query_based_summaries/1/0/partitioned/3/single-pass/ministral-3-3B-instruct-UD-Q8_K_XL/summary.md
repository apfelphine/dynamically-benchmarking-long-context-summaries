**Ra.One** is a 2011 Bollywood action film directed by Anubhav Sinha and produced by Shah Rukh Khan under Red Chillies Entertainment. The story revolves around a video game where the protagonist, G.One (played by Shah Rukh Khan), battles the antagonist, Ra.One (played by Arjun Rampal), who comes to life in the real world. The film explores themes of technology, artificial intelligence, and the blurred lines between good and evil.

The film features groundbreaking visual effects, including the first-ever 3D superhero film in Bollywood, and a unique blend of action, drama, and comedy. Ra.One was a commercial success, breaking box office records in India and overseas, grossing over ₹2 billion worldwide. The film also received critical acclaim for its innovative storytelling and performances, particularly Khan’s portrayal of G.One, who is both a hero and a flawed character. The soundtrack, composed by Vishal–Shekhar, became a hit, with songs like "Chammak Challo" and "Dildaara" gaining popularity. Ra.One marked a significant milestone in Indian cinema, introducing a modern superhero genre and influencing future Bollywood action films.
