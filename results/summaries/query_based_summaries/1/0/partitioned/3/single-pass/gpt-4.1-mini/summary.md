# Ra.One

**Plot:**  
Ra.One centers around Shekhar Subramaniam, a video game programmer at Barron Industries, who creates an innovative game featuring a powerful antagonist, Ra.One, and the protagonist, G.One—his own digital avatar. When Ra.One becomes self-aware and escapes into the real world, it begins hunting Shekhar's son, Prateek (alias Lucifer). G.One comes to life to protect Prateek and his family, leading to high-stakes battles across digital and real realms. The story follows their fight to defeat Ra.One, culminating in a final confrontation where Prateek and G.One use wit and strength to destroy the villain.

**Cast:**  
- Shah Rukh Khan in a dual role as Shekhar and G.One  
- Arjun Rampal as Ra.One  
- Kareena Kapoor as Sonia Subramaniam  
- Armaan Verma as Prateek Subramaniam/Lucifer  
- Supporting cast includes Shahana Goswami, Tom Wu, Dalip Tahil, and others. Rajinikanth reprised his role as Chitti (from *Enthiran*) in a special appearance.

**Production:**  
The film marked a major Bollywood techno-action project, with Shah Rukh Khan and director Anubhav Sinha collaborating after Khan’s childhood dream of starring as a superhero. The project was inspired by a 2005 TV ad about remotely controlled humans. Sinha and Khan worked extensively on story development, studying around 200 superhero films for inspiration. The title "Ra.One", referencing a powerful villain akin to the mythological "Raavan", was deliberately chosen to emphasize the antagonist. The cast underwent significant physical preparation, and production involved a mix of Indian and international crews, with filming in Mumbai, London, and studios in India and the UK. Special effects involved over 1,200 artists working for two and a half years, with extensive CGI, 3D conversion, and sound design to bridge real and virtual worlds.

**Marketing & Release:**  
Ra.One had a ₹520 million marketing budget, heavily invested in Internet promotions, merchandise, and interactive games. It premiered internationally with high-profile events and broke distribution records in India, releasing on over 4,000 screens worldwide with significant digital releases to combat piracy. Video games and digital comics expanded the franchise's universe. Despite careful marketing and technical advances, the film faced controversies including plagiarism allegations, cyberattacks on its website, and concerns over dangerous stunt imitations.

**Reception:**  
Ra.One had a record-breaking wide release and set pre-sale records. However, it experienced significant drops in box office collections shortly after release. The film received mixed reviews, with praise for its ambition, visual effects, and action sequences, while criticism was directed at its plot and character development. It won several awards mainly for technical achievements such as special effects and marketing.

**Legacy and Sequel:**  
Plans for a sequel titled *G.One* were discussed but delayed due to mixed reactions and Khan's commitments. There was divided opinion within the industry about continuing the series. Nonetheless, Khan expressed interest in exploring the film’s universe further.
