Ra.One faced controversies, including criticism from South Indian audiences regarding certain sequences, the character's use of "ayyo" and "rascal," and perceived inaccuracies in Tamil dialogue, alongside Kareena Kapoor's use of the phrase "condom condom."

Financially, the film was a success, earning a worldwide total of ₹2.081 billion ($45 million). It broke records upon its release in India, setting records for Diwali opening day, biggest single-day net revenue, and biggest three-day opening weekend for a Hindi film, though it later saw significant drops in collections. Critically, Ra.One received mixed reviews; Rotten Tomatoes reported a 61% approval rating, while positive reviews praised its ambitious nature and visual effects, although many criticized the plot's originality and the emotional scenes. The film received numerous accolades, including the National Film Award and the Filmfare Award for Best Special Effects.
