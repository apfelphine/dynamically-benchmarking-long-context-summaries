# Ra.One

**Ra.One** (2011) is a Hindi science fiction action film directed by Anubhav Sinha and starring Shah Rukh Khan in a dual role as the protagonist G.One and the villain Ra.One. The story follows a video game programmer who creates a superhero game that comes to life and goes on a rampage. The film was a commercial success, debuting at the Diwali weekend to set records for single-day net revenue and three-day opening weekend earnings for a Hindi film, with worldwide collections exceeding ₹2.08 billion. However, it received mixed critical reviews in India, particularly for its plot and pacing, though it was praised for its visual effects and action sequences. The film faced significant controversies, including allegations of plagiarism against screenwriter Yash Patnaik and backlash from South Indian audiences regarding cultural inaccuracies and offensive dubbed lines. Despite these issues, it won the National Film Award for Best Sound and the Filmfare Award for Best Special Effects.

Following the film's success, Shah Rukh Khan expressed interest in a sequel, tentatively titled *G.One*, but later stated that making another superhero film was unlikely due to his commitments and the high cost of production. While reports suggested pre-production for a sequel began around 2012, the project never materialized, and no official sequel has been produced since. The film also spawned a video game, *Ra.One – The Game*, and extensive merchandise, contributing to its status as one of the most expensive and technically ambitious Bollywood films of its time.
