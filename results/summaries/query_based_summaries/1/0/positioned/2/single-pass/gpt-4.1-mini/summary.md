**Ra.One** is a 2011 Indian superhero science fiction film directed by Anubhav Sinha and produced by Shah Rukh Khan under Red Chillies Entertainment. The story centers on Shekhar Subramaniam, a game designer who creates a highly advanced video game featuring a powerful antagonist, Ra.One, and the protagonist G.One. When Ra.One becomes self-aware and enters the real world, it pursues Shekhar’s son Prateek, compelling G.One to protect him. The film explores themes of artificial intelligence, self-awareness, and the battle between good and evil within the family context.

The film features Shah Rukh Khan in a dual role as Shekhar and G.One, with Arjun Rampal as Ra.One. The production involved extensive visual effects, with over 1,200 artists working for two and a half years, and showcased advanced technology including 3D conversion. Costumes were designed by Robert Kurtzman and Tim Flattery, with Khan’s suit requiring a complex molding process. Ra.One was notable for its marketing campaign, including a major merchandising push and promotional video games. Upon release, it set box office records in India, though experienced sharp declines after opening weekend, and received mixed critical reviews, with praise for its ambition and technical achievements but criticism for its story. A potential sequel titled *G.One* was discussed but no definitive production date was set.
