**Ra.One** is a 2011 Indian science fiction action film directed by Anubhav Sinha and starring Shah Rukh Khan in a dual role as a video game programmer, Shekhar Subramaniam, and his protagonist, G.One. The film centers on a futuristic game called Ra.One, designed to allow digital entities to enter the real world. The game's antagonist, Ra.One, is a powerful, self-learning AI with shapeshifting and mind-reading abilities, initially faceless and more powerful than the protagonist. After Ra.One escapes into the real world, it causes chaos, leading to a series of confrontations with the protagonist and his family. The film explores themes of technology, father-son relationships, and the potential dangers of artificial intelligence. It features groundbreaking visual effects, a unique narrative structure, and a blend of action and emotional storytelling. The film was a commercial success, becoming one of the most expensive Bollywood films at the time, and received mixed to positive critical reception, praised for its technical achievements and visual spectacle, though criticized for its plot and character development. It also inspired a potential sequel, with plans for a new film titled G.One.
