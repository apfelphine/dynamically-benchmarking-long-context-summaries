**Ra.One** is a 2011 Indian sci-fi superhero film centered around a revolutionary video game developed by Barron Industries in London. The story follows Shekhar Subramaniam, a programmer who creates a game with a powerful antagonist, Ra.One, imbued with self-learning AI capable of entering the real world through digital transmissions. After Ra.One escapes into reality and threatens Shekhar’s son Prateek (playing as Lucifer in the game), Shekhar’s avatar, the superhero G.One, also comes to life to protect Prateek. The film culminates with G.One defeating Ra.One after a series of battles that blend digital and real-world action, ultimately restoring peace and bridging the digital with the real.

Directed by Anubhav Sinha and produced by Shah Rukh Khan’s Red Chillies Entertainment, **Ra.One** employed cutting-edge visual effects and extensive 3-D technology, marking a high-profile Indian entry into the superhero genre. Its cast includes Shah Rukh Khan in dual roles as Shekhar and G.One, Arjun Rampal as Ra.One, and Kareena Kapoor as Sonia Subramaniam. Rajinikanth made a special appearance reprising his role as Chitti from *Enthiran*. The film received mixed to positive reviews, praised for its ambitious visuals and action sequences but critiqued for its story and character development. It set new box office records in India and internationally, particularly for a Hindi film’s 3-D and widescreen release, and garnered numerous awards for its technical achievements. A video game and extensive merchandising accompanied its release, positioning **Ra.One** as a notable attempt to establish a franchise, with plans for a sequel discussed though not yet realized.
