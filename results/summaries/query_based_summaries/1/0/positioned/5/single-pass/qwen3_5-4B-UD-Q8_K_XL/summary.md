**Ra.One** is a 2011 Indian science fiction action film directed by Anubhav Sinha and starring Shah Rukh Khan in a dual role as video game programmer Shekhar Subramaniam and the superhero character G.One. The film centers on a video game created by Shekhar and his son, Prateek, where the antagonist, Ra.One, is an artificial intelligence with powers such as shapeshifting and mind-reading. When Ra.One becomes self-aware and breaches the barrier between the digital and physical worlds, he begins to hunt the avatar of Prateek's in-game character, Lucifer. After killing Shekhar and infiltrating reality, Ra.One kidnaps Prateek and threatens G.One's mother, Sonia, forcing the digital hero to enter the real world to save them. The climax involves G.One and Prateek defeating the ten-fold Ra.One by using a hint from the game's design, allowing G.One to return to the digital realm. Rajinikanth makes a cameo appearance as the character Chitti from the film *Enthiran*, and the movie was noted for its extensive use of 3D technology, massive CGI effects, and high production costs, though it faced controversies regarding plagiarism and cultural representation.

The film is part of a broader landscape of science fiction media exploring the intersection of artificial intelligence and humanity, alongside other notable titles such as *The Terminator*, *WarGames*, *Her*, and *The Creator*. These works, ranging from 1980s classics to modern blockbusters, often feature themes of rogue AI threatening global stability, human-machine relationships, and the potential consequences of advanced technology. While *Ra.One* focuses on a family drama intertwined with a superhero narrative, others like *Atlas* and *The Creator* delve deeper into dystopian futures and the philosophical implications of AI sentience. Collectively, these films highlight the enduring fascination with artificial intelligence as a narrative device, utilizing visual spectacle and complex storylines to engage audiences with speculative science fiction concepts.
