**Ra.One** is a 2011 Indian superhero sci-fi film centered on a video game programmer, Shekhar Subramaniam, who creates a game featuring a powerful antagonist named Ra.One and a protagonist, G.One, modeled after himself. The self-aware villain Ra.One escapes into the real world via new wireless technology, causing havoc and threatening Shekhar’s family. Shekhar is killed by Ra.One while trying to save his son, Prateek ("Lucifer"), who then teams up with the game’s hero G.One to defeat Ra.One. The film features action sequences blending the digital and real worlds, with themes of good versus evil and familial bonds.  

Produced by Shah Rukh Khan’s Red Chillies Entertainment and directed by Anubhav Sinha, *Ra.One* was noted for its extensive visual effects, 3D conversion, and high production costs, making it one of the most expensive Indian films at the time. The casting included Shah Rukh Khan in dual roles, Arjun Rampal as Ra.One, Kareena Kapoor, and others, with significant preparation required for stunts and costumes. Despite record-breaking openings and wide international release, the film received mixed reviews, praised for its technical achievements but critiqued for its plot and originality. Plans for a sequel titled *G.One* were discussed, although its development status remains uncertain.
