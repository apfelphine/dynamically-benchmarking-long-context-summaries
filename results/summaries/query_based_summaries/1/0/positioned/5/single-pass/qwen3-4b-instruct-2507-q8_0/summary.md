**Ra.One** is a 2011 Indian science fiction action film that follows the story of a video game titled *Ra.One*, created by a video game programmer named Shekhar Subramaniam. The game features two main characters: G.One, a heroic protagonist with a human-like face, and Ra.One, a powerful antagonist with a faceless, self-learning artificial intelligence (AI). The game's AI, Ra.One, is designed to have greater powers than G.One, including shapeshifting and mind-reading abilities. 

The game is launched to public acclaim, but after a malfunction, Ra.One escapes into the real world and begins to exhibit self-awareness. The film explores the consequences of this event as Ra.One seeks to destroy G.One and take control of the world. The narrative follows the efforts of Shekhar and his family to stop Ra.One, who has become a threat to humanity. In the climax, G.One and Ra.One engage in a battle, with G.One ultimately defeating Ra.One and restoring balance. The film ends with a message about the importance of human values and the dangers of unchecked technological advancement.
