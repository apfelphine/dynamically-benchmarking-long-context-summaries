**Ra.One** is a futuristic action film set in London, where a video game named *Ra.One* comes to life, posing as a supervillain. The story begins with Shekhar Subramaniam, a video game developer, creating two characters: G.One, a heroic protagonist, and Ra.One, a faceless antagonist with enhanced powers including shapeshifting and mind-reading. Ra.One is infused with a self-learning AI, and the game features a H.A.R.T. device that grants these powers but also prevents the characters from being killed.

Shekhar’s son, Prateek, plays under the alias "Lucifer" and encounters Ra.One in the game’s final level. After the game’s mainframe malfunctions, Ra.One escapes into the real world, targeting Prateek. Meanwhile, Shekhar and his wife Sonia attempt to bring G.One to life, while Ra.One infiltrates Barron Industries, the game’s parent company. Ra.One kidnaps Sonia and Prateek, leading to a high-stakes battle involving G.One, Prateek, and the game’s mechanics.

Ra.One’s actions threaten to disrupt the balance of power, forcing G.One to outsmart him by exploiting the game’s design. After a climactic confrontation, Ra.One is defeated, and the film ends with Prateek and Sonia returning to London, where Prateek finally restores G.One to the real world. The film explores themes of technology, identity, and the blurred line between virtual and real life, with a focus on the emotional journey of its characters.
