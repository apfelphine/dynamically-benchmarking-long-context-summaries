**Ra.One** is a 2011 Indian superhero film centered on a UK-based video game company, Barron Industries, which develops an advanced game involving a protagonist, G.One, and an antagonist, Ra.One, the latter empowered by self-learning artificial intelligence with abilities like shapeshifting and mind reading. The film’s plot follows Ra.One breaking loose from the digital world to the real world, threatening the protagonist’s family, leading to a confrontation involving high-tech battles and the quest to restore G.One’s power to defeat Ra.One. The story emphasizes themes of good versus evil and integrates family dynamics, particularly the father-son relationship.

The film stars Shah Rukh Khan in dual roles as Shekhar Subramaniam (the game’s creator) and G.One, with Arjun Rampal as the villain Ra.One, Kareena Kapoor as Shekhar’s wife Sonia, and Armaan Verma as their son Prateek. Rajinikanth makes a special cameo as Chitti from *Enthiran* via motion capture. Directed by Anubhav Sinha and produced by Khan’s Red Chillies Entertainment, *Ra.One* was notable for its extensive use of digital effects, requiring 1,200 artists working over 2.5 years, and a significant marketing campaign including merchandise and a video game. Released on over 4,000 screens worldwide in multiple languages, it set records for the widest Hindi film release and Diwali opening day but faced mixed critical reviews and box office drops after opening. It received acclaim for its visual effects and action sequences, won several technical awards including National Film Award and Filmfare Award for Best Special Effects, and spawned plans for a sequel, although progress has been uncertain.
