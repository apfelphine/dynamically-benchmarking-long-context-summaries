# **Ra.One**

*Ra.One* (2011) is an Indian science fiction superhero film directed by Anubhav Sinha and starring Shah Rukh Khan in a dual role as the video game programmer Shekhar Subramaniam and the protagonist, G.One. The plot follows G.One, a digital superhero character with the ability to shapeshift and read minds, who breaks free from the digital world into reality after a malfunction in his game causes the villain, Ra.One, to enter the physical world. Ra.One, a faceless supervillain with greater powers than G.One, begins a deadly pursuit of the human characters, leading to the deaths of key figures and forcing G.One to fight for survival. The film explores themes of technology, identity, and the conflict between digital and real worlds, featuring extensive visual effects and action sequences.

The movie was a commercial and critical success, grossing over ₹2 billion worldwide and setting several box office records for Indian films, including the widest release for a Hindi film. It received praise for its groundbreaking visual effects, which were created in partnership with global studios, and its ambitious scope. However, it also faced controversy regarding plagiarism allegations and cultural insensitivity toward South Indian audiences. *Ra.One* was noted for its innovative marketing strategy, which included extensive merchandise, video games, and digital promotions, and it paved the way for future Indian superhero films by demonstrating the commercial viability of high-tech Bollywood productions.
