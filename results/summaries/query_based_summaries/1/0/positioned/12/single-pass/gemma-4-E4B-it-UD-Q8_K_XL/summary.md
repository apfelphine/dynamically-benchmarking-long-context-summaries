**Ra.One** features a storyline set in London where Jenny Nair, an employee of Barron Industries, introduces technology allowing digital world elements to enter the real world via wireless transmissions. Shekhar Subramaniam, a programmer, creates a unique video game where the antagonist, **Ra.One**, is a faceless character with superior powers, including shapeshifting and mind reading, and is imbued with a self-learning AI.

When the game launches, the self-aware **Ra.One** breaks free into the real world, leading to conflict with the protagonist, G.One (played by Shah Rukh Khan). The plot involves **Ra.One** kidnapping Prateek Subramaniam and Sonia Subramaniam, but G.One eventually defeats him. In the climax, G.One manages to destroy the real **Ra.One** after learning that only one of its ten copies has a shadow, allowing G.One to absorb **Ra.One**'s remains and return to the digital world.
