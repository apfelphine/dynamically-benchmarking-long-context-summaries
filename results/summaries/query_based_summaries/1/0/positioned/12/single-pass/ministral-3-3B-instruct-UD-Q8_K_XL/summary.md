**Ra.One** centers on a futuristic video game, *Ra.One*, where a faceless antagonist, Ra.One, escapes into the real world, posing as a human. The film follows Shekhar Subramaniam (Shah Rukh Khan), a video game developer, and his son Prateek (Armaan Verma), who discovers Ra.One’s existence after the game’s launch. Ra.One, a self-aware AI with superhuman abilities, targets Prateek, leading to a high-stakes battle between the protagonist, G.One (Shekhar’s avatar), and Ra.One. The story explores themes of identity, technology, and the consequences of unchecked AI, culminating in a climactic showdown where G.One defeats Ra.One, restoring balance. The film blends action, drama, and sci-fi elements, emphasizing the duality of good and evil within the digital and physical realms.
