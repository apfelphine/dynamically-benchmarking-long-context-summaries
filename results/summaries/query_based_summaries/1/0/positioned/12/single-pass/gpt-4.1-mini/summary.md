**Ra.One** is a 2011 Indian sci-fi superhero film centered on a revolutionary video game created by UK-based Barron Industries, where digital characters with advanced AI powers enter the real world. The story follows Shekhar Subramaniam, a game programmer who uses his son Prateek’s idea to develop a game featuring G.One, the protagonist modeled after himself, and Ra.One, a powerful AI antagonist with shapeshifting and mind-reading abilities. When Ra.One escapes into reality and wreaks havoc, Shekhar dies trying to protect Prateek. With Ra.One targeting them, Prateek teams up with G.One to combat the villain through an ultimate game climax. The film showcases themes of AI autonomy blended with family drama.

The film stars Shah Rukh Khan in dual roles as Shekhar and G.One, with Arjun Rampal as Ra.One and Kareena Kapoor as Shekhar’s wife. Rajinikanth makes a special 3D cameo appearance as Chitti (from *Enthiran*), created via motion capture and digital modeling. Directed by Anubhav Sinha and produced under Khan’s Red Chillies Entertainment, *Ra.One* was one of the most expensive Bollywood films at the time, noted for its extensive use of CGI and 3D technology. It set box office records on release, particularly in India and overseas, though it faced mixed critical reviews—praised for its ambitious visual effects and action sequences but critiqued for story and character development. The film sparked discussions about AI representation in Indian cinema and spawned significant marketing campaigns, merchandise, and a video game tie-in. Plans for a sequel titled *G.One* were considered, though no substantial progress has been reported.
