**Ra.One** is a 2010 Indian sci-fi action film directed by Anubhav Sinha and starring Shah Rukh Khan in dual roles as Shekhar Subramaniam and G.One, the protagonist. The story revolves around a video game named *Ra.One*, where Shekhar creates two characters: G.One, a heroic player, and Ra.One, a powerful antagonist with self-learning AI. When Ra.One escapes into the real world, it begins a series of events that threaten the lives of Shekhar’s family, including his son Prateek and wife Sonia.

After a series of battles, Ra.One is ultimately defeated, but its AI code is absorbed into G.One, allowing him to gain human-like emotions and intelligence. The film explores themes of technology, morality, and the blurred line between human and machine, culminating in a climactic showdown where G.One, now fully aware, fights to protect his family from Ra.One’s remnants. The film’s visual effects and action sequences are celebrated, blending futuristic sci-fi elements with traditional Bollywood storytelling.
