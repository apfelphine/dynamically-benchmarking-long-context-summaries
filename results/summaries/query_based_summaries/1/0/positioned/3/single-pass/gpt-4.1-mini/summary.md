# Summary of Ra.One

**Ra.One** is a 2011 Indian science fiction superhero film set in London, centering on the impact of a self-aware antagonist, Ra.One, emerging from a digitally connected video game into reality. The plot follows Shekhar Subramaniam, a video game programmer who creates a game where the antagonist, Ra.One, is intentionally more powerful than the protagonist, G.One. Due to glitches, Ra.One escapes into the real world, leading to a conflict where G.One protects Shekhar's son Prateek (alias Lucifer) and family from Ra.One, culminating in a battle blending virtual and real realms.

The film starred Shah Rukh Khan in dual roles (Shekhar and G.One), Arjun Rampal as Ra.One, Kareena Kapoor as Sonia, and Armaan Verma as Prateek. Its production involved extensive visual effects and 3D conversion, with a significant budget making it one of India's most expensive films then. The film's marketing campaign was extensive, including merchandise and gaming tie-ins, and it released worldwide in multiple languages across thousands of screens, setting records for Bollywood releases.

Despite setting box office records initially in India and overseas, **Ra.One** experienced sharp declines in subsequent weeks and received mixed critical reviews. While its visual effects and action sequences garnered praise, the story, character development, and originality attracted criticism. The film faced controversies including plagiarism allegations, cyberattacks on its website, and cultural representation criticisms. It won several awards for its technical achievements, such as special effects, and maintains interest for a potential sequel, although no definitive plans have been confirmed.
