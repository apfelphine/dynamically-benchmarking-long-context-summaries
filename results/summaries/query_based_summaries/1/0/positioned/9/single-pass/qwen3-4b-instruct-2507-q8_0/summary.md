**Ra.One** is a 2011 Indian science fiction action film directed by Anubhav Sinha and produced by Red Chillies Entertainment. The story centers on a video game developed by a programmer, Shekhar Subramaniam, which features two characters: G.One, a heroic protagonist, and Ra.One, a powerful antagonist with self-learning AI and shapeshifting abilities. When the game is launched, Ra.One enters the real world, causing chaos and threatening the protagonist, G.One, who is later brought to life through the game. The film explores themes of technology, father-son relationships, and the dangers of unchecked artificial intelligence. Ra.One features groundbreaking visual effects and a high-budget production, making it one of the most expensive Indian films ever made. It received mixed to positive critical reviews and became a commercial success, grossing over ₹2 billion worldwide. The film's success led to discussions about a potential sequel, though no official sequel has been released as of the provided text.
