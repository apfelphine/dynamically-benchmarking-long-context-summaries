Ra.One features a futuristic technology in London that allows digital elements to cross into the real world via wireless transmissions. The core conflict revolves around G.One, the protagonist, and Ra.One, the antagonist, who possesses substantially greater powers, including shapeshifting and mind reading, and is imbibed with a self-learning AI. Ra.One initially emerges from a video game, leading to the murder of Shekhar Subramaniam and his son, Prateek Subramaniam.

G.One eventually defeats Ra.One by tricking him into shooting G.One without his H.A.R.T., which leaves Ra.One helpless. After absorbing Ra.One's remains, G.One returns to the real world, and Prateek manages to restore G.One. The film was critically praised for its visual effects, though some reviews found the plot and emotional scenes lacking depth.
