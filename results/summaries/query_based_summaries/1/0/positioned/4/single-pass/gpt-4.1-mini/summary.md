**Ra.One** is a 2011 Indian superhero film centered around a cutting-edge video game technology developed by Barron Industries, where digital characters can enter the real world. The protagonist, Shekhar Subramaniam (played by Shah Rukh Khan), designs a game with a powerful antagonist called Ra.One, who becomes self-aware and escapes into reality. The story follows Shekhar's son, Prateek, who controls the game's hero G.One to battle Ra.One and protect his family. The film explores themes of good versus evil, artificial intelligence, and familial bonds, especially focusing on the father-son relationship.

Produced by Shah Rukh Khan's Red Chillies Entertainment and directed by Anubhav Sinha, *Ra.One* was notable for its extensive use of visual effects, 3-D conversion, and superhero costume design, involving both Indian and international teams. The film broke several box office records in India and overseas, particularly in terms of wide release and pre-release revenue. Critical reception was mixed, with praise for its ambitious technology and visual effects but criticism for its story and character development. A video game and extensive marketing campaigns supported the film. Rajinikanth made a special motion-capture cameo as Chitti from *Enthiran*. Though a sequel was discussed, it remained uncertain due to Khan's other commitments and mixed industry opinions.
