## Ra.One

**Ra.One** is a 2011 Indian sci-fi action film directed by Anubhav Sinha and produced by Shah Rukh Khan's Red Chillies Entertainment. Set in London and India, the plot follows Shekhar Subramaniam, a game designer who creates a video game featuring an antagonist, Ra.One, that is more powerful than its heroic counterpart, G.One. Using advanced technology, Ra.One gains self-awareness and escapes into the real world, seeking Prateek, Shekhar’s son (alias Lucifer), whom he battled in the game. In turn, G.One is also brought to life to protect Prateek and his mother Sonia from Ra.One’s relentless pursuit. The story unfolds through a series of action set pieces, including train rescues, digital world battles, and confrontations that blur the lines between gaming and reality, culminating in Ra.One’s defeat and G.One’s return to the digital realm—before he is restored by Prateek months later.

Ra.One was notable for its extensive use of CGI and 3D conversion, ambitious marketing, and a record-breaking wide release across India and multiple international markets. The film starred Shah Rukh Khan (dual role as Shekhar/G.One), Arjun Rampal (Ra.One), Kareena Kapoor, and featured Rajinikanth in a cameo as Chitti. The music was composed by Vishal–Shekhar with contributions from Akon and the Prague Philharmonic Orchestra, and the soundtrack itself was also a major promotional asset. Despite initial box office records and significant pre-sale revenue, the film suffered steep drop-offs and received mixed critical reactions, praised mainly for its technical achievements and criticized for its screenplay and originality. Ra.One won several awards for special effects and remained a landmark in Indian superhero cinema, sparking recurring rumors of a potential sequel. The film also faced plagiarism allegations, cybertheft controversies, and criticism over South Indian representation.
