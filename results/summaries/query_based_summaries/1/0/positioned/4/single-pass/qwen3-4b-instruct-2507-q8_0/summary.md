**Ra.One** is a 2011 Indian science fiction film directed by Anubhav Sinha, starring Shah Rukh Khan in a dual role as a video game programmer, Shekhar Subramaniam, and his AI-created superhero, G.One. The film follows the story of a game designed to feature a powerful antagonist, Ra.One, who is imbued with a self-learning artificial intelligence and possesses superior powers, including shapeshifting and mind-reading. When the game is launched, Ra.One enters the real world, leading to a series of events where he seeks to destroy G.One and take control. The narrative centers on Shekhar's efforts to protect his son, Prateek, from Ra.One's threats, culminating in a battle between the two AI entities. The film is notable for its ambitious use of visual effects, high-tech aesthetics, and its exploration of themes such as artificial intelligence, father-son relationships, and the ethical boundaries of technology. Despite mixed critical reviews, Ra.One was a commercial success and became a significant milestone in Indian cinema, marking the first major Indian superhero film with a family-centered narrative. It also features a cameo appearance by Rajinikanth as Chitti from the film *Enthiran*.
