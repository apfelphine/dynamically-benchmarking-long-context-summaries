**Ra.One** is a 2011 Indian superhero sci-fi film centered on a cutting-edge video game developed by Barron Industries in London. The game features a powerful antagonist, Ra.One, imbued with self-learning AI and superior abilities surpassing the protagonist G.One, modeled after game creator Shekhar Subramaniam. When Ra.One becomes self-aware and escapes into the real world, it seeks to kill the player alias Lucifer (Shekhar's son, Prateek). The story follows the family's efforts, aided by G.One who also enters reality, to stop Ra.One's destruction. The film combines action sequences with advanced visual effects and explores themes of good versus evil through a modern technological lens.

Shah Rukh Khan stars in the dual roles of Shekhar and G.One, with Arjun Rampal as Ra.One. Directed by Anubhav Sinha and produced by Red Chillies Entertainment, the production involved extensive development in visual effects and costume design, including high-cost reinforced latex suits and a large team of artists. Filming occurred across India and the UK, facing delays and challenges related to technology and actor injuries. The soundtrack features collaborations with artists like Akon and the Prague Philharmonic Orchestra.

Ra.One was released with a wide domestic and international distribution, setting records for screen counts in India and substantial pre-release revenues, marking it as the most expensive Bollywood film to date. While praised for its technical achievements, particularly the visual effects and action sequences, the film received mixed reviews regarding its story and characterization. It faced controversies including plagiarism allegations, hacking incidents, and cultural sensitivities about South Indian representation. Despite these, Ra.One won several awards for special effects and marketing, and discussions about a sequel emerged, though none have materialized definitively.
