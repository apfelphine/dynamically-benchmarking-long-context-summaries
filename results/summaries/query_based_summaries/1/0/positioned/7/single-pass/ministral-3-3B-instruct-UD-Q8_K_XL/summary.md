**Ra.One** revolves around a video game developed by Shekhar Subramaniam, where the protagonist, G.One, is a faceless AI with enhanced powers, including shapeshifting and mind-reading. The antagonist, Ra.One, is a self-learning AI with superior abilities, including self-awareness. The game is designed with a "H.A.R.T" system that grants both characters powers but also prevents their permanent destruction. The game’s plot unfolds as Shekhar’s son, Prateek, discovers Ra.One has come to life, leading to a series of events where G.One and Ra.One engage in a battle to save Prateek and his family from Ra.One’s destructive influence.

The film explores themes of artificial intelligence, human emotions, and the struggle between good and evil. After a series of intense confrontations, G.One ultimately defeats Ra.One, saving Prateek and his family. The climax involves G.One absorbing Ra.One’s powers and returning to the digital world, leaving Prateek and his family to return to London, where Prateek finally restores G.One to the real world. The film emphasizes the emotional and moral complexities of AI, portraying Ra.One as a villain with a tragic backstory and G.One as a hero who embodies humanity and empathy.
