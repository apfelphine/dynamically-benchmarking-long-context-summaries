## Ra.One Summary

*Ra.One* is a film set in London where Jenny Nair, an employee of Barron Industries, introduces new technology allowing digital content to enter the real world via wireless transmissions. Shekhar Subramaniam, a video game programmer, creates a game where the protagonist, G.One, is pitted against the antagonist, Ra.One. Ra.One is a faceless character endowed with a self-learning AI, shapeshifting, and mind-reading abilities, and possesses substantially greater powers than G.One.

When the game is launched, Ra.One manages to break free from the digital world and enter reality. After several dangerous encounters, G.One and Prateek work together to defeat Ra.One. G.One eventually absorbs Ra.One's remains, allowing him to return to the real world. Several months later, Prateek successfully restores G.One to reality.
