**Ra.One** is a 2011 Indian science fiction action film that blends futuristic technology with a family drama. The story centers on Shekhar Subramaniam, a video game programmer who creates a powerful game featuring two AI characters: G.One, a heroic protagonist, and Ra.One, a powerful and self-aware antagonist. The game's unique feature, the H.A.R.T. (Human Artificial Reality Technology), grants both characters superpowers but also makes them invulnerable to physical harm. 

After the game is launched, Ra.One escapes into the real world, leading to a series of high-stakes events involving the protagonist's son, Prateek. The film follows the struggle between G.One and Ra.One as they battle in both digital and physical realms. G.One eventually saves Prateek and the family, while Ra.One's actions create chaos and danger. The climax features a battle where G.One defeats Ra.One by tricking him into shooting himself without his H.A.R.T., leading to Ra.One's defeat and eventual disintegration. 

The film is celebrated for its groundbreaking visual effects, which were a major technical achievement in Indian cinema. It also explores themes of father-son relationships, the rise of artificial intelligence, and the consequences of technological overreach. Ra.One was a commercial success, becoming one of the highest-grossing Indian films of all time and setting numerous records for digital releases and pre-release buzz. Despite mixed critical reception, it is regarded as a landmark film in Indian sci-fi and action cinema.
