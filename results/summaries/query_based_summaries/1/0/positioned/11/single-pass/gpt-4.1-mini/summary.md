**Ra.One** is a 2011 Indian science fiction superhero film centered around a cutting-edge video game created by Shekhar Subramaniam, an employee at Barron Industries, who designs a game with a powerful antagonist, Ra.One, equipped with self-learning AI and abilities beyond the protagonist G.One. The technology enables digital characters to enter the real world, leading to Ra.One materializing and threatening Shekhar’s son, Prateek (alias Lucifer). The hero, G.One, also comes to life to protect them. The film follows the battle between G.One and Ra.One across real and digital realms, culminating in their confrontation and Ra.One’s defeat. The story incorporates themes of virtual and real-world interplay, family relationships, and heroism.

The film stars Shah Rukh Khan in dual roles as Shekhar and G.One, with Arjun Rampal as the villain Ra.One, Kareena Kapoor as Sonia, and Armaan Verma as Prateek. Directed by Anubhav Sinha and produced under Red Chillies Entertainment, Ra.One was noted for its technical achievements, extensive use of CGI and visual effects (completed by Red Chillies VFX and partners worldwide), and large-scale marketing campaign with significant merchandise tie-ins, including video games. It was the most expensive Bollywood film at the time of release, broke box office records for Hindi films in India and international markets upon release, and received mixed critical responses praising its ambition and visual effects while critiquing plot and character development. The film won several awards, notably for its special effects, and sparked discussions on a potential sequel, although no definitive follow-up was produced.
