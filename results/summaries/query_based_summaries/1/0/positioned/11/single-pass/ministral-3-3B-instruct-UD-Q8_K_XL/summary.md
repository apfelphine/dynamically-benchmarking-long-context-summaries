**Ra.One** follows the story of Shekhar Subramaniam, a video game developer who creates a game featuring a faceless antagonist, Ra.One, and a protagonist, G.One. The game's self-learning AI, Ra.One, escapes into the real world, leading to a high-stakes battle between Shekhar's son Prateek and Ra.One. Ra.One, initially appearing as a villain, becomes a complex and unpredictable force, forcing Prateek and his family to navigate its evolving nature. The film explores themes of technology, destiny, and the blurred line between good and evil, culminating in a dramatic confrontation that redefines the characters' fates.
