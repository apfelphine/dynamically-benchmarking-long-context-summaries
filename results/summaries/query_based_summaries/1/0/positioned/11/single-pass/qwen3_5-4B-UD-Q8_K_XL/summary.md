**Ra.One** is a 2011 Indian science fiction action film directed by Anubhav Sinha and starring Shah Rukh Khan in a dual role as video game programmer Shekhar Subramaniam and the game's protagonist, G.One. The story follows the release of a video game where the faceless antagonist, Ra.One, possesses a self-learning AI and superpowers that allow him to enter the real world. When Ra.One escapes the digital realm, he kills Shekhar's colleague and attempts to kill Shekhar, prompting Shekhar's son, Prateek, and Shekhar's wife, Sonia, to bring G.One into the real world to save him. G.One fights Ra.One, temporarily disables him, and they escape to India, where Ra.One assumes Sonia's identity to kidnap Prateek. After a climactic battle involving a train crash, G.One defeats Ra.One by tricking him into shooting G.One without his H.A.R.T (humanity). In the aftermath, G.One is restored to the digital world, and Prateek eventually brings him back to the real world in London.

The film was a massive commercial success, becoming the most expensive Bollywood film of its time with a production budget of approximately ₹1.3 billion. It received widespread acclaim for its groundbreaking visual effects, which were praised for pushing the boundaries of Indian cinema, though reviews were mixed regarding its plot and character development. The movie faced controversies, including plagiarism allegations from a screenwriter who claimed the game concept was his, and hacking incidents targeting the film's official website. Despite these issues, Ra.One is considered a landmark in Indian superhero cinema, featuring an extensive marketing campaign that included merchandise, video games, and global premieres, and it set records for pre-release buzz and digital print distribution.
