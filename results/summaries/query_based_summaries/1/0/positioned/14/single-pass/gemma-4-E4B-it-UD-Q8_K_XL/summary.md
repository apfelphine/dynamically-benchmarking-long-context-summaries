Ra.One is a film where the protagonist, G.One, is a superhero player character who emerges from a video game created by programmer Shekhar Subramaniam. The game's antagonist, Ra.One, is faceless, possesses shapeshifting and mind-reading abilities, and is imbued with a self-learning AI. The conflict escalates when Ra.One breaches the digital world and enters reality, leading to the murder of Akashi and Shekhar. G.One fights Ra.One, temporarily disabling the villain, before Ra.One returns, hypnotizes Sonia, and kidnaps Prateek. The climax involves G.One and Prateek defeating Ra.One by tricking it into being destroyed by a bulletless H.A.R.T. The film features Shah Rukh Khan in dual roles as Shekhar and G.One, and Arjun Rampal as Ra.One.

The film was noted for its ambitious technological aspects and visual effects, which received near-universal praise. Despite mixed reviews concerning the plot's concept and emotional scenes, Ra.One achieved significant commercial success in India, setting records for the largest single-day and three-day opening weekend for a Hindi film. The film won several technical awards in India, including the National Film Award and Filmfare Award for Best Special Effects.
