**Ra.One** is a 2011 Indian science fiction action film directed by Anubhav Sinha and starring Shah Rukh Khan in a dual role as a video game programmer and the protagonist, G.One, and as the antagonist, Ra.One. The film centers on a new technology that allows digital characters to enter the real world. Shekhar, a game developer, creates a game with a powerful AI-driven villain, Ra.One, who gains self-awareness and escapes into reality. After a series of events involving a failed game launch and a deadly confrontation, Ra.One infiltrates the real world, leading to a battle between the digital hero G.One and the villain. The film explores themes of father-son relationships, the power of technology, and the consequences of AI. It features high-tech action sequences, a unique blend of gaming and superhero elements, and a strong visual effects-driven narrative. The film was a commercial success, becoming one of the highest-grossing Indian films of its time, and was praised for its technical achievements, despite mixed critical reception. A potential sequel, titled *G.One*, was discussed but not confirmed.
