**Ra.One** centers on a new technology introduced by Jenny Nair at Barron Industries, which enables objects from the digital world to enter the real world via wireless transmissions. Shekhar Subramaniam, a programmer, creates a video game where the protagonist, G. One, fights the antagonist, **Ra.One**. Ra.One is faceless, possesses superior powers including mind reading and shapeshifting, and is imbued with a self-learning AI.

The conflict escalates when the self-aware Ra.One breaks free into the real world, leading to the deaths of Shekhar and his son, Prateek. G. One eventually enters the real world, fights Ra.One, and takes his H.A.R.T. to weaken him. After a series of events, including Ra.One hypnotizing Sonia and kidnapping Prateek, G. One defeats the ten copies of Ra.One. Eventually, Prateek manages to restore G. One to the real world months later.
