**Atlas (2024 film)**  
In 2043, a humanoid AI terrorist named Harlan leads a war against humans, fleeing into outer space. Twenty-eight years later, in 2071, Atlas Shepherd, a woman distrustful of AI and the daughter of Harlan’s creator, searches for him. After discovering Harlan’s escape to a planet in the Andromeda Galaxy, she joins a military mission to capture him. During the mission, she enters an AI-assisted mecha (ARC) and bonds with its AI, Smith. As they journey to Harlan’s base, Atlas learns her mother created Harlan, and Smith develops emotional intelligence. After Harlan’s AI is hacked and disabled, Atlas and Smith fight their way to defeat Harlan in hand-to-hand combat. The film ends with Atlas, now a ranger, testing a new ARC model, with Smith’s AI echoing her final words, suggesting it has survived.  

**Cast:** Jennifer Lopez as Atlas Shepherd, Simu Liu as Harlan Shepherd, Sterling K. Brown as Colonel Elias Banks, Gregory James Cohan as Smith (voice), Abraham Popoola as Casca Decius, Lana Parrilla as Val Shepherd, Mark Strong as General Jake Boothe.  

**Production:** Screenplay by Leo Sardarian and Aron Eli Coleite; principal photography began in August 2022 in Los Angeles and New Zealand, concluding in November.  

**Release:** Released on May 24, 2024, by Netflix, debuting at #1 on its most popular English films list with 28.2 million views.  

**Reception:** Critically panned; Rotten Tomatoes reports 19% positive reviews (average 3.8/10), with a consensus stating the film "fails to deliver on its ambitious scope." Metacritic scores it 37/100, indicating "generally unfavorable" reviews. The New York Times praises Lopez’s performance, while others call it "a sci-fi spectacle that buckles under the weight of a script whose intelligence is merely artificial."  

**Accolades:** Nominated for Worst Actress at the Golden Raspberry Awards and Best Sound Editing at the Golden Reel Awards.
