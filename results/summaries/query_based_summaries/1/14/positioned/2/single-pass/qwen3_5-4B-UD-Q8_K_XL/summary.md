# Atlas (2024 film)

Released by Netflix on May 24, 2024, *Atlas* is a science fiction film directed by Gareth Edwards, starring Jennifer Lopez as Atlas Shepherd, a distrustful analyst and the daughter of the creator of a humanoid AI terrorist named Harlan. Set in 2071, the plot follows Atlas as she joins a military mission to locate Harlan, who has escaped to a planet in the Andromeda Galaxy. During the mission, Atlas is forced to pilot an AI-assisted mecha known as an ARC, bonding with the onboard AI, Smith, who is based on the brain scan of her deceased mother. Despite her initial reluctance, Atlas and Smith overcome Harlan's plan to use carbon bombs to destroy Earth's atmosphere, culminating in a fight where Atlas is severely injured and Smith shuts down before she is rescued.

The film received generally unfavorable reviews from critics, with an average score of 3.8/10 on Rotten Tomatoes. While some praised Jennifer Lopez's performance and the film's visual effects, others criticized the script as having "artificial" intelligence and lacking substance. Following its release, Lopez was nominated for the Golden Raspberry Award for Worst Actress. The film also earned several technical nominations, including one for Outstanding Achievement in Sound Editing at the Golden Reel Awards.
