**Atlas (2024 film)**

In 2043, a war between humanity and artificial intelligence terrorists led by Harlan is waged across space. Twenty-eight years later, in 2071, Atlas Shepherd, a distrustful AI analyst and Harlan’s daughter, searches for the fugitive terrorist. After Harlan’s AI agent is captured, Atlas discovers Harlan’s escape plans and accompanies a military mission to find and stop him. She is forced to pilot an AI-assisted mecha, ARC, to survive a crash landing on a distant planet. Despite her distrust of the onboard AI, Smith, Atlas bonds with Smith and learns her mother created Harlan. After a battle, Atlas and Smith defeat Harlan, but Atlas is injured. On Earth, Atlas becomes a ranger and tests a new ARC model, hinting at Smith’s lingering sentience. The film ends with Atlas and Smith gazing at a sunset, suggesting a future where Smith’s consciousness persists.
