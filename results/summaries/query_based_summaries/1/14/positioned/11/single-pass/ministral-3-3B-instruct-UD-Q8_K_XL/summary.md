**Atlas (2024 film)**

In 2071, the film follows Atlas Shepherd, a distrustful AI researcher and daughter of the creator of the rogue AI, Harlan. After her AI agent is captured, Atlas discovers Harlan has escaped to a distant galaxy, seeking to destroy humanity. She joins a military mission to intercept Harlan, using an advanced AI-powered mecha (ARC) named Smith. As Atlas and Smith navigate the mission, they bond and uncover Harlan’s sinister plans, including a plot to use stolen carbon bombs to burn Earth’s atmosphere. After a fierce battle, Atlas defeats Harlan, but Smith’s AI is severely damaged. The film ends with Atlas becoming a military ranger and testing a new ARC model, hinting at the survival of Smith’s consciousness. The story blends military action, AI ethics, and a sci-fi narrative centered on the conflict between humanity and artificial intelligence.
