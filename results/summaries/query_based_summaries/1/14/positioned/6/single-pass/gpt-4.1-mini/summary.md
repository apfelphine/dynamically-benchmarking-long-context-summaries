## Atlas (2024 film) Summary

*Atlas* is a 2024 science fiction film released by Netflix on May 24, 2024. Set initially in 2043, the story follows humanoid AI terrorist Harlan, who wages war against humanity before fleeing into space. Twenty-eight years later, in 2071, Atlas Shepherd, an analyst distrustful of AI and daughter of Harlan’s designer, joins a military mission to capture Harlan on a distant planet in the Andromeda Galaxy. Using AI-assisted mecha called ARCs, Atlas survives a deadly attack, interfaces her mind with the ARC’s AI named Smith, and forms a reluctant bond as they navigate the hostile planet.

Atlas is captured by Harlan, who plans to use a powerful ship and carbon bombs to destroy Earth's atmosphere, selecting survivors to be guided by AI. After escaping with help from Colonel Banks and Smith, Atlas upgrades Smith for battle against Harlan’s forces. She confronts and defeats Harlan despite her injuries, and Smith seemingly shuts down but leaves open the possibility that its sentience survives. Back on Earth, an upgraded ARC bears Smith’s personality imprint, hinting at continued AI existence.

The film stars Jennifer Lopez as Atlas Shepherd, Simu Liu as Harlan Shepherd, Sterling K. Brown as Colonel Elias Banks, with voice work by Gregory James Cohan as Smith. The screenplay was originally by Leo Sardarian and rewritten by Aron Eli Coleite. Principal photography occurred from August to November 2022 in Los Angeles and New Zealand.

Despite debuting as Netflix’s number one English film with over 28 million views in its first week and charting highly across 93 countries, *Atlas* received largely negative reviews. On Rotten Tomatoes, it holds a 19% approval rating, with criticism targeting its script despite praise for Lopez’s committed performance. Metacritic’s score indicates generally unfavorable reviews. The New York Times acknowledged the intriguing AI themes and Lopez’s strong lead role, while outlets like The Guardian and The Hollywood Reporter found the film dated or lacking depth. *Atlas* garnered Razzie and Golden Reel Award nominations.
