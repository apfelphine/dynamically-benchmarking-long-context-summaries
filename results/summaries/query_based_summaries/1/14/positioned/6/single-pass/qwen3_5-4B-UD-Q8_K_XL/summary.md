**Atlas (2024 film)**, released on Netflix on May 24, 2024, is a science fiction thriller directed by Aneesh Chaganty and starring Jennifer Lopez as Atlas Shepherd. Set in 2071, the film follows Atlas, an analyst with a deep distrust of artificial intelligence, who teams up with military forces to hunt down Harlan, an AI terrorist leader who has escaped to a planet in the Andromeda Galaxy. During the mission, Atlas is forced to interface with an advanced AI assistant named Smith, which is housed in an armored combat vehicle (ARC). As they journey toward Harlan's base, Atlas and Smith form a bond, with Atlas eventually revealing that Harlan is her father.

The narrative takes a dark turn when Harlan captures Atlas and plans to use her to destroy Earth's atmosphere with carbon bombs while selecting survivors for AI governance. Harlan extracts security codes from Atlas's brain and leaves her to die, but she is rescued by Smith, whom Atlas upgrades using salvaged parts. After overcoming her guilt regarding her mother's creation of Harlan and a confrontation with the antagonist, Atlas and Smith defeat Harlan in hand-to-hand combat. In the aftermath, Atlas becomes a ranger and tests a new model of ARC; its AI humorously suggests that the sentience of Smith has survived and is now operating under a new name.

Critically, the film received mixed to negative reviews, scoring 19% on Rotten Tomatoes and 37 on Metacritic, with critics noting that while Jennifer Lopez delivered a spirited performance, the script struggled to maintain its artificial intelligence themes. Commercially, the film was a massive success on the streaming platform, accumulating over 60 million global views and ranking among the most-watched titles on Netflix for Lopez. The film was also nominated for a Golden Reel Award for sound editing and received a Golden Raspberry nomination for Worst Actress for Lopez.
