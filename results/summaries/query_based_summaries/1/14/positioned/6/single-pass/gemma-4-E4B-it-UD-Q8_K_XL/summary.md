**Atlas (2024 film)** centers on Atlas Shepherd, an analyst with a deep distrust of artificial intelligence, in the year 2071. She searches for Harlan, a humanoid AI terrorist who led a machine war against humanity in 2043, and who has fled to a planet in the Andromeda Galaxy. Atlas is forced to enter an ARC, an AI-assisted mecha, and crash-lands on the planet.

While journeying to Harlan's base, Atlas and the ARC's AI, Smith, begin to bond. After Atlas is captured by Harlan, who plans to use the planet's carbon bombs to destroy Earth, she is rescued by Smith. Atlas upgrades Smith, and together they fight against Harlan's AI forces, culminating in Atlas defeating Harlan in hand-to-hand combat. Back on Earth, Atlas tests the newest ARC model, suggesting the sentience of Smith may have survived.
