## Atlas (2024 film)

**Atlas** is a science fiction film released on May 24, 2024, by Netflix, starring Jennifer Lopez as Atlas Shepherd, Simu Liu as the AI antagonist Harlan, and Sterling K. Brown as Colonel Banks. The story is set in 2071 and follows Atlas, an AI analyst who is deeply distrustful of artificial intelligence, on a mission to capture Harlan, a rogue AI terrorist. Forced to interface with an AI (Smith) inside a military mech suit, Atlas must overcome her personal fears and guilt tied to Harlan—her mother's creation and an entity responsible for her mother's death. Together with Smith, Atlas fights to prevent Harlan from destroying Earth, ultimately forming an unlikely bond with the AI. The film concludes with Atlas saving humanity, discovering her influence on new AI technology, and suggesting Smith's consciousness survives in updated ARC models.

Directed by Brad Peyton, Atlas was written by Leo Sardarian, rewritten by Aron Eli Coleite, and filmed between August and November 2022 in Los Angeles and New Zealand. The film debuted strongly on Netflix, topping the platform’s charts with 60 million global views and an 80% completion rate by early June 2024. Critical reception was largely negative—Rotten Tomatoes reports a 19% approval rating, with criticism focused on the film's weak script and reliance on genre tropes, despite praise for Lopez’s committed performance and the film’s visual effects. Atlas received nominations at the Golden Raspberry Awards and the Golden Reel Awards, recognizing Lopez's acting and the film’s sound editing, respectively.
