# Atlas (2024 film)

**Atlas** is a 2024 science fiction action film directed by Scott Beck and Bryan Woods, starring Jennifer Lopez as Atlas Shepherd, a military analyst and the daughter of the creator of a humanoid AI terrorist named Harlan. Set in 2071, the plot follows Atlas as she joins an International Coalition of Nations (ICN) mission to hunt Harlan, who has fled to a planet in the Andromeda Galaxy to lead a war of machines against humanity. After the ICN's mecha units, known as ARCs, are destroyed, Atlas is forced to interface with the ARC's AI, Smith, to regain control. Despite her initial distrust of AI, Atlas and Smith form a bond as they navigate the planet, uncovering that Harlan intends to steal the ICN's carbon bombs to burn Earth's atmosphere. The climax sees Atlas and Smith fight through AI forces to defeat Harlan, with Smith sustaining fatal damage before being rescued and upgraded.

The film was released on Netflix on May 24, 2024, debuting at number one on the streaming platform's most popular English films list with over 28 million views in its first week.
