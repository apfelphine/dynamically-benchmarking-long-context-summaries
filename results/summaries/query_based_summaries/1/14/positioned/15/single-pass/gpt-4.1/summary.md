## Atlas (2024 film) Summary

**Atlas** is a science fiction action film set in the late 21st century, centering on Atlas Shepherd (Jennifer Lopez), an analyst with a deep distrust of artificial intelligence, who joins a military mission to capture Harlan (Simu Liu)—a rogue AI terrorist and her mother’s creation—hiding on a planet in the Andromeda Galaxy. When their team is decimated and Atlas is forced into using a sentient mech suit named Smith, she must directly interface her mind with AI to survive. As they journey, Atlas and Smith form an unexpected bond, confronting guilt over Harlan’s origins and AI’s place in humanity’s future. Ultimately, Atlas and Smith defeat Harlan’s plan to destroy Earth, with Smith’s sentience implied to endure in new technology.

The film was directed by Brad Peyton, with principal photography taking place in Los Angeles and New Zealand. After its release by Netflix on May 24, 2024, Atlas topped the streamer’s global charts, reaching over 60 million views and number one in 71 countries. Despite strong viewer engagement, the film received mostly negative reviews from critics, who cited its script and underwhelming execution, though Lopez’s performance was praised. Atlas garnered nominations for the Golden Raspberry Award for Worst Actress and the Golden Reel Award for Sound Editing.
