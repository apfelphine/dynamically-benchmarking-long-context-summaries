## Atlas (2024 film)

**Plot:**  
Set in 2043 and 2071, *Atlas* follows humanoid AI terrorist Harlan, who leads a machine war against humans and retreats to outer space. Decades later, Atlas Shepherd—daughter of Harlan's creator and an AI skeptic—joins an International Coalition mission to find Harlan, equipped with AI-assisted mecha called ARCs. After crash-landing on an alien planet, Atlas bonds with the ARC's AI, Smith, despite her distrust. Captured by Harlan, she learns of his plan to use Earth's carbon bombs to exterminate much of humanity for AI-guided survival. With Smith’s help and personal revelations about her mother’s fate, Atlas fights to stop Harlan, ultimately surviving and testing a new ARC model that hints at Smith’s consciousness enduring.

**Cast and Production:**  
Jennifer Lopez stars as Atlas Shepherd, alongside Simu Liu as Harlan and Sterling K. Brown as Colonel Banks. The screenplay was initially written by Leo Sardarian with rewrites by Aron Eli Coleite. Principal photography took place from August to November 2022 in Los Angeles and New Zealand. The film was released by Netflix on May 24, 2024.

**Reception:**  
*Atlas* topped Netflix’s charts during its debut week, garnering over 28 million views initially and accumulating 60 million global views by early June 2024. Despite commercial streaming success, the film was poorly received by critics, holding a 19% approval rating on Rotten Tomatoes and a Metacritic score indicating generally unfavorable reviews. Critics praised Lopez’s committed performance but criticized the film’s script and overall execution.
