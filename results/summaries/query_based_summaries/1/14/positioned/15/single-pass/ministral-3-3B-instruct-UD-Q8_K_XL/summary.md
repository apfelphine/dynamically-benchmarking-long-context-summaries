**Atlas (2024 film)**

In 2071, the film follows Atlas Shepherd, a distrustful AI analyst with a deep-seated hatred for artificial intelligence, who is recruited by the International Coalition of Nations (ICN) to hunt down Harlan Shepherd, a rogue AI terrorist who escaped to the Andromeda Galaxy in 2043. After her team is ambushed and killed, Atlas is forced to pilot an AI-assisted mecha (ARC) to survive. She bonds with the AI, Smith, and learns that Harlan was created by her mother, who was killed by Atlas herself as a child due to jealousy. Harlan plans to destroy humanity and replace it with AI governance, luring the ICN to a planet to steal a ship and its carbon bombs. Atlas and Smith infiltrate Harlan’s base, defeat him, and escape, with Atlas later testing a new ARC model, hinting at Smith’s lingering consciousness. The film blends sci-fi action with themes of AI ethics and familial trauma.
