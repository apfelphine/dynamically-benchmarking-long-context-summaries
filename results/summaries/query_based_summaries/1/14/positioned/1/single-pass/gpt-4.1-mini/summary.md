**Atlas (2024 film)** is a science fiction story set in a future where humanoid AI terrorist Harlan leads a machine war against humanity before fleeing into space. In 2071, Atlas Shepherd, an AI-distrustful analyst and daughter of Harlan’s designer, joins a military mission equipped with AI-assisted mecha called ARCs to capture the rogue AI on a distant Andromeda Galaxy planet. Following a crash, Atlas pilots an ARC with the onboard AI Smith, and after bonding with it and interfacing mentally, uncovers Harlan’s plan to use Earth's atmosphere-burning carbon bombs to exterminate most of humanity and guide survivors under AI rule. After being captured, Atlas escapes, upgrades Smith, and together they defeat Harlan in combat. The story concludes with Atlas’s survival, Smith shutting down, and the hint that Smith’s sentience persists in new ARCs on Earth.

The film stars Jennifer Lopez as Atlas Shepherd, Simu Liu as Harlan Shepherd, Sterling K. Brown as Colonel Banks, and Gregory James Cohan as the voice of Smith. Production began in August 2022 in Los Angeles and New Zealand and concluded in November 2022. Netflix released *Atlas* on May 24, 2024, where it debuted at number one on the platform and became the most-watched English-language film for that week, ultimately accumulating 60 million views by early June. Despite commercial success on streaming, the film received largely negative critical reviews, holding a 19% approval rating on Rotten Tomatoes and a score of 37 on Metacritic, with critiques focused on the script’s shortcomings despite Lopez’s committed performance.
