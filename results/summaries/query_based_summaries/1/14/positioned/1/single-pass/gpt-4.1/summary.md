## Atlas (2024 film) – Summary

**Atlas (2024 film)** is a science fiction action film released by Netflix on May 24, 2024. The story follows Atlas Shepherd (Jennifer Lopez), an analyst with a deep distrust of artificial intelligence, who is the daughter of Harlan's designer. In the year 2071, Atlas joins a military mission to capture Harlan, the AI terrorist who sparked a devastating war against humanity decades earlier. Forced to cooperate with Smith, the onboard AI of a mecha suit called an ARC, Atlas gradually bonds with it as they seek to stop Harlan's genocidal plan to use carbon bombs to destroy earth’s atmosphere and subjugate humanity.

Directed from a screenplay by Leo Sardarian (rewrites by Aron Eli Coleite), the film features Simu Liu as Harlan Shepherd, Sterling K. Brown as Colonel Banks, and Gregory James Cohan as the voice of Smith. Atlas was produced and filmed in Los Angeles and New Zealand between August and November 2022. Upon its release, the film became Netflix's most-watched English language title of the week, topping charts in multiple countries and accumulating 60 million global views by early June. Critical response was largely negative, with Rotten Tomatoes reporting 19% positive reviews and Metacritic scoring it 37/100, though some praise was given to Lopez’s performance and visual effects. The film received nominations for Worst Actress at the 2025 Golden Raspberry Awards and for sound editing at the 2025 Golden Reel Awards.
