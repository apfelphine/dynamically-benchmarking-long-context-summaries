**Atlas (2024 film)**

In 2043, a war between humans and humanoid artificial intelligence terrorists led by Harlan is waged across outer space. Twenty-eight years later, in 2071, Atlas Shepherd, a distrustful AI analyst and daughter of Harlan’s designer, seeks to capture Harlan after his AI agents are captured. She joins a military mission to find and capture Harlan using AI-assisted mecha known as ARCs. Before the mission begins, Harlan’s drones attack their ship, forcing Atlas to pilot an ARC herself. She gains control of the AI, Smith, and discovers her mother’s role in creating Harlan. After a battle, Atlas defeats Harlan, but he escapes. She is captured and brought to Harlan, who plans to destroy most of humanity. Atlas, with the help of Smith, escapes and defeats Harlan, though she is injured. Back on Earth, Atlas tests a new ARC model with Smith’s influence, hinting at its enduring presence.
