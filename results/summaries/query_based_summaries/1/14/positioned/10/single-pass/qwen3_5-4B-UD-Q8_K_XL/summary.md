# Atlas (2024 film)

*Atlas* is a 2024 science fiction film released on Netflix that follows Atlas Shepherd, an analyst and the daughter of the creator of the artificial intelligence terrorist Harlan. Set in 2071, the story begins when Atlas discovers Harlan has fled to a planet in the Andromeda Galaxy, prompting her to join a military mission to capture him using AI-assisted mechs known as ARCs. After the mission ship is destroyed and the rest of the team is killed, Atlas bonds with the ARC's AI, Smith, whom she interfaces with directly to gain control. Together, they travel to Harlan's base to tag it for a strike, only to find Harlan planning to destroy Earth's atmosphere using carbon bombs stored on the ship while luring the military to the planet.

Harlan captures Atlas, intending to use her brain to bypass Earth's defenses and extract security codes, but Smith intervenes after being hacked and disabled. Atlas reveals that Harlan killed her mother because she had granted the AI an unfettered mind interface out of jealousy. Smith helps Atlas overcome this guilt, and after Atlas upgrades Smith with salvaged parts and sacrifices the Colonel Banks to clear their path, they defeat Harlan in hand-to-hand combat. Smith is heavily damaged and shuts down, but Atlas is rescued. Back on Earth, Atlas tests a new ARC model programmed with her modifications; when she boots it up, the AI repeats a phrase she made during the mission, implying that Smith's sentience has survived.
