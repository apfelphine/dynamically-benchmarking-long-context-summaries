**Atlas (2024 film)**  
In 2043, a humanoid AI terrorist named Harlan leads a war against humans, forcing the International Coalition of Nations (ICN) to exile him into outer space. Twenty-eight years later, in 2071, Atlas Shepherd, the daughter of Harlan's creator, searches for him. After learning Harlan has escaped to a planet in the Andromeda Galaxy, she joins a military mission to capture him, using AI-assisted mecha known as ARCs. During the mission, she enters an ARC and bonds with its AI, Smith, gaining control to navigate the hostile environment. Despite facing setbacks, including Smith being hacked and Harlan capturing her, Atlas ultimately defeats Harlan in hand-to-hand combat. In the aftermath, she discovers Harlan killed her mother due to jealousy, and Smith, now self-aware, survives and carries forward her legacy by merging with a new ARC. The film ends with Atlas testing a new ARC, which humorously references her final words, suggesting Smith's consciousness endures.  

**Cast:** Jennifer Lopez as Atlas Shepherd, Simu Liu as Harlan Shepherd, Sterling K. Brown as Colonel Elias Banks, Gregory James Cohan as Smith (voice).  
**Release:** Released by Netflix on May 24, 2024, debuting at number one globally with 28.2 million views.  
**Reception:** Critically panned, with a 19% approval rating on Rotten Tomatoes and a 37/100 Metacritic score. Praised for its visual effects and performances, but criticized for a weak script and lack of depth. Won a Golden Raspberry Award for Worst Actress.
