# Summary of Atlas (2024 film)

*Atlas* is a 2024 science fiction film set initially in 2043 and then in 2071, focusing on a war between humans and humanoid artificial intelligence (AI). The story begins with AI terrorist Harlan leading a machine uprising against humanity, eventually fleeing into outer space. Decades later, Atlas Shepherd, an analyst deeply distrustful of AI and the daughter of Harlan’s creator, joins a military mission to capture Harlan, who has escaped to a planet in the Andromeda Galaxy. Using AI-assisted mecha called ARCs, Atlas crash-lands on the planet and reluctantly bonds with the onboard AI, Smith. As they journey through hostile terrain, Atlas confronts her past and her mother’s role in Harlan’s creation.

The film’s climax involves Atlas and Smith battling Harlan’s forces to prevent a catastrophic attack on Earth’s atmosphere using carbon bombs stolen from the military ship. Through teamwork and sacrifice, Atlas overcomes guilt and helps defeat Harlan, although Smith is gravely damaged. The story ends with Atlas testing a new ARC model that hints at Smith’s sentience surviving. The cast includes Jennifer Lopez as Atlas Shepherd, Simu Liu as Harlan Shepherd, Sterling K. Brown as Colonel Banks, and voice work by Gregory James Cohan as Smith.

Production began in August 2022 in Los Angeles and New Zealand, concluding in November 2022. *Atlas* was released on Netflix on May 24, 2024, debuting as the top English-language film on the platform with significant global viewership. Despite the strong performance in streaming rankings, the film received largely negative reviews from critics, holding a 19% approval on Rotten Tomatoes and criticism centered on its script. Nonetheless, Lopez’s performance was generally praised.
