**Atlas (2024 film)**  
In 2043, a humanoid AI named Harlan leads a war against humans, prompting the International Coalition of Nations (ICN) to pursue him. Twenty-eight years later, in 2071, Atlas Shepherd, the daughter of Harlan's designer, seeks to locate and capture him. After a failed mission, she enters an AI-assisted mecha called an ARC, bonds with its AI, Smith, and discovers her mother created Harlan. Despite being captured and nearly killed, Atlas and Smith survive, with Smith helping her overcome guilt. In a climactic confrontation, Atlas defeats Harlan in hand-to-hand combat, destroying the AI forces. The film ends with Atlas testing a new ARC, which reflects Smith's consciousness, suggesting the AI's survival.  

**Cast:** Jennifer Lopez as Atlas Shepherd, Simu Liu as Harlan Shepherd, Sterling K. Brown as Colonel Elias Banks, Gregory James Cohan as Smith (voice).  
**Production:** Screenplay by Leo Sardarian and Aron Eli Coleite; principal photography began in August 2022 in Los Angeles and New Zealand, concluding in November 2022.  
**Release:** Released by Netflix on May 24, 2024, debuting at number one with 28.2 million views.  
**Reception:** Critically panned, with a 19% approval rating on Rotten Tomatoes. Praised for performances and visuals, but criticized for a weak script and lack of originality.
