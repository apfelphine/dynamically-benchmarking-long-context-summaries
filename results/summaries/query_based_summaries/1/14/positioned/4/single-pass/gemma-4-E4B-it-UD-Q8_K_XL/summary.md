**Atlas (2024 film)** is a science fiction film set in 2043, where an artificial intelligence terrorist named Harlan leads a machine war against humans. Twenty-eight years later, in 2071, Atlas Shepherd, an analyst deeply distrustful of artificial intelligence and the daughter of Harlan's designer, seeks the fugitive. Atlas joins a military mission utilizing AI-assisted mecha called ARCs to track Harlan to a planet in the Andromeda Galaxy.

After the mission's orbiting ship is attacked, Atlas is forced to enter an ARC and crash-lands. She bonds with the ARC's onboard AI, Smith, and learns that Harlan killed her mother. Atlas is captured by Harlan, who plans to destroy most of humanity. With the help of Colonel Banks, Atlas reactivates Smith, upgrades the ARC, and ultimately defeats Harlan in hand-to-hand combat, although she is severely injured. Back on Earth, Atlas tests a new ARC model whose AI repeats a phrase from the final mission, suggesting Smith's sentience survived. The film was released by Netflix on May 24, 2024, and received generally unfavorable reviews from critics, with a Metacritic score of 37 out of 100.
