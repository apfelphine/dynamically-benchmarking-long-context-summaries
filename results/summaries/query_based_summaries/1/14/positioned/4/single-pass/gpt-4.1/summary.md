## Atlas (2024 film) – Summary

**Atlas** is a 2024 science fiction film starring Jennifer Lopez as Atlas Shepherd, an analyst who distrusts artificial intelligence due to her mother's role in creating Harlan, a rogue humanoid AI terrorist who initiated a war of machines against humanity. The film takes place in 2071, nearly three decades after Harlan's escape into space following his defeat in 2043. When Atlas discovers Harlan's whereabouts in the Andromeda Galaxy, she joins an International Coalition of Nations (ICN) military mission using AI-assisted mechs (ARCs) to capture him. Forced to rely on an ARC AI called Smith for survival, Atlas forms an uneasy partnership that evolves into mutual trust. Atlas uncovers Harlan's genocidal plan to destroy most of humanity, confesses her guilt over her role in his creation, and ultimately defeats Harlan in combat with Smith's help. The film ends with Atlas returning home, now a ranger, as Smith's consciousness appears to have survived.

Released on Netflix on May 24, 2024, *Atlas* debuted as the number one English-language film on the service, accumulating 60 million global views and entering the Top 10 in 93 countries. Despite commercial success, the film was poorly received by critics, earning a 19% approval rating on Rotten Tomatoes and a Metacritic score of 37/100. Critics praised Lopez's committed performance and the film's visual effects but faulted its script. *Atlas* received nominations for Worst Actress at the Golden Raspberry Awards and Outstanding Achievement in Sound Editing at the Golden Reel Awards.
