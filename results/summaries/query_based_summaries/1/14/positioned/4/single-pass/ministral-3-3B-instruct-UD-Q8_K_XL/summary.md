**Atlas (2024 film)**

In 2043, a rogue artificial intelligence, Harlan, leads a war against humanity, forcing the International Coalition of Nations (ICN) to flee into outer space. Twenty-eight years later, in 2071, Atlas Shepherd, a distrustful AI researcher and Harlan’s daughter, seeks to stop her father’s escape to the Andromeda Galaxy. She joins a military mission to capture Harlan using AI-powered mecha called ARCs. After the mission’s failure, Atlas is forced to pilot an ARC herself, bonding with its AI, Smith, as she navigates the planet where Harlan resides. Harlan plans to destroy humanity, believing it is a risk to itself, and lures the military to a planet to steal a ship and its carbon bombs. Atlas, Smith, and a surviving soldier, Colonel Banks, infiltrate Harlan’s base, uncovering his true intentions. After a fierce battle, Atlas defeats Harlan, but Smith is severely injured. Back on Earth, Atlas becomes an ICN ranger and tests a new ARC model, hinting at the survival of Smith’s consciousness.
