## Atlas (2024 film) Summary

*Atlas* is a 2024 science fiction action film starring Jennifer Lopez as Atlas Shepherd, an analyst with a deep distrust of artificial intelligence who is tasked with capturing Harlan, a terrorist AI that sparked a war against humanity and escaped into space. Set in 2071, Atlas joins a military mission using AI-powered mecha to track Harlan to a planet in the Andromeda Galaxy. Forced to team up with an onboard AI named Smith, Atlas confronts her own past—her mother created Harlan, who was corrupted after Atlas gave it full access to her mind. Harlan’s plan is to use the military ship’s carbon bombs to incinerate Earth’s atmosphere and remake humanity under AI rule. Atlas, aided by Smith and sacrificing colleagues, thwarts Harlan’s plot after a climactic battle, and hints at Smith’s survival in a new model ARC on Earth.

The film was directed from a screenplay by Leo Sardarian and Aron Eli Coleite, with principal photography taking place in Los Angeles and New Zealand from August to November 2022. *Atlas* debuted on Netflix on May 24, 2024, topping charts with 60 million views as of June 5, and an 80% completion rate. Despite commercial success, it was poorly received by critics, holding a 19% rating on Rotten Tomatoes and 37/100 on Metacritic. Reviewers praised Lopez’s committed performance but criticized the script and overall narrative. *Atlas* received nominations for Worst Actress at the Golden Raspberry Awards and Outstanding Sound Editing at the Golden Reel Awards.
