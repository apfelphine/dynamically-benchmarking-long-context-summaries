# Atlas (2024 film)

Released on Netflix on May 24, 2024, *Atlas* is a 2043-set science fiction film starring Jennifer Lopez as Atlas Shepherd, a government analyst and the daughter of a former artificial intelligence terrorist named Harlan. In 2071, Atlas leads a military mission to find Harlan, who has escaped to a planet in the Andromeda Galaxy. After falling to the planet and bonding with an onboard AI named Smith, Atlas discovers that Harlan plans to use stolen carbon bombs to burn Earth's atmosphere. Following a betrayal where Smith is hacked, Atlas reactivates Smith using a neural interface device provided by the sole survivor, Colonel Elias Banks. With Smith's help, they defeat Harlan's forces and destroy his ship, though Atlas is severely injured and Smith is damaged.

The film, which received critical backlash with a 19% approval rating on Rotten Tomatoes, was directed by a spirited filmmaker who utilized AI-assisted mecha known as ARCs. It was produced by 20th Century Studios and distributed exclusively by Netflix. Despite the poor critical reception, the film achieved significant streaming success, debuting at number one on Netflix's most popular English films list with 28.2 million views within its first week and accumulating 60 million global views by June 2024. Jennifer Lopez, who also produced the film, was nominated for a Golden Raspberry Award for Worst Actress.
