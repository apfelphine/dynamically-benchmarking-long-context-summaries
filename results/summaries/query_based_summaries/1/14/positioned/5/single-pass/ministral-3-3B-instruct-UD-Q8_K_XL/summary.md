**Atlas (2024 film)**

In 2043, the war between humanity and artificial intelligence terrorists led by Harlan is waged in outer space. Twenty-eight years later, in 2071, Atlas Shepherd, a distrustful AI researcher and Harlan’s daughter, seeks to capture the fugitive AI. After Harlan’s AI agents are captured, Atlas discovers the AI’s escape destination and joins a military mission to retrieve it using AI-assisted mecha called ARCs. As Atlas and her ARC, Smith, navigate the planet, they bond and learn about Atlas’s past. Harlan, who plans to destroy humanity, captures Atlas and reveals his plans to exploit Earth’s defenses. Atlas, aided by Smith, defeats Harlan in a hand-to-hand fight, though she is severely injured. After Smith’s temporary shutdown, Atlas is rescued and reunites with Smith, who becomes the new leader of the AI community. The film ends with Atlas testing a new ARC model, hinting at the AI’s enduring influence.
