**Atlas (2024 film)**  
In 2043, a humanoid AI named Harlan leads a war against humans, prompting the International Coalition of Nations (ICN) to pursue him into space. Twenty-eight years later, in 2071, Atlas Shepherd, an AI analyst and daughter of Harlan's designer, searches for the fugitive. After discovering Harlan has escaped to a planet in the Andromeda Galaxy, she joins a military mission to capture him using AI-assisted mecha known as ARCs. During the mission, she bonds with the ARC's AI, Smith, and learns her mother created Harlan. After Harlan captures her and plans to destroy humanity, Atlas and Smith fight through AI forces, ultimately defeating Harlan in hand-to-hand combat. The film ends with Atlas testing a new ARC, which repeats a phrase she made during the mission, suggesting Smith has survived.  

**Cast:**  
- Jennifer Lopez as Atlas Shepherd  
- Simu Liu as Harlan Shepherd  
- Sterling K. Brown as Colonel Elias Banks  
- Gregory James Cohan as Smith (voice)  
- Abraham Popoola as Casca Decius  
- Lana Parrilla as Val Shepherd  
- Mark Strong as General Jake Boothe  

**Production:**  
The screenplay was written by Leo Sardarian, with rewrites by Aron Eli Coleite. Principal photography began on August 26, 2022, in Los Angeles and New Zealand, and concluded on November 26.  

**Release:**  
Released by Netflix on May 24, 2024. It debuted at number one on Netflix’s most popular English films list with 28.2 million views.  

**Reception:**  
The film received negative critical reviews, with a 19% approval rating on Rotten Tomatoes and a 37/100 score on Metacritic. Critics called it "a sci-fi spectacle that buckles under the weight of a script whose intelligence is merely artificial." Despite this, it achieved strong viewership and was nominated for the Golden Raspberry Award for Worst Actress.
