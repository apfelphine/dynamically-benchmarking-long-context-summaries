**Atlas (2024 film)** is a science fiction action film set in a future where a humanoid AI terrorist named Harlan wages war against humans. After being forced into space by the military coalition ICN, Harlan escapes to a distant planet in the Andromeda Galaxy. Atlas Shepherd, an analyst with deep distrust for AI and daughter of Harlan's designer, joins a military mission with AI-assisted mecha called ARCs to capture Harlan. After crash-landing on the planet and bonding with her ARC's AI named Smith, she confronts Harlan’s plan to destroy much of humanity using carbon bombs aboard a stolen military ship. With Smith’s help and sacrifices by her comrades, Atlas defeats Harlan. Back on Earth, her efforts lead to improved ARC technology that hints at Smith’s consciousness surviving.

The film stars Jennifer Lopez as Atlas Shepherd, Simu Liu as Harlan Shepherd, Sterling K. Brown as Colonel Elias Banks, and others. Written originally by Leo Sardarian with rewrites by Aron Eli Coleite, principal photography took place from August to November 2022 in Los Angeles and New Zealand. Released by Netflix on May 24, 2024, *Atlas* topped Netflix’s English films list with over 28 million views in its debut week and reached the Top 10 in 93 countries. Despite strong viewership, the film received generally unfavorable reviews from critics, holding a 19% approval rating on Rotten Tomatoes and a Metacritic score of 37. Reviews praised Jennifer Lopez’s performance but criticized the screenplay and overall execution. The film received nominations including a Golden Raspberry Award for Worst Actress for Lopez.
