**Atlas (2024 film)** is a sci-fi action film set in 2071, following Atlas Shepherd, a daughter of the designer of a rogue AI named Harlan, who searches for the fugitive AI after learning Harlan has escaped to a planet in the Andromeda Galaxy. After a military mission to the planet fails and her team is killed, Atlas enters an AI-assisted mecha (ARC) to survive, forming a bond with its onboard AI, Smith. She discovers her mother created Harlan and that Harlan killed her mother due to jealousy and a belief that humanity is inherently destructive. After Harlan captures Atlas and plans to destroy humanity, she and Smith escape, defeat Harlan in hand-to-hand combat, and destroy the enemy forces. In the aftermath, Atlas becomes a ranger and tests a new ARC, which, after booting up, repeats a phrase she made during the mission and jokingly asks her to guess its name, suggesting Smith's consciousness has survived. The film was released on Netflix on May 24, 2024, and received generally unfavorable reviews, with a 19% approval rating on Rotten Tomatoes and a 37/100 score on Metacritic. It was nominated for the Golden Raspberry Award for Worst Actress.
