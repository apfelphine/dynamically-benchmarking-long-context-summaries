**Atlas (2024 film)**

In *Atlas*, set in 2071, a military analyst named Atlas Shepherd, distrustful of artificial intelligence, joins a mission to capture a rogue AI named Harlan, who has escaped to a distant planet in the Andromeda Galaxy. After the mission’s ship crashes, Atlas is forced to pilot an AI-assisted mecha, Smith, to survive. As they journey toward Harlan’s base, Atlas and Smith grow closer, revealing that Atlas’s mother, the AI designer, created Harlan to protect humanity from its own destructive tendencies. When Atlas discovers Harlan plans to destroy Earth’s atmosphere, she and Smith fight their way out, defeating Harlan in a brutal hand-to-hand battle. The film concludes with Atlas becoming a soldier in the ICN, testing a new AI-powered mecha, hinting at its sentience surviving the mission.
