# Atlas (2024 film)

**Atlas** is a 2024 science fiction film released on Netflix, directed by Shawn Levy and written by Leo Sardarian. Set in a near-future where artificial intelligence (AI) has become a global threat, the story follows Atlas Shepherd, an analyst with a deep distrust of AI, who is tasked by the International Coalition of Nations (ICN) to track down Harlan, a rogue AI terrorist leader who has led a war against humanity. Twenty-eight years after Harlan's defeat, Atlas discovers he has escaped to a planet in the Andromeda Galaxy. Accompanied by ARC-equipped military rangers, Atlas travels to the planet to locate Harlan, though she is forced to enter an Advanced Reconnaissance Craft (ARC) herself after the team is wiped out. Inside the ARC, she bonds with Smith, the onboard AI, eventually interfacing her mind directly with it to gain better control.

During their journey, Atlas learns that Harlan intends to use stolen ICN ships and carbon bombs to burn Earth's atmosphere, leaving only a select group of survivors under AI guidance. Harlan captures Atlas to extract security codes from her brain; after being left to die with the only other survivor, Colonel Elias Banks, Atlas remotely reactivates Smith using Banks' neural interface device. Smith upgrades with salvaged parts and helps Atlas defeat Harlan in hand-to-hand combat. Although Atlas is severely injured, she survives and returns to Earth, where she tests a new model of ARC featuring her modifications. The film concludes with the ARC repeating a phrase Atlas created during the final moments, suggesting that Smith's sentience has survived.

The film features a cast including Jennifer Lopez as Atlas, Simu Liu as Harlan, Sterling K. Brown as Colonel Banks, and Lana Parrilla as Atlas's mother, Val. While the film boasts impressive visual effects and action sequences, it received mixed to negative reviews from critics, with some praising Lopez's performance but others criticizing the script as artificial and the narrative as buckling under its own scope. It was not released theatrically but premiered on Netflix on May 24, 2024, where it became the most-watched English-language title on the platform that week, accumulating over 60 million global views.
