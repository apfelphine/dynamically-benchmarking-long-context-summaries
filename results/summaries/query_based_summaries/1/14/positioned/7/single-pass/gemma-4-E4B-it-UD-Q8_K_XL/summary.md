**Atlas (2024 film)** is a science fiction action film set in the year 2071. The plot centers on Atlas Shepherd, an analyst who distrusts artificial intelligence, as she searches for the fugitive terrorist Harlan, the designer of AI. Harlan, who led a war of machines against humans in 2043, has escaped to a planet in the Andromeda Galaxy.

Atlas joins a military mission using AI-assisted mecha called ARCs to find and capture Harlan. After her ship is attacked, Atlas enters an ARC, bonding with its onboard AI, Smith. They reach Harlan's base, where he plans to destroy much of humanity. Atlas fights against the AI forces, eventually defeating Harlan in hand-to-hand combat, though she is badly injured. The film concludes with Atlas receiving a new ARC model, suggesting the sentience of Smith has survived.
