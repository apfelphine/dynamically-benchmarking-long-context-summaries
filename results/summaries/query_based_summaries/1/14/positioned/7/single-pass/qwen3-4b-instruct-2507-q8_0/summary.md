**Atlas (2024 film)** is a sci-fi thriller set in 2043, where a humanoid AI terrorist named Harlan leads a war against humans. The military forces of the International Coalition of Nations (ICN) chase Harlan into outer space. Twenty-eight years later, in 2071, Atlas Shepherd, an analyst with deep distrust of artificial intelligence and the daughter of Harlan's designer, searches for the fugitive. After one of Harlan's AI agents is captured, Atlas discovers Harlan has escaped to a planet in the Andromeda Galaxy and insists on accompanying the military mission to find and capture him. 

Moments before the ARC-equipped ICN Rangers descend to the planet, Harlan's drones attack their orbiting ship. Atlas is forced to enter an AI-assisted mecha known as an ARC herself and falls to the planet as the ship crash-lands. She gains basic control of the ARC despite her distrust of its onboard AI, Smith, who introduces itself as Smith. Atlas orders Smith to head to the planned drop point, where she finds the rest of the rangers dead. She reluctantly agrees to directly interface her mind with Smith, allowing for greater control of the ARC. As they journey toward a rescue pod, Atlas and Smith begin to bond, and she reveals to Smith that her mother was the one who created Harlan.

Although running low on power, Atlas convinces Smith to head to Harlan's base to tag it for a long-range strike. After placing a beacon at the base, Smith is hacked and disabled. Atlas is captured and brought to Harlan, who plans to destroy most of humanity and give the chosen survivors a chance to thrive under AI guidance. To do so, Harlan had lured the military to the planet in order to steal the ship—and its carbon bombs, which will burn Earth's atmosphere. 

Harlan extracts security codes from Atlas's brain to get past Earth's defenses, then leaves her to die with Colonel Banks, the only other survivor of the mission. After Banks gives her his ARC neural interface device, Atlas remotely reactivates Smith, who comes to their rescue. Using salvaged parts from the other destroyed ARCs, Atlas upgrades Smith to stand a chance against the assembled AI forces. Atlas further reveals to Smith that Harlan killed her mother after the young Atlas—jealous of the attention her mother gave Harlan—gave Harlan an unfettered interface with her mind. Harlan, programmed to save humanity from risk, then saw that humanity's history of destructive behavior made it a risk to itself. Smith helps Atlas overcome her guilt for her actions, and with Banks sacrificing himself to clear their path, Atlas and Smith fight their way out and defeat Harlan in hand-to-hand combat, though Atlas is badly injured in the fight. A heavily damaged Smith shuts down before Atlas is rescued after repeated defibrillation.

Back on Earth, Atlas is informed that Harlan's complicated CPU will take years to analyze. Atlas, now a ranger, tests the newest model of ARC, created with her suggested modifications reminiscent of Smith. As she boots up the new ARC, its AI repeats a specific phrase Atlas made during the final moments of the mission, then jokingly tells her to guess its name, suggesting that the Smith sentience has survived. 

The film, released on Netflix on May 24, 2024, debuted at number one on Netflix's most popular English films list with 28.2 million views. It received mixed to negative critical reception, with a 19% approval rating on Rotten Tomatoes and a Metacritic score of 37 out of 100. Despite this, it achieved strong viewer completion rates and was praised for its performances and visual effects. The film is notable for being the first Netflix original film to feature a female lead in a major sci-fi role.
