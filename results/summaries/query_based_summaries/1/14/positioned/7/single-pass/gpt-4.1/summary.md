## Atlas (2024 film) — Summary

**Atlas** is a science fiction film released by Netflix on May 24, 2024, starring Jennifer Lopez as Atlas Shepherd, an analyst with a deep distrust of artificial intelligence. Set in 2071, the film follows Atlas as she joins a military mission to capture Harlan (Simu Liu), a rogue humanoid AI terrorist—who was originally created by Atlas's mother and has fled to a planet in the Andromeda Galaxy. Forced to interface with Smith, the onboard AI of a military mecha ("ARC"), Atlas gradually overcomes her hostility toward AI as they navigate hostile territory, attempt to thwart Harlan's plan to destroy most of humanity, and confront her own past guilt related to Harlan's rise. The story culminates in a battle to prevent Harlan from deploying carbon bombs to burn Earth's atmosphere. Atlas's bond with Smith is central, and the film ends with a hint that Smith's sentience may have survived in the next generation of ARC units.

The film was written by Leo Sardarian (with rewrites by Aron Eli Coleite) and directed with principal photography in Los Angeles and New Zealand. Upon release, Atlas topped Netflix's global charts, reaching 60 million views in less than two weeks and appearing in the platform’s Top 10 in 93 countries. Despite its popularity, critical response was largely negative: Rotten Tomatoes rated the film at 19% and Metacritic at 37/100, criticizing the writing and overall execution despite some praise for Lopez's performance and the film's visual effects. Lopez was nominated for a Golden Raspberry Award for Worst Actress, while the film also received a Golden Reel Award nomination for sound editing.
