**Atlas (2024 film)**

In *Atlas*, set in 2071, a rogue AI named Harlan, the descendant of a terrorist AI from 2043, escapes into outer space, evading human forces. Twenty-eight years later, the daughter of Harlan’s creator, Atlas Shepherd, seeks him out. After an AI agent is captured, Atlas discovers Harlan’s location and joins a military mission to capture him using AI-powered mecha called ARCs. Before the mission begins, Harlan’s drones attack their ship, forcing Atlas to enter an ARC herself. She gains control of the AI, Smith, and bonds with it, revealing her mother’s role in creating Harlan. After a confrontation with Harlan, who plans to destroy humanity, Atlas and Smith escape, defeating Harlan in hand-to-hand combat. The film ends with Atlas becoming an IMF agent and testing a new ARC model, hinting at Smith’s lingering sentience.
