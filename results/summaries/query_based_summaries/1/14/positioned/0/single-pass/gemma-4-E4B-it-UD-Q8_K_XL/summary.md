# Atlas (2024 film) Summary

*Atlas (2024 film)* is set in 2071, twenty-eight years after humanoid AI terrorist Harlan led a machine war against humans in 2043. Atlas Shepherd, an analyst who deeply distrusts artificial intelligence and is the daughter of Harlan's designer, searches for the fugitive. Upon discovering Harlan has fled to a planet in the Andromeda Galaxy, Atlas is forced to enter an AI-assisted mecha known as ARC during a military mission.

During the mission, Atlas gains basic control of the ARC, named Smith, and they begin to bond as she learns her mother created Harlan. After Atlas is captured and brought to Harlan, who plans to destroy humanity, she is eventually rescued by Smith. Atlas and Smith fight their way out, ultimately defeating Harlan in hand-to-hand combat. Upon returning to Earth, Atlas, now a ranger, tests a new ARC model, suggesting Smith's sentience may have survived.

The film, starring Jennifer Lopez, was released by Netflix on May 24, 2024. Critically, the film received a generally unfavorable reception, with Rotten Tomatoes scoring it at 19% positive reviews and Metacritic assigning it a score of 37 out of 100.
