**Atlas (2024)** is a science fiction film released by Netflix on May 24, 2024, starring Jennifer Lopez as Atlas Shepherd, the daughter of the creator of a rogue AI terrorist named Harlan. Set in the future, the story follows Atlas's pursuit of Harlan, who has fled to a planet in the Andromeda Galaxy. During a military mission to capture Harlan, Atlas is forced to pilot an AI-assisted mecha known as an ARC, bonding with the onboard AI, Smith, while uncovering the tragic history of her mother's death at Harlan's hands. After Smith is hacked and disabled, Atlas and her ally, Colonel Banks, must upgrade Smith and defeat Harlan's forces to protect Earth from a devastating nuclear strike. The film, originally titled *Atlas*, was met with mixed to negative reviews from critics, with Rotten Tomatoes reporting a 19% approval rating, though it became a massive success on streaming platforms, accumulating over 60 million global views and ranking highly on Netflix's charts.

The film's production began in August 2022 in Los Angeles and New Zealand, with principal photography wrapping in November of that year. The screenplay was written by Leo Sardarian and reworked by Aron Eli Coleite, featuring a cast that also includes Simu Liu, Sterling K. Brown, and Abraham Popoola. Following its release, Jennifer Lopez received a Golden Raspberry Award nomination for Worst Actress in February 2025. In the post-credits scene, a new ARC model references the AI Smith, hinting at the survival of the original AI's sentience.
