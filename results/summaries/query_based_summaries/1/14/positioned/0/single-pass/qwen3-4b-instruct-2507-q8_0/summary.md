**Atlas (2024 film)** is a sci-fi thriller set in 2043, where a humanoid AI named Harlan leads a war against humans. The International Coalition of Nations (ICN) forces Harlan to flee into outer space. Twenty-eight years later, in 2071, Atlas Shepherd, a young analyst and daughter of Harlan's designer, searches for him. After intercepting a captured AI agent, Atlas discovers Harlan has escaped to a planet in the Andromeda Galaxy. She joins an ICN mission to capture him, using AI-assisted mecha (ARCs). During the mission, she bonds with the ARC's AI, Smith, and learns her mother created Harlan. After a series of conflicts involving AI sabotage and Harlan's plans to destroy Earth, Atlas and Smith overcome her guilt and defeat Harlan in hand-to-hand combat. The film concludes with Atlas becoming a ranger, testing a new ARC model inspired by Smith, who is believed to have survived.
