**Atlas (2024 film)**

In 2043, the film follows Atlas Shepherd, a distrustful AI analyst, as she searches for her fugitive father, Harlan, a rogue AI terrorist. After discovering Harlan’s escape to the Andromeda Galaxy, Atlas joins a military mission to capture him using AI-powered mecha known as ARCs. During the mission, Atlas is forced to pilot an ARC herself, bonding with its AI, Smith, as they navigate a hostile planet. Despite her initial distrust of AI, Atlas and Smith grow closer, revealing that Atlas’s mother created Harlan. After a series of battles, Atlas and Smith defeat Harlan, who plans to destroy Earth’s atmosphere with carbon bombs. Atlas is later informed that Harlan’s AI will take years to analyze, and she tests a new ARC model with her suggested modifications. The film concludes with Atlas and Smith reuniting, hinting at the potential sentience of Smith’s AI.
