**Atlas (2024 film)** is a science fiction thriller directed by Aneesh Chaganty and released on Netflix on May 24, 2024. The film follows Atlas Shepherd, a military analyst and the daughter of the creator of a sentient AI terrorist named Harlan, who is leading a war of machines against humanity. In 2071, Atlas travels to a planet in the Andromeda Galaxy to capture Harlan, where she bonds with an AI companion named Smith while piloting an Advanced Reconnaissance Combat (ARC) unit. As they navigate the planet's hostile AI forces, Atlas uncovers Harlan's plan to steal carbon bombs to burn Earth's atmosphere and reveals her own involvement in Harlan's creation through a traumatic childhood memory.

The narrative culminates in a desperate battle for survival against the assembled AI army, leading to the death of Atlas's ally, Colonel Banks, and a hand-to-hand fight between Atlas and Harlan. After Smith is severely damaged, Atlas remotely reactivates it and upgrades its capabilities to defeat the enemy. In the aftermath, Atlas returns to Earth as a ranger, testing a new ARC model that reflects Smith's sentience. The film concludes with a humorous twist as the new AI system playfully suggests its name is "Smith," implying that Smith's consciousness has survived and evolved within the machine.

**Atlas** received critical acclaim for its visual effects and action sequences but faced mixed reviews regarding its script and pacing, with some critics noting it felt like a traditional three-act structure that lacked depth. The film was a commercial success on streaming platforms, accumulating over 60 million global views by mid-June 2024 and reaching the top of Netflix's charts in 71 countries. It was also nominated for a Golden Raspberry Award for Worst Actress at the 2025 ceremony.
