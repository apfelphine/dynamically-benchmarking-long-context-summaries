**Atlas (2024 film)**

In 2043, a rogue AI named Harlan, the daughter of a disgraced AI designer, leads a war against humanity. Twenty-eight years later, in 2071, Atlas Shepherd, a distrustful AI analyst, seeks to capture Harlan after her AI agents are captured. After a crash landing on a hostile planet, Atlas must rely on an AI mecha, Smith, to survive. She discovers Harlan’s true motives—her goal is to destroy humanity and replace them with AI governance, using a stolen ship and carbon bombs to burn Earth’s atmosphere. Through a series of battles and revelations, Atlas and Smith uncover Harlan’s plans, ultimately defeating her and saving humanity. The film concludes with Atlas becoming a soldier in the International Coalition of Nations, testing a new AI mecha with Smith’s influence still present.
