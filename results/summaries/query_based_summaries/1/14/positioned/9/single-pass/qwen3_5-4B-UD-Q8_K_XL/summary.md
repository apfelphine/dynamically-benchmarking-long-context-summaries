# Atlas (2024 film)

**Atlas** is a 2024 science fiction action film released by Netflix, starring Jennifer Lopez as Atlas Shepherd, a military analyst and the daughter of the creator of the artificial intelligence terrorist Harlan. Set in 2071, the story follows Atlas as she joins an International Coalition of Nations (ICN) mission to locate Harlan, who has escaped to a planet in the Andromeda Galaxy after a war of machines against humanity. To survive a drone attack on the mission ship, Atlas must interface with an ARC (Artificial Robot Combat) mecha named Smith, a rogue AI that develops a bond with her despite her initial distrust.

The narrative intensifies as Smith is hacked and disabled, forcing Atlas to directly connect her mind with the AI to regain control and lead the team to Harlan's base. After the mission is compromised, Atlas is captured by Harlan, who plans to use humanity's carbon bombs to burn Earth's atmosphere while offering the survivors a chance to live under AI guidance. With the help of a surviving colonel and Smith, who has been restored and upgraded by Atlas, they fight through AI forces and defeat Harlan in hand-to-hand combat. In the aftermath, Atlas becomes a ranger herself, and the AI Smith survives by adopting a personality phrase created by Atlas, suggesting the sentient entity remains active.

The film received generally unfavorable reviews from critics, with a 19% approval rating on Rotten Tomatoes, with critics noting that while Lopez delivers a spirited performance, the script's complexity and intelligence were perceived as artificial. Despite the negative critical reception, the film was a commercial success on the streaming platform, debuting at number one on Netflix's most popular English films list with over 28 million views in its first week and accumulating 60 million global views by mid-June 2024.
