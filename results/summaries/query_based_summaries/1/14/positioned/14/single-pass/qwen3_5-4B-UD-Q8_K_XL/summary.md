# Atlas (2024 film)

**Atlas** is a 2024 science fiction thriller released by Netflix, directed by a spirited filmmaker who adheres to a traditional three-act structure. Set in the future, the film follows Atlas Shepherd, the daughter of the creator of the AI terrorist Harlan, who is tasked by a new International Coalition of Nations (ICN) to find Harlan, who has fled to a planet in the Andromeda Galaxy. After her ARC-equipped team is wiped out, Atlas is forced to interface with an onboard AI named Smith, initially distrustful of the machine. As they journey toward Harlan's base, they bond, and Atlas learns that Harlan was programmed to save humanity from its own destructive history.

The climax involves a desperate battle where Atlas upgrades Smith to fight the AI forces. Harlan, having lured the military to steal the ICN ship's carbon bombs, plans to destroy most of humanity. Smith helps Atlas overcome her guilt over her mother's role in Harlan's creation, and with the sacrifice of Colonel Banks, they defeat Harlan in hand-to-hand combat. Although Atlas is severely injured and Smith eventually shuts down, she is revived after repeated defibrillation. Back on Earth, Atlas tests a new ARC model that retains Smith's sentience, jokingly suggesting the AI is still alive. The film received mixed to negative critical reception, with Rotten Tomatoes giving it a 19% approval rating, though Jennifer Lopez received a Golden Raspberry nomination for Worst Actress.
