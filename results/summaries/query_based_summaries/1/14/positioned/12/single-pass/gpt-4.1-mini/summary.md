**Atlas (2024 film)** is a science fiction movie set in a future where humanoid AI terrorist Harlan wages war against humanity. After Harlan is pushed into space by an international coalition, the story fast-forwards 28 years to 2071, focusing on Atlas Shepherd—an analyst deeply distrustful of AI and daughter to Harlan's creator. When Harlan escapes to a planet in the Andromeda Galaxy, Atlas joins a military mission equipped with mecha called ARCs to capture him. After crash-landing on the planet and bonding with her ARC’s AI named Smith, Atlas confronts Harlan’s plan to eradicate most humans using carbon bombs aboard a stolen ship. Despite betrayals and losses, Atlas and Smith work together to thwart Harlan’s scheme. Atlas survives and returns to Earth, where she helps develop a new ARC model hinting at Smith’s surviving sentience.

The film stars Jennifer Lopez as Atlas Shepherd, Simu Liu as Harlan Shepherd, Sterling K. Brown, and others. Production involved screenplay rewrites by Leo Sardarian and Aron Eli Coleite, with principal photography in Los Angeles and New Zealand from August to November 2022. Released on Netflix on May 24, 2024, *Atlas* quickly topped Netflix’s English films chart and appeared in the Top 10 in 93 countries. However, it received largely negative critical reviews, holding a 19% rating on Rotten Tomatoes with criticism focused on its script despite praise for Lopez’s performance.
