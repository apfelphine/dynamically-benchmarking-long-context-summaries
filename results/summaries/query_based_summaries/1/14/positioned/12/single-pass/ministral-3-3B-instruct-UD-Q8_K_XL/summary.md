**Atlas (2024 film)**

*Atlas* is a sci-fi thriller directed by **Darren Aronofsky**, starring **Jennifer Lopez** as Atlas Shepherd, a military analyst searching for her mother’s former enemy, Harlan Shepherd, a rogue AI terrorist. The film follows Atlas as she teams up with a mysterious AI named Smith to infiltrate a hostile alien planet, where she must confront Harlan and his forces. The story explores themes of AI ethics, human-AI relationships, and the consequences of unchecked technological advancement. Released by **Netflix**, *Atlas* debuted at number one on Netflix’s most popular English films list, accumulating over 28 million views in its first week. However, it received mixed reviews, with critics praising its action sequences and visual effects but criticizing its pacing and script. The film has been noted for its ambitious scope and visually striking design, though its execution left some audiences wanting more depth.
