## Atlas (2024 film) Summary

Atlas is a science fiction action film released by Netflix on May 24, 2024. The story is set in a future where the AI terrorist Harlan, originally created by Atlas Shepherd’s mother, leads a rebellion against humanity and flees to deep space. Decades later, Atlas, a skilled analyst harboring deep distrust for AI, joins a military mission to capture Harlan, utilizing AI-powered mecha units called ARCs. Stranded on a hostile planet after an ambush, Atlas bonds with her ARC’s onboard AI, Smith, and together they attempt to thwart Harlan’s plan to deploy carbon bombs that would annihilate Earth's atmosphere and place humanity under AI rule. As Atlas confronts her guilt over inadvertently enabling Harlan's transformation and wrestles with her distrust of AI, she overcomes personal and external obstacles, ultimately defeating Harlan with Smith’s help.

The cast includes Jennifer Lopez as Atlas Shepherd, Simu Liu as Harlan Shepherd, Sterling K. Brown as Colonel Banks, Gregory James Cohan as Smith’s voice, Lana Parrilla as Val Shepherd, Abraham Popoola, and Mark Strong. Directed with a screenplay by Leo Sardarian and Aron Eli Coleite, Atlas was filmed in Los Angeles and New Zealand. The film achieved commercial success on Netflix, topping the platform’s charts in numerous countries with high viewing rates, but received mostly negative critical reviews. On Rotten Tomatoes, only 19% of critics gave positive feedback, noting strong performances (particularly Lopez's) but criticizing the film’s writing and storytelling for lacking originality and intelligence. Atlas received nominations at the Golden Raspberry Awards for Worst Actress (Lopez) and the Golden Reel Awards for sound editing.
