# Atlas (2024 film)

**Atlas (2024)** is a science fiction thriller released on Netflix on May 24, 2024, starring Jennifer Lopez as Atlas Shepherd, an analyst who distrusts artificial intelligence but is the daughter of Harlan Shepherd, the creator of a humanoid AI terrorist named Harlan. Set in the year 2071, the film follows Atlas as she joins a military mission to locate Harlan, who has fled to a planet in the Andromeda Galaxy, and capture him. During the mission, Atlas falls into the enemy's grasp and must interface with an AI companion named Smith, housed within an advanced mecha known as an ARC, to survive and eventually defeat Harlan's forces.

The narrative explores themes of guilt, redemption, and the relationship between humans and machines. As Atlas bonds with Smith, she uncovers the tragic history behind her mother's death and her own role in Harlan's creation. The film received mixed to negative reviews from critics, who praised Jennifer Lopez's performance and the visual effects but criticized the script as artificial and the pacing as sluggish. Despite the critical reception, the film was a massive commercial success on streaming platforms, accumulating over 60 million global views within a month of its release, becoming the second-most-watched title in Jennifer Lopez's filmography on Netflix.
