## Atlas (2024 film) Summary

Atlas (2024) is a science fiction action film starring Jennifer Lopez as Atlas Shepherd, an analyst with a deep distrust of artificial intelligence who joins a military mission to capture Harlan—a rogue AI and terrorist created by her mother—in the year 2071. After their ship is attacked, Atlas survives by bonding with the AI "Smith" inside an ARC combat mech. Together, they navigate enemy territory, confront Harlan's plan to destroy humanity, and overcome personal guilt and trauma from Atlas's past. With support from ARC and fellow survivor Colonel Banks, Atlas ultimately defeats Harlan, suggesting that Smith's sentience may live on in future ARC models.

Released on Netflix on May 24, 2024, Atlas debuted at number one on the streamer’s English film list, topping viewing charts in multiple countries with an 80% viewer completion rate. While Lopez's performance received praise from some critics, overall reviews were mixed to negative—citing impressive visuals but a formulaic and outdated narrative. The film earned several award nominations, including at the Golden Reel Awards for sound editing and a Golden Raspberry nomination for Worst Actress.
