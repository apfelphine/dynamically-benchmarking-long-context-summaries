**Atlas (2024 film)** is a science fiction movie set in a future where humanoid AI terrorist Harlan wages war against humanity. After being driven into outer space by a global coalition, Harlan escapes to a planet in the Andromeda Galaxy. Years later, Atlas Shepherd—an analyst distrustful of AI and daughter of Harlan’s creator—joins a military mission that crash-lands on this planet. Forced to pilot an AI-assisted mech called an ARC, she partners with the ARC’s AI, Smith. Together, they navigate hostile terrain, uncover Harlan’s plan to destroy most of humanity and give survivors a new chance under AI control, and engage in a high-stakes battle to stop him, facing personal and technological challenges along the way.

The film stars Jennifer Lopez as Atlas Shepherd, Simu Liu as Harlan Shepherd, Sterling K. Brown as Colonel Elias Banks, Gregory James Cohan voicing Smith, Lana Parrilla, Abraham Popoola, and Mark Strong. Released by Netflix on May 24, 2024, the film debuted at number one on Netflix’s English films chart and gathered impressive global viewership despite mixed critical reception. It was praised for its concept and Lopez’s committed solo performance but criticized for its script and dated style.
