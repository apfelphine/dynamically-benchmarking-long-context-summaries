**Atlas (2024 film)** was released by Netflix on May 24, 2024. It performed strongly upon release, debuting at number one on Netflix's most popular English films list with 28.2 million views. By June 5, 2024, the film had accumulated 60 million global views, and the streamer reported an 80% viewer completion rate.

Critically, the film received mixed reviews; on Rotten Tomatoes, 19% of 110 critics' reviews were positive, with a consensus noting that Jennifer Lopez "admirably does her best to shoulder Atlas's seismic scope, but this sci-fi spectacle buckles under the weight of a script whose intelligence is merely artificial." Metacritic assigned the film a score of 37 out of 100, indicating "generally unfavorable" reviews. The film's cast included Jennifer Lopez as Atlas Shepherd, Simu Liu as Harlan Shepherd, Sterling K. Brown as Colonel Elias Banks, and Lana Parrilla as Val Shepherd.
