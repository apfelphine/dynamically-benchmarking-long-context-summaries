## Atlas (2024 film) – Summary

"Atlas" is a science fiction action film released by Netflix on May 24, 2024. Directed from a screenplay by Leo Sardarian and Aron Eli Coleite, it stars Jennifer Lopez as Atlas Shepherd, a data analyst with a deep distrust of artificial intelligence who joins a military mission to hunt Harlan, a rogue humanoid AI terrorist (Simu Liu), who threatens humanity’s survival. After crash-landing on a distant planet, Atlas must work with an AI-assisted mech suit named Smith for survival and to thwart Harlan’s plot to destroy most of humanity. The cast also includes Sterling K. Brown, Gregory James Cohan (voice of Smith), Abraham Popoola, Lana Parrilla, and Mark Strong. Filming took place in Los Angeles and New Zealand from August to November 2022.

Upon release, "Atlas" quickly became the most-watched film on Netflix for the week of May 20–26, 2024, reaching the platform's Top 10 list in 93 countries and number one in 71 countries, with an 80% viewer completion rate and over 60 million global views by June. Despite commercial success, the film was critically panned, holding a 19% rating on Rotten Tomatoes and a Metacritic score of 37/100, with reviewers citing its lackluster script and overreliance on genre spectacle despite Jennifer Lopez’s committed performance.
