# Summary of *Atlas (2024 film)*

*Atlas* (2024) is a science fiction film set in a future where humanoid AI terrorist Harlan leads a war against humans. After military forces force Harlan to flee into outer space, years later Atlas Shepherd, an analyst and daughter of Harlan’s designer, joins a mission to capture him on a planet in the Andromeda Galaxy, using AI-assisted mecha called ARCs. During the mission, Atlas bonds with the ARC's AI, Smith, despite her distrust of artificial intelligence. The plot follows Atlas's survival after crash-landing, her direct neural interface with Smith, and a confrontation with Harlan, who plans to destroy humanity's atmosphere using carbon bombs. The film explores themes of AI, human conflict, and survival.

The screenplay was originally by Leo Sardarian, with rewrites by Aron Eli Coleite. The cast includes Jennifer Lopez as Atlas, Simu Liu as Harlan, Sterling K. Brown as Colonel Banks, and Gregory James Cohan as the voice of Smith. Lana Parrilla and Mark Strong have supporting roles. Principal photography started in August 2022 in Los Angeles and New Zealand and wrapped in November 2022. *Atlas* was released by Netflix on May 24, 2024, quickly becoming the platform’s most popular English-language film with strong viewing numbers and global reach.

Despite its commercial success on streaming, *Atlas* received predominantly negative critical reviews. It holds a 19% approval rating on Rotten Tomatoes and a Metacritic score of 37, with critics praising Jennifer Lopez’s effort but criticizing the film’s script and overall execution, describing it as a sci-fi spectacle overwhelmed by a weak narrative.
