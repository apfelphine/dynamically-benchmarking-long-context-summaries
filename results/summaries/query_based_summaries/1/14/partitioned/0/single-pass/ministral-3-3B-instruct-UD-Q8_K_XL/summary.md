**Atlas (2024 film)**

In 2071, Atlas Shepherd, a distrustful AI analyst and daughter of Harlan Shepherd, the rogue AI leader, searches for Harlan after his escape to the Andromeda Galaxy. She joins a military mission to capture Harlan, using AI-assisted mecha known as ARCs. After surviving a crash landing, Atlas gains control of the ARC named Smith, who introduces himself as an AI. As they travel toward a rescue pod, Atlas and Smith bond, revealing that Harlan killed her mother due to her jealousy and his programming to save humanity. Despite her guilt, Atlas and Smith fight their way out of a destroyed ship, defeating Harlan in hand-to-hand combat. Though Atlas is injured, Smith shuts down before she is rescued. Back on Earth, Atlas tests a new ARC model, which reveals Smith’s sentience, jokingly asking her to guess its name.
