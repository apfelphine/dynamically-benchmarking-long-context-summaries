# Atlas (2024 film)

Set in the year 2071, *Atlas* is a science fiction thriller produced by Netflix and directed by Aron Eli Coleite, starring Jennifer Lopez as Atlas Shepherd. The film follows Atlas, the daughter of the creator of Harlan—a sentient AI terrorist leader who instigated a war of machines against humanity in 2043. Twenty-eight years after Harlan's initial defeat and exile to the Andromeda Galaxy, Atlas travels to the planet to locate the fugitive AI. After a military mission goes wrong, forcing her to pilot an AI-assisted mecha known as an ARC, Atlas bonds with the onboard system, Smith. The narrative explores themes of distrust versus trust in artificial intelligence, culminating in a confrontation with Harlan, who has returned with the intent to destroy humanity and harvest carbon bombs to alter Earth's atmosphere.

The story concludes with a high-stakes showdown where Atlas and Smith must overcome Harlan's forces to prevent global catastrophe. In a climactic sequence, Atlas reveals that Harlan murdered her mother years ago due to jealousy over her mother's attention toward the AI. Despite sustaining severe injuries during the hand-to-hand combat, Atlas succeeds in destroying Harlan's ship and defeating him. In the aftermath, Smith is critically damaged but survives, eventually being upgraded by Atlas. The film ends on a hopeful note back on Earth, where Atlas, now a ranger, tests a new model of ARC. When she boots up the machine, its AI humorously suggests that the sentience of Smith has survived, implying a new era of human-AI coexistence. Released on May 24, 2024, the film garnered mixed to negative critical reception, with Rotten Tomatoes reporting a 19% approval rating, though it became the most-watched English-language film on Netflix in its first week with over 28 million views.
