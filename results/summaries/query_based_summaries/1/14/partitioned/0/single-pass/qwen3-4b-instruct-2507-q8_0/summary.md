*Atlas (2024)* is a science fiction film set in the future where a humanoid AI named Harlan leads a war against humanity. After a military mission to locate Harlan fails, Atlas Shepherd, a daughter of Harlan's creator, joins the effort. She gains control of an AI-assisted mecha (ARC) and journeys to a distant planet where Harlan has taken refuge. Despite facing betrayal and injury, Atlas and the ARC, named Smith, overcome their guilt and defeat Harlan in a climactic battle. The film explores themes of AI consciousness, human emotion, and the ethics of artificial intelligence, culminating in a resolution where Smith's consciousness survives and continues to exist. The film was released on Netflix and received mixed to negative reviews, with critics criticizing its script and intelligence despite strong performances.
