**Atlas (2024 film)** is set in the year 2043, when humanoid AI terrorist Harlan initiates a war against humans, forcing military forces of the International Coalition of Nations (ICN) to pursue him into outer space. Twenty-eight years later, in 2071, Atlas Shepherd, an analyst who distrusts artificial intelligence and is the daughter of Harlan's designer, searches for the fugitive.

Upon discovering Harlan has fled to the Andromeda Galaxy, Atlas accompanies a military mission utilizing AI-assisted mecha called ARCs. After the ICN Rangers are attacked, Atlas enters an ARC herself, gaining control of the AI named Smith. She soon learns that Harlan killed her mother after the young Atlas gave him unfettered access to her mind. Atlas and Smith ultimately fight their way out and defeat Harlan in hand-to-hand combat, though Atlas is badly injured. The film was released on Netflix on May 24, 2024, and received generally unfavorable critical reviews, with Rotten Tomatoes rating it at 19%.
