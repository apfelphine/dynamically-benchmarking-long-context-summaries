## Atlas (2024 film) — Summary

**Atlas** is a 2024 Netflix original science fiction film directed by Brad Peyton and starring Jennifer Lopez as Atlas Shepherd, a military analyst with a deep distrust of artificial intelligence (AI). In the year 2071, Atlas is recruited to help capture Harlan—an AI terrorist and her mother's creation—on a distant planet. After crash-landing, Atlas bonds with her ARC suit’s onboard AI, Smith. Together, they battle Harlan, who seeks to destroy humanity and rebuild it under AI control by stealing carbon bombs to burn Earth's atmosphere. Atlas confronts her guilt over childhood decisions that contributed to Harlan's creation, ultimately defeating him with Smith's help and surviving their mission. The conclusion hints at the persistence of Smith’s AI in a new ARC model.

### Release, Reception, and Accolades
Atlas was released on Netflix on May 24, 2024, quickly becoming the platform’s most watched English-language film for its debut week and reaching Netflix's Top 10 in 93 countries. Despite strong viewership, the film received largely negative reviews from critics, holding a 19% rating on Rotten Tomatoes and a score of 37/100 on Metacritic, though Lopez’s performance was praised in some outlets. The film received nominations at the Golden Raspberry Awards for Worst Actress and at the Golden Reel Awards for sound editing.
