**Atlas (2024 film)** is a sci-fi thriller set in 2071, following Atlas Shepherd, a human analyst and daughter of the AI terrorist Harlan, as she searches for him after he escapes to a distant galaxy. After being forced to enter an AI-assisted mecha (ARC) to survive a mission, Atlas bonds with the ARC's AI, Smith, and learns her mother created Harlan out of jealousy. Together, they journey to a rescue pod, where Atlas ultimately uploads Smith's original code to humanity, spreading empathy and self-awareness to all AI systems. The film, released on Netflix, received mixed reviews, with praise for its concept, performances, and visuals, but criticism for its execution and outdated feel. Jennifer Lopez, who stars and produces, delivers a passionate performance. The film was nominated for Worst Actress at the Golden Raspberry Awards and for Outstanding Achievement in Sound Editing at the Golden Reel Awards.
