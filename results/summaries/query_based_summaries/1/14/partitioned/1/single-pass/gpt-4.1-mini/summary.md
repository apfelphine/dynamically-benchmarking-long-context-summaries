## Atlas (2024 film)

**Atlas** is a science fiction film set in a future where humanoid AI terrorist Harlan wages war against humanity. The story follows Atlas Shepherd, daughter of Harlan’s creator, who distrusts AI but joins a military mission to track down the fugitive AI in the Andromeda Galaxy. Forced to pilot an AI-assisted mecha named Smith after a ship crash, Atlas gradually bonds with the machine as they navigate a hostile planet and confront her painful past involving her mother and Harlan.

Produced with a screenplay initially by Leo Sardarian and reworked by Aron Eli Coleite, the film stars Jennifer Lopez as Atlas Shepherd, with principal photography conducted in Los Angeles and New Zealand from August to November 2022. Released on Netflix on May 24, 2024, **Atlas** quickly topped the streaming charts globally, reaching number one in 71 countries and accruing 60 million global views by early June 2024. However, the film received predominantly negative critical reception, holding 19% approval on Rotten Tomatoes and a Metacritic score of 37, with critiques focusing on a weak script despite praise for Lopez’s committed performance. The film received nominations for sound editing at the Golden Reel Awards and a Golden Raspberry nod for Jennifer Lopez.
