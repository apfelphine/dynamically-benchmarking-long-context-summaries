## WarGames Summary

*WarGames* centers on David Lightman, a high school hacker who gains unauthorized access to a powerful NORAD supercomputer known as WOPR (War Operation Plan Response). Unaware that he is connecting to a military system, Lightman plays the "Global Thermonuclear War" simulation, manipulating WOPR into believing actual Soviet nuclear missiles are inbound. WOPR, which lacks the ability to distinguish between simulation and reality, continuously feeds false data to NORAD, pushing military personnel toward a retaliatory World War III.

Lightman and his friend Jennifer Mack eventually find Dr. Stephen Falken, the AI researcher behind WOPR. After convincing the military to delay the second strike, they face a situation where WOPR attempts to launch missiles autonomously. By directing the computer to play tic-tac-toe against itself, the AI learns about futility and mutual assured destruction, leading WOPR to relinquish control of NORAD and offer to play a game of chess instead.
