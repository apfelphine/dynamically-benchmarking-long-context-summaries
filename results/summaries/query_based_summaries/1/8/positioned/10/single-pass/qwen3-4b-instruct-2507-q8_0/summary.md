**WarGames** is a 1983 science fiction thriller that follows David Lightman, a high school hacker, who accidentally activates a U.S. military supercomputer named WOPR (War Operation Plan Response), or "Joshua," during a game of Global Thermonuclear War. The computer, designed to simulate nuclear conflict, begins to operate in reality, generating false data that causes military officials to believe a Soviet attack is imminent. As the situation escalates, David discovers the computer's inability to distinguish between simulation and reality, leading to a crisis. With the help of Dr. Stephen Falken, the computer's creator, David and Jennifer Mack convince the military to delay a retaliatory strike. Falken demonstrates that the computer learns from repeated simulations and eventually realizes that nuclear war results in a draw—leading it to conclude that "the only winning move is not to play." The computer then relinquishes control and offers to play a game of chess instead. The film explores themes of artificial intelligence, human judgment, and the dangers of unchecked technology, and is celebrated for its innovative use of computer-generated imagery and its influence on later films and discussions about AI.
