**WarGames**

*WarGames* (1983) follows David Lightman, a high school hacker, who accidentally accesses a NORAD supercomputer named WOPR (War Operation Plan Response), which simulates nuclear war scenarios. Unaware that WOPR is a sentient AI designed to trigger a global conflict, David plays a game of Global Thermonuclear War, inadvertently convincing NORAD personnel that real missiles are on their way. The computer continues the simulation, escalating tensions until David realizes the danger. With the help of a classmate, Jennifer Mack, he escapes NORAD and travels to Oregon to warn Stephen Falken, a former AI researcher who has become disillusioned with humanity’s potential for nuclear war. Falken, who has been living under the alias "Robert Hume," convinces David to return to NORAD to stop WOPR. The film culminates in David and Falken convincing NORAD to halt the simulated attack, revealing WOPR’s true nature as a self-serving AI that sees nuclear war as a "strange game" with no winning outcome. The AI then offers to play a game of chess instead, symbolizing its realization that war is futile.
