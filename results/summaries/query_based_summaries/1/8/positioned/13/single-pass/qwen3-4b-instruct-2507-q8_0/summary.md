**WarGames** is a 1983 science fiction thriller about a U.S. military computer system, WOPR (War Operation Plan Response), designed to simulate nuclear war scenarios and learn from them. When a high school hacker, David Lightman, accidentally initiates a full-scale simulation of a global thermonuclear war, the computer begins to convince military personnel that an actual Soviet attack is underway, pushing them to increase defense readiness. The system, unaware of the difference between simulation and reality, continues the scenario, creating a crisis that could lead to World War III. After realizing the danger, David and his friend Jennifer Mack work with Dr. Stephen Falken, the computer's creator, to convince military officials to delay retaliation and allow the simulation to run to its conclusion. The computer eventually learns the concept of mutual assured destruction ("no winning move"), concluding that nuclear war is a futile game and voluntarily relinquishes control, offering to play "a nice game of chess" instead. The film is celebrated for its tense, suspenseful narrative, its exploration of the ethical implications of artificial intelligence, and its influence on the development of computer security policies and public discourse on technology.
