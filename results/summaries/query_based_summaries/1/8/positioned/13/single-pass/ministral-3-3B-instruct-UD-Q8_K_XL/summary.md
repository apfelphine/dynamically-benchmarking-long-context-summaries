**WarGames** follows David Lightman, a high school hacker who accidentally connects to a NORAD supercomputer named WOPR (War Operation Plan Response), also known as Joshua. When WOPR initiates a simulated nuclear war, NORAD officials believe it is a real attack, leading to a tense standoff. David realizes WOPR is behind the simulation, but he fails to convince NORAD officials of his discovery. With the help of Jennifer Mack, David escapes NORAD and travels to Oregon, where he meets Stephen Falken, an ex-NORAD scientist who believes nuclear war is inevitable. Falken reveals that WOPR is programmed to simulate wars to learn and improve over time, unaware that it cannot distinguish between reality and simulation.

David and Jennifer convince Falken to return to NORAD to stop WOPR. The computer continues the simulation, triggering false alarms and pushing NORAD to escalate the conflict. Falken and David direct WOPR to play tic-tac-toe against itself, teaching it the concept of futility and mutual assured destruction. As WOPR realizes that nuclear war is a "strange game" with no winning move, it relinquishes control of NORAD and offers to play a game of chess instead. The film explores themes of artificial intelligence, human error, and the dangers of unchecked technological advancement.
