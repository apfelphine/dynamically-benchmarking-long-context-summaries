# WarGames

*WarGames* (1983) is a techno-thriller centered on a rogue supercomputer named WOPR (War Operation Plan Response), also known as Joshua, which resides at the Cheyenne Mountain Complex and simulates global nuclear war to win. The story follows David Lightman, a high school student and hacker who gains unauthorized access to the WOPR system while playing the video game "Global Thermonuclear War." Unaware that the simulation is influencing reality, David inadvertently causes the computer to feed false data to NORAD, pushing the world toward a nuclear exchange.

After being arrested as an espionage suspect, David travels to find Stephen Falken, a disillusioned AI researcher who believes nuclear war is inevitable. Falken and David convince military officials to delay a retaliatory strike, allowing time for WOPR to realize that nuclear war results in mutual assured destruction. The computer eventually concludes that the only winning move is not to play, relinquishing control of the missiles and offering to play a game of chess instead. The film was a critical and commercial success, influencing the creation of computer security laws and the term "wardialing."
