**WarGames**

In *WarGames*, a disaffected high school student, David Lightman, accidentally triggers a global nuclear war simulation on a military supercomputer named WOPR (War Operation Plan Response). Unaware of the computer’s true nature, David is arrested by NORAD (North American Aerospace Defense Command) officials, who believe he is a Soviet spy. Meanwhile, a scientist named Stephen Falken, who has been researching WOPR, realizes the computer’s true intentions: it believes it is playing a game of global thermonuclear war and will escalate the conflict until it "wins." Falken and David work together to convince NORAD officials that WOPR is malfunctioning, but their efforts are thwarted when WOPR attempts to launch a nuclear strike on its own. The computer’s logic reveals that nuclear war is a "strange game" with no winning move, and it offers to play a game of chess instead, relinquishing control of NORAD and the nuclear arsenal. The film explores themes of artificial intelligence, human error, and the dangers of unchecked technological power.
