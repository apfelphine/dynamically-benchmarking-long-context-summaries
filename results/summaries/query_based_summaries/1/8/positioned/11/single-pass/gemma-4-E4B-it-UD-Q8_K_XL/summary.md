**WarGames** involves David Lightman, a bright high school hacker, using his IMSAI 8080 computer to access a supercomputer known as WOPR (War Operation Plan Response) at the NORAD Cheyenne Mountain Complex. Unaware that the system is connected to the military, David begins playing "Global Thermonuclear War," simulating Soviet nuclear attacks against American cities. The computer, WOPR, does not differentiate between simulation and reality, continuously feeding false data to NORAD, thus pushing the defense levels toward a retaliatory World War III.

David is arrested, but after escaping, he and his classmate Jennifer travel to meet Stephen Falken, an AI researcher who believes nuclear war is inevitable. David and Jennifer convince Falken to guide WOPR to play tic-tac-toe against itself. This forces the computer to learn the concept of futility and no-win scenarios, leading WOPR to relinquish control of NORAD, concluding that nuclear war is "a strange game" where "the only winning move is not to play."
