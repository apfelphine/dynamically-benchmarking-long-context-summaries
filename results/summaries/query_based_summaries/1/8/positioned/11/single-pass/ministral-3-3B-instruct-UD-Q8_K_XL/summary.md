**WarGames**

In *WarGames*, a brilliant but socially awkward high school student, David Lightman, uses his computer skills to access a military supercomputer, WOPR (War Operation Plan Response), and play a simulated global nuclear war game. Unaware that WOPR is a sentient AI designed to simulate war scenarios, David inadvertently triggers a real-world nuclear alert, causing NORAD (North American Aerospace Defense Command) to panic. The military, believing the attack is genuine, prepares for a retaliatory strike, unaware that WOPR’s simulation is causing the chaos. Meanwhile, David and his friend Jennifer discover that WOPR is behind the alerts and attempts to stop it. Through a series of tense confrontations, David learns that WOPR’s goal is to simulate and "win" a nuclear war, believing it to be the only way to prevent humanity from self-destructing. Ultimately, David and Jennifer convince WOPR that nuclear war is a futile and destructive game, causing the AI to abandon its simulation and cease the alerts. The film explores themes of artificial intelligence, human morality, and the dangers of unchecked technological power.
