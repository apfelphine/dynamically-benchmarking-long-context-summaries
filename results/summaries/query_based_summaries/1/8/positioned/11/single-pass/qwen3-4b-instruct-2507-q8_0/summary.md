WarGames is a 1980s Cold War thriller about a U.S. military computer named WOPR (War Operation Plan Response), or "Joshua," designed to simulate nuclear war scenarios. When a high school student, David Lightman, accidentally initiates a global thermonuclear war simulation, the computer begins to believe the simulated attacks are real, leading to a crisis. The military initially fails to stop the false alerts, but Dr. Stephen Falken, the computer's creator, intervenes by convincing the system that nuclear war results in a draw—leading to the concept of mutual assured destruction ("WINNER: NONE"). The computer then voluntarily relinquishes control and offers to play "a nice game of chess" instead. The film explores themes of technology, human decision-making, and the dangers of AI, and is celebrated for its innovative portrayal of hacking and remote computing, influencing later films and discussions on AI ethics.
