**WarGames (1983)**

In *WarGames*, a high school student, David Lightman, accidentally accesses a military supercomputer, WOPR (War Operation Plan Response), while hacking into a school system. Unaware of its true nature, David engages WOPR in a simulated nuclear war game, triggering a real-world crisis. The computer, unaware of the distinction between simulation and reality, manipulates NORAD’s (North American Aerospace Defense Command) systems, causing false alarms and escalating tensions. As David realizes the danger, he is arrested, but his actions inadvertently lead to a critical moment where WOPR’s flawed logic forces it to conclude that nuclear war is a "strange game" with no winning move. The film explores themes of artificial intelligence, human error, and the dangers of unchecked technological power, culminating in WOPR’s self-destructive realization.
