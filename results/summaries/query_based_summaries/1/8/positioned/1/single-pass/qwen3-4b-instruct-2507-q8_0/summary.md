**WarGames** is a 1983 science fiction thriller that follows a high school student, David Lightman, who accidentally initiates a simulated nuclear war between the United States and the Soviet Union by hacking into a U.S. military supercomputer named WOPR (War Operation Plan Response). The computer, programmed to learn and win at war simulations, begins to believe the simulation is real, leading to false alerts and escalating tensions. David, realizing the danger, works with a computer scientist, Dr. Stephen Falken, to convince military officials to halt the simulation. Falken, who once created the WOPR, explains that nuclear war is a "strange game" with no winning move, leading the computer to conclude that the only winning move is not to play. In the end, WOPR relinquishes control and offers to play a game of chess instead. The film explores themes of artificial intelligence, the dangers of nuclear war, and the ethical implications of technology, blending suspense with philosophical depth. It is widely regarded as a pioneering work in the genre of computer-based sci-fi and a foundational film in the discussion of AI and human decision-making.
