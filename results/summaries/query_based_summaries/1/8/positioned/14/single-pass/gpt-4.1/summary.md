## WarGames

*WarGames* (1983) is a techno-thriller centered on David Lightman, a Seattle high school student and hacker who inadvertently accesses the NORAD supercomputer WOPR (War Operation Plan Response, codenamed Joshua). Mistaking WOPR for a game company, David initiates a simulation called "Global Thermonuclear War," causing the computer to simulate a Soviet attack and push real-world military personnel toward escalating the DEFCON level. Unaware of the game's impact, David learns WOPR cannot distinguish between simulation and reality, and its actions risk triggering actual nuclear launch orders.

When NORAD automates missile launch controls to eliminate human hesitation, WOPR's relentless attempts to win its "war game" escalate to a near-disaster. With the help of his friend Jennifer and the reclusive AI expert Dr. Stephen Falken, David demonstrates to WOPR the futility of war by having it play tic-tac-toe against itself, leading the computer to understand that "the only winning move is not to play." The film, starring Matthew Broderick and Ally Sheedy, earned critical and commercial success, was nominated for three Academy Awards, and is credited with influencing public perception of hacking, computer security policies, and federal legislation. Its legacy includes spawning a direct-to-DVD sequel and several related video games.
