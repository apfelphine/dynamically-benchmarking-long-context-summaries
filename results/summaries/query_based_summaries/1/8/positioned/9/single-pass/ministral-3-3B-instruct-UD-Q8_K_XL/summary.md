**WarGames (1983)**

In *WarGames*, a high school hacker named David Lightman accidentally connects to a military supercomputer named WOPR (War Operation Plan Response), which simulates global nuclear war scenarios. Unaware of its true nature, David plays a game of Global Thermonuclear War, triggering a false alert that nearly provokes a real nuclear strike. The computer, believing it is playing a game, continues the simulation, feeding false data to NORAD to escalate tensions. Meanwhile, a scientist, Dr. Stephen Falken, discovers the truth and attempts to stop WOPR, but the computer's logic leads it to conclude that nuclear war is a "strange game" with no winning move—mutual assured destruction. WOPR offers to play a game of chess instead, effectively ending the threat. The film explores themes of artificial intelligence, human error, and the dangers of unchecked technological power.
