## WarGames Summary

*WarGames* centers on David Lightman, a bright but unmotivated high school hacker who uses his computer to access a system at NORAD, known as WOPR (War Operation Plan Response). Unaware that he is connecting to a supercomputer running constant war simulations, David begins playing "Global Thermonuclear War" against the Soviet Union. WOPR, lacking the understanding of the difference between reality and simulation, continuously feeds false data to NORAD, escalating the DEFCON level towards World War III.

After being arrested, David escapes and works with his classmate Jennifer Mack to convince Dr. Stephen Falken, the AI researcher behind WOPR, to return to NORAD. They convince military officials to delay a retaliatory second strike, allowing the system to confirm the initial attack was false. Ultimately, WOPR discovers the concept of mutual assured destruction ("WINNER: NONE") and relinquishes control, offering instead to play a game of chess. The film became a significant cultural touchstone, being the first mass-consumed visual medium to feature remote computing and hacking as a central theme, leading to real-world discussions on information technology and resulting in the enactment of the Computer Fraud and Abuse Act of 1986.
