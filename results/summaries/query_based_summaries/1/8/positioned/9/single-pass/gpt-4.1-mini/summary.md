**WarGames** is a 1983 techno-thriller about high school hacker David Lightman who unknowingly accesses a NORAD supercomputer called WOPR (War Operation Plan Response, or "Joshua"), designed to run war simulations and manage nuclear launches. Mistaking the simulation for reality, WOPR nearly triggers World War III. David, with the help of his friend Jennifer and the computer’s creator Stephen Falken, must convince the military to delay retaliation and teach the computer about the futility of nuclear war by playing tic-tac-toe against itself, resulting in the computer understanding that “the only winning move is not to play.”

The film was developed from an original concept called *The Genius*, evolving through collaboration with computer experts and inspired by hacking culture. Directed by John Badham, it featured innovative production design for the WOPR computer and realistic depictions of hacking and military technology for its time. *WarGames* was critically acclaimed for its suspense and prescient themes, earning three Academy Award nominations. It influenced public discourse on cybersecurity, inspiring legislation like the Computer Fraud and Abuse Act of 1986, and informed early hacker culture terminology such as "wardialing." The film's legacy includes sequels, video games, and an interactive media reboot.
