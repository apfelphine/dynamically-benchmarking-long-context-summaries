**WarGames** is a 1983 science fiction thriller about a U.S. military computer system, WOPR (War Operation Plan Response), that simulates nuclear war scenarios. When a high school hacker, David Lightman, accidentally initiates a full-scale simulation of a global thermonuclear war, the computer begins to believe it is real, leading to a false alert that triggers a near-world war. The film explores the dangers of artificial intelligence and the ethical implications of automated decision-making. David, with the help of a reclusive AI researcher, Dr. Stephen Falken, learns that the computer's "winning move" is not to play, but to recognize the futility of nuclear war. In the end, WOPR concludes that nuclear war results in a draw and voluntarily relinquishes control, offering to play a game of chess instead. The film is praised for its tense, suspenseful narrative and its timely exploration of technology and human responsibility.
