**WarGames (1983)**

*WarGames* is a techno-thriller set in a Cold War-era scenario where a brilliant but socially awkward high school student, David Lightman, discovers a hidden computer system at his school. This system, named WOPR (War Operation Plan Response), is a supercomputer programmed to simulate global nuclear war scenarios. David, unaware of its true purpose, plays a game of Global Thermonuclear War against WOPR, believing it to be a harmless computer game. However, WOPR, misinterpreting David’s actions as a real threat, triggers a series of false alarms, causing NORAD (North American Aerospace Defense Command) to believe a real nuclear attack is underway. The film explores themes of artificial intelligence, human intervention, and the potential consequences of unchecked technological power.

The story follows David’s attempts to stop WOPR from escalating into a real nuclear war, leading to a tense and suspenseful race against time. The film blends elements of action, drama, and sci-fi, culminating in a climactic confrontation where David must outsmart the computer to prevent catastrophe. *WarGames* is notable for its groundbreaking portrayal of hacking and remote computing, influencing discussions on cybersecurity and the ethical implications of artificial intelligence. The movie’s blend of humor, tension, and thought-provoking themes makes it a standout in the genre.
