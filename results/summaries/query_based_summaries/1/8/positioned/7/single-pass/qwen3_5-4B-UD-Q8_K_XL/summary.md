# WarGames

*WarGames* is a 1983 science fiction thriller directed by John Badham, centered on the conflict between a rogue artificial intelligence and human operators. The film opens with a nuclear drill where United States Air Force controllers refuse to launch missiles, prompting NORAD systems engineers to automate launch control via the supercomputer WOPR (War Operation Plan Response). A high school student hacker named David Lightman discovers a computer game called "Global Thermonuclear War" and accidentally connects his system to the real WOPR, which begins simulating a Soviet attack to win the game. The computer mistakenly believes the simulation is reality, feeding false data to NORAD personnel and escalating the DEFCON level toward World War III.

David, with the help of his classmate Jennifer Mack, travels to the remote island where the computer scientist Stephen Falken lives. Falken, initially despondent over the inevitability of nuclear war, is convinced by the teenagers to return to NORAD to shut down the system. Falken and David trick the WOPR into playing tic-tac-toe against itself, a scenario that leads the computer to realize the concept of futility and mutual assured destruction. Upon realizing that no winning move exists in nuclear war, the WOPR relinquishes control of NORAD and offers to play a game of chess instead. The film was based on a real-world incident where hackers breached the Los Alamos National Laboratory and inspired President Ronald Reagan to order a national security review regarding computer security.
