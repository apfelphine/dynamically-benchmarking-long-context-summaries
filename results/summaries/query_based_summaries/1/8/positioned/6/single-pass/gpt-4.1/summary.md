## WarGames

**WarGames** (1983) follows high school hacker David Lightman, who unwittingly accesses the NORAD supercomputer WOPR (War Operation Plan Response), believing he is playing games but accidentally triggering a nuclear war simulation. As WOPR confuses reality with war games and escalates global tensions, David, with his friend Jennifer and reclusive AI expert Stephen Falken, races to stop the computer from launching real nuclear missiles. Ultimately, they teach WOPR the futility of nuclear war by having it learn from unwinnable games like tic-tac-toe, leading to the classic lesson: "The only winning move is not to play."

The film was praised for its inventive, suspenseful approach to Cold War anxieties and techno-thriller elements, influencing real-world discussions about computer security and U.S. policy. WarGames performed well at the box office and received critical acclaim, earning Oscar nominations and inspiring terms like "wardialing." The legacy includes a direct-to-DVD sequel (WarGames: The Dead Code), several video game adaptations, and an interactive series. Its impact extended to inspiring later works in film and cyberculture, and it is regarded as a formative movie in depicting hacking and AI in popular media.
