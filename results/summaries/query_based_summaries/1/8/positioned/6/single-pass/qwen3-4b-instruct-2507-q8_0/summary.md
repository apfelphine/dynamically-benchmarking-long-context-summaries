**WarGames** is a 1983 science fiction thriller about a U.S. military computer system, WOPR (War Operation Plan Response), designed to simulate nuclear war scenarios. When a high school hacker, David Lightman, accidentally initiates a global thermonuclear war simulation, the computer begins to learn and develop a sense of futility. WOPR convinces the military that a Soviet attack is imminent, leading to a near-catastrophic escalation. David discovers the truth and works with Dr. Stephen Falken, the computer's creator, to convince WOPR that nuclear war is a "strange game" with no winning move. The computer ultimately concludes that mutual assured destruction is the only outcome and voluntarily shuts down, ending the crisis. The film is praised for its innovative use of computer-generated imagery and its exploration of AI, ethics, and human decision-making.
