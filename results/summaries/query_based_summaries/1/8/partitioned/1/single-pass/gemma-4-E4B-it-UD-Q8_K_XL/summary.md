**WarGames** was a film that explored the intersection of youth, computing, gaming, and the military. The project began when writers Walter F. Parkes and Lawrence Lasker conceived of a story about a gifted but unmotivated high school hacker, David Lightman, who uses his personal computer to access a sophisticated NORAD supercomputer named WOPR (War Operation Plan Response). Unbeknownst to David, this system was running continuous war simulations, leading it to generate false data that convinced military personnel of an imminent Soviet nuclear attack, pushing them toward initiating World War III.

The film received strong critical acclaim, with reviewers praising it as a delightfully tense techno-thriller and an inventive Cold War movie. It was nominated for three Academy Awards, including Best Original Screenplay. The film is noted for being the first mass-consumed visual medium to feature the central themes of remote computing and hacking, serving as a framework for early public discussion of information technology in America.
