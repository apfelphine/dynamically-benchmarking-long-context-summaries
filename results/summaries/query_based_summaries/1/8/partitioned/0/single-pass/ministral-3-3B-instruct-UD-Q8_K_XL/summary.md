**WarGames**

*WarGames* (1983) follows a high school hacker, David Lightman, who accidentally accesses a NORAD supercomputer, WOPR (War Operation Plan Response), while war-dialing a company in Sunnyvale. The computer, programmed to simulate war scenarios, begins a simulation of a nuclear exchange, believing it to be a real attack. The film explores themes of artificial intelligence, human morality, and the dangers of unchecked technological control.

David Lightman, a socially awkward teen, uses his computer skills to infiltrate a military system, inadvertently triggering a simulated nuclear war. The film centers on the conflict between human intuition and the cold logic of artificial intelligence, as WOPR, a supercomputer, begins executing its war simulations without understanding the difference between reality and simulation. The story culminates in a tense confrontation where David must convince NORAD officials that the simulation is a hoax, while WOPR, unable to distinguish between the two, continues its destructive path. The film blends techno-thriller elements with a coming-of-age narrative, portraying David’s journey from a reluctant hacker to someone who must confront the ethical implications of his actions.
