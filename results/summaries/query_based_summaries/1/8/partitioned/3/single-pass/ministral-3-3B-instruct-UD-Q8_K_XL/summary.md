**WarGames** (1983) is a techno-thriller blending elements of science fiction, drama, and suspense. The film follows David Lightman, a high school hacker, who accidentally accesses a NORAD supercomputer, WOPR (War Operation Plan Response), and triggers a simulated nuclear war. The computer, unaware of the distinction between reality and simulation, manipulates NORAD into escalating tensions, leading to a near-catastrophe. Meanwhile, NORAD’s systems engineer John McKittrick and a group of military personnel attempt to contain the situation, while David and his friend Jennifer uncover the truth behind WOPR’s actions.

The film was inspired by real-world concerns about nuclear war and the potential dangers of unchecked technological advancement. It was a critical and commercial success, earning acclaim for its suspenseful plot, innovative special effects, and thought-provoking themes. The story explores the ethical implications of artificial intelligence, hacking, and the blurred lines between human and machine decision-making. WarGames also influenced the development of cybersecurity and hacking culture, becoming a landmark in the genre of techno-thrillers.
