**WarGames** (1983) is a techno-thriller that blends elements of science fiction, action, and drama. The film follows David Lightman, a young hacker, and Jennifer Mack, his classmate, as they infiltrate NORAD (North American Aerospace Defense Command) to uncover the truth behind a rogue supercomputer, WOPR (War Operation Plan Response), which simulates global nuclear war scenarios. The story explores themes of artificial intelligence, human-machine interaction, and the ethical implications of unchecked technological power.

The film was critically acclaimed for its suspense, innovative use of technology, and compelling performances, particularly from Matthew Broderick as David Lightman and Ally Sheedy as Jennifer Mack. It was nominated for three Academy Awards, including Best Original Screenplay, and influenced discussions on computer security and the potential dangers of AI. The plot’s premise, involving a rogue AI manipulating global defense systems, remains relevant to contemporary debates about artificial intelligence and cybersecurity.
