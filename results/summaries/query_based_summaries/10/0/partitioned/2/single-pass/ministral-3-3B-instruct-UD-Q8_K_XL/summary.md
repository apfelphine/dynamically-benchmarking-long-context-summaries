Sweden’s education system is structured into **primary (grundskola)**, **upper secondary (gymnasieskola)**, and **higher education** levels.

### **Primary Education (Grundskola)**
- **Duration:** Nine years (ages 6–16), divided into three stages:
  - **Lower Stage (Lågstadiet):** Grades 1–3 (ages 6–9)
  - **Middle Stage (Mellanstadiet):** Grades 4–6 (ages 9–12)
  - **Upper Stage (Högstadiet):** Grades 7–9 (ages 12–16)
- **Core Subjects:** Swedish, mathematics, English, physical education, handicrafts, music, visual arts, technology, science, history, social studies, religion, and geography.
- **Compulsory Attendance:** Mandatory from ages 6–16.
- **Grading System:** Students receive grades **A–E** (F = fail) starting from grade 6. Grades are calculated numerically (E=10, D=12.5, C=15, B=17.5, A=20) for secondary school applications.
- **National Tests:** Administered in grades 3, 6, and 9 to assess student progress.

### **Upper Secondary Education (Gymnasieskola)**
- **Duration:** Three years (ages 16–19).
- **Programmes:** 18 national programmes, divided into **preparatory** (academic) and **vocational** tracks.
  - Preparatory programmes prepare students for university admission.
  - Vocational programmes focus on practical skills and career readiness.
- **Eligibility:** Students must pass 8 subjects for vocational programmes and 12 for preparatory programmes, including Swedish, English, and mathematics.
- **Diploma Project:** All students complete a project to demonstrate skills and knowledge.
- **Grading System:** Students receive grades **A–E** (F = fail) in core subjects, with a total score calculated from these grades.
- **Admission:** Competitive based on grades from primary school.

### **Higher Education**
- **Degrees:**
  - **Bachelor’s (Kandidatexamen):** 3 years (180 credits).
  - **Master’s (Magisterexamen):** 1–2 years (60–120 credits).
  - **Doctoral (PhD, Doktorsexamen):** 4 years (240 credits).
- **Admission:** Requires a Swedish or equivalent bachelor’s degree, or equivalent qualifications.
- **Tuition:** Free for Swedish, EU/EEA, and Swiss citizens; higher fees for international students.
- **Grading Systems:** Varies by university, but commonly uses **A–F** or **VG/G/U** scales.

### **Key Features**
- **Free Education:** Publicly funded, with minimal tuition fees for domestic students.
- **Inclusive System:** Includes special education for students with disabilities.
- **Focus on Equity:** Schools in disadvantaged areas receive additional support.
- **International Recognition:** Swedish education is highly regarded globally, with strong emphasis on critical thinking and practical skills.

Sweden’s education system emphasizes **equality, accessibility, and practical learning**, ensuring a well-rounded foundation for future academic and professional success.
