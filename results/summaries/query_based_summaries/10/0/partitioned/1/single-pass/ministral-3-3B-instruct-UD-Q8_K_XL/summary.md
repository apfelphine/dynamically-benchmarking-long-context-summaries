Sweden’s education system is structured into **preschool, compulsory primary (grundskola), upper secondary (gymnasieskola), and higher education** levels.

### **Preschool (Förskola)**
- **Eligibility:** All children aged 1–6 can attend preschool, with free education for those aged 3–6.
- **Duration:** Typically 2–4 years, focusing on early childhood development through play-based learning.
- **Cost:** Fees for children under 3 or attending more than 3 hours/day vary by family income, ranging from free to up to **€150/month**.

### **Compulsory Primary Education (Grundskola)**
- **Duration:** Nine years (ages 6–15), divided into three stages:
  - **Lower stage (Lågstadiet):** Grades 1–3 (ages 6–9)
  - **Middle stage (Mellanstadiet):** Grades 4–6 (ages 10–12)
  - **Upper stage (Högstadiet):** Grades 7–9 (ages 13–15)
- **Curriculum:** Covers core subjects like **Swedish, mathematics, English, physical education, and religion**, with a focus on foundational knowledge.
- **Grading:** Students receive grades from **A (best) to F (fail)**, with **E–D** as passing grades and **B/D** as intermediate grades. Grades start from **Grade 6** (previously Grade 4).

### **Upper Secondary Education (Gymnasieskola)**
- **Duration:** Three years (ages 16–19).
- **Programmes:** Offered in **preparatory** (academic) and **vocational** tracks, including:
  - **Social Science Programme** (Samhällsvetenskapsprogrammet)
  - **Business Management Programme** (Ekonomiprogrammet)
  - **Natural Science Programme** (Naturvetenskapsprogrammet)
  - **Vocational programmes** (e.g., Building and Construction, Health and Social Care)
- **Eligibility:** Students must pass **12 compulsory subjects** (including Swedish, English, and mathematics) for preparatory programmes or **8 compulsory subjects** for vocational programmes.
- **Grading:** Students must complete a **Diploma Project** (pass/fail) and receive grades in core subjects. The final score is calculated based on subject grades (e.g., **A=20, C=15, E=10**).
- **Admission:** Based on **elementary school grades** and, in some cases, entrance exams.

### **Introductory Programmes (Introduktionsprogram)**
- For students who do not meet eligibility requirements for upper secondary education, offering **language introduction (Swedish)** or individualized support.

### **Higher Education**
- **Degrees:** Follows the **Bologna Process** framework:
  - **Bachelor’s (Kandidatexamen):** 3 years (180 credits)
  - **Master’s (Magisterexamen):** 2 years (120 credits)
  - **Doctoral (PhD, Doktorsexamen):** 4 years (240 credits)
- **Admission:** Requires completion of **gymnasieskola** or equivalent. Some programmes require **SweSAT** (Swedish Scholastic Aptitude Test) scores.
- **Cost:** Higher education is **free for EU/EEA/Swiss citizens** (as of 2011), with financial aid available for others.

### **Key Features**
- **Free education** at all levels (except private schools).
- **Strong emphasis on equality** and inclusive learning environments.
- **Grading system** based on **criteria-referenced** standards.
- **Vocational and academic pathways** to prepare students for further education or the workforce.

Sweden’s education system is known for its **equality-focused approach**, **high standards**, and **flexible pathways** to ensure all students have access to quality education.
