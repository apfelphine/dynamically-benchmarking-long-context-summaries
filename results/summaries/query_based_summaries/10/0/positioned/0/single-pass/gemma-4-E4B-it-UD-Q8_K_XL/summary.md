## Education in Sweden

Education in Sweden encompasses various levels, ranging from preschool to postgraduate studies. Preschool is available for all children from age one, with free education guaranteed for at least three hours daily starting at age three.

The compulsory education system is a nine-year comprehensive school (*grundskola*), mandatory for ages six to sixteen. This system covers sixteen compulsory subjects across three stages: lower, middle, and upper stage. Students begin receiving grades from year six, using grades A, B, C, D, and E for passing, with F being a failing grade. National tests are administered in grades 3, 6, and 9 to ensure equivalent assessment.

Post-compulsory education includes upper secondary school (*gymnasieskola*), which usually lasts three years and offers both preparatory and vocational national programmes. For higher education, degrees are structured according to the Bologna Process into three cycles: Bachelor's level, Master's level, and Doctoral level, offered through public universities and university colleges.

## Independent and Private Education

Beyond the public system, Sweden has independent schools (*friskolor*), which are publicly funded based on enrollment, and private schools. These independent schools can offer alternative pedagogies or specialized profiles. Higher education is provided by public universities and university colleges, offering general academic degrees alongside specialized professional degrees, including higher vocational education which operates independently of the Bologna Process framework.
