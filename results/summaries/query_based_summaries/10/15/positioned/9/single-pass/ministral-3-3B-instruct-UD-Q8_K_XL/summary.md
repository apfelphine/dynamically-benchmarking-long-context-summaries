The education system in **Norway** is structured into three main stages: **primary (barneskole, grades 1–7)**, **lower secondary (ungdomsskole, grades 8–10)**, and **upper secondary (videregående skole, grades VG1–VG3)**. Education is compulsory from ages 6 to 16, with the first two stages being free and mandatory. The curriculum emphasizes secular values, critical thinking, and practical skills, though religious instruction is also included.

Upper secondary education is divided into general studies (preparatory for university) and vocational studies (combining classroom training with apprenticeships). Students must pass national exams to graduate, with a focus on academic rigor. Higher education is free for citizens and includes public universities and university colleges, offering bachelor’s, master’s, and doctoral degrees. The system prioritizes equality, with resources distributed based on regional needs, though disparities persist in rural areas.
