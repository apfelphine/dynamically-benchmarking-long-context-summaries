**Education in Norway**

The Norwegian school system is structured into three parts: elementary school (*barneskole*, ages 6–12), lower secondary school (*ungdomsskole*, ages 13–16), and upper secondary school (*videregående skole*, ages 16–19). Elementary and lower secondary education are compulsory for all children aged 6–16. Elementary school focuses on play and foundational skills, with no official grades given. Lower secondary school introduces grading, and students can begin choosing electives. Upper secondary school is primarily public, with 93% of students attending in 2007, and features paths in general studies (*studieforberedelse*) or vocational studies (*yrkesfag*).

In higher education, the system includes universities, which concentrate on theoretical subjects, and university colleges (*høgskole*), which offer diverse choices including professional vocations. The Norwegian Agency for Quality Assurance in Education (NOKUT) ensures the quality of higher education. Private schooling is available under the Independent Schools Act of 2003, though public schools remain dominant.
