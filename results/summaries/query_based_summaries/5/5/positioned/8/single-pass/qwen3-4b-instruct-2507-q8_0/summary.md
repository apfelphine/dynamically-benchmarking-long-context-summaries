Philip Rubin is a prominent researcher in embodied cognition, speech production, and perception, with a strong interdisciplinary background in computational, linguistic, physiological, and psychological approaches. He earned his BA in psychology and linguistics from Brandeis University and his PhD in experimental psychology from the University of Connecticut under the guidance of Michael Turvey, Ignatius Mattingly, Philip Lieberman, and Alvin Liberman.

Rubin is best known for his pioneering work in articulatory synthesis, including the development of the ASY and SWS programs, and the HADES signal processing system. He also designed the SPIEL programming language, a precursor to MATLAB. His theoretical contributions include a dynamical systems/action theory perspective on speech, perceptual organization, and event-based models of articulatory gestures, which have been applied in models like CASY and TADA.

He held significant leadership roles in science policy and administration, serving as Director of the Division of Behavioral and Cognitive Sciences at the National Science Foundation (NSF) and as Assistant Director for Social, Behavioral, and Economic Sciences at the White House Office of Science and Technology Policy (OSTP). He was also the Chair of the National Academies Board on Behavioral, Cognitive, and Sensory Sciences and President of the Federation of Associations in Behavioral and Brain Sciences (FABBS).

Rubin is actively involved in science advocacy, ethics, and public policy, particularly regarding human subjects research, the use of behavioral science in intelligence and security, and the ethical implications of emerging technologies. He has served on numerous advisory committees and contributed to reports on research ethics, the modernization of human subjects regulations, and the responsible use of technology.

He is a member of the American Academy of Arts and Sciences, the American Psychological Association, and the American Association for the Advancement of Science, and has received multiple fellowships and awards. Rubin is also a photographer, co-founder of the Yale Macintosh Users Group (YMUG), and a co-founder of the IS Group, a collaborative scientific network. He is currently President of FABBS and Chair of the Board of Directors of Haskins Laboratories.
