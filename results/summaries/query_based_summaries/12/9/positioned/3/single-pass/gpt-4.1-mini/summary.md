The **Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes** was initially founded in 1925 in Dresden as part of the Deutsche Studentenschaft, dissolved under National Socialism in 1934, and re-established in Cologne in 1948. It is a registered association that offers prestigious scholarships to exceptionally talented students and doctoral candidates, focusing on academic excellence, initiative, and responsibility without citizenship or age restrictions. Admission is through multiple channels including school nomination, university professor nomination, competition wins, a special assessment test, and alumni nomination. The scholarship provides both financial (monthly allowances, health insurance subsidies, need-based awards) and academic support (summer schools, workshops, research groups, mentoring). 

The Studienstiftung also administers internal and open scholarship programs in partnership with other organizations, including research internships and music scholarships. In 2023, nearly 2,500 scholarships were approved, with about 14,900 scholars total amounting to about 0.48% of German university students. Notable alumni include Nobel laureates, Fields Medalists, Leibniz Prize winners, scholars in natural and social sciences, prominent business leaders, politicians, artists, and journalists. The program emphasizes holistic development and supports scholars throughout their academic journey, extending up to doctoral studies.
