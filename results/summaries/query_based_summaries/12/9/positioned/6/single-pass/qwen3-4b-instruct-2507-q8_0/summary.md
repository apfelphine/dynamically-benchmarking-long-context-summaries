The Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes (German Students' Foundation) is a German scholarship organization founded in 1925, reestablished in 1948 after being dissolved during the Nazi era. It offers full-time undergraduate and graduate scholarships, as well as doctoral fellowships, to students who meet academic and personal criteria, including a strong academic record and demonstrated social commitment. The scholarship includes financial support such as a monthly allowance of €300, need-based subsidies, and health insurance, with additional funding available for students with children. Scholars receive academic support through summer schools, research groups, language courses, and mentoring. The foundation also offers internal and open scholarship programs, including partnerships with institutions like KAUST and the Beethoven House. As of 2023, the Studienstiftung awarded 2,179 undergraduate and 301 doctoral scholarships, with a high rate of student retention and renewal. The organization emphasizes excellence, initiative, and responsibility in its mission, and notable alumni include Nobel laureates and prominent figures in science, politics, and the arts.
