# Summary of Studienstiftung

The Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes, established originally in 1925, is Germany’s prestigious scholarship foundation that offers comprehensive financial and academic support to exceptionally talented full-time university students and doctoral candidates. Admission is highly selective and occurs via various channels including high school and university professor nominations, competition winners, self-assessment tests, and alumni recommendations. The scholarship encompasses a monthly allowance, health insurance subsidies, grants for international study, and an array of academic programs such as summer schools, workshops, research groups, and mentoring. Funding may continue beyond undergraduate studies into master's and doctoral levels, contingent upon academic performance and other personal circumstances.

In 2023, the Studienstiftung approved 2,179 general scholarships and 301 doctoral scholarships, supporting about 0.48% of the student population in Germany. Notable academic institutions like Heidelberg University and Hannover Medical School have significant shares of Studienstiftung scholars. The foundation also offers internal and open scholarship programs in cooperation with other institutions (e.g., KAUST-Studienstiftung, McCloy Academic Scholarship). Distinguished alumni include multiple Nobel laureates, Fields Medalists, leading scientists, economists, politicians, artists, and journalists who have significantly impacted their fields, reflecting the foundation’s mission centered on excellence, initiative, and responsibility.
