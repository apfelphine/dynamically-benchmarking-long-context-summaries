The Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes was initially established in Dresden in 1925 as a department of the Deutsche Studentenschaft, but was dissolved under National Socialism in 1934. It was reformed as a registered association in Cologne in 1948.

The Studienstiftung offers both financial and academic support to exceptionally talented students and doctoral candidates, based on the principles of "excellence, initiative and responsibility." Eligibility criteria vary depending on the admission channel, which includes nomination by a school, nomination by professors, nomination by an examination board, or through winners of national or federal competitions. Since 2010, candidates can also put themselves forward via an assessment test. The scholarship provides financial support, which can include a monthly allowance (e.g., €300 for general students, or €1,550 for PhD scholars), as well as academic support such as summer schools and mentoring.
