The **Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes** (Studienstiftung) is a prestigious German scholarship program founded in 1925, initially as a department of the Deutsche Studentenschaft. After being dissolved under National Socialism, it was reestablished in 1948 as a registered association in Cologne. The Studienstiftung provides scholarships to exceptionally talented students and doctoral candidates, emphasizing excellence, initiative, and responsibility.

### **Admission Criteria**
Applicants must demonstrate high academic achievement and social commitment. Admission occurs through several channels:
- **School nominations**: High school principals can nominate 30 senior students annually.
- **University nominations**: Professors can nominate students directly.
- **University examination boards**: One in every 50 students in the second academic year can be nominated.
- **Competitions**: Winners of national or international competitions may qualify.
- **Assessment test**: Candidates can apply directly via a standardized test followed by a selection seminar.
- **Alumni nominations**: Past scholars can nominate students.
- **Artists and musicians**: Universities can nominate students from the fine arts, music, and performing arts.

Scholarships are renewable based on academic performance and social engagement, with extensions possible under exceptional circumstances.

### **Scholarship Benefits**
The Studienstiftung offers both financial and academic support:
- **Financial support**: Monthly stipends (€300) and need-based scholarships (up to €855/month) for students with children. PhD scholars receive €1,550–1,650/month, including research allowances.
- **Academic support**: Summer schools, research groups, language courses, workshops, and mentoring programs. Scholarships include subsidized summer academies and support for organizing their own events.
- **Additional programs**: Open scholarships in cooperation with other organizations, such as the McCloy Academic Scholarship Program and the Max Weber scholarship for students in Bavaria.

### **Notable Alumni**
The Studienstiftung has produced influential figures across various fields, including:
- **Science and Mathematics**: Nobel Prize winners and prominent researchers like Manfred Eigen, Gerd Faltings, and Reinhard Genzel.
- **Social Sciences**: Scholars such as Ulrich Beck, Michèle Tertilt, and Reinhard Zimmermann.
- **Business and NGOs**: Leaders like Andreas von Bechtolsheim and Wolfgang Bernhard.
- **Politics**: Politicians and public servants, including Fritz Kuhn, Andreas Paulus, and Gesine Schwan.
- **Arts**: Writers, composers, and artists like Hans Magnus Enzensberger and Moritz Eggert.

The Studienstiftung remains a cornerstone of German higher education, fostering talent and promoting excellence in academia and beyond.
