The Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes, initially founded in Dresden in 1925 as part of the Deutsche Studentenschaft, was dissolved in 1934 under National Socialism and later re-established as a registered association in Cologne in 1948. The organization's mission is based on the principles of "excellence, initiative and responsibility."

The Studienstiftung offers general scholarships for bachelor's and master's studies, providing both financial and academic support. Eligibility is based on criteria similar to BAFöG, and accepted applicants must study full-time at a university in Germany, another EU country, or Switzerland. Scholars are nominated through various channels, including by high schools, university professors, examination boards, or through national/federal competition winners, with an assessment test available since February 2010.
