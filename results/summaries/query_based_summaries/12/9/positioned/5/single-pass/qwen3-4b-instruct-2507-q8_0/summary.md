The **Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes** (German Students' Foundation) is a German scholarship organization founded in 1925 as a department of the Deutsche Studentenschaft. It was dissolved in 1934 under National Socialism and re-established in 1948 in Cologne. The foundation offers scholarships for undergraduate and graduate studies, with financial and academic support based on eligibility criteria from BAFöG (German state support system). Applicants must study full-time at a university in Germany, another EU country, or Switzerland, and there are no age or citizenship requirements. Scholarships are awarded through nomination by schools, professors, examination boards, or competitions, and are granted for up to six semesters, with extensions possible based on academic performance. The foundation provides a monthly allowance, health insurance subsidies, and support for study-related expenses. It also offers academic support such as summer schools, language courses, and mentoring. The Studienstiftung has a long-standing tradition of supporting students in Germany and abroad, with notable alumni including Nobel laureates, leading scientists, and prominent figures in business and public service.
