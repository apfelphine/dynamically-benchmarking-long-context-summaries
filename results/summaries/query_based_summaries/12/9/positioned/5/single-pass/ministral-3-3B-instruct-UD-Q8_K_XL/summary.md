The **Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes** (Studienstiftung) is a prestigious German scholarship program founded in 1925, initially as a department of the Deutsche Studentenschaft. After being dissolved under National Socialism, it was revived in 1948 as a registered association. The Studienstiftung supports exceptionally talented students and doctoral candidates through both financial and academic support, adhering to criteria similar to those of the German state support system, BAFöG.

**Admission** is open to students from Germany, the EU, or Switzerland, with no citizenship requirement. Candidates are nominated through various channels, including schools, professors, examination boards, national competitions, or self-nomination via an assessment test. Scholarships are typically granted for full-time studies, with extensions possible based on academic performance and other criteria. The program also offers doctoral scholarships for highly qualified and socially engaged PhD students.

**Scholarships** include financial support such as a monthly allowance (€300) and subsidies for health and long-term care insurance, with additional need-based funding available. Academically, the Studienstiftung provides summer schools, research groups, language courses, workshops, and mentoring. Scholars can also apply for funding to organize their own conferences. The program emphasizes excellence, initiative, and responsibility, fostering leadership and personal development among its recipients.

The Studienstiftung has a long-standing history of supporting notable alumni across various fields, including natural sciences, social sciences, business, politics, arts, and journalism. Its mission is to nurture future leaders by providing a holistic support system that combines financial aid with mentorship and academic enrichment.
