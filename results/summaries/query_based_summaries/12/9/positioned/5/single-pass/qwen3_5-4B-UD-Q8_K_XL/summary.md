The **Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes** (Endowment Fund for the German People) is a German foundation established in 1948 to provide financial and academic support to exceptionally talented students pursuing bachelor's, master's, and doctoral degrees. Founded to replace the dissolved Nazi-era funding system, it offers scholarships based on merit rather than financial need, adhering to the principles of "excellence, initiative, and responsibility." Admission occurs through various channels, including nomination by schools, universities, or examination boards, as well as self-nomination via an assessment test. Financial support includes a monthly allowance (ranging from €300 to €1,550 depending on the degree level), subsidies for health insurance, and tax-free stipends, while academic support encompasses summer schools, research groups, and mentoring.

As of 2023, the Studienstiftung approved 2,179 undergraduate and graduate scholarships and 301 doctoral scholarships, representing approximately 0.48% of the total student population in Germany. The foundation also administers internal and open scholarship programs in collaboration with partners such as KAUST and the Bavarian government. Notable alumni include Nobel Prize winners in Chemistry, Physics, and Medicine, as well as prominent figures in science, politics, and the arts, reflecting the scholarship's role in fostering high-achieving individuals across diverse fields.
