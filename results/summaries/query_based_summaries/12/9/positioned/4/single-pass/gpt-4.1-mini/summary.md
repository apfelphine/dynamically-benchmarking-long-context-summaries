# Summary: Studienstiftung

The Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes, founded initially in Dresden in 1925 and re-established as a registered association in Cologne in 1948, is Germany's largest and oldest scholarship foundation. It offers general scholarships for bachelor’s, master’s, and doctoral studies combining financial and academic support, primarily to exceptionally talented full-time students studying at German or EU universities, though citizenship and age restrictions do not apply. Admissions occur through multiple channels such as school and professor nominations, competition winners, assessment tests, and alumni recommendations, focusing on excellence, initiative, and social responsibility.

Financially, the Studienstiftung provides allowances, need-based scholarships aligned with Germany’s BAFöG system, and subsidies for insurance and study-related expenses, including support for international visits. Academically, it arranges summer schools, workshops, research groups, and mentoring programs to enrich scholars’ intellectual growth. By 2023, it sponsored about 14,888 scholars across Germany, representing roughly 0.48% of all students. Notable alumni include numerous Nobel laureates in natural sciences, influential figures in social sciences, business leaders, politicians, artists, and journalists, reflecting the foundation's broad impact on German academia and society.
