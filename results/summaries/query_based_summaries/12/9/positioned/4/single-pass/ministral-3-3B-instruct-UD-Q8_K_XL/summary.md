The **Studienstiftung** is a prestigious German scholarship program founded in 1925, initially as a department of the Deutsche Studentenschaft, but revived in 1948 as a registered association. It supports exceptionally talented students and doctoral candidates based on criteria of academic excellence, initiative, and social responsibility, aligning with the principles of the **BAföG** (German state student support system). Admission is open to students worldwide, with no strict age or citizenship requirements, though applicants must study full-time at a university in Germany, the EU, or Switzerland.

Scholarships include financial support (monthly stipends, health insurance subsidies, and need-based grants) and academic benefits such as summer schools, mentoring, and workshops. The program also offers specialized scholarships for doctoral candidates and doctoral scholarships for highly qualified PhD students. Notable alumni include Nobel Prize winners, politicians, scientists, and artists, reflecting its impact on Germany’s intellectual and cultural elite.

The Studienstiftung operates through various nomination channels, including school recommendations, university nominations, national competitions, and self-nominations via assessment tests. Scholars are required to submit regular reports on their studies and engage in extracurricular activities. The organization has grown significantly, with over 14,000 scholars and 301 doctoral scholars approved annually, representing a small but influential fraction of Germany’s student population.
