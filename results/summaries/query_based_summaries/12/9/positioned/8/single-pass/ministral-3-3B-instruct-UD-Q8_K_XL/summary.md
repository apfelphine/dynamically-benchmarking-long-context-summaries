The **Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes** is a prestigious German scholarship program founded in 1925, initially as a department of the Deutsche Studentenschaft. After its dissolution under National Socialism, it was reestablished in 1948 as a registered association in Cologne. The program provides both financial and academic support to exceptionally talented students and doctoral candidates, emphasizing excellence, initiative, and responsibility.

**Admission** is open to students and doctoral candidates from any country, with eligibility based on high academic achievement and social commitment. Candidates are nominated by schools, universities, examination boards, national or international competitions, or self-nomination through an assessment test. Scholarships are granted for bachelor’s, master’s, and doctoral studies, with funding including a monthly stipend, health insurance subsidies, and support for study-related activities. The program also offers specialized summer academies, research groups, and mentorship programs.

The Studienstiftung has grown significantly over the years, with over 14,888 scholars in 2023, representing a small but influential fraction of Germany’s student population. Notable alumni include prominent figures in science, politics, business, and the arts, reflecting the program’s impact on fostering leadership and innovation.
