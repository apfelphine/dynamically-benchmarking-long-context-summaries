The Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes is a German foundation established in 1948 to support students in Germany, the EU, and Switzerland based on academic excellence, initiative, and responsibility. It provides general scholarships covering both financial needs (allowances and insurance) and academic support (summer schools, research groups, and mentoring). Admission is primarily through nomination by schools, professors, or examination boards, though candidates can also self-nominate via an assessment test. The foundation manages various internal and open scholarship programs, including partnerships with organizations like KAUST and specific initiatives for artists and musicians.

In 2023, the Studienstiftung approved 14,888 scholarships, representing approximately 0.48% of all students in Germany. The foundation serves a diverse student body, with notable alumni including Nobel laureates in physics, chemistry, and medicine, Fields Medalists, and prominent figures in politics, business, and the arts. Its statistics show a steady increase in scholar numbers from the 2000s through the 2010s, with a slight fluctuation in the 2020s, maintaining a significant presence among the top universities in the country.
