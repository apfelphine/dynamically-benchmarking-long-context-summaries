## Summary: Studienstiftung

The Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes, originally founded in Dresden in 1925 and re-established in Cologne in 1948, is Germany’s largest and oldest scholarship foundation dedicated to supporting exceptionally talented students and doctoral candidates. It combines financial aid (including up to €855 monthly plus allowances, tax-free under German law) with a comprehensive academic support program offering summer schools, workshops, mentoring, and networking opportunities. Admission is highly selective, based on excellence, initiative, and responsibility, and candidates enter through various channels such as school or professor nominations, competition winners, self-application via assessment tests, alumni nominations, and artistic field nominations. In 2023, approximately 14,888 scholars were supported (including doctoral recipients).

The foundation's funding typically covers full-time studies at German or EU universities without citizenship or age restrictions. Its academic programs also foster intellectual and social development, encouraging scholars to organize conferences and peer groups. The Studienstiftung collaborates on special scholarship programs (e.g., KAUST-Studienstiftung) and offers open scholarship programs with partner institutions. Notable alumni include numerous Nobel laureates in sciences, prominent social scientists, business leaders, artists, and political figures, highlighting the foundation’s significant role in nurturing Germany’s academic and societal elite.
