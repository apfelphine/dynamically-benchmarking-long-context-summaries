# **Concise Summary:**

The **Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes** (German People's Foundation for Scholarships) is the largest scholarship organization in Germany, established in 1948 in Cologne to promote academic excellence, initiative, and social commitment. Founded after the dissolution of its predecessor under National Socialism, the foundation awards financial and academic support to exceptional students pursuing full-time bachelor's, master's, or doctoral studies in Germany, the EU, or Switzerland. Eligibility is based on the criteria for BAFöG (German state support), with no citizenship or age restrictions. Admission occurs through various channels, including nominations by schools, professors, examination boards, and alumni, as well as competitive assessment tests. Scholars receive a monthly allowance (€300–€855 depending on need), insurance subsidies, and tax-free funding, alongside academic support such as summer academies, research groups, and mentoring.

As of 2023, the Studienstiftung approved 14,888 scholarships, representing 0.48% of all students in Germany. It has grown from a small department in 1925 to a major institution with a long list of notable alumni in science, politics, arts, and public service, including Nobel laureates and heads of state. The foundation also offers internal programs for scholars and alumni, as well as open scholarship programs in collaboration with other organizations.
