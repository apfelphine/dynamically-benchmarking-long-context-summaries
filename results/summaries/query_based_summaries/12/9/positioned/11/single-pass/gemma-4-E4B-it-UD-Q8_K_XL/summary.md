The Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes was initially founded in Dresden in 1925 as a department of the Deutsche Studentenschaft but was dissolved under National Socialism in 1934. It was re-established as a registered association in Cologne in 1948. The Studienstiftung offers general scholarships for bachelor's and master's studies, providing both financial and academic support, with eligibility tied to the BAFöG system.

The organization selects exceptionally talented students and doctoral candidates based on the principles of "excellence, initiative and responsibility." Admission occurs through several channels: nomination by a school, nomination by professors, nomination by an examination board, winners of national or federal competitions, or via an assessment test since February 2010. Scholarship holders receive a monthly allowance (€300) and may receive a need-based basic scholarship, with funding generally being tax-free.
