The **Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes** (German Academic Scholarship Foundation) is a German organization established in 1925, re-founded in 1948 after being dissolved under National Socialism. It offers scholarships to undergraduate, graduate, and doctoral students based on academic excellence, initiative, and responsibility, with no citizenship or age restrictions. Students are nominated by schools, professors, examination boards, or through competitions, and selected via interviews and group discussions. Scholarships include a monthly allowance, need-based financial support, and academic benefits such as summer academies, research groups, and mentoring. The foundation supports students in Germany, EU countries, and Switzerland, with a focus on full-time study. In 2023, it awarded 2,179 undergraduate/graduate and 301 doctoral scholarships. Notable alumni include Nobel laureates, Leibniz Prize winners, and prominent figures in business, politics, and the arts. The organization also runs internal and open scholarship programs in partnership with institutions like KAUST and the McCloy Academic Scholarship Program.
