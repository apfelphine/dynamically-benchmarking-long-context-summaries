# **Studienstiftung**

The Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes was founded in Dresden in 1925 as a department of the Deutsche Studentenschaft, dissolved in 1934 under National Socialism, and re-established as a registered association in Cologne in 1948. It provides a general scholarship for bachelor's, master's, and doctoral students based on BAFöG eligibility, offering both financial support (allowances up to €855/month for students and €1,550 for PhDs) and academic resources such as summer schools, research groups, and language courses. Admission is conducted through seven channels, including nominations by schools, professors, examination boards, and competitions, with a selection seminar assessing cognitive abilities, motivation, and social commitment.

In 2023, the foundation awarded 14,888 scholarships (including 1,262 doctoral scholarships), representing 0.48% of German university students. The program emphasizes excellence, initiative, and responsibility, with funding extended until studies are completed and further support approved in 93.5% of cases. Notable alumni include Nobel Prize winners in Physics (Wolfgang Ketterle, Reinhard Genzel), Chemistry (Manfred Eigen, Robert Huber, Joachim Frank), and Medicine (Erwin Neher), as well as prominent figures in mathematics, economics, and politics. The foundation also offers internal and open scholarship programs in cooperation with organizations like KAUST and the Bavarian government.
