The **Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes** (German People's Foundation for Studies) is a German scholarship organization established in 1925 as a department of the Deutsche Studentenschaft. It was dissolved in 1934 under National Socialism and re-established in 1948 in Cologne. The foundation offers scholarships for undergraduate, graduate, and doctoral studies, with eligibility based on BAFöG criteria, including full-time study at a German, EU, or Swiss university. Applicants are nominated by schools, professors, examination boards, competitions, or self-apply via an assessment test, with selection based on academic excellence, initiative, and responsibility. Scholarship holders receive financial support (including a monthly allowance and need-based grants), academic support (summer schools, workshops, mentoring), and are required to submit regular reports. The foundation also offers internal and open scholarship programs, such as partnerships with KAUST and the McCloy Academic Scholarship Program. In 2023, it approved 2,179 undergraduate/graduate and 301 doctoral scholarships. Notable alumni include Nobel laureates, Fields Medalists, and prominent figures in politics, business, and the arts. The foundation emphasizes service, excellence, and personal responsibility, with a mission to support students from diverse backgrounds.
