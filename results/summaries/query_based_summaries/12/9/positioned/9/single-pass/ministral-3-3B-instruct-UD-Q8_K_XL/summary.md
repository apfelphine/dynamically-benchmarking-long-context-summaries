The **Studienstiftung** is a prestigious German scholarship program founded in 1925, initially as a department of the Deutsche Studentenschaft. After being dissolved under National Socialism, it was reestablished in 1948 as a registered association. The program offers scholarships to exceptionally talented students and doctoral candidates, emphasizing excellence, initiative, and responsibility.

### **Admission Criteria**
Applicants must demonstrate outstanding academic achievement and social commitment. Admission occurs through various channels:
1. **School Nominations**: High school principals nominate 30 senior students annually.
2. **University Nominations**: Professors can nominate students directly.
3. **Examination Boards**: Universities select students based on academic performance.
4. **Competitions**: Winners of national competitions (e.g., Bundeswettbewerb Mathematik) may qualify.
5. **Assessment Test**: Open to self-nominated students, based on cognitive abilities.
6. **Alumni Nominations**: Past scholars can nominate students.
7. **Artists & Musicians**: Universities may nominate students in the performing arts.

Scholarships are awarded for bachelor’s, master’s, and doctoral studies, with financial support including monthly allowances, health insurance subsidies, and need-based scholarships. The program also offers academic support through summer schools, workshops, and mentoring.

### **Key Features**
- **No citizenship or age restrictions** for undergraduate and graduate students.
- **Financial support**: Monthly stipends, health insurance, and tuition subsidies.
- **Academic enrichment**: Summer academies, research opportunities, and networking events.
- **Doctoral scholarships**: For highly qualified PhD students, funded by the German Ministry of Education and Research.
- **Partnerships**: Collaborations with organizations like the KAUST-Studienstiftung program for STEM scholars.

The Studienstiftung has supported over 14,000 scholars since its inception, fostering leadership and innovation in academia, business, and public service. Its alumni include Nobel laureates, politicians, scientists, and artists.
