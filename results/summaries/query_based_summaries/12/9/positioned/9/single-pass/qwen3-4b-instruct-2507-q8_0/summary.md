**Studienstiftung** is a German foundation established in 1925, initially as a department of the Deutsche Studentenschaft, and later re-founded in 1948 after being dissolved under National Socialism. It offers scholarships for undergraduate and graduate studies, as well as doctoral programs, emphasizing excellence, initiative, and responsibility. Scholarships are awarded through various nomination channels, including schools, universities, competitions, and self-identification via an assessment test. Selected scholars receive financial support (including a monthly allowance and need-based grants) and academic benefits such as summer schools, mentoring, and research opportunities. The organization also provides internal and open scholarship programs, including partnerships with institutions like KAUST and the Beethoven House. As of 2023, it awarded 2,179 undergraduate/graduate scholarships and 301 doctoral scholarships, with a significant number of scholars in fields such as natural sciences, social sciences, business, politics, and the arts. Notable alumni include Nobel laureates, Fields Medalists, and prominent figures in law, politics, and the arts.
