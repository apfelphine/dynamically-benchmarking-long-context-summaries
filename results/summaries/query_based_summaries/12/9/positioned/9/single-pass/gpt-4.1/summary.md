## Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes – Summary

The Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes is Germany’s largest and oldest scholarship foundation, founded in Dresden in 1925, dissolved during the National Socialist era, and reestablished in Cologne in 1948. It offers scholarships to exceptionally talented students and doctoral candidates based on the principles of excellence, initiative, and responsibility. Admission is through various channels, including school and professor nominations, national competitions, assessment tests, nominations by alums, and partnerships. Both German/EU and non-EU students studying full-time in Germany, other EU countries, or Switzerland are eligible.

The scholarship combines financial support—with basic allowances up to €855 monthly for students and €1,650 for doctoral candidates (from October 2025), plus additional grants and allowances—and extensive academic support, such as mentoring, summer schools, research groups, workshops, and project funding. The Studienstiftung also runs internal and cooperative scholarship programs, including international partnerships and specialized grants. As of 2023, it supported nearly 15,000 scholars (about 0.48% of German university students). Its alumni include several Nobel laureates and prominent figures in science, politics, arts, and business.
