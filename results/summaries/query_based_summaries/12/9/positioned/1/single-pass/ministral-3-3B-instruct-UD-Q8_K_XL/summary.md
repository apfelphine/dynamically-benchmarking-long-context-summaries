The **Studienstiftung** (German Scholarship Foundation) is a prestigious merit-based scholarship program founded in 1925 in Germany. It supports exceptionally talented students and doctoral candidates in higher education, emphasizing excellence, initiative, and responsibility. Admission is based on academic achievement, social commitment, and leadership potential, with selection through nomination by schools, professors, examination boards, or self-nomination via an assessment test.

The program offers financial support (monthly stipends, health insurance subsidies) and academic mentorship, including summer schools, workshops, and mentoring programs. Scholarships are available for bachelor’s, master’s, and doctoral studies, with renewal contingent on academic performance. The Studienstiftung also supports internal scholarship programs, such as partnerships with institutions like KAUST, and open scholarships in cooperation with other organizations.

The foundation operates through a network of alumni associations, fostering lifelong connections and leadership development. It has a long-standing reputation for nurturing future leaders in academia, business, and public service, with notable alumni spanning science, arts, politics, and social sciences.
