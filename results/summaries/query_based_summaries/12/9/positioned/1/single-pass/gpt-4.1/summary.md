## Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes

The Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes, founded in Dresden in 1925, is a prestigious German scholarship foundation recognized for supporting exceptionally talented students and doctoral candidates. After its dissolution during the National Socialist era, it was re-established in Cologne in 1948. Its admissions process focuses on academic excellence, initiative, and responsibility, with candidates nominated by schools, university professors, examination boards, cooperation partners, via an assessment test, by alumni, or through special programs for musicians and artists. Eligibility for the general scholarship requires full-time university study in Germany, another EU country, or Switzerland, but there are no nationality or age restrictions.

The Studienstiftung offers both financial and academic support, including a monthly allowance, need-based scholarships calculated under BAföG guidelines (up to €855 per month for undergraduates and graduates, and €1,550–€1,650 plus research allowance for PhD students), and additional funds for students with children. Academic support encompasses mentoring, summer schools, research groups, workshops, and opportunities to organise conferences. The foundation also runs open and internal scholarship programs in partnership with institutions such as KAUST, McCloy, ERP, Carlo-Schmid, and the Max Weber program in Bavaria. In 2023, Studienstiftung supported 13,626 undergraduate/graduate and 1,262 doctoral scholars (0.48% of German students), with over 55% female scholars, and admitted recipients are required to maintain high academic and social standards to retain funding. Notable alumni include Nobel laureates, influential scientists, political leaders, business executives, artists, and journalists.
