# Summary of Information Regarding Studienstiftung

The **Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes** is a prestigious German scholarship foundation originally founded in Dresden in 1925 as part of the Deutsche Studentenschaft but was dissolved in 1934 during National Socialism and re-established as a registered association in Cologne in 1948. It is dedicated to supporting exceptionally talented students and doctoral candidates across Germany and beyond, regardless of citizenship or age, who study full-time at universities in Germany, the EU, or Switzerland.

Admission to the Studienstiftung is highly selective and occurs through multiple channels including nominations by schools, professors, examination boards, winners of national competitions, alumni, and a special assessment test introduced in 2010. The foundation emphasizes candidates' academic excellence, social commitment, initiative, and responsibility.

The scholarship provides both **financial support** (monthly allowances, health insurance subsidies, and need-based awards) and **academic support**, which includes summer schools, workshops, research groups, mentoring, and opportunities to organize conferences. Notably, doctoral scholars receive higher monthly stipends with added research allowances. The foundation also operates various internal and open scholarship programmes in partnership with other institutions.

With a sizable community of scholars, the Studienstiftung maintains alumni associations in Shanghai and Beijing for administration and coordination. As of 2023, approximately 13,626 undergraduate/graduate and 1,262 doctoral scholars were supported, representing about 0.48% of students in Germany. Leading German universities like Heidelberg, Freiburg, and Mannheim have notable representation among scholars. The foundation's alumni include distinguished figures across fields such as natural sciences, social sciences, business, politics, arts, and journalism, with multiple Nobel laureates, Fields Medalists, and influential public figures.
