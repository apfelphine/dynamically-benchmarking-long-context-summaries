The **Studienstiftung** (German: *Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes*) is a prestigious scholarship program in Germany founded in 1925, initially as a department of the *Deutsche Studentenschaft*. It was dissolved under National Socialism in 1934 but revived in 1948 as a registered association in Cologne. The organization supports exceptionally talented students and doctoral candidates through a combination of financial and academic support, adhering to principles of excellence, initiative, and responsibility.

### **Admission and Selection**
Admission is based on high academic achievement and social commitment, with no strict age or citizenship requirements. Students are nominated through various channels, including:
1. **School nominations** by high school principals.
2. **University nominations** by professors.
3. **Examination boards** selecting students from universities.
4. **Competitions** (e.g., national math, science, or language competitions).
5. **Self-nominations** via an assessment test.
6. **Alumni nominations** by former scholarship holders.
7. **Support for artists and musicians** through internal selection processes.

Scholarship holders are selected through a rigorous three-day seminar, where candidates are evaluated on academic performance, extracurricular activities, social engagement, and personal qualities.

### **Scholarship Benefits**
The Studienstiftung provides:
- **Financial support**: A monthly allowance (€300) and need-based subsidies (up to €855/month) based on BAföG (German state support for students). Additional funds are available for students with children.
- **Academic support**: Summer schools, research groups, language courses, workshops, and mentoring by liaison professors.
- **Doctoral scholarships**: For highly qualified PhD students, offering a monthly stipend of €1,650 (from October 2025) plus a research allowance.
- **Travel grants**: Support for international study visits and partial tuition fee coverage.
- **Special programs**: Open scholarships in cooperation with other organizations (e.g., McCloy Academic Scholarship Program, ERP-Stipendienprogramm) and regional scholarships (e.g., Max Weber scholarship for Bavaria).

### **Statistics and Demographics**
As of 2023, the Studienstiftung awarded **2,179 undergraduate/graduate scholarships** and **301 doctoral scholarships**. The scholarships are distributed across Germany’s universities, with a notable presence in public and private institutions. The organization supports a diverse range of fields, including natural sciences, social sciences, business, politics, arts, and journalism.

### **Notable Alumni**
The Studienstiftung has produced influential figures across academia, politics, business, and the arts, including:
- **Natural Sciences**: Nobel Prize winners and leading researchers (e.g., Manfred Eigen, Gerd Faltings).
- **Social Sciences**: Economists, historians, and legal scholars (e.g., Ulrich Beck, Michèle Tertilt).
- **Business and NGOs**: Executives and philanthropists (e.g., Andreas von Bechtolsheim, Wolfgang Bernhard).
- **Politics and Civil Service**: Politicians, judges, and public servants (e.g., Fritz Kuhn, Gesine Schwan).
- **Arts**: Writers, composers, and artists (e.g., Hans Magnus Enzensberger, Juli Zeh).

The Studienstiftung remains a cornerstone of Germany’s merit-based scholarship system, fostering talent and leadership across generations.
