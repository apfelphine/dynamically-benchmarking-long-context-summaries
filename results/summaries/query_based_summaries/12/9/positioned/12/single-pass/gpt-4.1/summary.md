## Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes: Summary

The Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes, founded in Dresden in 1925 and re-established in Cologne in 1948, is Germany’s largest and most prestigious scholarship foundation. It supports exceptionally talented undergraduate, graduate, and doctoral students across various disciplines based on the principles of excellence, initiative, and responsibility. Admission is highly selective, with candidates nominated through schools, university professors, examination boards, competition wins, application assessments, and alumni recommendations. Support is provided regardless of nationality, age, or subject, as long as candidates study full-time and partly at a German, EU, or Swiss university. In 2023, 2,179 scholarships and 301 doctoral scholarships were awarded, with about 0.5% of German students receiving Studienstiftung support.

Studienstiftung scholarships comprise financial aid (a €300 monthly allowance and need-based support up to €855 per month for students or €1,650 per month for PhD candidates from October 2025, plus health insurance subsidies and grants for study abroad) and comprehensive academic support. Academic enrichment includes summer schools, workshops, mentoring, and funded opportunities to organize conferences and events. The foundation also administers internal and open scholarship programmes in collaboration with organizations such as KAUST and the Bavarian Max Weber program. Notable alumni span Nobel laureates and leaders in natural sciences, humanities, government, business, journalism, and the arts, reflecting the foundation’s mission to promote academic excellence and leadership in German society.
