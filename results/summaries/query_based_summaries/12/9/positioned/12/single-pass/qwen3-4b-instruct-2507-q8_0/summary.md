**Studienstiftung** is a German foundation established in 1948, originally founded in 1925 as a department of the Deutsche Studentenschaft and later dissolved under National Socialism. It offers scholarships for undergraduate and graduate studies, as well as doctoral programs, emphasizing excellence, initiative, and responsibility. Scholarships are awarded through nominations by schools, professors, examination boards, competitions, or self-application via an assessment test. The scholarship includes financial support (a monthly allowance, need-based funding, and travel support) and academic support (summer schools, research groups, mentoring, and conferences). As of 2023, it approved 2,179 undergraduate/graduate scholarships and 301 doctoral scholarships. Notable alumni include Nobel laureates, leading scientists, and prominent figures in politics, business, and the arts. The organization is governed by a president and secretary general, with a long-standing tradition of supporting students from diverse backgrounds.
