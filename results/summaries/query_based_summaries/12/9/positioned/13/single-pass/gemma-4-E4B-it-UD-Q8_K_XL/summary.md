The Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes was initially founded in Dresden in 1925 as part of the Deutsche Studentenschaft, but it was dissolved in 1934 under National Socialism and replaced by the "Reichsförderung." It was re-established as a registered association in Cologne in 1948.

The Studienstiftung offers general scholarships for bachelor's and master's studies, which provide both financial and academic support, with eligibility based on the BAFöG criteria. It accepts exceptionally talented students and doctoral candidates adhering to its mission principles of "excellence, initiative and responsibility." Admissions channels include nomination by schools, nomination by university professors, nomination by an examination board, selection as winners/participants in national/federal competitions, or through an assessment test. The scholarship offers financial support, including a monthly allowance, and academic support such as summer schools and mentorship.
