## Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes: Overview

The **Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes** (German Academic Scholarship Foundation) is Germany’s largest and most prestigious scholarship organization for exceptionally talented students and doctoral candidates. Founded in 1925, dissolved under National Socialism, and re-established in 1948, it operates as a registered association with a mission rooted in "excellence, initiative and responsibility." Admission is based on academic achievement, initiative, and social responsibility, with candidates nominated via schools, universities, examination boards, competition results, alumni, or self-application through an assessment test. There are no strict quotas, age limits, or citizenship requirements, and selection involves rigorous evaluation including seminars and interviews.

## Scholarship Benefits and Programs

Studienstiftung scholarships provide both **financial support**—including a monthly allowance (€300), need-based basic scholarships up to €855 for undergraduates and postgraduates, and up to €1,550 (rising to €1,650 in October 2025) plus research allowance for PhD candidates—and **academic support** such as summer schools, language courses, workshops, mentoring, and funding for conferences or academic activities. The foundation also offers specialized programs (e.g., partnerships with KAUST, ERP, Carlo-Schmid-Programm, and Max Weber scholarships in Bavaria). As of 2023, Studienstiftung supported nearly 15,000 scholars (about 0.5% of all German university students), with a notable record of alumni who are recognized leaders in science, business, politics, arts, and journalism.
