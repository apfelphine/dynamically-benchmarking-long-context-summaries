The **Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes** (often referred to simply as the *Studienstiftung*) is a prestigious German scholarship program founded in 1948, originally as a successor to the dissolved *Deutsche Studentenschaft*. It supports exceptionally talented students and doctoral candidates in Germany, Switzerland, and the EU through both financial and academic support.

### **Admission and Selection**
Eligibility is based on academic excellence, social commitment, and leadership potential, aligning with the principles of "excellence, initiative, and responsibility." Admission occurs through various channels:
1. **School Nominations**: High school principals can nominate 30 senior students annually, who then attend a three-day selection seminar.
2. **University Nominations**: Professors can nominate students directly, with selection conducted similarly to school nominations.
3. **Examination Boards**: Universities can nominate one in every 50 students in the second academic year.
4. **Competitions**: Winners of national or international competitions (e.g., Bundeswettbewerb Informatik, Jugend forscht) may qualify.
5. **Assessment Test**: Applicants can self-nominate for an assessment test followed by a selection seminar.
6. **Alumni Nominations**: Past scholars can nominate students they have taught.
7. **Arts and Music**: Universities can nominate students in fine arts, music, and performing arts.

Scholarships are renewable based on academic performance, with extensions possible under exceptional circumstances. The program also offers doctoral scholarships for highly qualified PhD candidates.

### **Scholarships**
The Studienstiftung provides financial support (e.g., monthly allowances, health insurance subsidies) and academic resources such as summer schools, workshops, mentoring, and language courses. Scholars are required to submit regular reports on their studies and activities. The program also supports international mobility through grants for visits abroad and partial tuition fee coverage.

### **Notable Features**
- **Diversity**: Open to students from Germany, EU, Switzerland, and beyond, with no citizenship or age restrictions.
- **Leadership Development**: Emphasizes social engagement and responsibility alongside academic achievement.
- **Renewal Criteria**: Scholars must maintain high academic standards to renew their funding, with extensions possible for exceptional contributions.
- **Doctoral Support**: Offers specialized funding for doctoral research, particularly for socially engaged PhD candidates.

The Studienstiftung remains one of Germany’s most esteemed scholarship programs, fostering a network of future leaders in academia, business, and public service.
