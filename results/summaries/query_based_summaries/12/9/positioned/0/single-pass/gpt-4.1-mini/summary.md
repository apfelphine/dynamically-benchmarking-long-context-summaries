The Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes, originally founded in Dresden in 1925, is Germany’s premier scholarship foundation for exceptionally talented students and doctoral candidates. Dissolved during the Nazi era, it was re-established in 1948 as a registered association in Cologne. It offers scholarships combining financial aid and academic support to full-time university students, with eligibility aligned to German BAFöG criteria but without nationality or age restrictions. Admission is competitive, involving nominations from schools, professors, examination boards, competition winners, alumni, or by assessment tests, followed by selection seminars assessing academic excellence, initiative, responsibility, and social commitment.

Scholarship support includes monthly allowances (e.g., €300 plus need-based supplements up to €855 for undergraduates and €1,550 or more for PhD students), health insurance subsidies, and funding for study abroad. Academic support involves summer schools, workshops, mentoring, and events. The foundation also runs internal and cooperative scholarship programs, such as partnerships with KAUST and the Beethoven House for specific disciplines. As of 2023, about 14,888 scholars were supported, representing roughly 0.48% of German students, with notable concentrations at leading universities. Many distinguished scientists, social scientists, and public figures have been scholars or presidents of the foundation.
