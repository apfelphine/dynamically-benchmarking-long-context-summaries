Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes was initially founded in Dresden in 1925 as a department of the Deutsche Studentenschaft but was dissolved under National Socialism in 1934. It was re-established as a registered association in Cologne in 1948. The organization supports exceptionally talented students and doctoral candidates based on the principles of "excellence, initiative and responsibility."

The Studienstiftung provides a general scholarship for bachelor's and master's studies that includes both financial and academic support, with eligibility tied to the criteria for BAFöG. Admission can be gained through several channels: nomination by a school, nomination by a professor, nomination by an examination board, recognition as a winner of a national or federal competition, self-application via an assessment test, nomination by alumni, or through university nomination for the fine arts. The scholarship holders receive academic support such as summer schools, research groups, and workshops.
