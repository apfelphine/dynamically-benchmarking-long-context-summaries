The Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes is a German scholarship organization founded in 1925 as a department of the Deutsche Studentenschaft, dissolved in 1934 under National Socialism, and re-established in 1948 in Cologne. It offers general scholarships for undergraduate and graduate studies, as well as doctoral scholarships, based on criteria from Germany’s state student support system (BAföG). Applicants must study full-time at a university in Germany, another EU country, or Switzerland, and are selected through nomination by schools, professors, examination boards, or via an assessment test. Scholarship holders receive financial support including a monthly allowance, need-based scholarships, and subsidies for health and long-term care, with funding being tax-free under German income tax law. The organization also provides academic support through summer academies, research groups, language courses, and mentoring. In 2023, it approved 2,179 undergraduate and 301 doctoral scholarships. Notable alumni include Nobel laureates Manfred Eigen and Walter Kohn, and prominent figures in business, politics, and the arts. The Studienstiftung operates with a long-standing mission of promoting excellence, initiative, and responsibility in its scholars.
