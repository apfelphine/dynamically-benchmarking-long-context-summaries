# Studienstiftung

The Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes was founded in 1925 as a department of the Deutsche Studentenschaft and re-established as a registered association in Cologne in 1948 following the dissolution of its predecessor under National Socialism. It provides a general scholarship for bachelor's, master's, and doctoral students based on the principles of "excellence, initiative, and responsibility," with eligibility tied to the German state support system (BAFöG). Admission occurs through various channels, including nominations by schools, professors, examination boards, alumni, and cooperation partners, as well as a national assessment test for students in their first two semesters. Scholars receive financial support including a monthly allowance (€300) and need-based basic scholarships (up to €855), alongside academic support such as summer schools, research groups, and mentoring.

As of 2023, the foundation awarded 14,888 scholarships, representing approximately 0.48% of German university students. The program covers undergraduate, graduate, and doctoral studies at universities in Germany, the EU, and Switzerland, with citizenship or age restrictions not applying. Internal and open scholarship programs exist for specific disciplines and international partnerships, such as the KAUST-Studienstiftung program for STEM students. Notable alumni include Nobel Prize winners in physics, chemistry, and medicine, as well as prominent figures in politics, business, and the arts.
