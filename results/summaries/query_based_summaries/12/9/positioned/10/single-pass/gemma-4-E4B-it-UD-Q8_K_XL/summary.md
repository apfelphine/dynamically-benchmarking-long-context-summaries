The Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes was initially founded in Dresden in 1925 as part of the Deutsche Studentenschaft, but it was dissolved under National Socialism in 1934 and later reformed as a registered association in Cologne in 1948. The organization is dedicated to supporting exceptionally talented students and doctoral candidates based on the principles of "excellence, initiative and responsibility."

Scholarships are offered for bachelor's and master's studies, providing both financial and academic support, and eligibility is tied to the criteria of BAFöG. Admission occurs through several channels, including nomination by a school or professor, nomination by an examination board, recognition as a winner of a national or federal competition, or through an assessment test. Financial aid includes a standard monthly allowance, with doctoral scholars receiving a higher monthly award; scholarship payments are tax-free under German Income Tax Law.
