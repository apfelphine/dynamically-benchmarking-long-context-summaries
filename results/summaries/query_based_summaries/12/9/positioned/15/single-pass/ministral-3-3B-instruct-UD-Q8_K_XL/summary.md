The **Studienstiftung** is a prestigious German scholarship program founded in 1925, initially as a department of the *Deutsche Studentenschaft*. It was revived in 1948 as a registered association in Cologne and operates as a non-profit organization supporting exceptional students and doctoral candidates through financial and academic support. The program is open to students of all nationalities, regardless of age or citizenship, provided they study full-time at a German university or in an EU country or Switzerland.

### **Admission Process**
Admission to the Studienstiftung is based on the criteria of the *Bundesausbildungsförderungsgesetz* (BAföG), Germany’s state-funded student support system. Students are nominated through various channels:
1. **School nominations**: High school principals can nominate 1 in 30 senior students.
2. **University nominations**: Professors can nominate students directly.
3. **University-level selection**: Universities can nominate 1 in 50 students in their second year.
4. **Competitions**: Winners of national or federal competitions may be considered.
5. **Assessment test**: Candidates can apply directly for an assessment test.
6. **Alumni nominations**: Past scholars can nominate students.
7. **Artists and musicians**: Universities can nominate students in the fine arts, music, and performing arts.

Scholarship holders are selected based on excellence, initiative, and responsibility. Admission occurs before the end of the fourth semester, with funding usually extended until graduation.

### **Scholarship Benefits**
The Studienstiftung offers both financial and academic support:
- **Financial Support**: A monthly allowance of €300, plus need-based subsidies (up to €855/month) for health and long-term care insurance. Doctoral scholars receive €1,550 (as of 2025) plus a research allowance. Overseas visits are also supported.
- **Academic Support**: Summer schools, research groups, language courses, workshops, and mentoring by liaison professors. Scholars must submit semesterly reports until the fourth semester and annually thereafter. The program also funds conferences and events organized by scholars.

### **Statistics and Notable Alumni**
As of 2023, the Studienstiftung awarded 2,179 scholarships and 301 doctoral scholarships. The scholarships are distributed across various disciplines, with notable alumni including:
- **Natural Sciences**: Nobel Prize winners like Manfred Eigen and Gerd Faltings.
- **Social Sciences**: Sociologists like Ulrich Beck and economists like Michèle Tertilt.
- **Business and NGOs**: Founders of companies and leaders in non-governmental organizations.
- **Politics and Civil Service**: Politicians, judges, and public servants.
- **Arts**: Composers, writers, and artists.
- **Journalism**: Renowned journalists and media figures.

The Studienstiftung is widely recognized for fostering excellence and social responsibility among its scholars, contributing significantly to Germany’s academic and cultural landscape.
