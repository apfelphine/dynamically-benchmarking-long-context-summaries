# **Summary**

The **Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes** is a German foundation established in 1948 that provides scholarships for exceptionally talented students based on merit, initiative, and responsibility. It offers a general scholarship covering undergraduate, graduate, and doctoral studies, providing financial support such as a monthly allowance (€300 basic, up to €855 need-based) and health insurance subsidies. Scholarships are also tax-free under German law, and additional funds are available for students with children. Beyond financial aid, the foundation offers extensive academic support including summer academies, research groups, language courses, and mentoring, with scholars required to report on their studies and activities.

In addition to its general scheme, the Studienstiftung manages internal programs for selected alumni and partnerships with international institutions like KAUST for STEM researchers. It also runs open scholarship programs in cooperation with other organizations, such as the McCloy Academic Scholarship Program and the Max Weber scholarship for Bavaria. Since 2023, the foundation awarded 13,626 scholarships, representing approximately 0.48% of all students in Germany. Notable alumni include Nobel Prize laureates such as Manfred Eigen (Chemistry), Robert Huber (Chemistry), and Erwin Neher (Medicine), as well as Fields Medalists Gerd Faltings and Peter Scholze, highlighting the foundation's role in supporting high academic achievement across various disciplines.
