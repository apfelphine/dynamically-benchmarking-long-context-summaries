# **Concise Summary:**

The Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes (Foundation of the German People) was founded in 1925 in Dresden as a department of the Deutsche Studentenschaft but was dissolved in 1934 under National Socialism and re-established in 1948 in Cologne as a registered association. The foundation provides general scholarships for bachelor's, master's, and doctoral students based on the criteria of the BAFöG, supporting full-time university students in Germany, the EU, or Switzerland regardless of citizenship or age. Admission occurs through seven channels: nomination by schools, professors, examination boards, competition winners, assessment tests, alumni, or arts institutions. Scholars receive financial support (a monthly allowance of €300, or up to €855 need-based, plus tax-free status and insurance subsidies) and academic support including summer schools, research groups, and mentoring. Funding is typically granted until the sixth semester or until studies are completed, with extensions possible based on academic performance or exceptional circumstances.

The Studienstiftung operates as a key pillar of German higher education, awarding approximately 14,000 scholarships annually out of nearly 2.9 million students, representing roughly 0.48% of the student population. It distinguishes itself through a holistic selection process that values academic excellence alongside social commitment, initiative, and responsibility. In addition to its core program, the foundation manages internal scholarships for alumni and partners with organizations like KAUST and the Beethoven House to offer specialized programs in STEM and the arts. Notable alumni include Nobel Prize winners in physics, chemistry, and medicine, as well as prominent figures in science, politics, arts, and journalism, underscoring the foundation's role in fostering academic and professional excellence across disciplines.
