The **Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes** (Studienstiftung) is a prestigious German scholarship program founded in 1948, initially as a successor to the dissolved *Deutsche Studentenschaft*. It supports exceptionally talented students and doctoral candidates across Germany, Switzerland, and the EU, emphasizing excellence, initiative, and responsibility.

### **Admission & Selection**
The Studienstiftung offers scholarships through various nomination channels, including:
1. **School nominations** – High schools can nominate 30 senior students annually, selected based on academic and social commitment.
2. **University nominations** – Professors can nominate students directly, with a focus on academic achievements.
3. **Examination boards** – Universities can nominate one in every 50 students in the second academic year.
4. **Competitions & self-nominations** – Winners of national competitions or applicants submitting an assessment test (since 2010) may qualify.
5. **Alumni nominations** – Former scholarship holders can nominate students they know.
6. **Special programs** – Open scholarships in cooperation with other organizations (e.g., McCloy, ERP, Carlo-Schmid-Programm).

Scholarships cover financial support (€300 monthly allowance, need-based scholarships up to €855/month, and health insurance) and academic mentorship, including summer academies, workshops, and language courses. PhD scholars receive additional research funding.

### **Academic & Social Support**
The Studienstiftung provides:
- **Summer academies** (two-week subsidized events for scholarship holders).
- **Research groups, mentoring, and language courses** by liaison professors.
- **Conference funding** for organizing academic events.
- **Open scholarship programs** (e.g., KAUST-Studienstiftung for STEM students, Beethoven House residencies for composers).

### **Notable Features**
- **Diverse eligibility**: Open to all ethnicities, with no citizenship or age restrictions.
- **Need-based support**: Scholarships adjust to financial circumstances.
- **Global reach**: Supports students studying abroad (e.g., overseas allowances, partial tuition fee coverage).
- **Long-term commitment**: Scholarships are renewable based on academic performance and other criteria.

The Studienstiftung is one of Germany’s most respected merit-based scholarship programs, fostering leadership and academic excellence among its recipients.
