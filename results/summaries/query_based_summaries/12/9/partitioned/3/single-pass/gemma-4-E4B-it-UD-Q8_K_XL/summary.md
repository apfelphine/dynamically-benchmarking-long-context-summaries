The Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes, initially founded in Dresden in 1925 as part of the Deutsche Studentenschaft, was dissolved under National Socialism in 1934. It was re-established as a registered association in Cologne in 1948.

The organization provides a general scholarship for bachelor's, master's, and doctoral studies, which combines both financial and academic support, with eligibility determined by criteria aligned with BAFöG. Scholars are admitted through several channels, including nomination by a school or professor, nomination by an examination board, being a winner of a national or federal competition, or via an assessment test. Financial support includes a standard monthly allowance (€300), and need-based basic scholarships (up to €855 per month) are available, with PhD scholars receiving a higher monthly award. Academic support involves summer schools, research groups, and mentoring.
