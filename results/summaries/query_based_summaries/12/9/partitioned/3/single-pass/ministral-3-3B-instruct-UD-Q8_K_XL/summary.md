The **Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes** (Studienstiftung) is a prestigious German scholarship program founded in 1925, initially as a department of the Deutsche Studentenschaft. It was revived in 1948 after being dissolved under National Socialism. The organization supports exceptionally talented students and doctoral candidates through a combination of financial and academic support, emphasizing excellence, initiative, and responsibility.

### **Admission Criteria**
Admission is based on high academic achievement and social commitment, following the same criteria as the **BAföG** (German state student support system). Applicants can be nominated by:
1. **Schools**: High school principals can nominate 30 senior students annually.
2. **Universities**: Professors can nominate students directly.
3. **Examinations**: Students can apply through an assessment test.
4. **Competitions**: Winners of national or international competitions may be considered.
5. **Alumni**: Past scholars can nominate students they know.
6. **Artists/Musicians**: Universities can nominate students from the fine arts, music, and performing arts.

Scholarship holders are selected through a rigorous process, including interviews and group discussions. Admission is not limited by age or nationality, though students must study full-time at a university in Germany, another EU country, or Switzerland.

### **Scholarship Benefits**
The Studienstiftung provides both financial and academic support:
- **Financial Support**: Monthly allowances (€300) and need-based scholarships (up to €855/month) for undergraduate, graduate, and PhD students. Additional funds are available for students with children.
- **Academic Support**: Summer schools, research groups, language courses, workshops, and mentoring by liaison professors. Scholars must submit regular reports on their studies.
- **Doctoral Scholarships**: Monthly awards of €1,550 (or €1,650 from 2025) plus a research allowance, with tax-free status under German tax law.
- **Open Scholarship Programs**: Partnerships with external organizations (e.g., KAUST, McCloy Program) for specialized internships or study opportunities.

### **Statistics (2023)**
- **Total Scholarships**: 2,179 (undergraduate/graduate) + 301 (doctoral).
- **Gender Distribution**: ~55.4% women.
- **Financial Support**: 71.5% receive a basic allowance, 16.4% partial need-based scholarships, and 11.1% the maximum need-based scholarship.

The Studienstiftung is renowned for fostering future leaders in academia, business, and public service, with alumni contributing significantly to Germany’s intellectual and cultural landscape.
