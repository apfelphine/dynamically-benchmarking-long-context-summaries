# **Concise Summary:**

The Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes (Foundation for German Students) is a German foundation established in 1948 that provides financial and academic support to exceptionally talented German and EU students. Founded after being dissolved in 1934 under National Socialism, the foundation operates on principles of "excellence, initiative, and responsibility." It offers scholarships for bachelor's, master's, and doctoral studies, covering monthly allowances, insurance subsidies, and travel grants, with PhD scholars receiving significantly higher awards. Admission is primarily through nomination by schools, professors, or examination boards, though candidates can also apply via a competitive assessment test.

Statistics from 2005 to 2023 show a consistent growth in the number of scholars, rising from 6,966 in 2005 to 14,241 in 2021, representing approximately 0.40% to 0.49% of the total student population in Germany during this period. The foundation manages various internal and open scholarship programs, including partnerships with international institutions like KAUST, and maintains a rigorous selection process that evaluates not only academic achievement but also social commitment and extracurricular interests.
