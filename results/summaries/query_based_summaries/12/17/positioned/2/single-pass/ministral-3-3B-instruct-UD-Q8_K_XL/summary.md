The **King’s Scholar** program is a prestigious academic scholarship at **King’s College Cambridge**, awarded to undergraduates who achieve first-class degrees. Recipients receive a £350 prize and are invited to a ceremony in King’s College Chapel, where they sign their names in the **King’s Scholar Book**. They also attend a three-course formal dinner with their respective Directors of Studies, covering tuition and fees. The program is historically linked to King’s College’s founding by **Henry VI**, ensuring its continuity with Eton College’s King’s Scholars tradition.
