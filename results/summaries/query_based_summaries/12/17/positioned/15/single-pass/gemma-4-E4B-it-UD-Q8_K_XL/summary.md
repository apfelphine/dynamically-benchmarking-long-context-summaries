# King's Scholar Summary

The term "King's Scholar" refers to distinct scholarship programs across several institutions. At the **University of Cambridge**, the title is used for undergraduates who achieve first-class degrees from King's College, receiving a prize of £350 and the privilege of attending a ceremony in the King's College Chapel.

At **Eton College**, a King's Scholar (known as a "Colleger" or "tug") is admitted to the original ancient College house after passing the College Election examination and being awarded a Foundation Scholarship. These scholars live in College and wear a black academic gown over their uniform. Additionally, King's Scholars at **King's College Cambridge** are undergraduates who earn first-class degrees. The title is also awarded at the **King's Schools** in Canterbury, Chester, Ely, Gloucester, Peterborough, Rochester, and Worcester, where the scholarship recognizes academic or musical attainment, often accompanied by a fee discount.
