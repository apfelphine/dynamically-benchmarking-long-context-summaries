The **King’s Scholar** is a prestigious academic distinction associated with several prestigious institutions in the UK, particularly at **Eton College** and **King’s College Cambridge**.

### **Eton College**
At Eton, a King’s Scholar (or "Colleger") is a student who has passed the **College Election examination** and been awarded a **Foundation Scholarship**. These scholars are admitted to the oldest house at Eton, known as **"College"**, and live in a dedicated boarding area. They are distinguished by wearing a **black academic gown** over their school uniform and are part of a select group of around **70 students** aged 13–18. King’s Scholars share many aspects of school life with other students but eat meals exclusively in **College Hall**, participate in formal traditions, and engage in academic and extracurricular activities. The scholarship traditionally provided full tuition waivers, though modern scholarships may include partial fee reductions based on financial need. Notable alumni include former British Prime Ministers **Robert Walpole** and **Boris Johnson**.

### **King’s College Cambridge**
At **King’s College Cambridge**, the term "King’s Scholar" refers to undergraduates who achieve **first-class degrees**. These scholars receive a **£350 prize** and are invited to a ceremony in **King’s College Chapel**, where they sign their names in the **King’s Scholar Book**. They also attend a **three-course formal dinner** in the subsequent Michaelmas term, dining with their respective Directors of Studies. This tradition stems from scholarships originally endowed by **King Henry VI**, who also founded Eton College.

### **King’s Schools (Canterbury, Ely, Worcester, etc.)**
At the **King’s Schools** (Canterbury, Ely, Worcester, and others), King’s Scholarships are awarded to students with exceptional academic or musical attainment. These scholarships often come with **fee discounts** and include privileges such as:
- Admission to the **cathedral foundation** (e.g., Ely Cathedral for Ely Scholars).
- Special gowns (e.g., red gowns for King’s Ely Scholars).
- Rights to marry in the cathedral and be buried within its grounds.

The scholarships are historically tied to **Henry VIII’s re-endowment** of these schools after the Dissolution of the Monasteries, though modern criteria emphasize academic excellence.

### **Key Distinctions**
- **Eton’s King’s Scholars** are a select group of boarding students with a long-standing tradition of academic prestige.
- **King’s College Cambridge’s "King’s Scholar"** title is awarded post-graduation for first-class degrees.
- **King’s Schools’ King’s Scholars** are recognized for academic or musical achievement, often with fee reductions.

The King’s Scholar designation reflects a legacy of academic excellence and institutional tradition across these prestigious institutions.
