The **King’s Scholar** is a prestigious academic distinction at **King’s College, Cambridge**, awarded to undergraduates who achieve **first-class degrees**. This honor confers a prize of £350 and grants scholars the right to attend a ceremony in **King’s College Chapel**, where they sign their name in the **King’s Scholar Book**. Additionally, scholars dine for free with their respective **Directors of Studies** in the subsequent **Michaelmas term**. This tradition stems from scholarships established by King Henry VI, linking King’s College with Eton College, both founded by him. The distinction is distinct from other scholarships at Cambridge, emphasizing academic excellence rather than financial need.
