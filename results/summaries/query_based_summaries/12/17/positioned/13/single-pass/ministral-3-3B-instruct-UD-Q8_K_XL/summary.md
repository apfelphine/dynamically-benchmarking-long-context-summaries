The **King's Scholar** is a prestigious academic designation at **King's College Cambridge**, awarded to undergraduates who achieve **first-class degrees**. This honor confers a prize of £350 and grants access to a formal dinner with the college’s Directors of Studies, where scholars sign their names in the **King's Scholar book**. The tradition stems from scholarships established by **King Henry VI**, linking King’s College Cambridge with Eton College, both founded by the same monarch. While the title is not exclusive to King’s College, it is most closely associated with Cambridge’s academic excellence and the legacy of its founding royal patron.
