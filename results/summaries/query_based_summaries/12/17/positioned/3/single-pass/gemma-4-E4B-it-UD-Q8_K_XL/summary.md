**King's Scholar** programs exist at multiple institutions, each with distinct traditions and purposes:

*   **University of Cambridge (Eton College):** Here, a King's Scholar (or "Colleger") is admitted after passing the College Election examination and receiving a Foundation Scholarship. These scholars reside in the original college building, distinguished by wearing a black academic gown, and remain there for five years.
*   **Westminster School:** Foundation scholars are termed Queen's Scholars when a Queen reigns and King's Scholars when a King reigns; selection is via competitive examination called "The Challenge."
*   **King's College Cambridge:** The term King's Scholar applies to undergraduates who achieve first-class degrees, granting them a prize of £350 and entitlement to a special ceremony.
*   **The King's Schools (Canterbury, Chester, Ely, Gloucester, Peterborough, Rochester, Worcester):** These schools award King's Scholarships in the name of King Henry VIII, typically recognizing academic or musical attainment and often accompanied by a discount on school fees.

At the King's School, Worcester, the scholarship is the highest awarded, and the title is granted based on academic or musical attainment. The King's Scholars are members of the Worcester Cathedral Foundation.
