The **Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program** is a professional exchange initiative supported by the U.S. Department of State. Established in 1992, it provides summer internships, career training, and financial support to Eurasian scholars and graduate students studying in the United States. The program was originally administered by the International Research & Exchanges Board and later by Cultural Vistas, with a renewed focus and scope launched in 2015. It aims to foster international collaboration and professional development among participants.
