The Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program is a professional exchange initiative supported by the United States Department of State, designed to provide summer internship placements, career training, and financial assistance to Eurasian scholars and graduate students studying in the U.S. Established by the U.S. Congress in fiscal year 1992 under the Freedom Support Act (Pub. L. 102–511, Sec. 801), the program was originally administered by the International Research & Exchanges Board and the American Councils for International Education. It was later transferred to Cultural Vistas in 2015, which launched a renewed format and scope for the program.

Edmund S. Muskie, the program's namesake, was a prominent U.S. Senator from Maine who served from 1959 to 1980 and later held the office of U.S. Secretary of State from 1980 to 1981. A key figure in American environmental legislation, he championed the Clean Air Act of 1970, the Clean Water Act of 1972, and the Clean Water Act Amendments of 1977, earning the nickname "Mr. Clean." Muskie also played a pivotal role in civil rights, the anti-war movement, and the establishment of the federal budget process. He served as the chairman of the Senate Budget Committee and was appointed Secretary of State under President Jimmy Carter, where he managed diplomatic efforts during the Soviet invasion of Afghanistan and the Iran hostage crisis. Muskie passed away in 1996, and his legacy is commemorated through various honors, including the Edmund S. Muskie Distinguished Public Service Award and the annual Edmund S. Muskie Day in Maine.
