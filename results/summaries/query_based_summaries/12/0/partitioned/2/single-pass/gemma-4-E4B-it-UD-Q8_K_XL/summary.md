The Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program is a professional exchange program supported by the United States Department of State. It provides summer internship placements, career training, and financial support specifically to Eurasian scholars and graduate students who are studying in the United States.

The program was established by the United States Congress in the 1992 fiscal year (Pub. L. 102–138, Sec. 227) and was named under the Freedom Support Act of 1992 (Pub. L. 102–511, Sec. 801). Its scope and format were subsequently renewed and launched by Cultural Vistas in 2015. Historically, the program was administered by the International Research & Exchanges Board and the American Council of Teachers of Russian/American Council for Collaboration in Education and Language.
