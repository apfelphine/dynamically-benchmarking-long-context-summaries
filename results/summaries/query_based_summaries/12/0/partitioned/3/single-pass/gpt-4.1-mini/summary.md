The Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program is a professional exchange initiative supported by the United States Department of State. Established by the U.S. Congress in the 1992 fiscal year and named under the Freedom Support Act of 1992, the program offers summer internship placements, career training, and financial support specifically for Eurasian scholars and graduate students studying in the United States. Historically, it was administered by organizations such as the International Research & Exchanges Board and the American Council of Teachers of Russian/American Council for Collaboration in Education and Language. Since 2015, Cultural Vistas has relaunched the Muskie program with a renewed format and expanded scope.

Named after Edmund S. Muskie, a notable American politician with a distinguished career including serving as Secretary of State, the program aims to foster professional development and cross-cultural understanding by enabling graduates from Eurasian countries to gain valuable experience in the U.S. workforce and academic settings during the summer months.
