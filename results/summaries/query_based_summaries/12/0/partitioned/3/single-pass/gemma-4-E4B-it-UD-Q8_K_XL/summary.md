The Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program is a professional exchange program that is supported by the United States Department of State. It is designed to offer Eurasian scholars and graduate students studying in the United States opportunities for career training and financial assistance during their summer internships.

The program was officially established by the United States Congress in the 1992 fiscal year under Pub. L. 102–138, Sec. 227, and was subsequently named by the Freedom Support Act of 1992 (Pub. L. 102–511, Sec. 801). In 2015, the Muskie program was relaunched with a renewed format and scope by Cultural Vistas. Historically, the program was administered by the International Research & Exchanges Board and the American Council of Teachers of Russian/American Council for Collaboration in Education and Language.
