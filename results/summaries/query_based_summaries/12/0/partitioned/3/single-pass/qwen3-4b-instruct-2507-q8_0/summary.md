The Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program is a professional exchange program supported by the United States Department of State. It provides summer internship placements, career training, and financial support to Eurasian scholars and graduate students studying in the United States. The program was established by the United States Congress in 1992 and was named by the Freedom Support Act of 1992. The program was later restructured and expanded by Cultural Vistas in 2015. Previously, it was administered by the International Research & Exchanges Board and the American Council of Teachers of Russian/American Council for Collaboration in Education and Language.
