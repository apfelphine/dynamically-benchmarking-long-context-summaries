The **Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program** is a professional exchange initiative supported by the U.S. Department of State, established by Congress in fiscal year 1992 and named under the Freedom Support Act of 1992. It provides summer internships, career training, and financial support specifically for Eurasian scholars and graduate students studying in the United States. The program was relaunched with a renewed format and scope by Cultural Vistas in 2015. Previously, it was administered by organizations such as the International Research & Exchanges Board and the American Council of Teachers of Russian/American Council for Collaboration in Education and Language.

Named to honor Edmund S. Muskie—a prominent U.S. politician known for his environmental and civil rights leadership—the program reflects his legacy in fostering international cooperation and educational opportunity. Muskie himself held several significant offices, including U.S. Senator, Governor of Maine, and U.S. Secretary of State. His commitment to public service and environmental protection underscores the program’s focus on professional and cultural exchange aimed at empowering future leaders from Eurasia.
