The **Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program**, established in 1992, is a professional exchange initiative administered by the U.S. Department of State. It provides summer internships, career training, and financial support to graduate students and scholars from Eurasian countries studying in the United States. The program aims to foster international cooperation, cultural exchange, and professional development among participants. It was originally part of broader efforts to strengthen ties between the U.S. and post-Soviet nations, evolving into a structured initiative to enhance diplomatic and academic collaboration.
