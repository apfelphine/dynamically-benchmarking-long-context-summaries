The **Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program** is a professional exchange initiative supported by the U.S. Department of State, offering summer internships, career training, and financial support to Eurasian scholars and graduate students studying in the United States. Established in 1992, the program was named in honor of Edmund S. Muskie, a prominent U.S. politician and diplomat known for his contributions to environmental policy, civil rights, and international relations.

The program was originally administered by the International Research & Exchanges Board and American Council for Teachers of Russian before being reimagined in 2015 by Cultural Vistas. It aims to foster professional development, cultural exchange, and networking opportunities for participants, particularly those from countries in the former Soviet Union and Eastern Europe. The Muskie Internship Program emphasizes career advancement, exposure to U.S. institutions, and collaboration with peers in academic and professional fields.
