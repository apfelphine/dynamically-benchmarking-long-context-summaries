The Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program is a professional exchange program supported by the United States Department of State. It provides summer internship placements, career training, and financial support to Eurasian scholars and graduate students studying in the United States. Established by the United States Congress in 1992, the program was named by the Freedom Support Act of 1992. The program was later re-launched with a renewed format and scope in 2015 by Cultural Vistas. It was previously administered by the International Research & Exchanges Board and the American Council of Teachers of Russian/American Council for Collaboration in Education and Language.
