The Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program is a professional exchange initiative supported by the United States Department of State, established by the United States Congress in the 1992 fiscal year (Pub. L. 102–138, Sec. 227) and named under the Freedom Support Act of 1992 (Pub. L. 102–511, Sec. 801). The program provides summer internship placements, career training, and financial assistance to Eurasian scholars and graduate students studying in the United States. Historically administered by the International Research & Exchanges Board and various American Councils, the program underwent a renewed format and scope in 2015 when it was launched by Cultural Vistas.

The program's mission is to foster understanding and strengthen ties between the United States and Eurasian nations through educational exchange. It targets individuals with high academic achievement and leadership potential to facilitate direct engagement with U.S. institutions and culture. By offering support to scholars and students from regions including Russia, China, and other Eurasian countries, the program aims to contribute to mutual understanding, promote international cooperation, and enhance the intellectual and personal growth of future leaders in the region.
