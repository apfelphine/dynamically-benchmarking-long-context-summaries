# Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program

The Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program is a professional exchange initiative supported by the United States Department of State, designed to provide summer internship placements, career training, and financial assistance to Eurasian scholars and graduate students studying in the United States. Established by the United States Congress in the 1992 fiscal year under the Freedom Support Act, the program was originally administered by the International Research & Exchanges Board and the American Council of Teachers of Russian/American Council for Collaboration in Education and Language. In 2015, the program was relaunched with a renewed format and scope by Cultural Vistas, continuing its mission to foster international understanding through educational exchange.

The program focuses on supporting students from Eurasian regions, offering them opportunities to gain practical experience within the U.S. academic and professional sectors while receiving necessary funding for their studies. Its structure aligns with other major U.S. educational exchange initiatives, aiming to cultivate future leaders through direct engagement with American institutions and society. The program's administration and specific operational details have evolved over the decades to adapt to changing global contexts and administrative needs, maintaining its core objective of facilitating cross-cultural educational collaboration.
