The Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program is a professional exchange program supported by the U.S. Department of State. It provides summer internships, career training, and financial support to Eurasian scholars and graduate students studying in the United States. Established in 1992 by the U.S. Congress and named under the Freedom Support Act, the program was initially administered by the International Research & Exchanges Board and the American Council of Teachers of Russian/American Council for Collaboration in Education and Language. In 2015, the program was re-launched by Cultural Vistas, marking a renewed format and scope. The program aims to foster international academic and professional development through structured internships and support for Eurasian students pursuing advanced studies in the U.S.
