The **Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program** is a professional exchange initiative sponsored by the U.S. Department of State that offers summer internships, career training, and financial support to Eurasian scholars and graduate students studying in the United States. Established by the U.S. Congress in 1992 and named under the Freedom Support Act of the same year, the program has undergone various administrative changes over time and was relaunched in 2015 by Cultural Vistas with a renewed format. Previously, it had been administered by organizations such as the International Research & Exchanges Board and the American Council of Teachers of Russian/American Council for Collaboration in Education and Language.
