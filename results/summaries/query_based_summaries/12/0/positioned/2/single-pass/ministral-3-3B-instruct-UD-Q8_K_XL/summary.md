The **Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program** is a professional exchange initiative supported by the U.S. Department of State. Established in 1992, it provides summer internships, career training, and financial support to scholars and graduate students from Eurasian countries studying in the United States. The program was initially administered by the International Research & Exchanges Board and the American Council of Teachers of Russian/American Council for Collaboration in Education, but was later overseen by Cultural Vistas under a renewed format and scope. The Muskie program aims to foster international academic and professional exchange, emphasizing the development of skills and networks for future leaders in the region.
