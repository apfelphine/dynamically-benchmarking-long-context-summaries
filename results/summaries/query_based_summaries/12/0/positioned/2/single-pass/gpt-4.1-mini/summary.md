The **Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program** is a professional exchange initiative supported by the U.S. Department of State, established by Congress in the 1992 fiscal year and named under the Freedom Support Act of 1992. It provides summer internship placements, career training, and financial support specifically to Eurasian scholars and graduate students studying in the United States. The program was relaunched with a renewed format and scope by Cultural Vistas in 2015. Previously, administration was handled by organizations such as the International Research & Exchanges Board and the American Council of Teachers of Russian/American Council for Collaboration in Education and Language.
