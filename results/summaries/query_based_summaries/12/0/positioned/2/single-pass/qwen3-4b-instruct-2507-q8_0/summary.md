The Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program is a professional exchange initiative supported by the U.S. Department of State, providing summer internships, career training, and financial support to Eurasian scholars and graduate students studying in the United States. Established in 1992 by U.S. Congress (Pub. L. 102–138, Sec. 227) and named by the Freedom Support Act of 1992 (Pub. L. 102–511, Sec. 801), the program was restructured and expanded in 2015 through Cultural Vistas. It was previously administered by the International Research & Exchanges Board and the American Council of Teachers of Russian/American Council for Collaboration in Education and Language. The program aims to foster international understanding and professional development by offering structured placements and academic support to participants from Eurasia.
