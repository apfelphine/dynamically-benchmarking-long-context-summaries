The **Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program** is a professional exchange initiative supported by the U.S. Department of State, established by Congress in the 1992 fiscal year and named under the Freedom Support Act of 1992. The program offers summer internship placements, career training, and financial assistance specifically to Eurasian scholars and graduate students studying in the United States. Initially administered by organizations such as the International Research & Exchanges Board and American Council of Teachers of Russian, the program was relaunched in 2015 with a renewed format and scope by Cultural Vistas.

Named after Edmund S. Muskie, a prominent American statesman recognized for his environmental and civil rights leadership, the internship program reflects his legacy of fostering international collaboration and professional development. It aims to provide participants with experiential learning opportunities to enhance their careers while strengthening ties between the United States and Eurasian countries.
