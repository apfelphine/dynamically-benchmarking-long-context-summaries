The **Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program** is a professional exchange initiative supported by the U.S. Department of State. Established in 1992, it provides summer internships, career training, and financial support to scholars and graduate students from Eurasian countries studying in the United States. The program was initially administered by the International Research & Exchanges Board and later by Cultural Vistas, with its structure and scope updated in 2015. The internships aim to foster international cooperation and professional development among participants.
