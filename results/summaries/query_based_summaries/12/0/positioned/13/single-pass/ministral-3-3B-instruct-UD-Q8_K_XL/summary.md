The **Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program** is a professional exchange initiative supported by the U.S. Department of State. Established in 1992, it provides summer internships, career training, and financial support to scholars and graduate students from Eurasian countries studying in the United States. Originally administered by the International Research & Exchanges Board and American Council for Collaboration in Education and Language, the program was revamped in 2015 by Cultural Vistas. It aims to foster international cooperation, professional development, and cultural exchange among participants.
