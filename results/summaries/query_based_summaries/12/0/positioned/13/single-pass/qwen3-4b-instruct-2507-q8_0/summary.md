The Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program is a professional exchange initiative supported by the U.S. Department of State. Established in 1992 by the U.S. Congress and named under the Freedom Support Act, the program provides summer internships, career training, and financial support to Eurasian scholars and graduate students studying in the United States. Initially administered by the International Research & Exchanges Board and the American Council of Teachers of Russian/American Council for Collaboration in Education and Language, the program was restructured and expanded in 2015 through Cultural Vistas. The program aims to foster international academic and professional development by offering participants opportunities to engage with U.S. institutions and contribute to cross-cultural understanding.
