The **Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program** is a professional exchange initiative established by the United States Congress in the 1992 fiscal year and named through the Freedom Support Act of 1992 to honor Edmund S. Muskie, a notable American politician and statesman. Supported by the U.S. Department of State, the program offers summer internships, career training, and financial assistance to Eurasian scholars and graduate students studying in the United States. It aims to enhance professional development and cultural exchange, fostering connections between the U.S. and the Eurasian region. The program was originally administered by organizations such as the International Research & Exchanges Board and the American Council of Teachers of Russian but was relaunched with a renewed format and scope by Cultural Vistas in 2015.

The Muskie Internship Program reflects the legacy of Edmund S. Muskie, who played a significant role in environmental legislation, civil rights, and U.S. diplomacy during his extensive political career. The program contributes to the development of future leaders from Eurasia by providing them with practical experience and exposure to American professional environments, consistent with Muskie's dedication to public service and international cooperation.
