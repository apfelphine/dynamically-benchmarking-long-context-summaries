The Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program is a professional exchange initiative supported by the U.S. Department of State. It provides summer internships, career training, and financial support to Eurasian scholars and graduate students studying in the United States. Established by Congress in 1992 (Pub. L. 102–138, Sec. 227) and named under the Freedom Support Act of 1992 (Pub. L. 102–511, Sec. 801), the program was later restructured and expanded by Cultural Vistas in 2015. Previously administered by the International Research & Exchanges Board and the American Council of Teachers of Russian/American Council for Collaboration in Education and Language, the program aims to foster international engagement and professional development among Eurasian students.
