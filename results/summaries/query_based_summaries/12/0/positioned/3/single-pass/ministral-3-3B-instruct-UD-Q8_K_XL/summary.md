The **Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program** is a professional exchange initiative administered by the U.S. Department of State, offering summer internships, career training, and financial support to scholars and graduate students from Eurasian countries. Established in 1992, the program was initially administered by the International Research & Exchanges Board and later by Cultural Vistas. It aims to foster international cooperation and professional development among young professionals, particularly in fields like diplomacy, public policy, and international relations. The program is named in honor of former U.S. Secretary of State Edmund Muskie, reflecting its commitment to promoting global understanding and collaboration.
