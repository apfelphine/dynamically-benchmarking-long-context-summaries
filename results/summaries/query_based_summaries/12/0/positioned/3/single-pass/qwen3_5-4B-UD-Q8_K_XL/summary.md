# Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program

The Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program is a professional exchange initiative supported by the United States Department of State, designed to provide summer internship placements, career training, and financial assistance to Eurasian scholars and graduate students studying in the United States. Established by the United States Congress in the 1992 fiscal year under Public Law 102–138, Section 227, and named by the Freedom Support Act of 1992 (Public Law 102–511, Section 801), the program was previously administered by the International Research & Exchanges Board, the American Council of Teachers of Russian, and the American Council for Collaboration in Education and Language. In 2015, the program was relaunched with a renewed format and scope by Cultural Vistas.

The program's primary objective is to facilitate cultural and educational exchange between the United States and Eurasian nations by enabling advanced students to gain practical experience in U.S. professional settings. Selection and administration involve rigorous criteria aimed at identifying candidates with high academic achievement and leadership potential, ensuring they can contribute meaningfully to international understanding. While specific selection metrics and application processes vary by year and administering body, the program remains a significant mechanism for fostering transnational collaboration and exposing scholars to diverse career pathways within the United States.
