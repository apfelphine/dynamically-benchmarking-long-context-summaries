The **Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program** is a professional exchange initiative supported by the U.S. Department of State, designed to provide summer internships, career training, and financial assistance to Eurasian scholars and graduate students studying in the United States. Established by the U.S. Congress in fiscal year 1992 and named under the Freedom Support Act of 1992, the program has been administered by various organizations over time and was relaunched with an updated format by Cultural Vistas in 2015.

Beyond the internship program, Edmund S. Muskie's legacy includes significant contributions to environmental legislation, civil rights, and U.S. diplomacy. The program bearing his name enables promising students from Eurasian regions to gain professional experience in the U.S., continuing Muskie's commitment to public service and international cooperation.
