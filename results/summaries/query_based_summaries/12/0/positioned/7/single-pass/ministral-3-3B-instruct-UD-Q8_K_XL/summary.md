The **Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program** is a professional exchange initiative administered by the U.S. Department of State, offering summer internships, career training, and financial support to scholars and graduate students from Eurasian countries studying in the United States. Established in 1992, the program was named after former U.S. Senator Edmund Muskie, reflecting its focus on fostering international cooperation and professional development. It provides opportunities for young professionals to gain hands-on experience in various sectors, including government, business, and public service, while contributing to the exchange of ideas and expertise between the U.S. and international communities.
