# Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program

The Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program is a professional exchange initiative supported by the United States Department of State, designed to provide summer internship placements, career training, and financial assistance to Eurasian scholars and graduate students studying in the United States. Established by the United States Congress in fiscal year 1992 under the Freedom Support Act, the program was originally administered by the International Research & Exchanges Board and later by the American Council of Teachers of Russian and the American Council for Collaboration in Education and Language. In 2015, Cultural Vistas assumed administration of the program with a renewed format and scope.

Named in honor of former Senator Edmund S. Muskie, a prominent figure in American diplomacy and environmental policy, the program aims to foster international understanding and professional development among students from Eurasian regions. It serves as a vital channel for these scholars to gain direct experience within the U.S. academic and professional sectors while contributing to the broader goals of mutual understanding and educational exchange.
