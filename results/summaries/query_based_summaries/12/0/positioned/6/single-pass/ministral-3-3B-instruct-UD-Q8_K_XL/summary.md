The **Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program**, established by the U.S. Department of State, is a professional exchange initiative supporting Eurasian scholars and graduate students studying in the United States. Originally founded in 1992, the program was revamped in 2015 by **Cultural Vistas** and focuses on providing summer internships, career training, and financial support. It emphasizes fostering international collaboration and professional development among participants. The program is named in honor of former U.S. Secretary of State Edmund S. Muskie, who championed diplomacy and public service.
