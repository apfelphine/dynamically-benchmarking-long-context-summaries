The Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program is a professional exchange program administered by the U.S. Department of State, providing summer internships, career training, and financial support to Eurasian scholars and graduate students studying in the United States. Established in 1992 by U.S. Congress (Pub. L. 102–138, Sec. 227) and named under the Freedom Support Act of 1992 (Pub. L. 102–511, Sec. 801), the program aims to foster international understanding and professional development. It was later restructured and relaunched in 2015 by Cultural Vistas, with the original administration previously managed by the International Research & Exchanges Board and the American Council of Teachers of Russian/American Council for Collaboration in Education and Language. The program supports students from Eurasian nations, offering them opportunities for academic and professional growth within the U.S. educational and governmental systems.
