The **Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program** is a professional exchange initiative supported by the U.S. Department of State, offering summer internships, career training, and financial assistance to scholars and graduate students from Eurasia studying in the United States. Established by the U.S. Congress in the 1992 fiscal year and named under the Freedom Support Act of 1992, the program aims to enhance professional experience and foster mutual understanding. Since 2015, the program, with a renewed format and expanded scope, has been managed by Cultural Vistas; previously, it was administered by the International Research & Exchanges Board and the American Council of Teachers of Russian/American Council for Collaboration in Education and Language.
