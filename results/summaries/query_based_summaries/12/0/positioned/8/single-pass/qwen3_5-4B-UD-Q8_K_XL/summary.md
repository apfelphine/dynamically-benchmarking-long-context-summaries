The Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program is a professional exchange program supported by the United States Department of State that provides summer internship placements, career training, and financial assistance to Eurasian scholars and graduate students studying in the United States. Established by the United States Congress in fiscal year 1992 (Pub. L. 102–138, Sec. 227) and named by the Freedom Support Act of 1992 (Pub. L. 102–511, Sec. 801), the program was originally administered by the International Research & Exchanges Board and the American Council of Teachers of Russian/American Council for Collaboration in Education and Language. In 2015, the program was relaunched with a renewed format and scope under the administration of Cultural Vistas.

Edmund S. Muskie (1914–1996) was a prominent American politician from Maine who served as the 62nd Governor of Maine (1955–1959), a U.S. Senator (1959–1980), and U.S. Secretary of State (1980–1981). Known for his advocacy of environmental protection, civil rights, and bipartisanship, Muskie played a pivotal role in passing the Clean Air Act and the Clean Water Acts. His legacy includes the establishment of the Edmund S. Muskie School of Public Service at the University of Southern Maine, the Edmund S. Muskie Pro Bono Service Award by the American Bar Association, and the Edmund S. Muskie Distinguished Public Service Award by the Truman National Security Project.
