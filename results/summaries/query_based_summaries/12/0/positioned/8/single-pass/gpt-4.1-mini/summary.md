The **Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program** is a professional exchange initiative supported by the United States Department of State, established by U.S. Congress in the 1992 fiscal year and named under the Freedom Support Act of 1992. It offers summer internship placements, career training, and financial support specifically to Eurasian scholars and graduate students studying in the United States. The program aims to provide valuable professional experience to participants and was relaunched with a renewed format and scope by Cultural Vistas in 2015. Historically, it has been administered by organizations such as the International Research & Exchanges Board and the American Council for Teachers of Russian/American Council for Collaboration in Education and Language.

The program honors the legacy of Edmund Muskie, a prominent U.S. politician and statesman known for his public service and environmental advocacy. It is one of several awards and fellowships that commemorate Muskie’s contributions to government and civil society.
