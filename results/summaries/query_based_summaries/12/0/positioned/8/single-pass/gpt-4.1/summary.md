## Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program

The Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program is a professional exchange initiative administered and supported by the United States Department of State. Established by the United States Congress in the 1992 fiscal year (Pub. L. 102–138, Sec. 227) and named by the Freedom Support Act of 1992 (Pub. L. 102–511, Sec. 801), the program provides summer internship placements, career training, and financial support specifically for Eurasian graduate students and scholars studying at universities in the United States. Its primary aim is to facilitate professional development and cultural exchange by connecting participants with U.S. organizations and institutions.

The Muskie program has undergone transitions in its administration, with Cultural Vistas relaunching its renewed format and scope in 2015. Previously, it was managed by the International Research & Exchanges Board and the American Council of Teachers of Russian/American Council for Collaboration in Education and Language. Through structured internship placements and career development opportunities, the program supports Eurasian students in building practical skills, expanding professional networks, and enhancing cross-cultural understanding while in the United States.
