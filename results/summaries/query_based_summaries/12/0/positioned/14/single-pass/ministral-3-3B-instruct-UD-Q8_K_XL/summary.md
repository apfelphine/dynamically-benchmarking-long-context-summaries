The **Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program** is a professional exchange initiative administered by the U.S. Department of State, offering summer internships, career training, and financial support to Eurasian scholars and graduate students studying in the United States. Established in 1992, the program was named in honor of former U.S. Senator Edmund S. Muskie, who championed international diplomacy and public service.

The Muskie Internship Program provides opportunities for participants to engage in hands-on work at government agencies, nonprofits, and other organizations, fostering professional development and networking. It emphasizes the exchange of ideas, cultural understanding, and collaboration between the U.S. and Eurasian regions. The program’s mission aligns with Muskie’s legacy of promoting global cooperation and addressing pressing international challenges.
