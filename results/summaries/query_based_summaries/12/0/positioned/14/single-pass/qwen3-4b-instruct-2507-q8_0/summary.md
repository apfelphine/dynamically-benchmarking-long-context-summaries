The Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program is a professional exchange initiative supported by the U.S. Department of State, providing summer internships, career training, and financial support to Eurasian scholars and graduate students studying in the United States. Established by the U.S. Congress in 1992 (Pub. L. 102–138, Sec. 227) and named under the Freedom Support Act of 1992 (Pub. L. 102–511, Sec. 801), the program was administered previously by the International Research & Exchanges Board and the American Council of Teachers of Russian/American Council for Collaboration in Education and Language. In 2015, Cultural Vistas launched a renewed format and scope for the program. It aims to foster international academic and professional development, offering participants opportunities to engage with U.S. institutions and contribute to cross-cultural understanding.
