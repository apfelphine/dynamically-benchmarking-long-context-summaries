The Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program, established by the U.S. Congress in 1992 and named in the Freedom Support Act, is a professional exchange initiative supported by the U.S. Department of State. It provides summer internship placements, career training, and financial assistance to Eurasian scholars and graduate students studying in the United States. The program, revitalized in 2015 under Cultural Vistas, aims to enhance participants' professional development through experiential learning opportunities. Previously, it was administered by organizations including the International Research & Exchanges Board and the American Council of Teachers of Russian.

The program honors Edmund Muskie’s legacy, a prominent American politician noted for his public service, environmental advocacy, and diplomacy. Named memorials and awards, such as the Edmund S. Muskie Graduate Fellowship and Pro Bono Service Award, commemorate his contributions. The internship program reflects Muskie's commitment to fostering international understanding and leadership development among Eurasian scholars.
