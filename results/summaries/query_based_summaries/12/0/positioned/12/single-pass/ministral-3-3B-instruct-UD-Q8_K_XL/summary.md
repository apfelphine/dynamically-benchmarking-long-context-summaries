The **Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program** is a professional exchange initiative supported by the U.S. Department of State. Established in 1992, it provides summer internships, career training, and financial support to Eurasian scholars and graduate students studying in the United States. The program was formally reimagined and expanded in 2015 by **Cultural Vistas**, offering a renewed scope and structure to foster international academic and professional collaboration.

The Muskie Internship Program emphasizes experiential learning, allowing participants to engage in hands-on work within U.S. government agencies, nonprofits, and private sector organizations. It aims to enhance the skills and networks of young professionals from Eurasia, promoting mutual understanding and cooperation between the United States and its international partners. The program reflects the broader goals of the U.S. government to strengthen global academic and professional ties through targeted internship opportunities.
