The **Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program** is a professional exchange initiative supported by the United States Department of State, established by the United States Congress in fiscal year 1992 (Pub. L. 102–138, Sec. 227) and named in honor of former U.S. Senator and Secretary of State Edmund S. Muskie. Named by the Freedom Support Act of 1992 (Pub. L. 102–511, Sec. 801), the program provides summer internship placements, career training, and financial support to Eurasian scholars and graduate students studying in the United States. Originally administered by the International Research & Exchanges Board and other councils, the program was launched with a renewed format and scope by Cultural Vistas in 2015.

The Muskie program serves as a key mechanism for fostering international academic exchange and professional development among students from Eurasian regions. It aims to facilitate the integration of these scholars into the U.S. academic and professional environments through structured internships and targeted training. The program's focus on Eurasia distinguishes it from other international exchange initiatives, aligning with the broader U.S. foreign policy objectives of strengthening ties with these regions. While the program has evolved over time, its core mission remains the provision of high-quality educational and professional opportunities for international students, reflecting the legacy of Edmund Muskie's commitment to diplomacy, education, and public service.
