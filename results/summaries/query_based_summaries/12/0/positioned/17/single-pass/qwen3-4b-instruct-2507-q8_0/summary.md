The Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program is a U.S. Department of State-supported professional exchange program established by Congress in 1992 (Pub. L. 102–138, Sec. 227) and named under the Freedom Support Act of 1992 (Pub. L. 102–511, Sec. 801). It provides summer internships, career training, and financial support to Eurasian scholars and graduate students studying in the United States. Originally administered by the International Research & Exchanges Board and the American Council of Teachers of Russian/American Council for Collaboration in Education and Language, the program was re-launched in 2015 by Cultural Vistas with a renewed format and scope. The program aims to foster international academic and professional development, promoting cross-cultural understanding and global engagement.
