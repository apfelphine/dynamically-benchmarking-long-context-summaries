The Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program is a professional exchange initiative supported by the United States Department of State, established by Congress in fiscal year 1992 and later renamed by the Freedom Support Act of 1992. Originally administered by the International Research & Exchanges Board and the American Councils for International Education, the program provides summer internship placements, career training, and financial assistance to Eurasian scholars and graduate students studying in the United States. In 2015, the program was relaunched with a renewed format and scope under the administration of Cultural Vistas. The initiative aims to foster cross-cultural understanding and professional development among international students through direct engagement with U.S. academic and cultural institutions.
