## Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program

The **Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program** is a professional exchange initiative supported by the United States Department of State. Established by the U.S. Congress in 1992 (Pub. L. 102–138, Sec. 227; Freedom Support Act of 1992, Sec. 801), the program offers summer internship placements, career training, and financial support to Eurasian scholars and graduate students studying in the United States. The Muskie program aims to foster professional development and cross-cultural understanding. Originally administered by organizations such as the International Research & Exchanges Board and American Council of Teachers of Russian, the program received a renewed format and scope under Cultural Vistas in 2015.

The Muskie Internship Program is named in honor of Edmund S. Muskie and functions as a component of a broader set of Muskie-branded exchange and fellowship programs designed to encourage the exchange of ideas and expertise between the U.S. and Eurasia. Its primary focus is on professional internships, providing participants with practical experience in their fields of study while facilitating cultural exchange and career advancement.
