The Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program is a professional exchange program supported by the United States Department of State. It provides summer internship placements, career training, and financial support to Eurasian scholars and graduate students studying in the United States. Established by the U.S. Congress in 1992 (Pub. L. 102–138, Sec. 227) and named by the Freedom Support Act of 1992 (Pub. L. 102–511, Sec. 801), the program was restructured and expanded in 2015 by Cultural Vistas. It aims to foster international understanding and professional development through hands-on experience in U.S. institutions.
