The **Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program** is a professional exchange initiative administered by the U.S. Department of State, offering summer internships, career training, and financial support to Eurasian scholars and graduate students studying in the United States. Established in 1992, the program was named in honor of former U.S. Senator Edmund S. Muskie, who championed international cooperation and public service. It provides opportunities for participants to engage in policy, diplomacy, and public administration roles while gaining hands-on experience in U.S. institutions.

The program emphasizes fostering connections between scholars from Eurasia and U.S. government agencies, think tanks, and nonprofits. It supports academic research, policy analysis, and professional development, aligning with Muskie’s legacy of promoting global understanding and collaborative governance. The initiative reflects the broader U.S. commitment to strengthening international academic and professional networks.
