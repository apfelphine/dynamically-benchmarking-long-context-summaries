The Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program, established by the U.S. Congress in the 1992 fiscal year and named under the Freedom Support Act of 1992, is a professional exchange initiative supported by the U.S. Department of State. It offers summer internships, career training, and financial support to Eurasian scholars and graduate students studying in the United States. The program was relaunched in 2015 with a renewed format by Cultural Vistas and has previously been administered by the International Research & Exchanges Board and the American Council of Teachers of Russian/American Council for Collaboration in Education and Language.

The program honors Edmund S. Muskie, a notable American statesman known for his contributions to environmental laws, civil rights, and diplomacy. His legacy includes extensive public service as Governor of Maine, U.S. Senator, Vice Presidential candidate, and U.S. Secretary of State. The internship program aligns with his commitment to fostering leadership, educational exchange, and professional development among emerging leaders from Eurasia.
