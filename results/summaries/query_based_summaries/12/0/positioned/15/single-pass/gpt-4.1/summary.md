## Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program

The **Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program** is a professional exchange initiative supported by the United States Department of State, established by the U.S. Congress in 1992 and named through the Freedom Support Act of 1992. Its primary purpose is to provide summer internship placements, career training, and financial support to Eurasian scholars and graduate students who are studying at higher education institutions in the United States. Starting in 2015, Cultural Vistas launched the program in its renewed format and scope; previously, it was administered by organizations including the International Research & Exchanges Board and the American Council of Teachers of Russian/American Council for Collaboration in Education and Language.

Through this program, participants gain practical professional experience along with targeted career development support, helping bridge academic study and career ambitions. This hands-on internship program has become a key opportunity for Eurasian students in the U.S. to develop professional skills, expand their networks, and enhance employability through real-world work experiences during the summer.
