The Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program is a professional exchange initiative supported by the U.S. Department of State, providing summer internships, career training, and financial support to Eurasian scholars and graduate students studying in the United States. Established by the U.S. Congress in 1992, the program was named under the Freedom Support Act of 1992. It was originally administered by the International Research & Exchanges Board and the American Council of Teachers of Russian/American Council for Collaboration in Education and Language. In 2015, the program was re-launched by Cultural Vistas with a renewed format and scope. The program aims to foster international academic and professional engagement between Eurasian students and the United States.
