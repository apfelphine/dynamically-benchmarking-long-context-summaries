The **Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program** is a professional exchange initiative administered by the U.S. Department of State. Established in 1992, it supports summer internships, career training, and financial assistance for Eurasian scholars and graduate students studying in the United States. Originally administered by the International Research & Exchanges Board and the American Council of Teachers of Russian, the program was later managed by Cultural Vistas. It aims to foster international cooperation and professional development among participants from Eurasia.
