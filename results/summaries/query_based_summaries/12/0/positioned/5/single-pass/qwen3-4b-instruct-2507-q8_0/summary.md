The Edmund S. Muskie Internship Program is a professional exchange program supported by the United States Department of State. It provides summer internship placements, career training, and financial support to Eurasian scholars and graduate students studying in the United States. Established by the U.S. Congress in 1992 and named by the Freedom Support Act of 1992, the program was initially administered by the International Research & Exchanges Board and the American Council of Teachers of Russian/American Council for Collaboration in Education and Language. In 2015, the program was relaunched with a renewed format and scope by Cultural Vistas. The program aims to foster professional development and international understanding through hands-on experience and academic training in the United States.
