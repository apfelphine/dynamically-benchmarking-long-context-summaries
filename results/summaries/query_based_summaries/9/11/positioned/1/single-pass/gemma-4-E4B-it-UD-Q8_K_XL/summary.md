Education in Poland is structured with compulsory schooling encompassing eight years of primary school (szkoła podstawowa). Prior to 2017, this was followed by a three-year mandatory *gimnazjum* (middle school); however, the 2016 "Law on School Education" abolished the *gimnazjum*, extending primary school to eight years.

Upper secondary education offers pathways including general secondary schools (*liceum*), which prepare students for higher education by completing the *Matura* exam. Vocational training is provided through technical schools (*technikum*) and basic vocational schools (*zasadnicza szkoła zawodowa*). Tertiary education follows the Bologna scheme, with Bachelor's degrees typically lasting three years, followed by Master's degrees, and Doctor studies. The school year runs from the first weekday of September to the last weekday of June.
