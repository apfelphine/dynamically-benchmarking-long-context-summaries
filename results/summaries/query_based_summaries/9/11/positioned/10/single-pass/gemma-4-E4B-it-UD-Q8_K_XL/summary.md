## Education in Poland

**Primary and Secondary Education:**
In Poland, children typically begin primary school (*szkoła podstawowa*) at ages six or seven, and this stage currently lasts eight years (grades 1–8), replacing a previous system of six years followed by a three-year *gimnazjum*. Upper secondary education follows, which can be pursued in general secondary schools (*liceum*) or vocational/technical schools (*technikum* and *zasadnicza szkoła zawodowa*). General secondary schools allow students to pass the *Matura* examination to access higher education, while technical schools focus on trades and occupations.

**Higher Education:**
Poland's tertiary education follows the Bologna scheme, generally comprising a three-year bachelor's degree followed by a two-year master's degree. Some specialized fields, such as medicine (six years) and pharmacy (five years), involve longer cycles. University-level grading utilizes a numerical system from 2.0 (failing) to 5.0 (highest grade), with evaluations occurring every semester.
