Poland’s education system is structured into **compulsory education** (ages 6–18) and **upper secondary education**, with a strong emphasis on academic rigor and vocational training.

### **Compulsory Education**
- **Primary School (Szkoła Podstawowa, Grades 1–8):** Begins at age 6, covering core subjects like Polish, mathematics, science, and foreign languages (primarily English). The reform of 2017 extended primary education to eight years, replacing the previous six-year primary plus three-year junior high system.
- **Junior High School (Gimnazjum, Grades 9–11):** Introduced in 1999, this three-year stage prepares students for upper secondary education. Students take exams in humanities, science, and foreign languages to qualify for further studies.

### **Upper Secondary Education**
Poland follows a **dual-track system**, offering both **academic (liceum)** and **vocational (technikum, zasadnicza szkoła zawodowa)** pathways:
- **Academic Pathway (Liceum):**
  - **General Secondary School (Liceum):** Four years of study leading to the *Matura* exam, granting access to higher education.
  - **Profilowane Liceum:** Four-year vocational-academic schools in specialized fields (e.g., IT, business) aligned with the Polish Classification of Activities (PKD).
  - **Matura Exam:** A comprehensive test in Polish, mathematics, and a foreign language (usually English), required for university admission.

- **Vocational Pathway:**
  - **Technikum:** Five-year programs combining academic and vocational training, leading to the *Matura* exam. Popular fields include accounting, electronics, and sales.
  - **Zasadnicza Szkoła Zawodowa:** Three-year vocational schools offering certificates in trades like cooking, gardening, or automobile mechanics. Graduates can later pursue the *Matura* exam or enter the workforce.

### **Tertiary Education**
Poland adheres to the **Bologna Process**, with tertiary education divided into:
- **Bachelor’s Degree (3 years):** Followed by a **master’s degree (2 years)**. Some specialized programs (e.g., medicine, pharmacy) take 5–6 years.
- **Doctoral Studies (3 years):** Requires a master’s degree and original research.
- **Vocational Education:** Post-secondary schools (szkoła policealna) offer two-and-a-half-year programs leading to diplomas in trades like nursing or IT.

### **Grading System**
University-level grading ranges from **2.0 (fail)** to **5.0 (highest)**, with intermediate grades (e.g., 3.5, 4.0). Exams are held twice yearly, with retakes allowed for failing grades.

### **Key Features**
- **Compulsory Education:** Mandatory from ages 6–18, ensuring universal access.
- **Vocational Focus:** Strong emphasis on technical and trade skills, with options for both academic and vocational pathways.
- **Bologna Process Alignment:** Structured higher education to facilitate international recognition.
