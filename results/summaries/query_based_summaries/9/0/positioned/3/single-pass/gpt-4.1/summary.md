## Education in Pakistan: Key Features and Challenges

Education in Pakistan is divided into several stages: primary, secondary, and tertiary. Primary education starts at age 3 with preschool and continues from years 1 to 8, followed by secondary education (years 9–12), with standardized exams leading to SSC and HSSC certificates. The curriculum typically covers arts, sciences, mathematics, languages (Urdu and English), religious studies, and Pakistan Studies. Provincial languages may be taught regionally, while English and Urdu serve as main instruction mediums depending on school type. Secondary education includes various streams such as pre-medical, pre-engineering, computer science, and humanities; alternative international qualifications such as GCE O/A Levels also exist primarily in private institutions. Technical and vocational education is available from middle school through post-secondary diplomas.

Tertiary education encompasses professional and academic tracks with bachelor's, master's, MPhil, and PhD programs. Pakistan's higher education is overseen by various accreditation councils and offers degrees in fields like engineering, medicine, law, architecture, and business. Teacher education faces challenges including outdated curricula, lack of qualified trainers, and underfunding. Gender disparity remains a prominent issue, with female literacy and enrollment rates lagging due to cultural barriers and infrastructure challenges, though recent reforms have improved parity somewhat. The literacy rate has increased to approximately 61% in 2023, with notable regional and gender gaps. Public spending on education remains low, around 2% of GDP, leading to underinvestment, deficient infrastructure, and gaps in access and quality. Non-formal, informal education, distance learning (e.g., Teleschool), and madrassas supplement the formal system, especially for marginalized groups. The education sector in Pakistan continues to grapple with issues of equity, quality, religious influence in curriculum, and chronic underfunding.
