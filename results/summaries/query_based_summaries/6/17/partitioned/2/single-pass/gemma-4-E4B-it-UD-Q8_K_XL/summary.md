**Lynn Conway's Contributions to Computer Science and Technology:**

Lynn Conway was a pioneer in Very Large-Scale Integration (VLSI) design. Working at Xerox PARC, she collaborated with Carver Mead to co-author *Introduction to VLSI Systems*. Her contributions included inventing dimensionless, scalable design rules and creating a new internet-based infrastructure for rapid prototyping and short-run fabrication of chip designs, which was institutionalized as the Metal Oxide Semiconductor Implementation Service (MOSIS) in 1981. This Mead–Conway VLSI design methodology spurred the growth of the electronic design automation industry and the foundry model in the 1980s and 1990s.

Beyond technology, Conway was active in transgender rights activism, providing assistance and maintaining a website with medical and emotional advice for transgender women. She also advocated for equal opportunities and employment protections for transgender individuals in the high-technology industry. She has received numerous honors, including being named one of the "Stonewall 40 trans heroes" in 2009 and being named one of "21 Transgender People Who Influenced American Culture" by Time Magazine in 2014.
