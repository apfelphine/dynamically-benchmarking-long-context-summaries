Greece’s education system is structured into **formal and non-formal** education, governed by the **Hellenic Qualification Framework (HQF)** and aligned with the **European Qualifications Framework (EQF)**.

### **Education in Greece: Structure and Key Features**
#### **Preschool (Pre-Kindergarten & Kindergarten)**
- **Compulsory** for ages 4–6, divided into two years: Pre-Kindergarten (Προνηπιαγωγείο) and Kindergarten (Νηπιαγωγείο).
- **No formal grading** until the end of Kindergarten.
- **Foreign language instruction** begins in Kindergarten, with English as the primary foreign language.

#### **Primary Education (Dimotiko)**
- **Compulsory** for 6 years (ages 6–12), structured into six grades (1st–6th).
- **No formal grades** in the first two years; grades (A–D or 1–10) introduced from **3rd–6th grade**.
- **Curriculum** includes core subjects like Modern Greek, Mathematics, Science, Social Studies, and Foreign Languages (English, French, or German).
- **Automatic progression** to **Gymnasium** (lower secondary education) upon completion.

#### **Lower Secondary Education (Gymnasium)**
- **Compulsory** for 3 years (ages 12–15), divided into three grades (1st–3rd).
- **No formal grades** until the end of the 3rd grade.
- **Curriculum** includes core subjects like Modern Greek, Mathematics, Science, Social Studies, and Foreign Languages.
- **Graduates** receive the **Gymnasium Certificate (Απολυτήριο Γυμνασίου)**, qualifying them for **Upper Secondary Education (Lyceum)**.

#### **Upper Secondary Education (Lyceum)**
- **Non-compulsory**, lasting 3 years (ages 15–18).
- **Three main types of Lyceums**:
  - **General Lyceum (ΓΕΛ)**: Prepares students for higher education.
  - **Vocational Lyceum (ΕΠΑΛ)**: Offers specialized vocational training.
  - **Specialized Lyceums (Music, Art, etc.)**: Focus on specific fields.
- **Graduates** receive the **Lyceum Certificate (Απολυτήριο Λυκείου)**, required for admission to **Higher Education (Universities)**.
- **Pan-Hellenic Examinations (Πανελλαδικές Εξετάσεις)**: Mandatory standardized tests for university admission, based on grades from the 3rd grade of Lyceum.

#### **Vocational and Specialized Education**
- **Vocational Lyceum (ΕΠΑΛ)**: Offers specialized diplomas in fields like IT, Health, Business, and Engineering.
- **Vocational Schools (ΕΠΑΣ)**: Two-year apprenticeship programs for students aged 15–23.
- **Specialized Schools**: Offer advanced training in fields like Tourism, Hospitality, and Applied Arts.

#### **Higher Education (Universities & Academies)**
- **Public Universities**: Free tuition for domestic students (some exceptions for international students).
- **Private Universities**: Offer English-taught programs with tuition fees.
- **Degrees**: Bachelor’s (Πτυχίο, Level 6), Master’s (Μεταπτυχιακό, Level 7), and Doctoral (Διδακτορικό, Level 8).

#### **Non-Formal Education**
- **Open Courses**: Free online courses offered by public universities.
- **Digital Tutorials & Webinars**: Government-funded digital learning platforms.
- **Vocational Training Centers**: Provide specialized training for adults and working professionals.

### **Key Features of Greece’s Education System**
- **Compulsory Education**: Primary (6 years) and Gymnasium (3 years) are mandatory.
- **Standardized Examinations**: Pan-Hellenic Examinations for university admission.
- **Vocational Integration**: Strong emphasis on vocational training and apprenticeships.
- **Equality of Opportunity**: Public education is free, though some disparities exist in quality and resources.

Greece’s education system balances **academic rigor** with **vocational training**, ensuring a structured pathway from primary to higher education while promoting lifelong learning.
