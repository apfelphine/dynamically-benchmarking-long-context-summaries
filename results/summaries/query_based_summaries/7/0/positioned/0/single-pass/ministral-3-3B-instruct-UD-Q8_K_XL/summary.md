### **Education in Greece**

Greece’s education system is structured into **three main stages**: **preschool, primary education**, and **secondary education**, with additional vocational and higher education pathways.

#### **Preschool (Pre-Kindergarten & Kindergarten)**
- **Compulsory for ages 4–6**, lasting **two years** (1 year Pre-Kindergarten, 1 year Kindergarten).
- **Preschool (Προνηπιαγωγείο, Νηπιαγωγείο)** is mandatory, with no formal grades until Year 3.
- **Foreign language instruction**: English begins in Kindergarten, with scientific evidence supporting early foreign language learning.
- **Curriculum**: Focuses on early childhood development, with descriptive assessments rather than numerical grades.

#### **Primary Education (Δημοτικό Σχολείο, Dimotiko)**
- **Compulsory for 6 years (Grades 1–6)**, starting at age **6**.
- **School year**: September 11 to June 15, with a standard school day from **08:15–13:15**.
- **Grading System**:
  - **Grades 1–2**: No grades.
  - **Grades 3–4**: Alphabetical grades (A–D).
  - **Grades 5–6**: Numerical grades (1–10).
- **Leaving Certificate**: Awarded after Grade 6 (**Απολυτήριο Δημοτικού**), granting automatic admission to **Gymnasium (lower secondary)**.

#### **Secondary Education**
Greece’s secondary education is divided into **two compulsory stages**:
1. **Lower Secondary (Γυμνάσιο, Gymnasium, Grades 7–9)**
   - **Compulsory for ages 12–15**, lasting **3 years**.
   - **Leaving Certificate**: Awarded after Grade 9 (**Απολυτήριο Γυμνασίου**), granting admission to **Lyceum (upper secondary)**.
   - **Curriculum**: Includes English, French, or German as a second foreign language.

2. **Upper Secondary (Λύκειο, Lyceum, Grades 10–12)**
   - **Non-compulsory**, lasting **3 years**.
   - **Leaving Certificate**: Awarded after Grade 12 (**Απολυτήριο Λυκείου**), required for **higher education**.
   - **Grading System**: 1–20 scale (100% equivalent to a 20).
   - **Orientation Groups (Ομάδες Προσανατολισμού)**: Students choose between **Humanities, Sciences, or Economics** in Grade 3, with specialized subjects in later grades.

#### **Vocational & Higher Education**
- **Vocational Lyceum (ΕΠΑΛ, EPAL)**: 3-year program offering specialized diplomas in fields like IT, healthcare, and engineering.
- **Higher Education**: Accessible via **Pan-Hellenic Examinations (Πανελλαδικές Εξετάσεις)**, with admission determined by weighted exam scores, university preferences, and available placements.
- **Public Onassis Schools**: Free, socially focused schools in disadvantaged areas, emphasizing STEM, arts, and digital literacy.

#### **Key Features**
- **Compulsory Education**: 9 years (Pre-Kindergarten to Gymnasium).
- **Grading System**: Transition from descriptive to numerical grades as students progress.
- **Foreign Language**: English begins in Kindergarten, with French/German as optional second languages.
- **Higher Education**: Requires the **Lyceum Apolytirio**, with admission based on standardized exams and university preferences.
- **Vocational Pathways**: EPAL provides specialized training for careers in technical and professional fields.
