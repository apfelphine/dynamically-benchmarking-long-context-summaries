## Education in Greece

Education in Greece encompasses a multi-level structure, ranging from pre-kindergarten through tertiary education. Preschool education is compulsory and lasts two years, consisting of Pre-kindergarten ($\Pi\rho o\nu\eta\pi\iota\alpha\gamma\omega\gamma\epsilon\acute{\iota}o$) and Kindergarten ($\Nu\eta\pi\iota\alpha\gamma\omega\gamma\epsilon\acute{\iota}o$). Primary education ($\Delta\eta\mu o\tau\iota\kappa o\ \sigma\chi o\lambda\epsilon\acute{\iota}o$) is compulsory for six years.

Secondary education is divided into Lower Secondary Education (Gymnasium, compulsory until age 15) and Upper Secondary Education (Lyceum, non-compulsory). Vocational pathways exist through institutions like the Vocational Lyceum (EPAL). The National Qualification Framework (HQF) aligns Greek qualifications with the European Qualifications Framework (EQF), featuring levels from 0 to 8, which map onto specific educational certificates and diplomas. Additionally, the Ministry of Education runs digital platforms and educational radio-television to support learning.
