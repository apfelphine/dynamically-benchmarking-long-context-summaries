In Greece, **education is compulsory and structured into three main stages**:

### **Preschool (Pre-Kindergarten & Kindergarten)**
- Compulsory for **2 years**, starting at **4–5 years old** (Pre-Kindergarten) and **5–6 years old** (Kindergarten).
- No formal grading; students receive descriptive assessments.
- English is introduced as a core subject from kindergarten, with early language acquisition encouraged.

### **Primary Education (Dimotiko)**
- **6 years compulsory**, ages **6–12**, covering **grades 1–6**.
- Students earn an **"Απολυτήριο Δημοτικού"** (primary school leaving certificate) upon completion.
- Grading begins in **grades 3–6**, with a **1–10 scale** (A–D in grades 3–4).

### **Secondary Education**
#### **Lower Secondary (Gymnasium)**
- **3 years compulsory**, ages **12–15**, covering **grades 1–3**.
- Students earn an **"Απολυτήριο Γυμνασίου"** (gymnasium leaving certificate) at **HQF level 3**.
- Three types of gymnasiums exist: **General, Special, and Vocational**.

#### **Upper Secondary (Lyceum)**
- **3 years non-compulsory**, ages **15–18**, covering **grades 1–3**.
- Students earn an **"Απολυτήριο Λυκείου"** (upper secondary leaving certificate) at **HQF level 4**.
- **35 weekly hours** per grade, with a **1–20 grading scale**.
- **Orientation Groups (streams)** determine subject focus (e.g., Humanities, Sciences, Economics).

### **Vocational Education**
- **Vocational Lyceum (EPAL)** and **Vocational Schools** offer specialized training in fields like IT, healthcare, engineering, and hospitality.
- **Dual VET (Vocational Education and Training)** programs combine classroom learning with workplace experience.
- **Post-Lyceum Year Apprenticeship Class** allows students to upgrade their qualifications to **HQF level 5**.

### **Higher Education**
- **Hellenic Qualification Framework (HQF)** aligns with **EQF and ISCED**, with **8 levels** (0–8).
- **Public universities** offer free education, while **private institutions** require accreditation.
- **Pan-Hellenic Examinations** determine admission to higher education, with a **20-point grading system**.

### **Key Features**
- **Compulsory education** starts at **6 years** and lasts **9 years** (primary + gymnasium).
- **Vocational pathways** integrate into the system, offering practical training alongside academic education.
- **Higher education** is free at public institutions, with standardized qualifications ensuring comparability across Greece and Europe.
