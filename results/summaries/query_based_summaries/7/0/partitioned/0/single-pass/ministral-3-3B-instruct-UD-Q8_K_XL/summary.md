Greece’s education system is structured into **formal** and **non-formal** education stages, with a strong emphasis on **compulsory education** from early childhood through higher education.

### **Education in Greece: Overview**
#### **Preschool (Pre-Kindergarten & Kindergarten)**
- **Compulsory** for children aged **4–6 years**, split into **Pre-Kindergarten (Προνηπιαγωγείο)** and **Kindergarten (Νηπιαγωγείο)**.
- **Pre-Kindergarten** (1 year) is for ages **4–5**, while **Kindergarten** (1 year) covers ages **5–6**.
- **English** is introduced in kindergarten, with a focus on early language acquisition.
- **No formal grades** are given; assessments are descriptive.
- **Start date**: Children aged 4 by December 31 must begin attending by September 11 of the same year.

#### **Primary Education (Dimotiko)**
- **Compulsory** for **6 years**, from **grades 1–6** (ages **6–12**).
- **School year**: September 11 to June 15.
- **Daily schedule**: Starts at **08:15 AM**, ends at **1:15 PM** (6 academic years).
- **Grading system**:
  - **Grades 1–2**: No grades.
  - **Grades 3–4**: Alphabetical grades (A–D).
  - **Grades 5–6**: Numerical grades (1–10).
- **Leaving certificate**: **Απολυτήριο Δημοτικού (Apolytirio Dimotikou)**, granting automatic admission to **Gymnasium (lower secondary education)**.

#### **Secondary Education**
##### **Lower Secondary (Gymnasium, 3 years)**
- **Compulsory** until age **15**, covering **grades 7–9** (ages **12–15**).
- **Types of Gymnasium**:
  - **General Gymnasium** (standard academic track).
  - **Special Gymnasium** (for students with disabilities).
  - **Art Gymnasium** (art-focused).
  - **Music Gymnasium** (music-focused).
  - **Vocational Gymnasium** (integrated vocational training).
  - **Experimental Gymnasium** (innovative programs).
- **Leaving certificate**: **Απολυτήριο Γυμνασίου (Apolytirio Gymnasium)**, granting admission to **Lyceum (upper secondary education)**.
- **Foreign languages**: English mandatory; French or German optional.

##### **Upper Secondary (Lyceum, 3 years)**
- **Non-compulsory**, covering **grades 10–12** (ages **15–18**).
- **Types of Lyceum**:
  - **General Lyceum (ΓΕΛ)** (standard academic track).
  - **Vocational Lyceum (ΕΠΑΛ)** (vocational training).
  - **Art Lyceum (Καλλιτεχνικό Λύκειο)** (art-focused).
  - **Music Lyceum (Μουσικό Λύκειο)** (music-focused).
  - **Experimental Lyceum (Πειραματικό Λύκειο)** (innovative programs).
- **Leaving certificate**: **Απολυτήριο Λυκείου (Apolytirio Lykeio)**, required for **higher education** and granting admission to universities.
- **Grading system**: **1–20** (equivalent to **A=20**).
- **Orientation Groups (Oμάδες Προσανατολισμού)**: Students choose a track (e.g., **Humanities, Sciences, Economics**) for specialized study.

##### **Vocational Education**
- **Vocational Lyceum (ΕΠΑΛ)**:
  - **2-year post-Gymnasium program** (ages **15–17**).
  - **Dual VET system**: Combines **school-based training** with **workplace apprenticeships**.
  - **Specialties**: Agriculture, Business, Engineering, Health, Tourism, and more.
  - **Leaving certificate**: **Πτυχίο Επαγγελματικής Εκπαίδευσης και Κατάρτισης (Level 4 Ptychio)**.
- **Post-Lyceum Year Apprenticeship Class (Μεταλυκειακό Έτος Τάξη Μαθητείας)**:
  - **1-year post-secondary apprenticeship** (ages **18–20**).
  - **Leaving certificate**: **Πτυχίο Επαγγελματικής Ειδικότητας και Κατάρτισης (Level 5 Ptychio)**.
  - **Eligibility**: EPAL graduates or Lyceum graduates with vocational training.

#### **Higher Education (Tertiary Education)**
- **Public Higher Education Institutions (HEIs)**:
  - **Universities** (e.g., Aristotle University of Thessaloniki, National and Kapodistrian University of Athens).
  - **Technological Educational Institutes (TEIs)** (now merged into universities).
  - **Free tuition** for most students; **tuition-free programs** for 25% of postgraduate students.
- **Private Higher Education Institutions**:
  - **Tuition fees** apply (varies by program).
  - **International programs** (e.g., English-taught degrees).
- **Admission**: Requires **Apolytirio Lykeio** and participation in **Pan-Hellenic Examinations (Πανελλαδικές Εξετάσεις)**.

#### **Non-Formal Education**
- **Open Courses**: Free online courses offered by public HEIs.
- **Vocational Training Centers (ΚΕΚ, ΚΑΤΕ, etc.)**: Short-term vocational programs.
- **Continuing Education**: Adult education programs for career advancement.

Greece’s education system ensures **compulsory education** from early childhood through secondary school, with **vocational and higher education** options for those seeking specialized training or academic degrees. The **grading system** evolves from descriptive assessments in primary school to numerical grades in secondary education, culminating in standardized exams for university admission.
