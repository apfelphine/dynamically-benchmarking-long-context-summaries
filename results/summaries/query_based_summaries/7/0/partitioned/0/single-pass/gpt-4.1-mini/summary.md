# Education in Greece

Education in Greece is structured into formal and non-formal systems encompassing preschool, primary, secondary, vocational, and tertiary levels, aligned with the Hellenic Qualification Framework (HQF) which corresponds to European standards.

Preschool education is compulsory, starting at age 4 with Pre-kindergarten and Kindergarten stages. Primary education lasts six years, followed by compulsory lower secondary education (Gymnasium) until age 15. Upper secondary education includes general and vocational lyceums (Lyceum and EPAL), with the latter offering specialized fields such as Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Health, and IT. Vocational education is emphasized through dual training systems combining classroom instruction and practical apprenticeship under regulated contracts, leading to recognized diplomas at various HQF levels. Adult education and experimental vocational schools also provide alternative pathways. Higher education is offered by public and private accredited institutions, with public universities being largely tuition-free. Non-formal education includes online courses, lifelong learning centers, and specialized training programs supported by governmental and academic bodies.
