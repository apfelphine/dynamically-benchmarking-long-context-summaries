Greece’s education system is structured into **three main stages**: preschool, primary, and secondary education, with higher education following later.

**Preschool (Pre-Kindergarten & Kindergarten, ages 4–6):**
- Compulsory for two years, divided into **Pre-Kindergarten (Προνηπιαγωγείο, 1 year)** and **Kindergarten (Νηπιαγωγείο, 1 year)**.
- Focuses on early childhood development and prepares children for formal schooling.
- English is introduced early, with studies showing success in learning foreign languages before age 11.

**Primary Education (Δημοτικό Σχολείο, ages 6–12):**
- Compulsory for **6 years**, from **Grades 1 to 6 (τάξεις 1–6)**.
- Begins with **no grades** in Grades 1–2, transitioning to **alphabetical grades (A–D)** in Grades 3–4 and **numerical grades (1–10)** from Grade 5 onward.
- Students receive a **"Απολυτήριο Δημοτικού"** (Primary School Leaving Certificate) upon completion, granting automatic admission to **Gymnasium (lower secondary school)**.

**Secondary Education (Gymnasium & Lyceum, ages 12–18):**
Greece’s secondary education is divided into **two compulsory stages**:
1. **Gymnasium (Γυμνάσιο, ages 12–15):**
   - **3-year compulsory program** (Grades 1–3).
   - Students receive a **"Απολυτήριο Γυμνασίου"** (Gymnasium Leaving Certificate), qualifying them for **Lyceum (upper secondary school)**.
   - Types include **General Gymnasium**, **Vocational Gymnasium (EPAL)**, and specialized schools (e.g., **Music, Art Gymnasium**).

2. **Lyceum (Λύκειο, ages 15–18):**
   - **3-year non-compulsory program** (Grades 1–3).
   - Students take the **"Πανελλαδικές Εξετάσεις"** (Pan-Hellenic Examinations) to earn a **"Απολυτήριο Λυκείου"** (Lyceum Leaving Certificate), required for higher education.
   - Types include **General Lyceum**, **Vocational Lyceum (EPAL)**, and specialized schools (e.g., **Art, Music Lyceum**).
   - Students choose an **"Orientation Group"** (specialization) in Grade 3, focusing on **Humanities, Science & Health, or Economic & Computer Studies**.

**Vocational Education:**
- **Vocational Lyceum (ΕΠΑΛ, EPAL)** offers **dual-diploma studies**, combining a **Lyceum Diploma** with a **Vocational Training Diploma (Level 4 HQF)**.
- Students train in specialized fields (e.g., **Healthcare, IT, Tourism, Engineering**) and can later upgrade to **Level 5 Diploma** via apprenticeship programs.

**Higher Education:**
- Accessible to students with a **Lyceum Diploma** via **Pan-Hellenic Examinations**.
- Public universities are tuition-free, while private institutions may charge fees.
- The system follows the **Hellenic Qualification Framework (HQF)**, aligning with European standards.
