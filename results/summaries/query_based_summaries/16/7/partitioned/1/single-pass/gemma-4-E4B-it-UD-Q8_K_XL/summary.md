### University of Jordan Summary

The University of Jordan was established on September 2, 1962, and its main campus is located in the Jubaiha area of Amman. It commenced teaching on December 25, 1965, starting with only the Faculty of Arts. The university has experienced significant growth, including the establishment of its Aqaba branch in 2008, which began operating in 2009. In the early 2010s, the university adopted a plan to become an internationally oriented, research-focused institution, allocating 5% of its annual budget to scientific research.

Academically, the university offers both undergraduate and postgraduate programs across faculties organized into Scientific, Humanitarian, and Medical (Health) streams. Notable academic units include the School of Medicine (established in 1971), the School of Engineering (established in 1975), and the King Abdullah II School of Information Technology (founded in 2000). The university's research output is supported by institutions such as the Center for Strategic Studies Jordan, and its library, UJL, holds a collection exceeding one million print and electronic items. The Jordan University Hospital (JUH), incorporated in 1975, is a public teaching and research hospital and the first university teaching hospital in Jordan.
