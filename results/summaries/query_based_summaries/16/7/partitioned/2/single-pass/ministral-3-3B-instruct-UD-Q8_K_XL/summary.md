The **University of Jordan** was established in 1962 as a national university, founded on the vision of providing higher education and fostering professional development for Jordanians. Located in Amman, its main campus in Jubaiha spans 0.463 square miles (1.20 km²) and features a blend of modern and traditional architecture, surrounded by historical landmarks and scenic views. The university operates on two campuses: the central Amman campus and the Aqaba branch, established in 2009 to expand educational opportunities in the region.

The University of Jordan is organized into **10 faculties**, divided into three main streams: **Scientific, Humanitarian, and Medical (Health) faculties**. Key scientific faculties include the **School of Science, School of Agriculture, School of Engineering, and King Abdullah II School of Information Technology**, while humanities faculties cover areas like **Arts, Sharia (Islamic Studies), Business, Educational Sciences, Law, and Physical Education**. Medical faculties include the **School of Medicine, Nursing, Pharmacy, Dentistry, and Rehabilitation Sciences**.

The university is known for its **diverse academic programs**, including 78 undergraduate and 143 postgraduate degrees, with a focus on research and innovation. Notable research centers include the **Center for Photonics Research, Islamic Cultural Center, and the Jordan Academy of Arabic**, which contributes to Jordan’s cultural and academic heritage. The **University of Jordan Library** is one of the largest in Jordan, housing over one million print and electronic resources, and supports a wide range of academic and research activities.

The university has gained recognition for its **academic excellence and global engagement**. It has been ranked among the top universities in Jordan and the Arab world, with a strong emphasis on research output and international collaborations. The **Jordan University Hospital (JUH)**, established in 1975, is a prominent teaching and research facility, contributing significantly to Jordan’s healthcare system.

Student life at the University of Jordan is vibrant, with a focus on community engagement and extracurricular activities. The university promotes cultural and social events, student organizations, and sports, fostering a dynamic academic environment. Notable alumni include prominent figures in politics, science, business, and academia, reflecting the university’s impact on Jordan’s intellectual and professional landscape. The University of Jordan continues to play a pivotal role in shaping the future of education and development in Jordan.
