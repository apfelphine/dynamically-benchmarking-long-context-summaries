The University of Jordan is a prominent educational institution with a history dating back to its founding in September 1962. Its main campus is situated in Jubaiha, north of Amman, and spans 1,200 dunums. The university has expanded its presence with a southern branch campus in Aqaba, which began operations in 2009.

Academically, the university is structured into scientific, humanitarian, and medical (health) faculties. Key scientific faculties include the School of Engineering, established in 1975, and the King Abdullah II School of Information Technology, founded in 2000. The humanities faculties cover a broad range of disciplines, including the School of Arts, which was the first school established. The medical and health faculties include the School of Medicine (founded 1971), School of Nursing (founded 1972), School of Pharmacy (founded 1979), and School of Dentistry (founded 1982). The University of Jordan Library (UJL), established in 1962, holds a collection exceeding one million print and electronic items.
