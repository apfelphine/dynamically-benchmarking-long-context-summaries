The University of Jordan, established in 1962, is located in Amman and features a main campus in Jubaiha and an Aqaba branch established in 2009. It began with a small student body and faculty, expanding over time to include 159,383 graduates by 2012. The university offers 78 undergraduate and 143 postgraduate programs across three streams: Scientific (School of Science, Agriculture, Engineering, and King Abdullah II School of Information Technology), Humanitarian (School of Arts, Sharia, Business, Law, Education, and International Studies), and Medical (School of Medicine, Nursing, Pharmacy, Dentistry, and Rehabilitation Sciences). Faculty include notable scholars such as Nasir al-Din al-Asad, Abdelsalam al-Majali, and Shaher Momani. The university operates the Jordan University Hospital (JUH), a teaching and research hospital, and has a large library with over one million items. It also hosts research centers such as the Center for Photonics Research and the Water and Environment Research and Study Center. The university has a strong academic reputation, ranking among the top universities in Jordan and the Arab world, and is recognized for its contributions to research, with over 8,000 research papers and 78 patents from 2000 to 2011. Alumni include prominent political and academic figures, and the institution is actively involved in student activism and international academic collaborations.
