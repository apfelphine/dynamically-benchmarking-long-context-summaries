# University of Jordan: Summary

The University of Jordan (UJ), established in 1962 with its main campus located in the Jubaiha area of Amman, is Jordan's first and largest university. Spanning 1.20 km², the campus includes twelve gates and multiple parking zones, situated along a major thoroughfare with access to cafés, restaurants, and student housing. The university expanded in 2009 by establishing a southern branch campus in Aqaba, aimed at supporting regional development, featuring modern academic and recreational facilities alongside ongoing construction for women's dormitories.

Academically, UJ has 78 undergraduate and 143 postgraduate programs across three streams: Scientific (including Science, Agriculture, Engineering, and the King Abdullah II School of Information Technology), Humanitarian (including Arts, Sharia, Business, Law, Education, Physical Education, International Studies, and Languages), and Medical faculties (including Medicine, Nursing, Pharmacy, Dentistry, and Rehabilitation Sciences). The School of Engineering is notably the largest faculty, with ABET accreditation across all departments. UJ emphasizes research with dedicated centers such as the Center for Strategic Studies Jordan and a strong focus on scientific outputs, including multiple international peer-reviewed journals published by the University of Jordan Press. Its affiliated Jordan University Hospital, a leading teaching and research institution, is recognized for pioneering advanced medical procedures and international accreditations. The university’s library holds over one million items, historic manuscripts, and international documents, serving a wide academic community.

UJ maintains numerous international affiliations and bilateral agreements facilitating student exchanges, particularly with U.S. institutions. The university has experienced growth in enrollment, faculty, and research output since 2000, aiming to enhance international orientation and research focus. Its student union is active, and the institution has produced notable academics and public figures, including past presidents and ministers in Jordan. The university consistently ranks as Jordan’s top institution and holds strong positions in regional and global rankings within the Arab world and internationally.
