## University of Jordan: Overview

Founded in 1962, the University of Jordan (UJ) is the oldest and largest university in Jordan, located in Amman with a significant branch in Aqaba established in 2009. The Amman campus spans 1,200 dunums, features twelve gates, and is bordered by University Street, supporting student life with cafés, hotels, and housing. UJ has evolved from a single arts faculty to offering 78 undergraduate and 143 postgraduate programs in scientific, medical, and humanitarian faculties, including accredited departments in engineering (ABET) and business (AACSB). The institution operates a comprehensive library with over one million items, multiple research centers, and maintains hundreds of international agreements. The Jordan University Hospital serves as both a major healthcare provider and a teaching facility.

## Academic Profile, Research, and Reputation

UJ is recognized for strong research output, boasting the highest admission scores for most majors in Jordan, top national and regional rankings, and international affiliations. It prioritizes graduate studies and scientific research, publishing several peer-reviewed journals. The university supports student activism and engagement through an active student union. UJ alumni include former prime ministers, ministers, diplomats, scientists, artists, and athletes who have held prominent roles in government and international organizations. Its faculty features leading scholars in humanities, sciences, law, and technology, many of whom have served in high-profile national and international positions.
