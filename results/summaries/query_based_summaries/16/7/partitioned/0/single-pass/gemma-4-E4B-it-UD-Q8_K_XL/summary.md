The University of Jordan was established in 1962, following national calls for a national university, with its main campus located in the Jubaiha area of Amman. Its history includes commencing teaching on December 25, 1965, beginning with the Faculty of Arts, and expanding to include the Faculty of Science, the Faculty of Economics and Commerce in 1965, and subsequently adding ten faculties between 1970 and 1990. The university's educational offerings are structured into three streams: Scientific, Humanitarian, and Medical (Health) faculties.

The University of Jordan operates multiple branches, including the Aqaba Campus, which was established in 2009. Academically, it offers 78 undergraduate and 143 postgraduate programs, organized across specialized faculties such as the School of Engineering (the largest faculty), the School of Medicine (established in 1971), and the School of Arts. The university's library, UJL, was established in 1962, and its research output is supported by the University of Jordan Press, which publishes various peer-reviewed scientific and cultural journals.
