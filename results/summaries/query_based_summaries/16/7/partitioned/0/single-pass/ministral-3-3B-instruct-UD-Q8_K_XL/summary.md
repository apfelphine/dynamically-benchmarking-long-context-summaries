The **University of Jordan** was established in 1962 as a national university in response to calls for higher education in Jordan. Founded under King Hussein bin Talal, it initially operated from three buildings with minimal faculty and student numbers but quickly expanded its academic offerings and infrastructure.

### **Campuses**
The university operates two main campuses:
- **Amman Campus**: Located in the Jubaiha area, north of the capital, it spans 1,200 dunums and includes 12 gates, student parking, and facilities for the **School of Science, Agriculture, Engineering, and Information Technology**. The campus also houses the **University Library (UJL)**, one of Jordan’s largest, with over a million print and electronic resources.
- **Aqaba Campus**: Established in 2009 as part of Jordan’s regional development strategy, it focuses on applied sciences, engineering, and business studies. Construction of a women’s dormitory began in 2024.

### **History & Expansion**
In its early years, the university faced financial constraints but expanded significantly in the 2000s, introducing new admission categories, scholarship programs, and graduate offerings. By 2011, it had established 111 master’s, 38 doctoral, and 16 higher-specialization programs. The university also expanded its research output, with 78 patents, 752 books, and 8,764 research papers by faculty. In 2012, it celebrated its Golden Jubilee with King Abdullah II, marking its growth as a national institution.

### **Academics & Research**
The university is organized into **three main streams of faculties**:
- **Scientific Faculties**: Includes the **School of Science, Agriculture, Engineering, and Information Technology**, with all engineering departments accredited by ABET.
- **Humanitarian Faculties**: Covers arts, Islamic studies, business, education, law, physical education, and international studies.
- **Medical Faculties**: Includes the **School of Medicine, Nursing, Pharmacy, Dentistry, and Rehabilitation Sciences**, the latter being one of the youngest faculties.

Research centers include the **Institute of Archaeology, International Institute for Teaching Arabic, and the Islamic Cultural Center**. The university also operates the **Jordan University Hospital**, the first university teaching hospital in Jordan, and has established collaborations with international institutions, including the **University of Jordan Press**, which publishes peer-reviewed journals in medical, social, and applied sciences.

### **Rankings & Reputation**
The University of Jordan ranks among the top universities in Jordan, consistently appearing in regional rankings. In 2021, it was ranked **#1 in Jordan** and **#11 in the Arab world** by Webometrics. It has also been recognized for its high admission scores across most majors in the country.

### **Student Life & International Engagement**
The university supports student life through its **Student Union** and offers exchange programs with institutions worldwide. It has campuses in **Amman and Aqaba**, with plans for further expansion, including a dedicated women’s dormitory in Aqaba. The university emphasizes research, innovation, and community engagement, positioning itself as a key institution in Jordan’s higher education landscape.
