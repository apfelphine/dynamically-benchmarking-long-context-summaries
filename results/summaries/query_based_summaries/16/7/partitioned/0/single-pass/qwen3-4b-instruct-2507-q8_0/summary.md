The University of Jordan is a public university established in 1962, with its main campus located in Jubaiha, Amman. It operates a southern branch in Aqaba, established in 2009. The university began teaching in 1965 with a single faculty, the Faculty of Arts, and has since expanded to include 109 master's programs, 34 doctoral programs, and 143 postgraduate programs across scientific, humanitarian, and medical faculties. Key academic units include the School of Science, School of Engineering, King Abdullah II School of Information Technology, School of Business, and the School of Medicine. The university's library, established in 1962, holds over one million items and serves as a major research and academic resource. It also operates the Jordan University Hospital (JUH), a teaching and research hospital established in 1973, and the Jordan University Press, which publishes peer-reviewed journals in medical, pharmaceutical, and social sciences. The university has a strong research focus, with notable achievements in areas such as water management, and its faculty includes prominent scholars and public figures. The university is recognized internationally, ranking in the top 10 in the Arab world and within the global top 900 in several university rankings, and has a high admission score for its programs. Student life is supported by the University of Jordan Student Union, and the university maintains numerous international exchange agreements, particularly with U.S. institutions.
