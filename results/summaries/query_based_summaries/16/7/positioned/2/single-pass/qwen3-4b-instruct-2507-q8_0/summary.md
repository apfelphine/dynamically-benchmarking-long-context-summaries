The University of Jordan is a public university located in Amman, Jordan. Established in 1962, it began operations in 1965 with a single faculty, the Faculty of Arts, and an initial student enrollment of 167. The university expanded significantly over the decades, adding faculties in science, economics, commerce, medicine, law, engineering, and nursing. By 2012, it had graduated 159,383 students. Its main campus in Jubaiha spans 1,200 dunums and features twelve gates, student parking areas, and a central location near major roads. A satellite campus in Aqaba was established in 2009 to support regional development and student access. The university operates a library with over one million print and electronic items, organized into technical, library services, and information departments. It also houses research centers such as the Institute for the Study of Islam in the Contemporary World, the Center for Documents and Manuscripts, and the Center for Photonics Research. The university's academic structure includes 78 undergraduate programs and 143 postgraduate programs across ten faculties. Notable alumni include former Jordanian Prime Ministers Bisher Khasawneh, Marouf al-Bakhit, and Fayez al-Tarawneh, as well as prominent figures in politics, science, business, and the arts. The university is recognized globally, ranking in the top 100 in the 2022 Webometrics Ranking of World Universities and the 801–900 band in the Shanghai Academic Ranking of World Universities (ARWU) in 2024. It has a strong research output, with over 78 patents, 752 books, and 8,764 research papers published between 2000 and 2011. The university also has international affiliations, including partnerships with universities in the United States, Germany, and the United Arab Emirates.
