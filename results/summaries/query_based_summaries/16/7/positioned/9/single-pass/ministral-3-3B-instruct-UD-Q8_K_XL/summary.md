The **University of Jordan** was founded in 1962 as a national institution to provide higher education in Jordan. Located in Amman, its main campus spans 1,200 dunums (0.463 sq mi) in the Jubaiha area, offering a blend of academic and administrative facilities. The university was established through royal decree, with King Hussein bin Talal playing a pivotal role in its inception. Initially, it operated from three buildings with limited resources, but it expanded significantly over the decades, adding new faculties and research centers.

The University of Jordan is organized into three main academic streams: **Scientific, Humanitarian, and Medical (Health) faculties**. Key scientific faculties include the School of Science, School of Agriculture, School of Engineering, and the King Abdullah II School of Information Technology. Humanitarian faculties encompass the School of Arts, School of Sharia (Islamic studies), School of Business, School of Educational Sciences, School of Law, and School of Physical Education, among others. Medical faculties include the School of Medicine, School of Nursing, School of Pharmacy, School of Dentistry, and the School of Rehabilitation Sciences.

The university’s **Library (UJL)** is a central resource, housing over one million print and electronic items, and serving as a repository for historical documents, parliamentary records, and international-organization documents. Notable centers and institutes include the Institute of Archaeology, the International Institute for Teaching Arabic Language to Speakers of Other Languages, and the Islamic Cultural Center. The **Jordan University Hospital (JUH)**, established in 1973, serves as a major teaching and research facility.

The University of Jordan has grown into a prominent institution in the Arab world, with a strong emphasis on research and academic excellence. It has been ranked among the top universities in Jordan and the Arab world, with notable contributions in various fields. The university continues to play a significant role in Jordan’s educational landscape, fostering innovation, research, and community engagement.
