The University of Jordan is a public university located in Amman, Jordan. It was established in 1962 by royal decree and began teaching in December 1965. The university's main campus is in Jubaiha, north of Amman, spanning 1,200 dunums and featuring twelve gates, student parking areas, and a central location near major roads. A satellite campus, the Aqaba Campus, was established in 2009 in Aqaba, Jordan, to support regional development and expand higher education. The university has expanded significantly since its founding, adding new faculties including Medicine, Dentistry, Pharmacy, Engineering, Nursing, Law, and Physical Education. It now offers 78 undergraduate programs and 143 postgraduate programs across 109 master’s degrees and 34 doctoral degrees. 

The University of Jordan has a strong academic structure divided into three streams: Scientific, Humanitarian, and Medical (Health) faculties. The Scientific faculties include the School of Science, School of Agriculture, School of Engineering, and the King Abdullah II School of Information Technology. The Humanitarian faculties include the School of Arts, School of Sharia, School of Business, School of Educational Sciences, School of Law, and School of Physical Education. The Medical faculties include the School of Medicine, School of Nursing, School of Pharmacy, School of Dentistry, and School of Rehabilitation Sciences. The university also operates a central library with over one million print and electronic items, and a hospital, Jordan University Hospital (JUH), which serves as a teaching and research center for medical education.

The university has a strong research profile, with over 78 patents, 752 books, and 8,764 research papers published between 2000 and 2011. It has been recognized in international rankings, including being ranked 11th in the Arab world and 301–500 globally in the 2022 QS World University Rankings. The university also promotes internationalization through student exchange programs with over 100 partner institutions worldwide. 

The student body is diverse, with a mix of nationalities and gender representation. The university has a student union that represents student interests and advocates for student rights. Notable alumni include prominent figures in politics, science, business, and the arts, such as former Jordanian Prime Ministers, a U.S. Army general, and a Nobel laureate in medicine. The university continues to grow and strengthen its academic and research programs, contributing significantly to Jordan's higher education and development landscape.
