The **University of Jordan** was established in 1962 as a national university in Jordan, founded to provide higher education and foster scientific, financial, and administrative autonomy. Located in Amman, its main campus spans 1,200 dunums (0.463 sq mi) and includes twelve gates, student parking areas, and a central location near significant landmarks. The university began operations in 1965 with three faculties (Arts, Science, and Economics) and 167 students, later expanding to include medical, engineering, and humanitarian faculties.

The university has grown significantly over the decades, adding new campuses, including Aqaba in 2009, and has developed into a major research institution. It offers 78 undergraduate programs and 143 postgraduate programs across scientific, humanitarian, and medical faculties. The **University of Jordan Library (UJL)**, established in 1962, holds over one million items and serves as a key academic resource. The university has also established research centers, including the **Center for Photonics Research** and the **Jordan Academy of Arabic**, and collaborates with international organizations.

Ranked among the top universities in Jordan and the Arab world, the University of Jordan emphasizes academic excellence, research, and community engagement. It has produced notable alumni in politics, science, business, and academia, contributing to Jordan’s intellectual and professional landscape.
