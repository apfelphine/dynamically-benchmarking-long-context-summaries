The University of Jordan is a public university established in 1962, located in Amman, Jordan. It began operations with a small student body and expanded significantly over the decades, adding new faculties such as Medicine, Engineering, Law, and Business. The university has a main campus in Jubaiha, Amman, and an Aqaba branch established in 2009 to promote regional development. It also operates a student hospital (Jordan University Hospital) and a library system with over one million items. The university emphasizes research and innovation, having published 78 patents, 752 books, and 8,764 research papers between 2000 and 2011. It is recognized globally, ranking 11th in the Arab world and 801–900 in the Shanghai Academic Ranking of World Universities. The university is known for its strong academic programs, international student body, and active student life, including student unions, clubs, and research centers. Notable alumni include political leaders, scientists, and public figures.
