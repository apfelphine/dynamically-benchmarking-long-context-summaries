The University of Jordan, established in 1962, is Jordan's first and foremost public university with campuses in Amman and Aqaba. Its main campus in Jubaiha, Amman, spans 1.2 km² and is centrally located near major city landmarks, while the Aqaba campus, opened in 2009, supports regional development with modern academic and recreational facilities. The university began with the Faculty of Arts and gradually expanded to include faculties of Science, Economics and Commerce, Medicine, Dentistry, Pharmacy, Engineering, Nursing, Agriculture, Law, and Physical Education, among others. It offers 78 undergraduate and 143 postgraduate programs spanning scientific, humanitarian, and medical fields, including a prominent engineering school accredited by ABET and a fully accredited business school. The Jordan University Hospital serves as a leading teaching and research hospital affiliated with the university.

With over 159,000 graduates as of 2012, the University of Jordan emphasizes research and internationalization, having published numerous patents, books, and research papers. Its library, established alongside the university, is one of Jordan's largest, housing over a million items including historical archives. The university holds top national rankings and maintains extensive bilateral agreements for student exchange with institutions worldwide, including 16 from the U.S. Student life features an active student union with evolving governance and participation. Notable alumni and faculty include former prime ministers, government officials, academic leaders, and internationally recognized scholars, underscoring the university’s central role in Jordan's education, research, and public service sectors.
