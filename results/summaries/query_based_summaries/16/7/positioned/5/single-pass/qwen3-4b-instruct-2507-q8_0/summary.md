The University of Jordan is a public university located in Amman, Jordan, with its main campus in the Jubaiha area, situated north of the capital. The campus spans 1,200 dunums (0.463 square miles) and features twelve gates, including one on the southern side, two on the eastern side, two on the northern side, and seven on the western side. The campus borders University Street (Queen Rania Street), a major road connecting eastern and western Amman, and includes cafés, restaurants, hotels, and student housing. The university also has an Aqaba branch established in 2009 as part of a national strategy to expand higher education in the Aqaba region. The Aqaba campus includes modern academic buildings, recreational facilities, and a multi-purpose gymnasium.

Founded in 1962, the University of Jordan was established to meet national aspirations for higher education, with the royal decree stating that it would be named the University of Jordan and located in Jubaiha. The university began teaching on December 25, 1965, with 167 students, including 17 female students, operating from three buildings with a budget of 50,000 dinars and eight faculty members. Early faculties included the Faculty of Arts, Faculty of Science, and Faculty of Economics and Commerce. Over the decades, the university expanded, adding ten new faculties, including Medicine, Dentistry, Pharmacy, Engineering, Nursing, Agriculture, Law, and Physical Education, and establishing research centers and a teaching hospital (JUH). In 2000, the university introduced parallel and international admission programs and adopted blended learning. In 2008, it established its Aqaba branch and began operating in 2009.

The university offers 78 undergraduate programs and 143 postgraduate programs, organized into three streams: Scientific, Humanitarian, and Medical (Health) faculties. The Scientific faculties include the School of Science, School of Agriculture, School of Engineering, and the King Abdullah II School of Information Technology. The Humanitarian faculties include the School of Arts, School of Sharia, School of Business, School of Educational Sciences, School of Law, and School of Physical Education. The Medical and Health faculties include the School of Medicine, School of Nursing, School of Pharmacy, School of Dentistry, and School of Rehabilitation Sciences.

The University of Jordan Library (UJL), established in 1962, occupies over 12,000 square meters with an additional 4,000 square meters of reading rooms distributed across faculties and research centers. It holds over one million print and electronic items and serves faculty, students, researchers, and visiting scholars. The library is organized into three departments: Technical, Library Services, and Information. It provides facilities for visually impaired students and serves as a depository for theses and dissertations from Arab universities accredited by the Association of Arab Universities.

The university has a rich history of academic and research achievements, including 78 patents, 752 books, and 8,764 research papers published between 2000 and 2011. The university is recognized for its research, with the Jordan University Press publishing several internationally peer-reviewed scientific journals in applied and social sciences, medical and pharmaceutical sciences. The university is ranked in the top 100 in the Arab world and the 801–900 band in the Shanghai Academic Ranking of World Universities (ARWU) as of 2024, with a 498th position in the QS World University Rankings. It is also noted for its high admission scores and strong student outcomes.
