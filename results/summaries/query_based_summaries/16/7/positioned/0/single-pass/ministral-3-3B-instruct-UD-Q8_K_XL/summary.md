The **University of Jordan** was founded in 1962 as a national institution to provide higher education in Jordan, initially named the University of Amman. Established under royal decree, it became the first university in the country, offering a range of academic programs from its inception.

### **Campuses**
The university operates two main campuses:
- **Amman Campus**: Located in Jubaiha, near the capital, it spans 1,200 dunums (0.463 square miles) and includes twelve gates, student parking, and facilities for scientific, humanitarian, and medical studies. It houses the main administrative offices, libraries, and academic buildings.
- **Aqaba Campus**: Established in 2009 as part of a national strategy to expand higher education in the region, it focuses on academic and recreational facilities, including modern buildings and a dedicated women’s dormitory under construction in 2024.

### **History**
The university’s founding was driven by a national aspiration for higher education, with British support and royal approval. Early years saw rapid expansion, including the establishment of faculties in Science, Economics, and Medicine. Despite political challenges, the university grew significantly, adding new faculties and research centers. It became a key institution in Jordan’s academic landscape, fostering research and innovation.

### **Academics**
The University of Jordan offers a diverse range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs across three main streams: **Scientific, Humanitarian, and Medical**. Key faculties include:
- **Scientific Faculties**: School of Science, School of Agriculture, School of Engineering, and King Abdullah II School of Information Technology.
- **Humanitarian Faculties**: School of Arts, School of Sharia, School of Business, School of Educational Sciences, and School of Law.
- **Medical Faculties**: School of Medicine, School of Nursing, School of Pharmacy, School of Dentistry, and School of Rehabilitation Sciences.

The university also features a prominent **Library (UJL)**, one of the largest in Jordan, with over one million print and electronic resources, including historical documents and archives. Research centers and institutes focus on areas like archaeology, social work, and environmental studies.

### **Research and Rankings**
The University of Jordan is recognized for its contributions to research and academic excellence. It has been ranked among the top universities in Jordan and the Arab world, with notable achievements in scientific publications and international collaborations. The university’s focus on applied research and innovation aligns with its mission to advance knowledge and address regional challenges.

### **Student Life and International Engagement**
The university supports a vibrant student life through student unions, clubs, and extracurricular activities. It also engages in international partnerships, offering exchange programs and collaborations with universities worldwide. The university emphasizes inclusivity, with efforts to support diverse student populations and foster a dynamic academic environment.
