The University of Jordan is a public university established in 1962 in Amman, Jordan. It began as a national initiative to provide higher education and was officially founded with a royal decree from King Hussein bin Talal. The university's main campus is located in the Jubaiha area, north of Amman, spanning 1,200 dunums and featuring twelve gates and modern academic buildings. An Aqaba branch was established in 2009 to expand access to higher education in the southern region. 

The university offers 78 undergraduate and 143 postgraduate programs across three academic streams: Scientific, Humanitarian, and Medical (Health) faculties. The Scientific faculties include the School of Science, School of Agriculture, School of Engineering, and the King Abdullah II School of Information Technology. The Humanitarian faculties include the School of Arts, School of Sharia, School of Business, School of Law, and School of Physical Education, among others. The Medical faculties consist of the School of Medicine, School of Nursing, School of Pharmacy, School of Dentistry, and School of Rehabilitation Sciences.

The University of Jordan Library (UJL) was established in 1962 and holds over one million print and electronic items. It is organized into three departments and serves faculty, students, researchers, and visiting scholars. The university also has several research centers and institutes, including the Institute of Archaeology, the Center for Photonics Research, and the Islamic Cultural Center.

The university operates Jordan University Hospital (JUH), a public teaching and research hospital in Amman, which serves as a major center for medical education and research. JUH is the first university teaching hospital in Jordan and has achieved international accreditation, including from the Joint Commission International (JCI).

The university has a strong academic reputation, ranking first in Jordan and 11th in the Arab world in the 2021 Webometrics Ranking. In global rankings, it is positioned in the 801–900 band in the Shanghai Academic Ranking of World Universities and improved to 498th in the 2024 QS World University Rankings. 

The University of Jordan is actively involved in international partnerships, with over 16 bilateral agreements, including with universities in the United States. It is a member of the Federation of the Universities of the Islamic World, the Mediterranean Universities Union, and the Association of Arab Universities. 

The student body is diverse, with a significant number of international students, and the university has a strong emphasis on student life, including a student union and various student organizations. Notable alumni include prominent figures in politics, science, business, and the arts, such as former Jordanian prime ministers and international diplomats. Faculty members include scholars in engineering, medicine, and the humanities.
