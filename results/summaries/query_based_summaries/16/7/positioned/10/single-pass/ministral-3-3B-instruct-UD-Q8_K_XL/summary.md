The **University of Jordan** was established in 1962 as a national university in Jordan, founded to provide higher education and foster academic and scientific development in the country. Its founding was a response to the need for a university that would serve as a center for professional training, research, and cultural enrichment, aligning with Jordan’s aspirations for modernization and national progress.

### **Campuses**
The university operates two main campuses:
- **Amman Campus**: Located in the Jubaiha area of Amman, the main campus spans 1,200 dunums (0.463 sq mi) and is the central hub for academic activities, housing faculties such as the School of Science, School of Engineering, and the School of Medicine. It features twelve gates, including prominent entrances along University Street, and is surrounded by student housing, cafes, and recreational areas.
- **Aqaba Campus**: Established in 2009, this campus in Aqaba focuses on regional development and serves as a branch for undergraduate and postgraduate programs, particularly in fields related to the Red Sea region.

### **History**
The university began operations in 1965, initially offering programs in the Faculty of Arts, Science, and Economics. Over the years, it expanded significantly, adding new faculties such as Medicine, Dentistry, Pharmacy, and Engineering. The university’s early years were marked by political and economic challenges, including regional conflicts and financial constraints, but it persevered through these difficulties to grow into a prominent institution.

By the 1970s, the university had established its teaching hospital, Jordan University Hospital (JUH), and expanded its research centers. The 1990s saw further growth, including the establishment of the Aqaba branch and increased international collaborations. Today, the University of Jordan is recognized for its academic excellence, research contributions, and commitment to fostering innovation and leadership in Jordan and the broader region.

### **Academics**
The university offers a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs across ten faculties, including:
- **Scientific Faculties**: School of Science, School of Agriculture, School of Engineering, and King Abdullah II School of Information Technology.
- **Humanitarian Faculties**: School of Arts, School of Sharia, School of Business, School of Educational Sciences, School of Law, and School of Physical Education.
- **Medical Faculties**: School of Medicine, School of Nursing, School of Pharmacy, School of Dentistry, and School of Rehabilitation Sciences.

The university is known for its rigorous academic standards, with a focus on both theoretical and practical learning. It emphasizes research and innovation, particularly in fields such as medicine, engineering, and information technology.

### **Library and Research**
The **University of Jordan Library (UJL)**, established in 1962, is one of the largest public libraries in Jordan, housing over one million print and electronic items. It serves as a key resource for faculty, students, and researchers, offering access to a wide range of academic materials, including historical documents, manuscripts, and international publications. The library is also a repository for the university’s research outputs, including journals, books, and dissertations.

The university is recognized for its contributions to scientific research, with a strong emphasis on interdisciplinary studies. It hosts several research centers, including the Center for Photonics Research, the Jordan Academy of Arabic, and the Islamic Cultural Center, among others. These centers focus on areas such as Arabic language studies, Islamic heritage, and advanced scientific research.

### **Rankings and Reputation**
The University of Jordan is consistently ranked among the top universities in Jordan and the Arab world. According to the **2021 Webometrics Ranking of World Universities**, it was the top-ranked university in Jordan and the 11th best in the Arab world. In recent years, it has also been recognized for its academic reputation, research output, and contributions to regional development.

### **International Affiliations**
The university has established numerous bilateral agreements and partnerships with universities worldwide, facilitating student exchange programs, research collaborations, and academic exchanges. These partnerships include institutions in the United States, Europe, and the Middle East, enhancing the university’s global reach and academic influence.

### **Student Life**
The **University of Jordan Student Union** represents the interests of students and organizes various activities, events, and initiatives to promote a vibrant campus life. The university also provides student housing, dining facilities, and recreational spaces to support the well-being of its students.

### **Notable Contributions**
The University of Jordan has produced numerous notable alumni and faculty members who have made significant contributions to academia, science, business, and public service in Jordan and beyond. The university’s graduates include politicians, scientists, business leaders, and scholars who have played pivotal roles in shaping the country’s future.

### **Future Vision**
The University of Jordan continues to strive for academic excellence, innovation, and global engagement. It aims to strengthen its research capabilities, expand its international collaborations, and provide high-quality education to meet the needs of Jordan’s evolving workforce and society. With its rich history and commitment to progress, the university remains a cornerstone of higher education in Jordan.
