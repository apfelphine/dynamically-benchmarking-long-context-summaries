The **University of Jordan** was established in 1962 as a national university to meet Jordan’s higher education needs. Founded under the patronage of King Hussein bin Talal, it was created to provide scientific, financial, and administrative autonomy, initially operating independently of the government. The university’s main campus is located in Jubaiha, Amman, with a second campus in Aqaba, established in 2009 to support regional development.

The university offers a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs across three main streams: **Scientific Faculties** (including Engineering, Agriculture, Science, and Information Technology), **Humanitarian Faculties** (such as Arts, Law, Education, and Physical Education), and **Medical Faculties** (including Medicine, Nursing, Pharmacy, Dentistry, and Rehabilitation Sciences). It is known for its strong emphasis on research, with notable contributions in fields like medicine, engineering, and social sciences. The university’s **Jordan University Hospital** serves as a major teaching and research facility, and its **Library** is one of the largest in Jordan, housing over one million print and electronic resources.

Ranked among the top universities in Jordan and the Arab world, the University of Jordan has expanded its academic and research capabilities, including partnerships with international institutions and initiatives to promote scientific innovation. It has also played a significant role in Jordan’s educational and cultural development, fostering a diverse and inclusive academic environment.
