## University of Jordan: Overview and Key Facts

The University of Jordan, founded in 1962, is the oldest and largest university in Jordan and the “Mother University” of the country. Its main campus is located in the Jubaiha area north of Amman, occupying 1.2 km², with twelve gates and amenities such as student parking, cafés, and housing along University Street. In 2009, the university further expanded by establishing a southern branch in Aqaba to support regional development. The original establishment involved significant national effort with royal patronage—King Hussein bin Talal formally inaugurated the university, marking a milestone in Jordanian education.

Initially starting with the Faculty of Arts, the university rapidly expanded through the 1970–1990 period, adding numerous faculties and research centers, including Medicine, Dentistry, Pharmacy, Engineering, Nursing, Agriculture, Law, Physical Education, and establishing its teaching hospital (Jordan University Hospital). Despite economic and political challenges, the university maintained autonomy and continued growth, implementing various academic reforms, adopting research-centered policies, and launching parallel and international programs. By 2012, it had celebrated its Golden Jubilee, graduating over 159,000 students.

## Academics, Research, and Community Impact

The University of Jordan offers 78 undergraduate and 143 postgraduate programs across scientific, humanitarian, and medical faculties. Noted for its School of Engineering (ABET accredited), School of Business (AACSB accredited), and pioneering faculties such as Medicine, Nursing, and Pharmacy, the university emphasizes both teaching and research. With robust research output—78 patents, over 8,700 papers between 2000–2011—and a large library (UJL) serving thousands daily, the university is a leader in academic publishing via its press and various journals. Its teaching hospital is internationally accredited and serves as the country’s main medical education and research center. The university maintains extensive international affiliations and student exchange agreements, and according to rankings (QS, THE, ARWU), it is consistently ranked at the top nationally and well-placed globally.

Notable alumni include several former prime ministers, ministers, and figures in science, arts, and media, reflecting the university's role in national development. The student body is active, and governance is shaped by a student union and ongoing campus activism. Faculty and alumni have achieved recognition domestically and abroad, with contributions across academia, government, diplomacy, science, and the arts.
