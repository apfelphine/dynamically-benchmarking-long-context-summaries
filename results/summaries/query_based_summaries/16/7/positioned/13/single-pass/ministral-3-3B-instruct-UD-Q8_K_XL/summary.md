The **University of Jordan** was established in 1962 as a national aspiration to provide higher education in Jordan. Founded under the patronage of King Hussein bin Talal, it became the first university in the country, offering a blend of scientific, humanities, and medical programs. The university operates primarily from its main campus in **Jubaiha**, Amman, with a secondary campus in **Aqaba**, established in 2009 to support regional development.

### **Academic Structure**
The University of Jordan is organized into three main streams of faculties:
- **Scientific Faculties**: Includes the School of Science, School of Agriculture, School of Engineering, and the King Abdullah II School of Information Technology.
- **Humanitarian Faculties**: Encompasses the School of Arts, School of Sharia (Islamic studies), School of Business, School of Educational Sciences, School of Law, and others.
- **Medical Faculties**: Consists of the School of Medicine, School of Nursing, School of Pharmacy, School of Dentistry, and the School of Rehabilitation Sciences.

The university offers 78 undergraduate programs and 143 postgraduate programs, including 109 master’s degrees and 34 doctoral degrees. It emphasizes research and innovation, with a focus on applied sciences, technology, and healthcare.

### **Campuses**
- **Amman Campus**: Located in Jubaiha, the main campus spans 1,200 dunums and features modern academic buildings, libraries, and research centers. It serves as the central hub for teaching and administrative activities.
- **Aqaba Campus**: Established in 2009, this campus in southern Jordan supports regional development and offers specialized programs in engineering, business, and health sciences.

### **Research and Innovation**
The University of Jordan is committed to advancing scientific research and fostering innovation. Key research centers include:
- **Jordan University Hospital (JUH)**: A public teaching and research hospital providing advanced medical services and training.
- **Jordan Academy of Arabic**: A leading institution for Arabic language and literature, recognized globally.
- **Center for Strategic Studies Jordan**: Focuses on political and economic studies.
- **Jordan University Press**: Publishes peer-reviewed journals in medical, pharmaceutical, and social sciences, contributing to regional and international academic discourse.

### **Global Recognition**
The University of Jordan has gained recognition for its academic excellence and research contributions. It ranks among the top universities in Jordan and the Arab world, with notable rankings in fields such as engineering, business, and social sciences. The university has established partnerships with international institutions for student exchange programs and collaborative research initiatives.

### **Student Life**
The university supports a vibrant student life through various student unions, cultural events, and extracurricular activities. The **University of Jordan Student Union** plays a key role in representing student interests and promoting academic and social engagement.

### **Contributions and Legacy**
The University of Jordan has played a pivotal role in shaping Jordan’s higher education landscape, producing graduates who have made significant contributions to academia, business, politics, and the arts. Its commitment to research, innovation, and community engagement continues to position it as a leading institution in the region.
