The University of Jordan is a public university located in Jordan, with its main campus in Jubaiha, Amman, and an Aqaba branch established in 2009. Founded in 1962, it was established to meet national educational needs and became the first university in Jordan. The university began with a small number of students and faculties and has since expanded significantly, adding new faculties such as Medicine, Dentistry, Pharmacy, Engineering, Nursing, Law, and Agriculture. It offers 78 undergraduate and 143 postgraduate programs across 10 faculties, including scientific, humanitarian, and medical faculties. The university operates a central library with over one million items and has a strong research focus, with over 8,700 research papers and 78 patents reported by faculty between 2000 and 2011. The university has a strong international presence, with student exchange programs in over 100 countries and affiliations with institutions such as the International Association of Universities and the Federation of the Universities of the Islamic World. Notable alumni include prominent political figures such as former Jordanian prime ministers and ministers, as well as international figures like UN Women Executive Director Sima Sami Bahous and U.S. diplomat Jeffrey D. Feltman. The university also has a well-regarded hospital, Jordan University Hospital, which serves as a teaching and research center. It is recognized for its academic excellence, with high admission scores and strong student outcomes, and is ranked among the top universities in the Arab world.
