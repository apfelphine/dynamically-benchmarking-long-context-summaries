The University of Jordan is a public university located in Amman, Jordan, established in 1962. It has two main campuses: the Jubaiha campus in Amman, which is the primary campus and spans 1,200 dunums, and the Aqaba campus, established in 2009 as part of a national strategy to expand higher education in the region. The university began as a national initiative to provide higher education, with early operations in a small area and a budget of 50,000 dinars. It expanded over the decades, adding faculties such as Science, Economics and Commerce, Medicine, Dentistry, Pharmacy, Engineering, Nursing, Law, and Physical Education. The university introduced a credit hour system, considered the first in the Arab world, and established a teaching hospital (Jordan University Hospital) in 1973. It also created research centers, including the Institute of Archaeology, and launched the Jordan University Press to publish peer-reviewed scientific journals. The university is known for its strong academic programs, with 78 undergraduate and 143 postgraduate programs across 109 master's degrees and 34 doctoral degrees. It is organized into three streams: Scientific, Humanitarian, and Medical (Health) faculties. The university has a library with over one million print and electronic items, serving faculty, students, and researchers. The university is ranked among the top universities in Jordan and the Arab world, with a high admission score and strong research output. It is recognized for its contributions to science, medicine, and research, and is a key institution in Jordan's higher education system.
