# University of Jordan

The University of Jordan, established by royal decree in 1962, is the country's first and largest public university, headquartered in the Jubaiha district of Amman with a satellite campus in Aqaba opened in 2009. The main campus, spanning 1.2 square kilometers, is strategically located near central Amman and features extensive academic facilities, student housing, and support services. Aqaba Campus was developed to support regional higher education expansion and includes modern academic and recreational facilities, with ongoing development such as a dedicated women's dormitory.

Academically, the university offers a broad spectrum of programs organized into scientific, humanitarian, and medical faculties. The School of Engineering, the largest faculty established in 1975, boasts ABET accreditation across its departments. Humanities faculties include Arts, Sharia, Business (AACSB accredited), Law, and others, while medical faculties cover Medicine, Nursing, Pharmacy, Dentistry, and Rehabilitation Sciences. The University of Jordan Library, founded alongside the university, is a major resource with over one million items, including rare manuscripts and archives. The university actively promotes research with numerous institutes and centers and runs Jordan University Hospital, a leading teaching hospital accredited regionally and internationally.

The university holds a leading academic position in Jordan and the Arab world, with notable rankings in global university evaluations and a role as a hub for bilateral academic exchanges, including partnerships with several U.S. institutions. Its faculty includes prominent scholars and professionals with distinguished contributions in academia, politics, and research. Alumni have held significant governmental positions, including prime ministers and ministers in Jordan and the region, as well as influential roles in diplomacy, science, business, arts, and media. Student life is represented by an evolving and legislated Student Union, integral to campus governance and activities.
