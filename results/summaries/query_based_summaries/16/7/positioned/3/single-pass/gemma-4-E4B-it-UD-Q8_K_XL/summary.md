The University of Jordan has two campuses: the main campus in Jubaiha, north of Amman, and the Aqaba Campus in the south, established in 2009. The main Jubaiha campus spans 1,200 dunums and borders University Street, which features cafés, restaurants, hotels, and student housing.

The university was founded following repeated national calls for a national university, with its royal decree issued in 1962. Teaching commenced on December 25, 1965, starting with the Faculty of Arts. Over time, the university expanded significantly, adding faculties such as Medicine (established in 1971), Science, Economics and Commerce, Engineering (established in 1975), and the King Abdullah II School of Information Technology (founded in 2000). It houses the University of Jordan Library (UJL), which was established in 1962.
