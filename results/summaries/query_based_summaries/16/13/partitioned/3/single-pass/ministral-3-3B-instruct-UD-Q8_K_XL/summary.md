The **University of the Andes (Uniandes)** in Colombia was founded in 1949 as the first non-sectarian and politically neutral university in the country. Its establishment was driven by the need for skilled professionals to support Colombia’s rapid industrialization and modernization during the Liberal Republic era. Uniandes was modeled after American universities, emphasizing both technical and liberal education, and aimed to produce leaders capable of addressing post-war challenges.

### **Campus and Facilities**
Uniandes’ campus is located in Bogotá’s historical center, a vibrant area rich in cultural and academic institutions. The campus includes a mix of renovated industrial and religious buildings, offering over 180,551 square meters of space. Key facilities include:
- **Classrooms and Laboratories:** Approximately 116 classrooms and 219 laboratories.
- **Library:** Central library and five satellite libraries with over 445,000 books.
- **Sports Facilities:** La Caneca, a sports center with a gym, pool, and courts for sports like tennis, squash, and football.
- **Cafeterias and Health Services:** Multiple food stations and health services for students.
- **Internships Center:** Located at Fundación Santa Fe de Bogotá for the Medicine Faculty.

Uniandes also operates an **Internships Center for the Medicine Faculty** in Fundación Santa Fe de Bogotá, a joint academic venture with the institution.

### **Academic Structure**
Uniandes is organized into **14 academic units**, including 10 faculties, the CIDER (Center for Interdisciplinary Studies on Development), the Alberto Lleras Camargo School of Government, Conecta-te (Center for Innovation in Technology and Education), and the Dean of Students. Students can design their own curricula by taking classes from all programs, with guidance from academic advisors. The university emphasizes interdisciplinary learning, allowing students to explore diverse fields.

### **International Programs**
Uniandes offers **international exchange programs** with over 119 universities in 34 countries worldwide, enabling students to study abroad or participate in joint academic initiatives.

### **Notable Features**
- **Mascot:** The university’s mascot is a goat named Seneca, inspired by Lucius Annaeus Seneca, the Roman Stoic philosopher. The tradition dates back to the late 1940s, though the goat is no longer kept on campus.
- **Alumni:** Uniandes has produced notable figures, including politicians, scientists, and artists. Notable alumni include César Gaviria (former President of Colombia), María Ángela Holguín (Minister of Foreign Affairs), and Gabriel Silva Luján (Minister of National Defense).

### **Mission and Values**
Uniandes’ mission is to foster **pluralism, diversity, dialogue, and respect for ideas and values**, while promoting **academic excellence** and ethical formation. It aims to strengthen students' awareness of their social and civic responsibilities, encouraging critical thinking and problem-solving for Colombia’s development.

Uniandes remains a prominent institution in Colombia, known for its commitment to education, innovation, and social impact.
