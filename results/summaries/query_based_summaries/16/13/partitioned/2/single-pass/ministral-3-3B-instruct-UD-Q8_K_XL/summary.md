The **University of the Andes (Colombia)**, officially known as *Universidad de los Andes*, is a prestigious private university founded in 1949 in Bogotá, Colombia. It operates under a nonprofit corporate structure and was granted university status by the Colombian government in 1964. The university is governed by a **Board of Trustees**, a **Board of Directors**, and a **Rector** (president), with administrative oversight by the **Academic Council** and **Deans** of its academic units.

Uniandes is organized into **14 academic units**, including 10 faculties, specialized centers (e.g., CIDER, Alberto Lleras Camargo School of Government), and a dean of students. Its infrastructure spans **180,551 square meters**, featuring classrooms, laboratories, a central library, and satellite libraries, along with **26 computer rooms** and **2,000+ computers** for student use. The campus is located in Bogotá’s historical center, near cultural landmarks, government offices, and major institutions.

The university is known for its **interdisciplinary approach**, allowing students to design their own curricula with academic advisors. It emphasizes **critical and ethical formation**, fostering social and civic responsibility. Notable programs include a **three/two-year exchange program** with U.S. universities, enabling students to complete part of their degree abroad. Uniandes also offers **international exchange programs** with over 119 universities in 34 countries.

Uniandes’ student body is highly selective, with **95% of undergraduates** being the top 1% of high school students in Colombia. The university has a **diverse student population**, with **55% male and 45% female**, and **30% of students** coming from outside Bogotá. Its academic structure includes four cycles: a **Basic Professional Cycle**, **Elective Courses in Socio-humanistic Issues**, a **Professional Cycle**, and a **Professional Complementary Cycle**.

The university’s **mascot is a goat named Seneca**, symbolizing its founding philosophy. Notable alumni include **César Gaviria** (former President of Colombia), **María Ángela Holguín** (Minister of Foreign Affairs), and **Gabriel Silva Luján** (Minister of National Defense). Uniandes is recognized for its **contributions to Colombian society**, including its role in fostering innovation, leadership, and critical thinking among its graduates.
