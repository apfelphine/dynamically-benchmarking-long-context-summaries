## University of the Andes (Colombia)

**History and Mission:**
The Universidad de los Andes (Uniandes) was founded in November 1948 by Mario Laserna Pinzón as Colombia's first nonsectarian and non-politically affiliated university. Its primary goals were to provide professionals with technical knowledge to aid national development and to cultivate leaders capable of addressing Colombia's problems. Uniandes models itself after the American university system, providing students with both technical and liberal education to foster critical and ethical formation.

**Administration and Academics:**
The Board of Trustees is the supreme governing body, while the Board of Directors handles the university's administration. The Rector serves as the university's legal representative. Academically, Uniandes offers an interdisciplinary approach, allowing students to design their own curricular programs across four cycles: a Basic Professional Cycle (CBP), a Cycle of Elective Courses in Socio-humanistic issues (CSH), a Professional Cycle (CP), and a Professional Complementary Cycle (CPC). The university's assets totaled COL$1.74 trillion by the end of 2020.
