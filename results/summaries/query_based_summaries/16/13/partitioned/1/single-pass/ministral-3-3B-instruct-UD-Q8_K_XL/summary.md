The **University of the Andes (Colombia)**, officially known as *Universidad de los Andes*, was founded in 1949 as Colombia’s first nonsectarian and non-politically affiliated university. Its mission emphasizes academic excellence, critical thinking, and ethical formation, aiming to foster socially responsible leaders and contribute to the nation’s development. The university was established in response to Colombia’s need for skilled professionals and intellectuals to drive post-war modernization, aligning with the liberal reforms of the era.

### **Key Features**
- **Admissions**: The university attracts the top 1% of high school graduates in Colombia, reflecting its rigorous selection process.
- **Academic Structure**: Students can design their own curricula through an interdisciplinary approach, combining foundational courses with electives in socio-humanistic studies, professional training, and complementary subjects.
- **International Engagement**: Uniandes offers exchange programs with over 119 universities worldwide, fostering global academic collaboration.
- **Campus**: Located in Bogotá’s historic center, the campus blends restored industrial and religious buildings with modern facilities, including classrooms, laboratories, libraries, and sports amenities like La Caneca, a sports center with a gym, pool, and courts.
- **Notable Contributions**: Alumni include political leaders, scientists, and cultural figures, such as former President César Gaviria and architect Simón Vélez, known for innovative bamboo architecture.

Uniandes stands out for its commitment to fostering a pluralistic, critical, and socially engaged academic community, positioning it as a key institution in Colombia’s higher education landscape.
