## University of the Andes (Colombia): Key Information

The University of the Andes (Colombia), founded in 1948 as a nonsectarian and non-politically affiliated institution, aimed to address the country's need for technically skilled professionals serving national development. Modeled after the American university system, it offers students a liberal education alongside technical training. Its administration includes a Board of Trustees, Board of Directors, Rector, Vice-Rectors, Deans, faculty councils, and a secretary, with 14 academic units (including 10 faculties) and 20 administrative units. The university emphasizes academic excellence, pluralism, diversity, and a commitment to ethical and social responsibility.

Located in Bogotá's historical center, Uniandes' campus comprises 180,551 square meters and features a blend of restored historic buildings and modern facilities, including 116 classrooms, 219 laboratories, a central library (plus five specialized ones with over 445,000 books), sports facilities, health services, and technology resources. The mascot, Seneca the goat, is a symbol of campus tradition. As of 2014, 70% of faculty held PhDs from leading global universities, and the student body is highly selective, with 95% of undergraduates ranked among Colombia's top 1% academically; 30% come from outside Bogotá. The university supports interdisciplinary study, international exchange programs with universities in 34 countries, and maintains a prominent list of alumni, including former Colombian presidents, ministers, and distinguished artists, scientists, and public figures.
