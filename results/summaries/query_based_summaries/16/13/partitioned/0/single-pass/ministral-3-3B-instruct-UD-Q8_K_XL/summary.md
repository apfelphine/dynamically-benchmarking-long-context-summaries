The **University of the Andes (Colombia)**, officially *Universidad de los Andes*, was founded in 1949 by intellectuals and elites from both political parties in Colombia. Established as the first non-sectarian and politically neutral university in the country, it aimed to provide skilled professionals and foster leadership in a post-war Colombia. Initially modeled after American universities, Uniandes emphasized both technical and liberal education, with a focus on developing critical thinking and civic responsibility.

Uniandes operates on a sprawling campus in Bogotá’s historic center, blending renovated industrial and religious buildings with modern facilities. Its academic structure includes 14 units: 10 faculties, interdisciplinary centers like CIDER and Conecta-te, and a School of Government. The university is known for its rigorous admissions process, with 95% of undergraduates being the top 1% of high school students in Colombia. Its faculty comprises predominantly full-time academics with advanced degrees from top global institutions, reflecting its commitment to academic excellence.

Uniandes is recognized for its interdisciplinary approach, allowing students to design their own curricula across diverse fields. It has strong international ties, offering exchange programs with over 119 universities worldwide. The university’s mission emphasizes pluralism, critical analysis, and ethical formation, aiming to address Colombia’s social and civic challenges through education. While it has faced challenges, including political neutrality during turbulent times, Uniandes remains a prestigious institution dedicated to fostering innovation and leadership in Colombia.
