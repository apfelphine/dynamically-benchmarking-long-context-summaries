# University of the Andes (Colombia) Summary

The Universidad de los Andes was legally established as a common-utility nonprofit corporation in February 1948, and the Colombian government granted it University status in 1964. Founded by Mario Laserna Pinzón in November 1948, it was established as the first nonsectarian and non-politically affiliated university in Colombia. Its primary goals were to equip Colombia with professionals whose technical knowledge could benefit national development and to foster future national leaders.

The university's early years involved modeling itself after the American university, providing students with both technical and liberal education. It also established the "three/two program" starting in 1950 with the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign, allowing students to study for three years at home before transferring to an American university. The university is structured with a Board of Trustees setting general policies, and the Rector implementing the development program. Its mission is to foster pluralism, diversity, dialogue, and academic excellence while instilling in students a critical and ethical awareness of their social and civic responsibilities in solving Colombia's problems.
