## University of the Andes (Colombia) – Concise Summary

The University of the Andes (Universidad de los Andes, Uniandes), founded in Bogotá in 1949 by Mario Laserna Pinzón and a group of Colombian intellectuals, is recognized as the first nonsectarian and non-politically affiliated university in Colombia. Created during a period of national modernization, its founding mission was to educate professionals with both technical expertise and a liberal perspective, modeled after U.S. universities, to advance the country’s development. Uniandes began with interdisciplinary programs and quickly established international academic partnerships, notably its three/two exchange model with U.S. institutions. Its principle of political neutrality and emphasis on pluralism, diversity, and academic excellence remain central to its governance and educational philosophy.

Today, Uniandes operates as a private, autonomous university with a campus located in Bogotá’s historic center. The institution offers a wide range of undergraduate and graduate programs through 10 faculties and several interdisciplinary centers, supporting an interdisciplinary approach and personalized curriculum plans. The campus features modern facilities including laboratories, libraries with over 445,000 books, computer rooms, sports facilities, and specialized centers for medicine and internships. The student body consists primarily of top-performing students from across Colombia, and the faculty includes a high proportion of internationally recognized PhDs. Uniandes maintains active international exchange programs with over 119 universities worldwide and has produced many influential alumni in fields such as politics, art, science, and academia.
