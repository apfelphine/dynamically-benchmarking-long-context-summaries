The **University of the Andes (Colombia)**, established in 1949, is a private, non-sectarian university founded to meet Colombia’s need for skilled professionals and intellectual development. Located in Bogotá, its campus blends historical and modern architecture, offering state-of-the-art facilities, including classrooms, laboratories, and specialized libraries. The university emphasizes interdisciplinary education, allowing students to design their own curricula, and fosters a commitment to critical thinking, ethics, and social responsibility.

**Academic Structure:**
Uniandes comprises 14 academic units, including 10 faculties, the CIDER (Center for Interdisciplinary Studies on Development), the Alberto Lleras Camargo School of Government, and Conecta-te (Center for Innovation in Technology and Education). It offers a wide range of undergraduate and graduate programs, with a strong focus on research and practical applications. The university is known for its rigorous academic standards, with faculty members often holding advanced degrees from top global institutions.

**International Engagement:**
Uniandes has partnerships with over 119 universities worldwide, facilitating exchange programs and fostering global collaboration. It attracts a diverse student body, with around 30% of undergraduates coming from outside Bogotá, and a significant number of international students.

**Campus and Facilities:**
The main campus is situated in Bogotá’s historic center, surrounded by cultural landmarks, government offices, and major corporations. Key facilities include a central cafeteria, sports and health services, and extensive libraries. Notable structures include the **La Caneca** sports center and the university’s iconic mascot, **Seneca**, a goat symbolizing the institution’s values.

**Notable Features:**
- **Mission:** Uniandes aims to foster pluralism, critical thinking, and ethical leadership, preparing students for Colombia’s challenges.
- **Admissions:** The university is highly selective, with a focus on merit-based admissions.
- **Research and Innovation:** Strong emphasis on applied research, with partnerships in technology, medicine, and social sciences.
- **Global Recognition:** While not globally ranked among the top universities, Uniandes is respected for its academic rigor and contributions to Colombian education.

Uniandes stands as a prominent institution in Colombia, blending tradition with innovation to cultivate future leaders and scholars.
