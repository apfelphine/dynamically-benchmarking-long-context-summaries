The **University of the Andes (Colombia)**, established in 1949, is Colombia’s first non-sectarian and politically neutral university. Founded in response to the need for skilled professionals to support the country’s post-war industrialization and modernization efforts, Uniandes initially modeled itself after American universities. It was created to provide technical education and foster critical, ethical formation, emphasizing both professional training and broader intellectual development.

Uniandes operates under a governance structure defined by its 2009 Statutes, with key bodies including the **Board of Trustees**, **Board of Directors**, **Rector (President)**, and **Academic Council**. The university’s mission is to promote academic excellence, pluralism, and social responsibility, offering interdisciplinary programs across 14 academic units, including 10 faculties, the CIDER (Center for Interdisciplinary Development Studies), the Alberto Lleras Camargo School of Government, and Conecta-te (Center for Innovation in Technology and Education). Its faculty comprises highly qualified academics, many with PhDs from top global institutions.

The university’s student body is highly selective, with 95% of undergraduates being the top 1% of high school graduates. It attracts students from across Colombia and abroad, fostering a diverse and intellectually engaged community. Uniandes is located in Bogotá’s historical center, blending renovated industrial and religious buildings with modern facilities, including a central library, sports centers, and health services. The campus is also home to the university’s iconic mascot, **Seneca the Goat**, symbolizing its heritage and values.

Uniandes is recognized for its commitment to research, innovation, and social impact. It has established partnerships with international universities and hosts programs for visiting and exchange students. The university’s academic reputation is supported by its focus on interdisciplinary learning, critical thinking, and ethical leadership, positioning it as a key institution in Colombia’s educational landscape.
