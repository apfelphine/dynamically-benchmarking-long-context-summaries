The University of the Andes (Colombia), founded in 1949, was established as the first nonsectarian and non-politically affiliated university in Colombia, modeled after the American university system. Located in Bogotá, it aims to provide technical and liberal education to cultivate national leaders who can contribute to the country's development. The university operates as a common-utility nonprofit corporation, legally recognized with university status in 1964, and is governed by a Board of Trustees, a Board of Directors, and various academic councils. Its campus, situated in the historical center of Bogotá, features a mix of renovated industrial and religious buildings, including the iconic "La Caneca" sports center, and offers diverse academic programs across ten faculties and several interdisciplinary centers.

The institution boasts a significant international presence, maintaining academic exchange programs with over 119 universities in 34 countries and offering opportunities for visiting and exchange students.
