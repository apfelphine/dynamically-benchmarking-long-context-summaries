The **University of the Andes (Colombia)**, officially known as *Universidad de los Andes*, was founded in 1949 as Colombia’s first non-sectarian and politically independent university. Established during a period of political transition in Colombia, its mission was to provide skilled professionals and foster intellectual development for the nation’s growth. The university operates under a governance structure with a Board of Trustees, Board of Directors, and a Rector (president), reflecting its autonomous and independent nature.

The university is organized into 14 academic units, including 10 faculties, the CIDER (Center for Interdisciplinary Studies on Development), the Alberto Lleras Camargo School of Government, Conecta-te (Center for Innovation in Technology and Education), and the Dean of Students. Its faculty comprises primarily full-time academics with advanced degrees from top global institutions, emphasizing research and interdisciplinary learning. The student body is highly competitive, with 95% of undergraduates being the top 1% of high school graduates in Colombia.

**Academic Structure:**
Uniandes offers a flexible curriculum where students can choose courses across all programs, allowing for interdisciplinary study. The university is divided into four academic cycles: a Basic Professional Cycle, a Cycle of Elective Courses in Socio-humanistic issues, a Professional Cycle, and a Professional Complementary Cycle. The university also supports international students through exchange programs with over 119 universities worldwide.

**Campus:**
The university’s main campus is located in Bogotá’s historical center, featuring a mix of restored industrial and religious buildings alongside new facilities. Key amenities include classrooms, laboratories, a central library, satellite libraries, sports facilities (including La Caneca, a sports center), and dining options. The campus is surrounded by cultural institutions, government offices, and major corporations, providing a vibrant urban environment.

**Notable Features:**
- **Mascot:** Seneca, a goat symbolizing the university’s spirit.
- **International Programs:** Exchange and visiting student opportunities with institutions across 34 countries.
- **Research and Innovation:** Focused on practical and ethical education, with a strong emphasis on critical thinking and civic responsibility.

The University of the Andes remains a prominent institution in Colombia, recognized for its academic rigor, interdisciplinary approach, and commitment to fostering a diverse and engaged student body.
