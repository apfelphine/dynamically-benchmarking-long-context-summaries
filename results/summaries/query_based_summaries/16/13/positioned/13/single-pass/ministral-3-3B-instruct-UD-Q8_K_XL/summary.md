The **University of the Andes (Colombia)**, officially known as *Universidad de los Andes*, was founded in 1948 in Bogotá, Colombia, as the first non-sectarian and politically independent university in the country. Its establishment was driven by the need to modernize education and foster professional development for Colombia’s growing industrial and economic needs, particularly after the Liberal Republic’s rise in the 1930s and 1940s. The university’s mission emphasizes academic excellence, critical thinking, and ethical formation, aiming to address Colombia’s social and civic challenges.

### **Structure and Governance**
Uniandes operates under a structured governance model with key bodies including the **Board of Trustees**, **Board of Directors**, the **Rector (President)**, and the **Academic Council**. The university comprises **14 academic units**, including **10 faculties**, the **CIDER** (Center for Interdisciplinary Studies on Development), the **Alberto Lleras Camargo School of Government**, **Conecta-te** (Center for Innovation in Technology and Education), and the **Student Dean’s Office**. It also has **20 administrative units**, supporting its operations.

### **Academic Offerings**
Uniandes is known for its interdisciplinary approach, allowing students to design their own curricula through a combination of core professional courses, elective socio-humanistic courses, and specialized professional studies. The university offers a wide range of undergraduate and graduate programs across fields such as **Engineering, Medicine, Law, Social Sciences, Humanities, and Business**. It also has a strong emphasis on research and international collaboration, with partnerships in over **119 universities worldwide**.

### **Campus and Facilities**
The university’s main campus is located in Bogotá’s historic center, featuring a mix of renovated industrial and religious buildings alongside modern facilities. Key amenities include classrooms, laboratories, a central library, sports centers (such as **La Caneca**), health services, and dining options. The campus also houses specialized libraries for various disciplines. Additionally, Uniandes operates an **Internships Center** for the Medicine Faculty, located at Fundación Santa Fe de Bogotá.

### **International Engagement**
Uniandes supports international students through **exchange programs** with over **119 universities** in **34 countries**. It also offers visiting and exchange student opportunities, fostering global academic collaboration. The university’s commitment to diversity and inclusion is reflected in its student body, which includes students from all regions of Colombia and beyond.

### **Notable Features**
- **Mascot**: The university’s mascot is a goat named **Seneca**, symbolizing its values of resilience and tradition.
- **Research and Innovation**: Uniandes emphasizes applied research and innovation, with a focus on addressing Colombia’s development challenges.
- **Global Recognition**: The university is recognized for its academic rigor and contributions to education in Latin America, particularly in fields like engineering, medicine, and social sciences.

Uniandes remains a prominent institution in Colombia, known for its commitment to academic freedom, social responsibility, and the development of future leaders.
