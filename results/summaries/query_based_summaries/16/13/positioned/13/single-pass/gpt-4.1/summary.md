## University of the Andes (Colombia)

Founded in 1948 in Bogotá, the University of the Andes (Uniandes) was established as Colombia’s first nonsectarian, non-politically affiliated university, modeled after the American university system. Its creation aimed to address the national need for technically skilled professionals to support Colombia’s modernization and development. The university quickly became recognized for its academic excellence, adopting innovative approaches such as interdisciplinary curriculum design, international exchange programs, and active recruitment of foreign academics. Today, Uniandes is governed as an autonomous, nonprofit institution with robust academic and administrative bodies, including 10 faculties and several interdisciplinary centers. Most faculty are full-time, highly qualified academics, and the student body is distinguished by high achievement, with 95% of undergraduates from the top 1% of Colombian high school students.

The university’s campus is located in Bogotá’s historical center, featuring a blend of renovated historic buildings and modern facilities, including numerous libraries, laboratories, sports, and health services. Uniandes promotes both technical and liberal education, fostering pluralism, diversity, and academic excellence. It maintains extensive international exchange programs and offers students flexibility in designing their curricular paths. Notable alumni include national leaders, government ministers, distinguished scientists, artists, and professionals, reflecting the university’s role in shaping Colombian society. Its mascot, the goat Seneca, is a cherished campus symbol.
