The **University of the Andes (Colombia)**, officially known as *Universidad de los Andes*, was founded in 1949 as Colombia’s first nonsectarian and politically independent university. Established in response to the country’s post-war educational needs and political instability, it aims to provide Colombia with skilled professionals and foster intellectual development through a liberal arts and sciences curriculum. The university operates under a governance structure including a Board of Trustees, Board of Directors, and an Academic Council, with its mission centered on academic excellence, critical thinking, and social responsibility.

The university’s academic structure includes 14 faculties, a CIDER (Interdisciplinary Center for Development Studies), the Alberto Lleras Camargo School of Government, and Conecta-te (Center for Innovation in Technology and Education). It offers interdisciplinary programs, allowing students to design their own curricula, and emphasizes research, innovation, and international collaboration. The campus is located in Bogotá’s historic center, blending restored industrial and religious buildings with modern facilities, including libraries, laboratories, and sports amenities. Notable traditions include the university’s goat mascot, Seneca, and the annual "Students’ Day" celebration.

Internationally, Uniandes is recognized for its academic rigor and contributions to Colombian society. It has partnerships with over 119 universities worldwide and supports diverse student populations, including international students through exchange programs. The university’s focus on fostering critical and ethical formation aligns with its commitment to addressing Colombia’s social and economic challenges.
