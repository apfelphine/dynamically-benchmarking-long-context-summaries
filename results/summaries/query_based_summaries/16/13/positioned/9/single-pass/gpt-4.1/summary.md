## University of the Andes (Colombia)

Founded in 1948, the University of the Andes (Universidad de los Andes, Uniandes) is a nonsectarian, independent institution established in Bogotá, Colombia, with the goal of providing technical and liberal education to foster national development and leadership. Inspired by American models, Uniandes maintains political and religious neutrality and has consistently focused on academic excellence, pluralism, and critical formation, as reflected in its governing statutes and mission. Early initiatives included partnerships with U.S. universities and philanthropic organizations to support international study and the development of a skilled workforce. The university began with a small student body in rented facilities and quickly expanded its campus, integrating restored historical buildings and new construction into Bogotá’s cultural center.

Today, Uniandes comprises 14 academic units, including 10 faculties and specialized centers, and offers interdisciplinary programs where students can design their own curricula with guidance from advisors. It supports a diverse staff, with 70% of faculty holding PhDs from top global universities, and actively encourages international exchanges with over 119 partner institutions in 34 countries. The university serves a predominantly high-achieving student population, with 55% male and 45% female representation, and draws 30% from outside Bogotá. The campus spans over 180,000 square meters, featuring extensive academic and recreational facilities, including laboratories, libraries, sports centers, and health services. Notable alumni include leaders in government, science, arts, and business, such as former Colombian presidents, ministers, and renowned professionals. Uniandes is also recognized for its financial stability and commitment to social responsibility, maintaining assets valued at over US$400 million. The university's mascot is Seneca the goat, symbolizing its unique institutional identity and student tradition.
