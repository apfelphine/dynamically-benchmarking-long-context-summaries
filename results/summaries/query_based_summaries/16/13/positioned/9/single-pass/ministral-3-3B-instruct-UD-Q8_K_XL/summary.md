The **University of the Andes (Colombia)**, officially known as *Universidad de los Andes*, was founded in 1949 as the first non-sectarian and politically neutral university in Colombia. Established in response to the need for skilled professionals to drive Colombia’s post-war industrial and economic growth, Uniandes was modeled after American universities, emphasizing both technical education and liberal arts. Its founding mission was to foster critical thinking, ethical leadership, and civic responsibility, aligning with Colombia’s post-war aspirations.

Uniandes operates on a **non-profit, autonomous** basis, governed by a Board of Trustees, a Board of Directors, and a Rector (president). The university’s **Statutes** emphasize pluralism, diversity, and academic excellence, aiming to prepare students for leadership roles in a globalized world. Its **admissions process** is merit-based, with a significant portion of students coming from the top 1% of high school graduates, reflecting its rigorous academic standards.

The university’s **campus** is located in Bogotá’s historic center, blending renovated industrial and religious buildings with modern facilities. It houses **14 academic units** (10 faculties, CIDER, Alberto Lleras Camargo School of Government, Conecta-te, and the Dean of Students), offering **70 undergraduate programs** and **153 postgraduate programs**. Uniandes is known for its **interdisciplinary approach**, allowing students to design their own curricula, and its **strong emphasis on international exchange programs**, with partnerships in over 34 countries.

Uniandes has a **diverse student body**, with 95% of undergraduates being the top 1% of high school graduates. The university’s **faculty** includes many PhD holders from top global universities, and its **research focus** spans areas like bioenergy, biotechnology, and climate change. The university’s **financial assets** totaled approximately **$426 million** (COL$1.74 trillion) as of 2020, reflecting its robust endowment and institutional stability.

Uniandes is recognized for its **contributions to Colombia’s development**, particularly in education, research, and social responsibility. It has produced notable alumni in politics, business, science, and the arts, and remains a key institution in Colombia’s higher education landscape.
