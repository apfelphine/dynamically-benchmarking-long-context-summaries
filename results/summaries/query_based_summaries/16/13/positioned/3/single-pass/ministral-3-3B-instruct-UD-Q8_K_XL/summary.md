The **University of the Andes (Uniandes)** in Colombia was founded in 1949 as the first non-sectarian and politically neutral university in the country. Established by intellectuals from both political parties, its primary goals were to provide Colombia with skilled professionals and foster a critical, ethical education to address post-war development needs. Classes began in 1949 with just 80 students, expanding to over 400 by 1950.

Uniandes operates on a **non-partisan** and **non-sectarian** basis, ensuring a balanced academic environment. It has 14 academic units, including 10 faculties, the CIDER (Center for Interdisciplinary Studies on Development), the Alberto Lleras Camargo School of Government, Conecta-te (Center for Innovation in Technology and Education), and the Dean of Students. The university is governed by a Board of Trustees, a Board of Directors, and the Rector, with a focus on academic excellence and social responsibility.

The **student body** is highly selective, with 95% of undergraduates being the top 1% of high school students. The university emphasizes interdisciplinary learning, allowing students to design their own curricula. Uniandes has a strong reputation for fostering critical thinking and ethical leadership, aligning with its mission to prepare students for Colombia’s social and civic challenges.

Uniandes is located in Bogotá’s historical center, offering a mix of renovated industrial and religious buildings, modern facilities, and a vibrant campus life. It is known for its **academic rigor, international exchange programs, and commitment to social responsibility**, making it a prominent institution in Colombia’s higher education landscape.
