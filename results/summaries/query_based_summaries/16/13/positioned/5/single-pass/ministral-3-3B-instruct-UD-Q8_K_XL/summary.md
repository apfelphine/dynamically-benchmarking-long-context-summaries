The **University of the Andes (Colombia)**, established in 1949, is a private, non-sectarian, and politically neutral university founded to provide Colombia with skilled professionals and leaders for post-war development. Located in Bogotá, its campus integrates renovated industrial and religious buildings with modern facilities, totaling 180,551 square meters. The university emphasizes interdisciplinary education, offering students flexibility to design their curricula through a combination of core courses, socio-humanistic electives, professional studies, and complementary subjects.
