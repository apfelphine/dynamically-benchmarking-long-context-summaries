The **University of the Andes (Colombia)**, officially *Universidad de los Andes*, was founded in 1948 as Colombia’s first non-sectarian and politically neutral university. Established in Bogotá, it initially operated in rented spaces before purchasing its own land in 1956. The university emphasizes interdisciplinary education, allowing students to design their own curricula through a flexible system combining core professional courses, socio-humanistic electives, and specialized professional cycles.

**Key Features:**
- **Mission:** Focuses on academic excellence, critical thinking, and ethical formation to address Colombia’s social and civic challenges.
- **Admissions:** Highly competitive, with 95% of undergraduates being the top 1% of high school graduates. Admissions are merit-based, with no racial or socioeconomic quotas.
- **Campus:** Located in Bogotá’s historical center, blending renovated industrial and religious buildings with modern facilities. It includes classrooms, laboratories, a central library, and sports facilities like *La Caneca*, a sports complex.
- **International Programs:** Offers exchange and visiting student opportunities with over 119 universities worldwide.
- **Notable Alumni:** Includes political leaders like César Gaviria (former president), Fernando Botero (former defense minister), and Nobel laureate Tu Youyou (pharmaceutical chemist).

The university is recognized for its commitment to fostering innovation, leadership, and social responsibility, positioning itself as a key institution in Colombia’s higher education landscape.
