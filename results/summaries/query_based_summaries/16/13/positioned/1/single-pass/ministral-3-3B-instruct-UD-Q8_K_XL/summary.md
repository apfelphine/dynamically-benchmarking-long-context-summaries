The **University of the Andes (Colombia)**, founded in 1949, is Colombia’s first nonsectarian and politically independent university. Established by intellectuals from both the Liberal and Conservative parties, its mission was to provide skilled professionals and foster critical, ethical education. Inspired by American university models, Uniandes initially offered a liberal arts curriculum alongside technical training.

The university operates on a four-cycle academic structure, blending foundational professional courses with interdisciplinary electives. It emphasizes student autonomy, allowing students to design personalized curricula. Uniandes has a strong international presence, with over 119 exchange programs across 34 countries, and a diverse student body, with 95% of undergraduates being the top 1% of high school graduates.

The university’s main campus is located in Bogotá’s historical center, integrating renovated industrial and religious buildings with modern facilities. Key features include the **La Caneca** sports center, a central cafeteria, health services, and a mascot, the goat Seneca. Notable alumni include politicians like César Gaviria and Alejandro Gaviria, as well as scientists, artists, and activists.

Uniandes is governed by a Board of Trustees, a Board of Directors, and a rector, with a focus on academic excellence, social responsibility, and pluralism. The university’s financial model relies heavily on government funding, with a notable endowment valued at approximately $426 million as of 2020. It is committed to fostering innovation, entrepreneurship, and global engagement while maintaining its commitment to fostering critical and ethical education.
