The University of Edinburgh is a prominent research-intensive institution located in Scotland, recognized as a founding member of the Russell Group and a member of the Coimbra Group and the League of European Research Universities. It is distinguished as the only British university to be a member of both the Coimbra Group and the League of European Research Universities. The university operates across five main campuses in Edinburgh, including the historic Central Area, King's Buildings, BioQuarter, Easter Bush, and Western General, housing diverse faculties such as Medicine & Veterinary Medicine, Science & Engineering, and Arts, Humanities and Social Sciences. It maintains historically strong ties with the neighboring Heriot-Watt University and offers a wide range of free online MOOC courses on platforms like Coursera, EdX, and FutureLearn.

Academically, the university boasts a robust research profile, ranking highly in specific disciplines such as Computer Science, Informatics, Sociology, and Medicine. It is associated with numerous Nobel Prize laureates and has founded or significantly influenced medical schools at major institutions globally, including Columbia, Harvard, and Yale. The student body is characterized by a high proportion of female students (approximately 61%) and a significant international presence, with over 28% of undergraduates coming from outside the EU. The university maintains a rich tradition of student life, featuring the world's oldest purpose-built student union building, Teviot Row House, and vibrant cultural and sporting activities, including the "world's oldest varsity match" against the University of St Andrews.
