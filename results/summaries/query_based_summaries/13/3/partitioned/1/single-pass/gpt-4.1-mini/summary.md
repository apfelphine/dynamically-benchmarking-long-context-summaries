## University of Edinburgh

The University of Edinburgh, founded in 1583 as Tounis College, is a historic institution with multiple campuses across Edinburgh, including the Central Area, King's Buildings, BioQuarter, Easter Bush, and Western General. It holds several significant buildings like St Cecilia's Hall, the oldest purpose-built concert hall in Scotland, and Teviot Row House, the world's oldest purpose-built students' union building. The Central Area hosts the oldest university buildings and faculties such as Arts, Humanities, Social Sciences, and Informatics, while King's Buildings focus on Science and Engineering. The BioQuarter campus accommodates medical facilities alongside the Royal Infirmary, and Easter Bush hosts veterinary and animal welfare education institutes. The university has a distinctive governance structure established by the Universities (Scotland) Acts, comprising the University Court, General Council, and Senatus Academicus, led by key officials including the chancellor (Princess Anne since 2011), rector, and principal.

Academically, the university is divided into three colleges: Arts, Humanities and Social Sciences; Science and Engineering; and Medicine and Veterinary Medicine, comprising 21 schools as of 2021. It played a pivotal role during the Scottish Enlightenment and has been associated with numerous significant intellectual and scientific contributions, including in medicine, economics, and physics. The university maintains strong international links and exchange programs with over 300 partner institutions globally. In the 2021 Research Excellence Framework, it ranked 4th by research power in the UK with notable strengths in Computer Science, Sociology, and Anthropology. Student life features over 250 societies managed by the Edinburgh University Students' Association, housed in historic union buildings, and includes a vibrant arts and sports culture, with notable achievements in Olympic sports. The university is also active in student activism and sustainability initiatives, reflecting its commitment to social and environmental issues.
