University College London (UCL) is primarily based in the Bloomsbury area of Central London, with a second campus, UCL East, located at the Queen Elizabeth Olympic Park in Stratford. UCL owns 26 halls of residence, and the university has implemented several environmental initiatives, such as the "Positive Climate" initiative, aiming for net zero carbon emissions by 2030, and "The Loop," which promotes a circular economy.

UCL engages in various partnerships, including the Science and Engineering South research alliance with Cambridge, Oxford, Southampton, and Imperial College London, and it was a founding member of the Alan Turing Institute. Academically, UCL is organized into eleven faculties, with research and teaching organized within these units. The university has established UCL Press, one of the UK's first fully open access university presses, and operates its own wholly-owned technology transfer company, UCL Business.
