University College London (UCL) is a prestigious research-intensive university founded in 1826 and granted full university status in 2023. Located primarily in the Bloomsbury area of London, with additional campuses in UCL East and Here East, the institution is organized into eleven faculties covering a wide range of disciplines. UCL is a founding member of the Russell Group and holds significant international partnerships, including a major collaboration with Yale University. The university is renowned for its research output, having been ranked highly in global university rankings and submitting the largest number of units of assessment to the UK's Research Excellence Framework.

UCL places a strong emphasis on widening access through initiatives like the Access UCL scheme, which provides contextual offers to applicants from disadvantaged backgrounds, including those from deprived areas, care leavers, or young adult carers. The university also operates foundation courses and summer schools to support students who may not meet standard entry requirements. Student life is characterized by a vibrant culture supported by the Students' Union UCL, one of England's oldest student unions, which offers over 250 societies and clubs. The campus features historic and modern buildings, including the grade I listed Main Building, and has implemented extensive sustainability strategies, such as a 2030 net-zero carbon emissions target.
