**University College London (UCL)** is a leading research university located in London, with its main campus in Bloomsbury and a secondary campus in Stratford (UCL East). The Bloomsbury campus includes historic buildings such as the Grade I-listed Main Building (designed by William Wilkins) and the Grade II-listed Cruciform Building (by Alfred Waterhouse), alongside modern structures like the Sainsbury Wellcome Centre for Neural Circuits and Behaviour. UCL East, opened in 2022 and expanded with additional buildings, serves as a hub for teaching and research, including a new student accommodation complex.

UCL offers a wide range of academic programs across eleven faculties, including Arts and Humanities, Engineering Sciences, and Medical Sciences, and operates several research centres, such as the Centre for the Study of the Legacies of British Slave-ownership and the Sainsbury Wellcome Centre. The university has a strong focus on sustainability, with its Student Centre certified outstanding by BREEAM and a strategy targeting net zero carbon emissions by 2030.

UCL's governance includes a Council and Academic Board, with the President and Provost as the principal academic and administrative officer. The university is a member of the Russell Group, the League of European Research Universities, and the Alan Turing Institute, and maintains partnerships with institutions such as Cambridge, Oxford, and Imperial College London. It also operates UCLPartners, a major academic health science centre.

Student life at UCL is vibrant, with over 250 clubs and societies, including the Debating Society and UCL Snowsports. The Students' Union UCL provides student representation and services, while the university hosts over 70 sports clubs and competes in British Universities and Colleges Sport (BUCS). UCL has a long-standing rivalry with King's College London, marked by historical "rags" and cultural exchanges.

UCL is highly selective in admissions, offering contextual support through programs like Access UCL and foundation years for disadvantaged applicants. It serves a diverse student body, with over 24,000 international students in 2021/22, including a significant number from Asia. The university is recognized globally for its research, ranking among the top 25 in the world and holding a top-10 position in several subject-specific rankings.

UCL has a strong commitment to diversity and inclusion, holding institutional awards for gender equality and race equality, and has been a leader in student activism, including campaigns for climate action, staff pay equity, and divestment from fossil fuels. The university also has a notable history in science and medicine, with alumni and staff including Nobel laureates such as Francis Crick and Peter Higgs.
