University College London (UCL) is a historic federal university located primarily in the Bloomsbury district of Central London, with a second campus at the Queen Elizabeth Olympic Park in Stratford (UCL East). Its Bloomsbury estate includes numerous grade-listed buildings such as the Wilkins Building and the Cruciform Building, many situated within the Bloomsbury Conservation Area. UCL has expanded its footprint with UCL East, which opened in phases starting in 2022, and other sites including the Institute of Ophthalmology in Clerkenwell, the School of Management in Canary Wharf, and the Mullard Space Science Laboratory in Surrey. The university guarantees accommodation to first-year students from disadvantaged backgrounds and operates 26 halls of residence. UCL is committed to sustainability, having achieved top-tier BREEAM certification for its Student Centre and launched a strategy targeting net-zero carbon emissions by 2030.

Governed by a council and academic board established under royal charter, UCL is organized into eleven faculties covering disciplines from arts and humanities to medicine and engineering. It operates as a member institution of the University of London, the Russell Group, and the League of European Research Universities, while maintaining partnerships with global institutions like Yale University. UCL's research output is significant, with substantial income from research grants and a high volume of staff submissions to the Research Excellence Framework. The university offers dual degrees with international partners and has a major commitment to widening access through contextual offers for students from deprived areas and foundation courses for international students. UCL is also a key academic health science centre partner, collaborating extensively with NHS trusts through UCLPartners.
