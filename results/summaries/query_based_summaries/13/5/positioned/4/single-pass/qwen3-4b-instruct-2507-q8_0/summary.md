University College London (UCL) is a leading research university in the United Kingdom, founded in 1826 as London University and granted full university status in 2023. Located primarily in the Bloomsbury area of Central London, UCL's main campus includes historic buildings such as the Wilkins Building and Cruciform Building, and a newer campus, UCL East, at the Queen Elizabeth Olympic Park in Stratford. The university operates a large library system with over 2 million books and offers a wide range of academic programs across eleven faculties, including Arts and Humanities, Engineering Sciences, and Medical Sciences.

UCL is a member of the Russell Group and the League of European Research Universities, and is known for its strong research output, with 58% of its research rated as world-leading in the 2021 Research Excellence Framework. It has a significant global reputation, consistently ranked in the top 25 of international university rankings, including 14th in the 2025 Academic Ranking of World Universities. UCL is also recognized for its sustainability initiatives, such as its Positive Climate initiative and the creation of biodiverse green spaces, and is ranked joint fifth globally for sustainability in the QS World University Rankings: Sustainability 2025.

The university emphasizes inclusivity and diversity, with a student body that is highly international, including 45% of students from outside the UK. UCL offers a range of support programs, including the Access UCL scheme for disadvantaged applicants and foundation years for students who do not meet standard entry requirements. The student life at UCL includes over 250 clubs and societies, a vibrant student union, and a strong tradition of activism, including protests on issues such as climate change, student housing, and university policies.

UCL has a long history of notable contributions to science and society, including the development of eugenics research, the establishment of the Petrie Museum of Egyptian Archaeology, and a strong legacy in medical and scientific research. The university has also faced scrutiny over its historical links with eugenics and has taken steps to address these, including renaming buildings and launching internal inquiries. UCL is also known for its innovative academic practices, such as the preceptorial system and its commitment to open access publishing through UCL Press.
