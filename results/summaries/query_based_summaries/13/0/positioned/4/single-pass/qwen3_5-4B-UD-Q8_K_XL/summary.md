Yale is known for its strong alumni network in politics and business, having produced numerous U.S. presidents. It has faced significant recent scrutiny regarding its endowments linked to the slave trade and its handling of sexual harassment complaints.

It is known for its "One University Policy," which allows students to take courses across all schools, and its rigorous liberal arts curriculum.
