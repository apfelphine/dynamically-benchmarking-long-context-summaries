The University of Cambridge is a prestigious collegiate university with 31 colleges, each offering a range of academic specialties and student accommodation. The university's academic structure includes over 150 departments, faculties, and schools, with teaching organized through a system of supervisions—small, weekly sessions led by faculty or doctoral students—central to its pedagogical approach. The academic year is divided into three terms, and students are required to live within three miles of the Church of St Mary the Great during term time. 

Admissions are highly competitive, with an average entry requirement of A*AA in A-Levels, and a foundation course available for disadvantaged students with BBB. The university offers a wide range of academic programs, including a strong focus on research, with over 165 research centers and a history of groundbreaking scientific discoveries, such as the structure of DNA and the development of the first general-purpose electronic computer. 

Cambridge's governance is led by the Regent House, which oversees graduations and academic policy, with the Vice-Chancellor serving as the de facto administrative head. The university maintains a significant endowment of £3.736 billion (excluding colleges), with a strong financial base from research grants and donations. It is a member of the Russell Group, the G5, and the League of European Research Universities, and is part of the "golden triangle" of research-intensive universities in southern England. 

The university is renowned for its academic excellence, with notable alumni in science, politics, literature, and the arts, including Isaac Newton, Stephen Hawking, and John Maynard Keynes. Its academic traditions, such as the Mathematical Tripos and the Festival of Nine Lessons and Carols, are deeply embedded in student life. Cambridge also has a strong tradition of student activism and social engagement, including campaigns on issues like climate change and university investment policies. 

The university's global reputation is reflected in its high rankings, including being ranked sixth in the world by QS, fourth by ARWU, and fifth by Times Higher Education in 2025. It is also known for its rich cultural heritage, historic buildings, and contributions to science, technology, and the arts.
