**Technological singularity** refers to a hypothetical point where technological progress accelerates so rapidly that it leads to the advent of superhuman intelligence, often called an "intelligence explosion." This concept suggests that an "ultraintelligent machine" could surpass all human intellectual capabilities, leading to a transformative "intelligence explosion" where human intelligence would be left far behind.

Proponents predict various timelines for this event, with figures like Vernor Vinge suggesting it would signal the end of the human era, while Ray Kurzweil has predicted a singularity by 2045, based on a "law of accelerating returns" in technological change. The resulting superintelligence is envisioned to be able to recursively self-improve its own software and hardware, leading to profound qualitative changes beyond current human understanding.
