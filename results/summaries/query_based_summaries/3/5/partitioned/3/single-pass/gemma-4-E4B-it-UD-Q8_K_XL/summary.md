Accelerationism encompasses varied, often contradictory, ideas centered on the idea that contradictions within systems, particularly capitalism, must be exacerbated to drive transformation. Accelerationists generally assess that these internal contradictions must be countered by their own aggravation. The movement is closely tied to posthumanism and antihumanism, with left-accelerationists employing the term "inhumanism."

Key variants include **Left-wing accelerationism**, which seeks to accelerate past capitalism by democratizing productive technologies, utilizing concepts like "Prometheanism" to champion innovation to surpass natural limits. **Right-wing accelerationism**, associated with Land, views unregulated capitalism as an intrinsic, self-escalating, and deterritorializing force leading toward a "technocapital singularity," often embracing human extinction as a path to the inhuman Real. Other forms include **Effective accelerationism (e/acc)**, which advocates for unrestricted technological progress "at all costs" based on effective altruism.
