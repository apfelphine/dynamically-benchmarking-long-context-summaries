Accelerationism encompasses various, often contradictory ideas, centering on the belief that the accelerating forces of capitalist production should be directed toward destabilizing capitalism itself. The movement has seen three waves: early French post-Marxists (late 60s/early 70s), the 1990s work of Nick Land and the CCRU, and the 2010s Promethean left-accelerationism.

Key concepts include adherence to posthumanism and antihumanism, with Prometheanism advocating for using innovation and technology to surpass human limits. Right-wing accelerationism, associated with Land, views unregulated capitalism as an engine of exponential growth, often embracing human extinction in the context of a "technocapital singularity." In contrast, left-accelerationism seeks to accelerate past capitalism by democratizing productive technologies, aiming for a post-capitalist future.
