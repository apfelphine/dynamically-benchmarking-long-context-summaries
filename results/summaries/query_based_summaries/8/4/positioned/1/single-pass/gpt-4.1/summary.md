## Education in Australia – Concise Summary

Education in Australia is predominantly regulated and funded by the states and territories, with significant support from the federal government. Compulsory education was introduced between 1871 and 1916, and is currently required from ages 5–6 to 15–17, depending on jurisdiction. As of 2023, about 64% of students are educated in government schools, while the remainder attend Catholic (19.7%) or independent (16.3%) non-government schools. Preschool is not mandatory but widely attended, with government initiatives supporting quality early childhood education; the nationally approved Early Years Learning Framework guides preschool programming. Primary education typically lasts from kindergarten or pre-Year 1 through Year 6, followed by secondary education which goes to Year 12, with a range of subject specializations and opportunities emerging in senior years. The school year generally runs from January/February to December.

Australia’s education system is underpinned by the Australian Qualifications Framework and a standard national curriculum that has been progressively implemented since 2010, ensuring consistency across school sectors. Qualifications at the secondary level include various state-issued certificates (e.g. HSC, VCE, QCE) and the option of the International Baccalaureate. Assessment of basic skills is standardized nationally via NAPLAN and complemented by international tests such as PISA, TIMSS, and PIRLS. While Australia ranks highly in the global Education Index and attracts significant numbers of international students (being one of the top providers globally), recent PISA studies indicate a declining trend in student achievement, especially in maths and reading. The country’s tertiary sector includes 42 universities (mostly public), with student fees supported through income-contingent loans (HECS). Significant federal and state funding reforms were enacted in line with equity goals, and new agreements target fully funding public schools. Australia’s education system also deals with issues like Indigenous educational disparity, bilingual education, school violence, religious education in schools, and the integration of international students.
