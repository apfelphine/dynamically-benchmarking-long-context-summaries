The **National Autonomous University of Mexico (UNAM)** is a prestigious public university founded in 1910 by Justo Sierra, aiming to create a modern, secular, and national institution. Originally merging several colleges, UNAM became a central hub for higher education in Mexico, emphasizing academic freedom, research, and social responsibility.

### **Campuses & Facilities**
UNAM operates from its iconic **Ciudad Universitaria** (University City) in Coyoacán, Mexico City, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. The campus features a mix of modern and historic buildings, including the **National Library of Mexico**, **Palacio de Minería**, and **Museo Experimental El Eco**. It also has satellite campuses across Mexico, including in **Querétaro, Morelia, Mérida, and León**, as well as international centers in the **U.S. and Canada**.

### **Academics & Research**
UNAM is organized into **schools and national schools**, offering undergraduate, graduate, and professional programs across disciplines like engineering, medicine, law, and humanities. It is renowned for its **strong research focus**, particularly in fields like **artificial intelligence, robotics, and renewable energy**. UNAM is recognized globally for its contributions to science, with many faculty and alumni earning Nobel Prizes.

### **Notable Features**
- **Motto:** *"For my people, the spirit shall speak"* (a call to cultural and educational reform).
- **Symbol:** A shield featuring a Mexican eagle and an Andean condor, symbolizing unity and progress.
- **Student Activism:** UNAM has a long history of political engagement, with students playing key roles in Mexico’s social movements.
- **Global Reach:** The university collaborates with international institutions and hosts global research initiatives.

UNAM remains one of Mexico’s most influential and respected universities, blending tradition with innovation.
