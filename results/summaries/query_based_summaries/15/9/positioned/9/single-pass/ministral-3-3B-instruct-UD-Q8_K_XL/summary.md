The **National Autonomous University of Mexico (UNAM)** is Mexico’s premier public research university, founded on **September 22, 1910**, by Minister of Education Justo Sierra. Unlike its predecessor, the Royal and Pontifical University of Mexico (founded in 1551), UNAM was established as a secular, national institution aligned with liberal ideals and the Mexican Revolution’s vision for educational reform. Its mission was to centralize and modernize higher education in Mexico, fostering scientific, cultural, and social progress.

### **Key Features**
- **Campus:** UNAM’s flagship **Ciudad Universitaria** (University City) in Coyoacán, Mexico City, is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. The campus was designed in the 1950s to replace the scattered buildings in the city center, offering a self-contained academic environment with libraries, museums, and cultural centers.
- **Structure:** UNAM organizes its academic activities into **schools and colleges**, with both undergraduate and graduate programs. It also oversees the **Escuela Nacional Preparatoria (ENP)** and the **Colegio de Ciencias y Humanidades (CCH)**, which provide high school education.
- **Research:** UNAM is a leader in research across disciplines, including **artificial intelligence, robotics, astronomy, and renewable energy**. It hosts prestigious institutes like the **Institute of Astronomy, the Institute of Nuclear Sciences, and the Center for Energy Research**.
- **Global Reach:** Beyond Mexico, UNAM operates campuses and centers in the U.S., Canada, and Latin America, focusing on language, culture, and academic exchange. It also collaborates with international institutions, such as the **Cervantes Institute in Spain** and **King’s College London**.
- **Notable Contributions:** UNAM has produced **three Nobel laureates** (Alfonso García Robles, Octavio Paz, Mario Molina) and numerous other distinguished figures in science, politics, arts, and academia. It is also recognized for its commitment to **social inclusion**, particularly in technology and women’s empowerment.

### **Legacy and Impact**
UNAM remains a symbol of Mexico’s intellectual and cultural heritage, blending tradition with innovation. Its emphasis on **critical thinking, research, and social responsibility** has made it a cornerstone of Mexican academia and a model for universities worldwide. The university’s motto, *"Para mi pueblo el espíritu hablará"* ("For my people, the spirit shall speak"), reflects its commitment to fostering a new era of cultural and educational unity.
