The University of Melbourne was established in 1853 and is located in Parkville, Melbourne. It has a diverse range of campuses including Southbank, Burnley, and Werribee. The university is organized into nine faculties and has a strong focus on research, with a large cohort of research students. It is known for its academic excellence, with notable rankings in global and national university rankings. The university has a significant international student population and offers a range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs. It has a history of academic and research innovation, including the development of the "Melbourne Model" in 2008, which restructured undergraduate degrees into a two-degree system. The university has faced criticism over its handling of student mental health and student protests, including over investments and research ties. It has a strong student life with over 200 clubs and societies, and a robust student union. The university is also known for its notable alumni, including Nobel laureates, politicians, and prominent public figures.
