The University of Melbourne is an established institution with its primary campus in Parkville, which was founded in 1853. It has expanded its presence with other metropolitan campuses at Burnley, Southbank, and Werribee, as well as regional campuses in Creswick, Dookie, and Shepparton. The university is also planning a new campus in Fishermans Bend, which will serve the Faculty of Engineering and Information Technology and the Faculty of Architecture, Building and Planning.

Academically, the university operates with nine faculties and maintains a diverse collection of libraries across its twelve locations, including the Baillieu Library. It houses specialized museums, such as the Grainger Museum and the Ian Potter Museum of Art, on its Parkville campus. The University of Melbourne has a significant endowment and is recognized internationally, holding a position of #23 in the 2024 Aggregate Ranking of Top Universities, with graduates showing high employer satisfaction rates.
