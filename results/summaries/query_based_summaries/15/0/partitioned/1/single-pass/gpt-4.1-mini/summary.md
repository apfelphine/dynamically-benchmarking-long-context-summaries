# University of Melbourne

The University of Melbourne, established in 1853, is a prominent Australian university with multiple campuses across metropolitan Melbourne including Parkville (the primary campus), Southbank, Burnley, Creswick, Dookie, Shepparton, Werribee, and a planned new campus at Fishermans Bend. The Parkville campus hosts a diverse range of facilities including libraries, gyms, cafés, and is part of a broader knowledge precinct with numerous hospitals and research institutes. The Southbank campus focuses on creative arts and music, housing the Victorian College of the Arts and the Melbourne Conservatorium of Music. Regional campuses such as Creswick and Dookie specialize in forestry and agriculture, respectively, while Werribee is dedicated to veterinary science.

The university is organized into ten faculties encompassing architecture, arts, business, dentistry, education, engineering, law, science, social sciences, and medicine. It has a notable research profile, with extensive library collections and museums like the Grainger Museum and the Ian Potter Museum of Art. The Melbourne Model, implemented in 2008, restructured its curriculum toward generalist undergraduate degrees followed by specialized graduate study. Student life includes over 200 clubs affiliated with the University of Melbourne Student Union and a strong sporting culture managed by Melbourne University Sport, which offers numerous facilities and clubs. Notable alumni include multiple Australian Prime Ministers and Nobel laureates. The University maintains a high national and global academic ranking, with strong graduate employment outcomes and employer satisfaction.
