# University of Melbourne

The University of Melbourne, established by Act of Incorporation in 1853, is a leading Australian research university with its primary campus at Parkville, Melbourne. It has expanded over time to include multiple metropolitan campuses specializing in fields such as horticulture (Burnley), performing and visual arts (Southbank), veterinary science (Werribee), forestry (Creswick), agriculture (Dookie), and rural health (Shepparton). A new engineering campus at Fishermans Bend is planned to open in 2026. The Parkville campus blends historic Gothic Revival architecture with modern facilities and serves as a central knowledge precinct with nearby hospitals and research institutes. The university comprises nine faculties, including Arts, Business and Economics, Engineering and IT, Fine Arts and Music, Law, Medicine, Dentistry and Health Sciences, and Science, offering a range of generalist undergraduate degrees followed by specialized graduate programs per its 2008 "Melbourne Model".

Governance is overseen by a 13-member Council with a Chancellor and Academic Board, while leadership includes a Vice-Chancellor and senior executives. The university has a substantial research profile, with expenditures second only nationally to the CSIRO, and is connected to over 100 research centers and institutes. It manages extensive library resources across twelve libraries and houses specialized museums such as the Grainger Museum, Ian Potter Museum of Art, and the Science Gallery Melbourne. It holds a strong national ranking (#1 in Australia by several metrics) and ranks within the global top 40-50 universities.

Student life at Melbourne features a range of sports teams and esteemed clubs, facilitated by Melbourne University Sport. The university hosts nine independent affiliated residential colleges, primarily around the northern campus, alongside six university-owned residence halls. Its student organizations include the University of Melbourne Student Union and the Graduate Student Association, providing extensive club affiliations and campus services. Notable alumni include multiple Australian Prime Ministers, Nobel Laureates, prominent scientists, artists, and public intellectuals.
