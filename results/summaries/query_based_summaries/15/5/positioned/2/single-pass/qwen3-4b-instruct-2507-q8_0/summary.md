Cornell University was founded in 1865 by Ezra Cornell and Andrew Dickson White as New York's land-grant institution. It began with a campus in Ithaca, New York, and has since expanded to include a main campus, a medical campus in New York City (Weill Cornell Medicine), and a technology-focused campus in New York City (Cornell Tech). The university is known for its strong research programs, with a focus on science, technology, engineering, and medicine. It has a decentralized structure with 16 colleges, including privately endowed and publicly supported statutory colleges, each with significant autonomy. Cornell is renowned for its academic excellence, with a highly competitive admissions process and a strong emphasis on research, innovation, and entrepreneurship. The university has a large endowment of $10.7 billion and is recognized globally in research and education, with consistent top rankings in world university rankings. It is also known for its commitment to sustainability, with initiatives such as the Atkinson Center for a Sustainable Future and a goal to achieve net carbon neutrality by 2035. The university is a leader in entrepreneurship, with the NUS Entrepreneurship Centre and a strong emphasis on student innovation and leadership. Cornell has a diverse student body and a strong international presence, with over 50% of its students from outside the United States. It has a rich history of academic and research achievements, including Nobel laureates, and is a leader in fields such as computer science, engineering, and medicine. The university's academic structure includes a 4–1–4 academic calendar, and it offers a wide range of undergraduate and graduate programs. Cornell's campus life includes a vibrant student life with over 1,000 student organizations, a strong emphasis on community and service, and a strong tradition of student activism. The university is also known for its strong commitment to diversity, equity, and inclusion, with a focus on creating a welcoming and inclusive environment for all students. The university's research output is among the highest in the United States, with significant contributions to fields such as artificial intelligence, energy, and biotechnology. Cornell is also recognized for its strong academic reputation, with consistently high rankings in global university rankings. The university's commitment to innovation and entrepreneurship is reflected in its strong partnerships with industry and its focus on developing student leaders and innovators. The university's history of academic excellence and its continued investment in research and innovation make it one of the most prestigious and influential universities in the United States.
