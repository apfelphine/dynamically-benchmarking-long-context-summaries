Cornell University, established in 1865, is a prominent land-, space-, and sea-grant research university located in Ithaca, New York. It operates on a 4–1–4 academic calendar, with terms beginning in late August, January, and late January, respectively. The university is highly selective, with an acceptance rate of 6.9% for undergraduate admissions in 2022, and a diverse student body, including a significant proportion of first-generation and underrepresented minority students. Cornell is need-blind for U.S. applicants and offers substantial financial aid, including scholarships and need-based grants.

Cornell is renowned for its academic excellence, particularly in research and innovation. It has a large endowment and is classified as an "R1: Doctoral University – Very High Research Activity," ranking 14th among U.S. universities for research expenditures in 2021. The university is known for its contributions to fields like nuclear physics, space exploration, and computing technology, and it regularly ranks among the top U.S. universities for patenting and startup formation.

Cornell’s academic structure includes 16 colleges and schools, with notable programs in the College of Arts and Sciences, College of Engineering, College of Agriculture and Life Sciences, and the College of Veterinary Medicine. The university emphasizes interdisciplinary research and has partnerships with global institutions, including medical schools in Qatar and collaborations with universities worldwide. Cornell’s campus is expansive, featuring historic and modern buildings, extensive green spaces, and a variety of residential options.

Student life at Cornell is vibrant, with over 1,000 student organizations, including clubs, honor societies, and Greek life. The university offers a wide range of extracurricular activities, from sports and performing arts to cultural and political groups. Cornell’s commitment to sustainability includes initiatives like the Atkinson Center for a Sustainable Future and significant investments in renewable energy. The university also provides robust support for student health, housing, and academic advising. Cornell’s legacy includes numerous notable alumni and faculty, contributing to its reputation as one of the leading research institutions in the United States.
