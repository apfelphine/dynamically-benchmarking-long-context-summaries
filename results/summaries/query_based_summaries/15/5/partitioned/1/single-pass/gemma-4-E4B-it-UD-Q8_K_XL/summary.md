**Cornell University** is a large, primarily residential research university that operates on a 4–1–4 academic calendar. It is classified as a land-, space-, and sea-grant university. Cornell is a nonprofit organization with a decentralized structure, comprising 16 colleges, which includes four publicly supported statutory colleges and twelve privately endowed colleges that operate with significant autonomy.

Cornell has several campuses and programs, including its main campus in Ithaca, New York, and the specialized **Cornell Tech** campus on Roosevelt Island in New York City. It also operates **Weill Cornell Medical College in Qatar**, the first U.S. medical school established outside the United States. The university supports international engagement through various study abroad and scholarship programs, and its off-campus research facilities include the Shoals Marine Laboratory and the New York State Agricultural Experiment Station. Cornell University maintains a substantial endowment and is recognized for its research achievements across various disciplines.
