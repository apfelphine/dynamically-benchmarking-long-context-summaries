The **University of Chicago** is a prestigious private research university founded in 1890, initially as a coeducational institution supported by contributions from John D. Rockefeller and other Chicago philanthropists. Located in Chicago’s Hyde Park neighborhood, its main campus spans 217 acres, blending historic architecture with modern facilities. The university is renowned for its rigorous academic programs, particularly in the sciences, social sciences, and humanities, and is a founding member of the Association of American Universities.

### **Academics and Research**
The University of Chicago is organized into the College, four graduate divisions, seven professional schools, and the Graham School of Continuing Liberal and Professional Studies. It offers 51 undergraduate majors, 33 minors, and a wide range of graduate programs, including the Pritzker School of Molecular Engineering, Harris School of Public Policy, and Booth School of Business. The university is known for its core curriculum, which emphasizes critical thinking and interdisciplinary learning.

Research at the University of Chicago is extensive, with over 140 research centers and institutes, including the Fermi National Accelerator Laboratory (Fermilab) and the Argonne National Laboratory. The university has been a leader in fields such as physics, economics, and sociology, with contributions to the Chicago School of Economics and sociology. Notable discoveries include the first controlled nuclear chain reaction (Chicago Pile-1) and the development of radiocarbon dating.

### **Admissions and Reputation**
The University of Chicago is highly selective, with an acceptance rate of around 4.7% for undergraduates. It is consistently ranked among the top universities globally, including 6th in the U.S. News & World Report rankings (2026) and 13th in the QS World University Rankings (2025). The university is known for its intellectual rigor, academic freedom, and commitment to research, attracting students and faculty from around the world.

### **Campus and Student Life**
The main campus features historic buildings like the Main Quadrangles, Rockefeller Chapel, and the Joe and Rika Mansueto Library, which houses a large study space. The university also operates satellite campuses and facilities, including the Chicago House in Luxor, Egypt, and centers in Hong Kong, London, and Paris. Student life includes a variety of clubs, organizations, and traditions, such as the University of Chicago Scavenger Hunt and the annual Kuviasungnerk/Kangeiko winter festival.

### **Notable Features**
- **Core Curriculum**: A foundational set of courses designed to foster critical thinking and interdisciplinary understanding.
- **Research Excellence**: Home to world-class research institutions and collaborations with national laboratories.
- **Professional Schools**: Strong programs in business, law, medicine, and public policy.
- **Global Presence**: Offers study programs and research opportunities in multiple international locations.

The University of Chicago remains a leading institution in higher education, known for its academic excellence, research contributions, and commitment to fostering intellectual inquiry.
