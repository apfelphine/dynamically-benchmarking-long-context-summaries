## University of Chicago

Founded initially in 1857 and re-established in 1890, the University of Chicago is a private, coeducational research university situated in Hyde Park, Chicago. Its modern incarnation was made possible through significant donations from John D. Rockefeller and local philanthropists and leaders such as Marshall Field and Charles L. Hutchinson. The university is known for its distinctive quarter system, rigorous core curriculum, and contributions to academic thought, including founding the Chicago school of economics and sociology. Notable initiatives include the Divinity School, University of Chicago Press, and influential curricular reforms under presidents like William Rainey Harper and Robert Maynard Hutchins. Major achievements include academic movements in the social sciences, critical contributions to the Manhattan Project (the first controlled, self-sustaining nuclear chain reaction in 1942), and ongoing innovations in professional and graduate education.

The campus encompasses 217 acres in Hyde Park and adjacent Woodlawn and is renowned for Collegiate Gothic architecture modeled after Oxford, as well as significant modern additions. The university maintains multiple branch campuses worldwide. Its academic structure comprises a college and divisions in biological, physical, social sciences, humanities, plus seven professional schools. The library system holds over 11 million volumes. Research is central to the university’s mission, managing major national laboratories and leading historic discoveries across science and economics. The University of Chicago consistently ranks among the world’s top universities, with highly selective admissions and a $10.1 billion endowment as of 2024. Its faculty, students, and alumni have earned 101 Nobel Prizes and numerous other honors, and its influence extends deeply into business, government, literature, and the sciences. The university also features a robust student life, with more than 400 organizations, competitive varsity athletics, celebrated traditions, and a vibrant residential system.
