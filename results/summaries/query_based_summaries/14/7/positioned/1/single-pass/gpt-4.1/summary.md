## University of Chicago Summary

The University of Chicago, established in 1890 through donations from the American Baptist Education Society and John D. Rockefeller, is a private research university located on a 217-acre campus in the Hyde Park and Woodlawn neighborhoods of Chicago. Its main campus is noted for its Gothic and modern architectural styles, including landmarks such as the Main Quadrangles, Rockefeller Chapel, Regenstein Library, and Mansueto Library. The university also maintains satellite campuses abroad in Hong Kong, London, and Paris, as well as research centers in Beijing and Delhi. UChicago operates a large private police force for campus safety and collaborates with the city to support Hyde Park’s economy.

Academically, the university comprises an undergraduate college, four divisions of graduate research, and seven professional schools, including notable programs in law, business, medicine, and public policy. UChicago pioneered the core curriculum model, the Socratic method, the Great Books program, and is known for its strong liberal arts foundation. It operates on a quarter system and has a selective admissions process with an acceptance rate of around 4.7%, enrolling over 7,500 undergraduates and nearly 11,000 graduate students as of 2024. The university is accredited by the Higher Learning Commission and manages significant research facilities, including the Fermi National Accelerator Laboratory, Argonne National Laboratory, and the Marine Biological Laboratory. UChicago's libraries house over 11 million volumes, making it one of the largest systems in the nation. The university is recognized globally, consistently ranking among the top institutions for research, business, law, and graduate employability, and boasts 101 Nobel laureates among its faculty and alumni. Numerous traditions, over 400 student organizations, varsity athletics, and a distinctive house-based student residence system contribute to vibrant campus life. The endowment stood at $10.1 billion in 2024, supporting an annual budget of $5.2 billion despite ongoing financial pressures.
