The University of Chicago is a prestigious research university noted for its strong academics, with its law and business schools consistently ranking among the top three professional schools in the United States. In 2025, its business school ranked highly across several reports, and its law school was ranked third in the US by US News & World Report. The university's endowment was the 21st largest in American educational institutions in 2024.

Administratively, the university is governed by a board of trustees, with Paul Alivisatos serving as president since September 1, 2021, and Katherine Baicker as provost since March 2023. Academically, it offers undergraduate degrees in 51 majors, structured across four divisions, and oversees seven professional schools, including the Booth School of Business and the Pritzker School of Medicine. The university is also known for its contributions to academic movements, such as the Chicago school of economics, and for its role in major scientific discoveries.
