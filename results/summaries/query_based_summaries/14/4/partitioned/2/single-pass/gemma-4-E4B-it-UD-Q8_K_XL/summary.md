Stanford University is recognized globally for its success in fostering business and innovation, often serving as a model for technology transfer. Its Office of Technology Licensing is responsible for commercializing university research. The university has a strong venture culture that encourages students to start companies, leading to Stanford alumni-founded companies generating over $2.7 trillion in annual revenue and creating approximately 5.4 million jobs since the 1930s.

The university's academic structure includes seven schools: Humanities and Sciences, Engineering, Sustainability, Law, Medicine, Education, and Business. Stanford operates on a quarter system, and it is classified as an "R1: Doctoral University – Very high research activity." The institution maintains one of the four largest academic endowments in the United States, valued at $36.5 billion as of August 2023. Student life includes traditions such as "Hail, Stanford, Hail!" and the central football rivalry, the Big Game, with UC Berkeley, which involves the exchange of the Stanford Axe.
