**Stanford University** is a private, non-profit research university founded in 1885 by Leland and Jane Stanford as a tribute to their son, Leland Stanford Jr. It opened in 1891 on a 8,180-acre campus in Palo Alto, California, modeled after Cornell University. The university is renowned for its progressive values, including accessibility, inclusivity, and non-sectarian education, and is one of the most prestigious institutions in the United States.

Stanford is classified as an "R1: Doctoral Universities – Very high research activity" and boasts one of the largest academic endowments in the U.S., valued at $36.5 billion as of 2023. Its endowment, which includes $6.6 billion in real estate, supports a wide range of academic and research initiatives. The university is known for its strong emphasis on innovation, with a pioneering technology transfer program that has led to the founding of numerous high-impact companies, including Google, Hewlett-Packard, and Apple.

Academically, Stanford offers a highly selective admissions process with a 4% acceptance rate, emphasizing strong academic performance, extracurricular involvement, and personal qualities. The curriculum follows a quarter system, and the university offers a broad range of undergraduate and graduate programs across seven academic schools, including Humanities and Sciences, Engineering, and Business. The university is particularly noted for its Stanford Graduate School of Business, consistently ranked as the most selective and top-ranked business school globally, and its Stanford Law School, which is consistently ranked among the top law schools.

Stanford's research centers include the SLAC National Accelerator Laboratory, the Hoover Institution, and the Hasso Plattner Institute of Design. The university is home to the Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, a leading online academic resource. It has a strong tradition of student life, with over 600 student organizations, including cultural, academic, and social groups, and a vibrant student government.

The university is known for its innovative student culture, including traditions such as "Fountain Hopping," "Full Moon on the Quad," and the "Big Game" rivalry with the University of California, Berkeley. Stanford has a strong commitment to student well-being and has implemented policies to address issues such as sexual misconduct, including the establishment of a student-led review process and a focus on creating a safe and inclusive campus environment.

Stanford has a diverse student body, with a strong representation of international students and a commitment to equity and access. The university is also recognized for its leadership in sustainability, with a focus on environmental responsibility and innovation in clean energy research.

Stanford's alumni include numerous Nobel laureates, technology leaders, and influential figures in business, politics, and science. The university is widely regarded as a leader in innovation, entrepreneurship, and academic excellence, and continues to shape the future of technology, education, and society.
