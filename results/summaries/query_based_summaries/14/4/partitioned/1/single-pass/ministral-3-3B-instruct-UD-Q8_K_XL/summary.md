Stanford University is a prestigious private research university known for its exceptional academic reputation, groundbreaking research, and significant contributions to technology and innovation. Founded in 1885 by Leland and Jane Stanford, the university is renowned for its endowment valued at $36.5 billion, which supports extensive research and financial aid initiatives. Stanford is highly selective, with an acceptance rate of 4%, and emphasizes a rigorous academic curriculum across its seven schools, including the renowned Stanford Graduate School of Business and Stanford Law School.

The university operates on a quarter system and is recognized for its interdisciplinary approach, fostering collaboration across diverse fields. Stanford’s research expenditures are substantial, with over $1.82 billion spent annually on sponsored projects, and it is classified among "R1: Doctoral Universities – Very high research activity." Stanford is home to numerous influential research centers, including the SLAC National Accelerator Laboratory and the Hoover Institution, and has a strong tradition of intellectual property transfer, leading to the creation of numerous high-impact companies.

Admissions are highly competitive, with a strong emphasis on academic excellence and extracurricular activities. Stanford offers extensive financial aid, including need-based support and a no-loan policy, ensuring accessibility for a diverse student body. The university is also known for its vibrant student life, with over 600 student organizations, a robust Greek life, and a variety of cultural, athletic, and social activities. Traditions such as "Fountain Hopping," the Stanford Marriage Pact, and the Big Game football rivalry against UC Berkeley are integral parts of campus culture.

Stanford’s global reputation is underscored by its rankings, including top positions in U.S. News & World Report, Times Higher Education, and QS World University Rankings. The university’s alumni and faculty have made significant contributions across various fields, including science, technology, business, and politics, with many alumni founding influential companies and holding prominent positions in global leadership.
