Stanford University was founded in 1885 by Leland and Jane Stanford as a tribute to their only child, Leland Stanford Jr. The university officially opened in 1891 on the Stanfords' former Palo Alto farm. Modeled after Cornell University, Stanford emphasized non-sectarian, inclusive education and was known as the "Cornell of the West" in its early years. The campus was designed to emulate English university buildings with local California heritage, incorporating elements of Spanish adobe architecture.

Stanford played a pivotal role in the development of Silicon Valley. In the 1950s, engineering professor Frederick Terman encouraged Stanford graduates to start their own companies, leading to the creation of the Stanford Industrial Park and the rise of the tech industry in the region. The university's aggressive pursuit of government research grants and its culture of innovation fostered a strong venture culture, with over 5.4 million jobs created by companies founded by Stanford alumni.

Stanford is one of the most selective universities in the United States, with an acceptance rate of 4%. It offers a quarter system and is known for its strong emphasis on undergraduate education, research, and entrepreneurship. The university has a large endowment valued at $36.5 billion as of 2023, one of the largest in the U.S., and is home to the Stanford Research Institute, the Hoover Institution, and the SLAC National Accelerator Laboratory.

Stanford's academic programs are organized into seven schools, including the School of Humanities and Sciences, Engineering, and the Graduate School of Business. The university is classified as an R1: Doctoral University – Very high research activity. It is highly ranked globally, with consistent top placements in U.S. News & World Report, Times Higher Education, and QS World University Rankings. Stanford's alumni include numerous leaders in technology, business, and politics, such as Google co-founders Larry Page and Sergey Brin, and former U.S. President Barack Obama.

The university is known for its strong emphasis on innovation, entrepreneurship, and research, with a history of producing groundbreaking discoveries and technologies. Stanford has a vibrant student life with a range of student organizations, sports, and traditions, including the "Big Game" football rivalry with the University of California, Berkeley, and the "Big Game Gaieties" performance. The university also has a strong commitment to academic freedom and has faced criticism regarding its handling of sexual misconduct and diversity issues.
