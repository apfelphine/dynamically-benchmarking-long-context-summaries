**Stanford University** was founded in 1885 by Leland and Jane Stanford to honor their only child, Leland Stanford Jr., and officially opened in 1891. It was initially modeled after Eastern universities, particularly Cornell University, and quickly embraced ideals of being non-sectarian and inclusive of both men and women. The Stanfords designed the campus to emulate English university buildings while incorporating local California heritage, and hired Frederick Law Olmsted to design the campus grounds.

Stanford has established several key academic areas: the Stanford University School of Medicine (established in 1908), the Stanford University Graduate School of Education (1917), and the Stanford Graduate School of Business (1925). The university has a strong reputation for innovation, as its alumni-founded companies generate significant annual revenue. The university is highly ranked globally by various publications, and it is recognized as one of the world's "six super brands" by Times Higher Education.
