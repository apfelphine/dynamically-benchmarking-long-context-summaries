**Stanford University** was established in 1885 by Leland and Jane Stanford, opening in 1891 on their former Palo Alto farm. Initially modeled after Cornell University, Stanford quickly adopted progressive ideals, making higher education non-sectarian and inclusive of both men and women. Its architectural design emulated English university buildings while incorporating California heritage.

The university has seen significant growth, adding four professional graduate schools in the early 20th century, including the Stanford University School of Medicine (established in 1908). Key developments include the founding of the Stanford Graduate School of Business in 1925 and the establishment of the Hoover Institution. During the 1950s, Stanford fostered a high-tech innovation hub with the establishment of Stanford Industrial Park, contributing significantly to the development of Silicon Valley. Stanford is a private, non-profit university administered by a board of trustees, and its endowment is one of the largest academic endowments in the United States.
