## Stanford University Summary

Stanford University was founded in 1885 by Leland and Jane Stanford to commemorate their only child, Leland Stanford Jr., and officially opened in 1891 on the Stanfords' former Palo Alto farm. Modeled after Eastern universities, the Stanfords intended the campus to incorporate elements of local California heritage, specifying designs that resembled early Spanish adobe houses. Key milestones include the establishment of the Stanford University School of Medicine in 1908 and the founding of the Stanford Graduate School of Business in 1925.

The university has a history marked by significant contributions to innovation and research. Engineering professor Frederick Terman encouraged Stanford engineering graduates to start companies, leading to the establishment of Stanford Industrial Park in the 1950s, which contributed to the naming of Silicon Valley. The university has maintained a strong commitment to academic excellence, with its endowment valued at $36.5 billion as of August 2023, making it one of the largest academic endowments in the United States. Stanford is highly regarded in global rankings, having been ranked 1st by Washington Monthly in 2022.
