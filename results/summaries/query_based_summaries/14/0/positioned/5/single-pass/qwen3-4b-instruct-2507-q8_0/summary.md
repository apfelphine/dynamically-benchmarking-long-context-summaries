King's College London is a prestigious research university in central London, founded in 1829 with royal patronage from King George IV. It is part of the "golden triangle" of UK research universities alongside Oxford and Cambridge and is a member of the Russell Group. The university operates across five campuses: the Strand, Waterloo, Guy's, St Thomas', and Denmark Hill, with the Strand Campus housing its main academic faculties and the Guy's Campus serving as a hub for life sciences and medicine. 

Academically, King's offers nine faculties, including arts and humanities, business, dentistry, law, life sciences and medicine, natural sciences, nursing, and social sciences. It is renowned for its research, particularly in medicine, with a strong presence in healthcare education and biomedical research through its King's Health Partners network. The university has a long-standing tradition of academic excellence, with 14 Nobel laureates among its alumni and faculty, and is recognized globally for its contributions to science, medicine, and the humanities.

King's College London is known for its inclusive and research-led culture, including the Associateship of King's College (AKC) and the Fellowship of King's College (FKC), which honor academic and service contributions. The university has a diverse student body, with a significant international student population, and offers a wide range of student life activities, including over 300 societies, sports clubs, and student media. It has a strong reputation for graduate employability, with a gold rating in the Teaching Excellence Framework and high rankings in subject-specific areas such as Health Studies, Social Policy, and Law.

The university has a robust administrative structure governed by a board of trustees and a council, with the President and Principal serving as chief academic and executive officer. King's has a substantial endowment, the fourth-largest in the UK, and is committed to innovation through partnerships such as the Francis Crick Institute and MedCity. Despite historical controversies, including its past ties to eugenics, the university has taken steps to address these issues by renaming buildings and promoting inclusive values. King's is a leading institution in the UK and globally, consistently ranked among the top universities in the world for research, teaching, and graduate outcomes.
