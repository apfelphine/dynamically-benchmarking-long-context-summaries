## King's College London Summary

King's College London operates across several campuses in central London, including the Waterloo Campus, St Thomas' Campus, Guy's Campus, Strand Campus, and Denmark Hill Campus. Key features of its campuses include the Waterloo Campus housing the Florence Nightingale Faculty of Nursing and Midwifery, the Denmark Hill Campus being home to the Institute of Psychiatry, Psychology and Neuroscience (IoPPN), and St Thomas' Campus being located at St Thomas' Hospital, facing the Houses of Parliament. The Strand Campus houses the faculties of arts and humanities, law, natural and mathematical sciences, and social science and public policy.

The institution maintains a comprehensive academic profile, comprising nine faculties, and is involved in numerous affiliations, including being one of the two founding colleges of the federal University of London and a member of the Russell Group. King's offers joint degrees with various international institutions. Its medical research is spread across multiple faculties, with the Faculty of Life Sciences and Medicine operating three main teaching hospitals, and it is a founding member of the Francis Crick Institute. The university is recognized internationally, holding rankings such as 31st by the 2026 QS World University Rankings.
