# Summary of King's College London

King's College London, founded in 1829 under royal patronage, is a prestigious institution rooted in the Church of England tradition but has evolved into a major secular university. It offers a comprehensive range of academic programs across nine faculties, including arts and humanities, business, dentistry, law, life sciences and medicine, natural sciences and engineering, nursing, psychiatry and neuroscience, and social science and public policy. The university is part of the University of London federation and holds memberships in notable academic groups such as the Russell Group and the "golden triangle" of UK research universities. King's has significant partnerships like King's Health Partners, the Francis Crick Institute, and international alliances including the PLuS Alliance. It runs several joint ventures in mathematics and nanotechnology with neighboring universities, and its research income totaled £256.9 million in 2023/24, with high rankings in national and international subject areas.

The university operates multiple campuses in central London: the historical Strand Campus hosts faculties such as arts, humanities, law, science, and business; Guy’s Campus focuses on life sciences, medicine, and dentistry; Waterloo Campus supports nursing, social sciences, and parts of medicine; Denmark Hill Campus houses the Institute of Psychiatry, Psychology and Neuroscience; and St Thomas' Campus provides medical and dental training. King's provides extensive library resources led by the Maughan Library, along with several specialized libraries and museum collections. Its medical education legacy includes the Florence Nightingale Faculty of Nursing, the oldest professional nursing school in the world. Students graduate wearing academic dress designed by Vivienne Westwood, with ceremonies held at notable London venues. The institution boasts a rich history of contributing to significant scientific discoveries, such as the DNA double helix structure. In governance, the university is led by a President and Principal (currently Shitij Kapur), supported by a council and academic board, with a Dean responsible for maintaining King's Anglican heritage.
