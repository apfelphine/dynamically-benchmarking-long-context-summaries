**King's College London** is a prestigious research university in central London, founded in 1829 as a response to the secular nature of London University. It is a constituent college of the University of London and a member of the Russell Group, known for its strong academic reputation, particularly in medicine, law, and the sciences. The university operates across five campuses, including the Strand, Waterloo, Guy's, St Thomas', and Denmark Hill, and has a strong focus on medical education and research, with the Faculty of Life Sciences and Medicine being a major strength.

King's offers a wide range of academic programs through nine faculties, including arts and humanities, business, dentistry, law, life sciences and medicine, and social sciences. It is renowned for its teaching in medicine, nursing, and public policy, and is home to the largest centre for healthcare education in Europe. The university is also known for its unique traditions, such as the Associateship of King's College (AKC), a research-led program in religious and ethical issues, and the Fellowship of King's College (FKC), the highest honor awarded to staff and alumni.

Student life is vibrant, with a large student union offering over 300 societies, sports clubs, and media outlets like *Roar News* and *KCL Radio*. The university has a strong student-led think tank, *King's Think Tank*, and a rich tradition of student activism and engagement. King's has a strong international presence, with a diverse student body and global partnerships, and is recognized for its high graduate employability, ranking 7th in the UK for graduate employability in the Times Higher Education Global Employability University Ranking 2023.

Notable alumni include Nobel laureates such as Peter Higgs, Sir Michael Houghton, and Sir Francis Galton, as well as prominent figures in politics, science, and the arts. The university has a long-standing tradition of religious and ethical engagement, with the Dean of King's College London being an ordained minister of the Church of England. King's is also known for its strong research output, with a significant research income and a strong presence in biomedical sciences, and is recognized globally in rankings such as the QS World University Rankings and Times Higher Education.
