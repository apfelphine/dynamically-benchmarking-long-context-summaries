Noam Chomsky is a prominent American linguist, philosopher, and political activist. Born in 1928, he developed a groundbreaking theory of universal grammar, arguing that humans are born with an innate linguistic capacity, which he detailed in works such as *Syntactic Structures* and *Aspects of the Theory of Syntax*. His theory of generative grammar, which posits that language is governed by internal, biologically inherited rules, revolutionized the field of linguistics and laid the foundation for cognitive science. Chomsky's work challenged behaviorist psychology, emphasizing that language acquisition is not solely learned through environmental stimuli but is instead rooted in an innate cognitive structure.

Beyond linguistics, Chomsky is a leading political dissident known for his critiques of U.S. foreign policy, capitalism, and media propaganda. He has been a vocal critic of American imperialism, advocating for democratic socialism and libertarian socialism, and has argued that the U.S. government systematically distorts information through state-controlled media. His influential work *Manufacturing Consent* outlines a propaganda model for how mainstream media serves elite interests. He has also been a strong opponent of the Israeli–Palestinian conflict, criticizing Israeli military actions and advocating for a two-state solution. Chomsky's political activism includes participation in anti-war protests, co-founding the RESIST collective, and supporting movements for social justice.

Chomsky is widely regarded as one of the most important intellectuals of the 20th century, with a profound influence across linguistics, philosophy, political theory, and social activism. He is known for his commitment to intellectual honesty, free speech, and public education, and has maintained a strong presence in academic and activist circles. Despite facing criticism and controversy—especially regarding his views on Israel and his defense of Holocaust denial—his work continues to inspire global movements for social and political change.
