Garry Kasparov, born Garik Kimovich Weinstein in Baku, Azerbaijan, is a world-renowned chess grandmaster and former world champion. He began studying chess at age seven and rose rapidly through the ranks, winning the Soviet Junior Championship at 13 and becoming the youngest-ever world No. 1 at age 22 in 1984. He defeated Anatoly Karpov in the 1985 World Chess Championship, becoming the youngest world champion in history, and went on to defeat Karpov in five world title matches, with a total of 21 wins, 19 losses, and 104 draws.

Kasparov is known for his dynamic, aggressive playing style, extensive opening preparation, and contributions to opening theory, including co-developing the "Kasparov Gambit" and introducing novel ideas in games. He held the record for the longest time as the world's top-rated player—255 months—from 1985 to 2006, and achieved a peak Elo rating of 2851 in 1999, a record that stood until Magnus Carlsen surpassed it in 2013.

After retiring from competitive chess in 2005, Kasparov remained active in the chess world, coaching Magnus Carlsen and participating in numerous post-retirement events, including simultaneous exhibitions, blitz tournaments, and public games. He also launched Kasparov Chess, an online community offering educational content, and contributed to books and documentaries on chess and decision-making.

Politically, Kasparov has been a vocal critic of Russian President Vladimir Putin, advocating for democracy and opposing authoritarianism. He was a key figure in the "Putin Must Go" campaign, faced multiple arrests and harassment in Russia, and fled the country in 2013 due to fear of persecution. He has remained a prominent voice in international politics, criticizing Putin's policies, the annexation of Crimea, and Russia's actions in Ukraine, and has called for sanctions and regime change.

Kasparov has also been involved in promoting peace and human rights, supporting Armenia’s right to independence, and advocating for democratic reforms. He has been a strong proponent of the use of chess as a tool for education and decision-making, and has written extensively on the parallels between chess and real-world strategy. He is widely regarded as one of the most influential figures in modern chess and a significant voice in global political discourse.
