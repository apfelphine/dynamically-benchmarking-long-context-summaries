**Garry Kasparov** has had a distinguished career in chess and has been highly active in politics and commentary. In chess, he achieved numerous feats, including winning the World Junior Chess Championship in 1980 and becoming the world's No. 1 ranked player in 1984, a record that lasted for twelve years. He was the youngest-ever world champion at age 22, winning the title in 1985 against Anatoly Karpov, and subsequently retained the title through several matches.

Following his retirement from regular competitive chess in 2005, Kasparov became a prominent political commentator, particularly concerning Russia and global affairs. He has been a vocal opponent of the Putin regime, leading opposition efforts and criticizing its policies. His views extend to advocating for human rights and international cooperation, and he has maintained an outspoken stance on various international issues, such as the situation in Ukraine and the Israeli-Palestinian conflict.
