**Frankenstein** centers on Victor Frankenstein, a young scientist obsessed with alchemy and modern sciences, who creates a grotesque humanoid creature from stolen body parts. After the creature awakens, Victor flees in terror, leaving it to fend for itself. The creature, initially fearful of humans, learns language and survival skills, eventually revealing its origins to a family before being chased away. Over time, it grows bitter toward humanity, seeking revenge. Victor, now married to Elizabeth, encounters the creature in Geneva, where it kills his brother William and frames Justine for the crime. Victor, consumed by guilt, travels to the Arctic to confront the creature, who pleads for a female companion to restore its happiness. Victor reluctantly agrees, leading to the creation of a female creature, which the original creature warns will destroy it. The film culminates in the creature’s vengeful attack on Victor’s family, culminating in Victor’s death aboard a ship, while the creature mourns and contemplates self-destruction.

The novel explores themes of creation, responsibility, and the consequences of defying nature, with the creature embodying themes of loneliness, guilt, and the struggle for acceptance. The story’s ambiguous ending leaves the creature’s fate uncertain, reinforcing the novel’s philosophical questions about humanity’s place in the world.
