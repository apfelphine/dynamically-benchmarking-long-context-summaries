**Frankenstein** centers on Victor Frankenstein, a brilliant but obsessive scientist who, after years of experimentation, creates a grotesque yet sentient humanoid creature from stolen body parts. Initially fascinated by his creation, Victor becomes horrified by its monstrous appearance and flees in terror. The creature, now self-aware and driven by loneliness and a desire for companionship, wanders the world, learning human language and observing society. Over time, it grows bitter toward humanity, driven by its own isolation and the cruelty it witnesses.

The creature eventually seeks Victor out, revealing its origins and pleading for a mate to restore its sense of belonging. Victor, torn between his scientific curiosity and his revulsion, agrees to create a female companion. However, his fear and guilt lead him to destroy the incomplete creature before it can be completed, leaving the male alone. The creature, now fully realized, becomes a vengeful force, killing Victor’s loved ones and ultimately confronting him in the Arctic. Victor, consumed by grief and guilt, dies, and the creature, now free from his control, departs, leaving behind a haunting legacy of humanity’s moral dilemmas and the consequences of defying nature. The novel explores themes of creation, responsibility, and the ethical implications of playing god.
