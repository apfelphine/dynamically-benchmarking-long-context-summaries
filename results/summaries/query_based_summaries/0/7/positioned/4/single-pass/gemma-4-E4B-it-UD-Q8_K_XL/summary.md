# Frankenstein Summary

Victor Frankenstein, a young man from an upper-class Genevese family, initially obsessed with alchemy, later pursues modern sciences like chemistry and electricity. Driven by his studies, he creates a large and grotesque humanoid creature by assembling ingredients from charnel houses and fresh graves. Upon its awakening, Victor recoils in terror, and the creature flees. The newly conscious being learns language and literacy by observing a family and reading books, ultimately learning the truth of its origin.

Embittered by humanity's reaction to its appearance, the creature seeks out Victor. It murders William, Victor's brother, and frames the family servant, Justine, for the crime, though Victor does not intervene. Later, the creature asks Victor to create a female companion, which he agrees to. Victor then attempts to destroy the incomplete female, but the original creature warns him it will meet Victor on his wedding night and murders Victor's bride, Elizabeth Lavenza. Victor, who subsequently vows revenge, pursues the creature across the Arctic, ultimately dying from exhaustion while chasing it. The creature boards Victor's ship and mourns his death before departing.
