**Frankenstein** centers on Victor Frankenstein, a brilliant but obsessive scientist who seeks to create life through alchemy and electricity. After assembling a grotesque humanoid creature from stolen body parts, Victor becomes horrified by his creation, abandoning it in the wilderness. The creature, initially isolated and fearful, learns to survive and eventually discovers Victor’s laboratory notes, revealing his true origins. Over time, the creature develops intelligence, language, and a deep sense of loneliness and despair, mirroring the themes of isolation and existential questioning.

Victor, driven by guilt and a desire for redemption, attempts to create a female companion for his creature. However, his obsession leads to tragedy: the creature kills Victor’s loved ones, including his brother and fiancée, before turning on Victor himself. Victor, consumed by grief and vengeance, pursues the creature across the Arctic, only to die aboard a ship while the creature, now consumed by grief and a desire for self-destruction, departs in despair. The novel explores themes of creation, responsibility, guilt, and the consequences of defying nature, with the creature embodying the tragic fallout of Victor’s hubris.
