**Frankenstein** centers on Victor Frankenstein, a scientist obsessed with creating life, who assembles a grotesque humanoid creature from stolen body parts. Initially horrified by his creation, Victor flees, but the creature, now conscious, learns to survive and eventually seeks revenge. Victor’s guilt and obsession lead him to create a female companion for the creature, only to be consumed by his own guilt and the creature’s vengeful actions. The novel explores themes of creation, responsibility, and the consequences of defying nature, with Victor’s tragic downfall serving as a cautionary tale about the dangers of unchecked ambition and the ethical implications of playing god.
