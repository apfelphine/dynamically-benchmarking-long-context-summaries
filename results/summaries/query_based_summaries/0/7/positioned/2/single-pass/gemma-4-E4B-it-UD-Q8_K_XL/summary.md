***Frankenstein*** details Victor Frankenstein's obsession with alchemy and modern sciences, leading him to create a grotesque humanoid creature by assembling ingredients from charnel houses and graves. Upon the creature's awakening, Victor flees in terror. The newly conscious creature learns to speak and write from observing a family, eventually discovering his creator's identity in the books he reads. Embittered by humanity, the creature travels to Geneva to confront Victor and his brother, William, whom the creature subsequently kills and frames the family servant, Justine, for. Victor later consents to the creature's request to create a female companion, but Victor destroys the incomplete female creature. The monster confronts Victor on Mer de Glace, recounting his story, and asks for a mate. Victor agrees, but the creature murders Victor's bride, Elizabeth Lavenza, on their wedding night. Victor vows revenge, pursues the creature to the Arctic, and nearly dies of exhaustion, being rescued by Captain Robert Walton. The creature boards the ship, mourns Victor's death, and departs, intending to burn himself on a pyre.

The Creature expresses deep misery, lamenting that he was benevolent but misery turned him into a fiend, appealing to Victor for happiness and virtue. Victor's subsequent pursuit of the creature is a quest for vengeance. The Creature compares himself to Adam and the fallen angel, Lucifer, after reading Milton's *Paradise Lost*. The novel explores themes of loss, guilt, and the consequences of defying nature, stemming from Mary Shelley's personal experiences with her mother's death and the loss of her first child.
