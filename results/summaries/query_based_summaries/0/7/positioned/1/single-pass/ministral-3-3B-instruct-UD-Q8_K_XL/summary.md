**Frankenstein** centers on Victor Frankenstein, a brilliant but obsessive scientist who creates a grotesque humanoid creature from body parts. Initially repelled by his creation, Victor abandons it, which grows angry and embittered, seeking revenge. The creature, now aware of its origins, travels to Geneva to confront Victor, revealing his true nature and pleading for a female companion to ensure his happiness. Victor reluctantly agrees, but his obsession leads to a tragic confrontation, culminating in the creature’s destruction of Victor’s family and his own demise. The novel explores themes of isolation, the consequences of defying nature, and the moral dilemmas of scientific ambition. The story is framed by the creature’s reflections on his creation and the tragic consequences of Victor’s hubris.
