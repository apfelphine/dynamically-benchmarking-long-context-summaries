# Star Trek: The Motion Picture

*Star Trek: The Motion Picture* is set in the 23rd century when Starfleet monitoring station Epsilon Nine detects an alien entity moving toward Earth within a massive energy cloud, which destroys three Klingon warships and Epsilon Nine. The starship Enterprise, undergoing a major refit, is assigned by Starfleet Command to intercept this cloud entity.

During the interception, the Enterprise is attacked by an alien vessel, which sends a robotic replica to study the ship's "carbon units." Commander Willard Decker becomes troubled by the replica's connection to the navigator, Ilia. Upon an unauthorized spacewalk, Spock discovers that the entire vessel is V'Ger, a non-biological living machine that is actually a damaged 20th-century NASA space probe, Voyager 6. V'Ger had been upgraded by an alien race of living machines after gathering vast amounts of knowledge, causing it to find its existence meaningless. To finish its mission, V'Ger demands a "Creator" come in person. Decker offers himself, merging with the Ilia probe and V'Ger, which results in the creation of a new life form that disappears into space, saving Earth.
