## Star Trek: The Motion Picture Summary

The film involved extensive redesigns of the *Enterprise* sets; the engine room was designed to appear vast using forced perspective, while the corridors moved away from the original "Des Moines Holiday Inn Style" to feature an angular, floor-radiated lighting scheme. The transporter was redesigned to appear more powerful, incorporating a sealed control room and complex machinery. Major construction efforts included the recreation deck, which occupied an entire soundstage, and the V'ger set, known by staff as "the Coliseum."

Technically, the film relied heavily on special effects, with Douglas Trumbull later taking primary responsibility for optical effects after early issues with Robert Abel and Associates. The V'Ger entity was revealed to be a 20th-century NASA probe, Voyager 6, which achieved sentience after gathering knowledge. The story culminates when Commander Decker merges with the Ilia probe and V'Ger, creating a new life form, while James T. Kirk directs the *Enterprise* into space for future missions. The film earned multiple nominations, including for Best Art Direction and Best Original Score.
