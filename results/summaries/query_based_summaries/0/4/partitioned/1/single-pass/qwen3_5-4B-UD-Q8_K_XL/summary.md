# The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy

*The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy* is a science fiction saga originally conceived as a trilogy of radio episodes, later expanded into five novels by Douglas Adams, a sixth novel written by Eoin Colfer, and a 2005 film. The narrative follows Arthur Dent, an ordinary Englishman saved from Earth's destruction by the Vogons to make way for a hyperspace bypass, who joins forces with Ford Prefect, Zaphod Beeblebrox, Marvin the Paranoid Android, and Trillian. Their journey includes the discovery that Earth was a supercomputer used to calculate the "Answer to the Ultimate Question of Life, the Universe, and Everything," which was revealed to be the simple number 42. The story spans various adaptations, including a 1981 BBC television series and multiple radio phases, with plot variations across versions.

The franchise features numerous adaptations and cultural impacts, including an 2005 film directed by Garth Jennings and a 2018 radio series. Merchandising includes "Hitch-Hikeriana" such as towels, which became the basis for "Towel Day," and various toys. The series has influenced popular culture, inspiring works like Radiohead's song "Paranoid Android" and numerous video games. Douglas Adams passed away in 2001, having planned a sixth novel that was completed by Colfer, titled *And Another Thing...*, which continues the adventures of the characters in a new timeline.
