The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy follows the adventures of Arthur Dent, an Englishman whose life is upended by the Vogons, who are destroying Earth to make way for a hyperspace bypass. Arthur's journey intersects with several key characters: Ford Prefect, an alien researcher for the eponymous guidebook; Zaphod Beeblebrox, the eccentric Galactic President; the depressed robot Marvin the Paranoid Android; and Trillian, another human survivor.

During their travels, Arthur learns that Earth was actually a giant supercomputer designed by Deep Thought to calculate the "Ultimate Question of Life, the Universe, and Everything," which yielded the answer "42." The narrative, which follows the events of the first two novels, features the characters traveling to Magrathea and eventually heading toward The Restaurant at the End of the Universe. The series has been adapted across various media, including radio, novels, and film.
