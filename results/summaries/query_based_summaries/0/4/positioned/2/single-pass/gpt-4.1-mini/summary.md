# Summary of *The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy*

*The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy* is a multi-format science fiction series created by Douglas Adams, following the adventures of Arthur Dent after Earth's destruction by bureaucratic Vogons. Central characters include Ford Prefect, an alien researcher for the Guide; Zaphod Beeblebrox, the eccentric Galactic President; Marvin, a depressed robot; and Trillian, the other surviving human. The narrative reveals Earth as a massive supercomputer designed to discover the "Ultimate Question of Life, the Universe, and Everything," providing the answer "42," though the true question remains unknown.

Originating from a late-1970s BBC radio series, the story evolved through radio sequels, novels, a 1981 television series, stage shows, and a 2005 film adaptation featuring Martin Freeman and Stephen Fry. The Guide itself serves as a fictional electronic manual, with excerpts punctuating the narrative. The series is notable for its varied spellings of the title and its meta-humor, often addressing scientific concepts with satirical wit. Its enduring cultural impact includes inspiring "Hitch-Hikeriana" merchandise, conventions like Towel Day, and numerous adaptations across different media, cementing its place as a seminal work of comic science fiction.
