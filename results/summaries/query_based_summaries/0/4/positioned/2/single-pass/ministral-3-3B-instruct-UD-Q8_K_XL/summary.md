*The Hitchhiker’s Guide to the Galaxy* is a science fiction series centered around the adventures of Arthur Dent, a hapless Englishman, as Earth is destroyed by the Vogons to make way for a hyperspace bypass. Arthur is rescued by Ford Prefect, an alien guidebook researcher, who introduces him to the eccentric semi-cousin Zaphod Beeblebrox, the Galactic President, and his daughter Trillian (formerly Tricia McMillan). The group embarks on a journey through the galaxy, encountering various characters, including the depressed robot Marvin the Paranoid Android and the enigmatic Guide itself, which narrates their escapades.

The series explores themes of absurdity, existential questions, and the absurdity of human existence. The narrative frequently incorporates excerpts from the fictional *Hitchhiker’s Guide to the Galaxy*, which serves as a running commentary on the absurdities of life and the universe. Arthur and his companions visit planets, encounter alien civilizations, and navigate the complexities of interstellar politics and technology. The story culminates in a journey to the *Restaurant at the End of the Universe*, symbolizing the ultimate absurdity and the futility of seeking meaning in a vast, indifferent cosmos.
