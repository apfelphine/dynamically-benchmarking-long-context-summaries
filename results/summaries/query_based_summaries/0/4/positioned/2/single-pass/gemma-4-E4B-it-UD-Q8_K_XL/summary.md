*The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy* follows the adventures of Arthur Dent, an Englishman, after Earth is destroyed by the Vogons, a race of bureaucratic aliens, to make way for a hyperspace bypass. Arthur's journey intersects with several key characters, including Ford Prefect, an alien researcher for the eponymous guidebook, Zaphod Beeblebrox, the Galactic President, and Trillian, the only other human survivor of Earth's destruction.

Throughout their travels, Arthur learns that Earth was actually a massive supercomputer created by another, known as Deep Thought. This computer was built to calculate the "Ultimate Question of Life, the Universe, and Everything," which, after eons of computation, yielded the answer "42." Arthur and his companions eventually embark on further adventures, including a stop at The Restaurant at the End of the Universe, before continuing their travels through the galaxy.
