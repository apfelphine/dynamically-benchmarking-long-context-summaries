## The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy

**The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy** is a comedic science fiction franchise created by Douglas Adams, initially as a BBC Radio 4 series in 1978. The story follows Arthur Dent, an Englishman whose adventures begin when the Earth is destroyed by Vogons to make way for a hyperspace bypass. He is rescued by alien Ford Prefect, and joined by eccentric figures including Zaphod Beeblebrox, Trillian, and Marvin the Paranoid Android. Central to its plot is the titular Guide, an electronic compendium of intergalactic knowledge. Across various adaptations—radio, novels, television, film, and stage—the narrative retains core elements like the search for the "Ultimate Question of Life, the Universe, and Everything" (with the answer humorously revealed as "42") and the revelation that Earth itself was a supercomputer designed to find this Question.

The Guide has appeared in numerous formats: five primary novels (later six, with Eoin Colfer's **And Another Thing...**), multiple radio adaptations (latest being the Hexagonal Phase with contributions from Stephen Hawking), a much-loved 1981 BBC TV series, a 2005 film, and stage productions. Its legacy is marked by cultural references (such as the annual "Towel Day"), predictions about real-world technology (like tablet computers and instant translation), and widespread influence in science fiction. The series is distinguished by its satirical take on bureaucracy, technology, and cosmic absurdity, spawning a dedicated fanbase and various merchandising items ("Hitch-Hikeriana"), as well as references and homages throughout popular culture.
