The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy follows the adventures of Arthur Dent, a hapless Englishman, after the Earth is destroyed by the Vogons, an unpleasant and bureaucratic alien race, to make way for a hyperspace bypass. Dent's journey intersects with several characters, including Ford Prefect, an alien and researcher for the eponymous guidebook, Zaphod Beeblebrox, the Galactic President, the depressed robot Marvin the Paranoid Android, and Trillian, the only other human survivor of Earth's destruction.

Through their travels, Arthur discovers that the Earth was actually a giant supercomputer built to calculate the "Ultimate Question of Life, the Universe, and Everything," which Deep Thought determined was "42." Following the destruction of the Earth supercomputer, Arthur becomes a target of the descendants of the Deep Thought creators, believing his mind holds the true Question. With the help of his friends, Arthur escapes, leading to further galactic adventures before they eventually reach The Restaurant at the End of the Universe.
