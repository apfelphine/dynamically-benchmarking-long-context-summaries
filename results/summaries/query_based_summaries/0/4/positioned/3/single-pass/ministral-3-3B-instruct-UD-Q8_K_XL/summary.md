*The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy* is a satirical science fiction series centered on the adventures of Arthur Dent, a hapless human who survives the destruction of Earth by the Vogons to embark on a journey through the galaxy. The story follows Arthur alongside his alien friend Ford Prefect, who is a researcher for the fictional guidebook *The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy*. Along the way, Arthur encounters eccentric characters like Zaphod Beeblebrox, the Galactic President, Marvin the Paranoid Android, and Trillian, a woman who becomes Arthur’s companion. The series explores themes of absurdity, existentialism, and the futility of human existence, often through the lens of the Guide’s cryptic entries and the bizarre encounters Arthur and his friends have.

The narrative unfolds across multiple media, including novels, radio series, television adaptations, and a film. The core plot revolves around Arthur’s attempts to navigate the absurdities of interstellar travel, encountering both humorous and tragic situations. The Guide itself serves as a running gag, providing nonsensical advice and commentary on Arthur’s journey. The series is renowned for its wit, humor, and philosophical musings on life, the universe, and everything—culminating in the iconic line, *"The answer to the Ultimate Question of Life, the Universe, and Everything is 42."*
