**The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy** chronicles the adventures of Arthur Dent, an Englishman, following the destruction of Earth by the Vogons, a bureaucratic race, to make way for a hyperspace bypass. Arthur's journey intersects with several characters, including Ford Prefect, an alien researcher for the eponymous guidebook, Zaphod Beeblebrox, the Galactic President who possesses the Heart of Gold, the depressed robot Marvin the Paranoid Android, and Trillian.

During their travels, Arthur learns that Earth was actually a massive supercomputer built by another supercomputer, Deep Thought, to calculate the "Ultimate Question of Life, the Universe, and Everything," which yielded the answer "42." Moments after these calculations, Earth was destroyed, leading Arthur to become the target of the descendants of the Deep Thought creators, who believe his mind holds the actual Question. With his friends' aid, Arthur escapes and embarks on further adventures, eventually heading toward the Restaurant at the End of the Universe.
