The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy is a science fiction series created by Douglas Adams, following the adventures of Arthur Dent, a hapless Englishman, after the destruction of Earth by the Vogons to make way for a hyperspace bypass. Dent is rescued by Ford Prefect, a roving alien researcher for the eponymous guidebook, and joins him on a journey that intersects with other characters including Zaphod Beeblebrox, the eccentric Galactic President, Marvin the Paranoid Android, and Trillian, a woman Arthur once met. Throughout their travels, they learn that Earth was actually a giant supercomputer built by another supercomputer, Deep Thought, which had calculated the answer to the "Ultimate Question of Life, the Universe, and Everything" as "42." After Earth is destroyed, Arthur becomes a target of Deep Thought's creators, who believe his mind holds the actual question. With the help of his friends, Arthur escapes and they decide to have lunch at The Restaurant at the End of the Universe, beginning a series of further adventures.

The series originated as a radio show in 1978 and has since been adapted into novels, a television series, a film, and stage productions. The novels, described as a "trilogy in five parts," expand on the radio episodes with more detailed plots and character development. The stories are punctuated by excerpts from the Guide, an electronic book that serves as a narrative device. The series is known for its humor, absurdity, and philosophical themes, including the nature of humanity, the meaning of life, and the absurdity of bureaucracy. The title is spelled with or without a hyphen, and Adams himself preferred "Hitchhiker's Guide" as the standard spelling. The series has had a lasting cultural impact, with references to its themes and characters appearing in popular culture, including the number 42 being widely recognized as a humorous answer to a profound question. The series has also inspired a range of merchandise, including towels, T-shirts, and collectibles, with Towel Day being a fan-created event celebrated on May 25. The series has been praised for its originality and wit, and has become a landmark work in science fiction.
