The *Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy* is a science fiction series by Douglas Adams, following the adventures of Arthur Dent, an ordinary Englishman, after the destruction of Earth by the Vogons to make way for a hyperspace bypass. Dent is rescued by Ford Prefect, an alien researcher for the eponymous guidebook, and joins a group of travelers including Zaphod Beeblebrox, the eccentric Galactic President, Marvin the Paranoid Android, and Trillian, a woman Arthur once met. Their journey reveals that Earth was actually a supercomputer created by another supercomputer, Deep Thought, which calculated the answer to the "Ultimate Question of Life, the Universe, and Everything" as "42." After the Earth is destroyed, Arthur and his companions face various challenges, including encounters with alien races and the pursuit of answers to profound existential questions. The story culminates with the group preparing to visit the Restaurant at the End of the Universe, where they confront the deeper mysteries of existence. The series has been adapted into multiple formats, including radio series, novels, a film, stage shows, audiobooks, video games, and comic books, and has become a cultural touchstone, popularizing concepts like the number 42 and the use of a towel as a universal tool for hitchhikers.
