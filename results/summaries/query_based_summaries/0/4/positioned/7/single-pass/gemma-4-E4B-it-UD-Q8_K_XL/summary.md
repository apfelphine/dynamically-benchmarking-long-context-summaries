# The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy

The core narrative of *The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy* follows Arthur Dent, a hapless Englishman, after Earth is destroyed by the Vogons, an unpleasant and bureaucratic alien race, to make way for a hyperspace bypass. Arthur's adventures intersect with several characters, including Ford Prefect, an alien researcher for the eponymous guidebook; Zaphod Beeblebrox, Ford's eccentric semi-cousin and Galactic President who possesses the Heart of Gold; the depressed robot Marvin the Paranoid Android; and Trillian.

During their travels, Arthur discovers that the Earth was actually a massive supercomputer built by another supercomputer, Deep Thought. Deep Thought was tasked with calculating the "Ultimate Question of Life, the Universe, and Everything," which, after eons, yielded the absurdly simple answer, "42." The Earth supercomputer was destroyed just before the answer could be communicated, and Arthur becomes the target of the descendants of Deep Thought's creators, believing his mind holds the true Question. The series follows their escape, leading them to The Restaurant at the End of the Universe before further galactic adventures.
