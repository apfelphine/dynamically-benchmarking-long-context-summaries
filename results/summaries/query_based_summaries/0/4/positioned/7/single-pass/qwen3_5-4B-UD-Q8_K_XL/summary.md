**The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy** is a science fiction franchise created by Douglas Adams, spanning radio series, novels, a film, and various adaptations. The story primarily follows Arthur Dent, an ordinary Englishman who is rescued from Earth's destruction by the Vogons—a bureaucratic alien race clearing the planet for a hyperspace bypass—alongside the intergalactic researcher Ford Prefect. Arthur discovers that Earth was actually a supercomputer built by Deep Thought to solve the "Ultimate Question of Life, the Universe, and Everything," which was revealed to be the number "42." Despite the answer being known, the question itself remains unsolved, prompting the Vogons to destroy Earth before the answer could be communicated.

The narrative evolves across multiple versions, featuring eccentric characters such as Zaphod Beeblebrox, the Galactic President with two heads and three hearts, and Marvin the Paranoid Android. Key plotlines include the group's journey to the Restaurant at the End of the Universe, encounters with the planet Magrathea, and adventures with the Golgafrinchan civilization. The franchise is noted for its humor, satire of science and bureaucracy, and iconic elements like the "Babel Fish" and the "Do Not Panic" badge. The story has been adapted into numerous formats, including a 2005 film and multiple radio series, cementing its legacy as a seminal work in science fiction.
