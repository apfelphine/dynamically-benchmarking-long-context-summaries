*The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy* follows the adventures of Arthur Dent, an Englishman who survives the destruction of Earth by the Vogons, a group of unpleasant and bureaucratic aliens tasked with making way for a hyperspace bypass. Dent's journey intersects with several characters: Ford Prefect, an alien researcher for the eponymous guidebook who rescues Dent, Zaphod Beeblebrox, the eccentric Galactic President who possesses the Heart of Gold, and Trillian.

Throughout their travels, Arthur learns that Earth was actually a giant supercomputer, created by another, called Deep Thought, to calculate the "Ultimate Question of Life, the Universe, and Everything," which yielded the simple answer, "42." The Guide itself is an electronic guidebook to the universe, and the series is frequently punctuated with excerpts from it. The narrative in various adaptations is substantially rewritten by Adams, though the core plot remains consistent across the different versions.
