*The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy* follows the adventures of Arthur Dent, an Englishman, after his Earth is destroyed by the Vogons, an alien bureaucratic race, to make way for a hyperspace bypass. Dent is rescued by Ford Prefect, an alien researcher for the eponymous guidebook. Their journeys involve several other characters, including Zaphod Beeblebrox, the Galactic President, the depressed robot Marvin the Paranoid Android, and Trillian. Arthur eventually learns that the Earth was a giant supercomputer designed to determine the "Ultimate Question of Life, the Universe, and Everything," which Deep Thought calculated to be "42."

The series is extensive, with various novels and radio adaptations that can be contradictory due to substantial rewrites by Adams for each new version. The fictional Guide itself is an electronic guidebook to the universe. The original concept for the series arose from a proposal called "The Ends of the Earth," and the title was reportedly inspired by a 1971 incident while Adams was hitchhiking in Europe.
