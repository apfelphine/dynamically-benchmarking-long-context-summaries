The *Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy* is a science fiction series created by Douglas Adams, following the adventures of Arthur Dent, a hapless Englishman, after Earth is destroyed by the Vogons to make way for a hyperspace bypass. Dent is rescued by Ford Prefect, a mysterious alien researcher, and joins a group of companions including Zaphod Beeblebrox, the eccentric Galactic President, Marvin the Paranoid Android, and Trillian, a woman Arthur once met. Throughout their journey, they learn that Earth was actually a giant supercomputer created by another supercomputer, Deep Thought, which calculated the answer to the "Ultimate Question of Life, the Universe, and Everything" as "42." After Earth's destruction, Arthur and his friends seek to uncover the true nature of the question and the fate of the Earth's creators. The series includes a variety of adaptations, such as radio series, novels, a television series, films, stage shows, and video games, each expanding on the original story with different plotlines and character developments. The title has been spelled in various ways across editions, with Adams later stating he preferred "Hitchhiker's Guide" as the correct spelling. The franchise is known for its humor, absurdity, and philosophical undertones, with the number 42 becoming a cultural reference in popular culture.
